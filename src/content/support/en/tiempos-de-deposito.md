Since Cord does not hold funds, the time it takes for the money to reach your bank account depends entirely on the payment method your client chose.

### 1. Direct transfer (SPEI in Mexico, bank transfer elsewhere)

If the client pays you manually by making a bank transfer to the payout account you registered in Cord Settings — the CLABE in Mexico, or the IBAN, routing number, sort code, or other local format depending on your country — the money **goes directly to your bank immediately**. Cord is not in the middle of this transaction. You must log in to Cord and mark the quote as paid.

### 2. Online payments

If you activated Cord Payments and the customer pays by card (or by SPEI, for businesses billing in Mexican pesos), the money enters your connected payment account.
- Timing depends on method, country, risk review, and account history; there is no universal deposit date.
- Go to **Payments** to review the status and estimated date of each deposit. A successful payment can appear before its bank deposit.
