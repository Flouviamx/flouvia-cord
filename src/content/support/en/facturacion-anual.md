If you already use Cord on a monthly plan, you can switch to **annual billing** and save: the annual cycle charges you for 10 months instead of 12 — that is, **2 months free**.

### How to switch to annual

1. Go to **Settings > Plans & billing > Subscription** (`/app/ajustes/plan`).
2. Toggle **Annual** in the plan selector.
3. Confirm in the secure form. The annual charge is made upfront and your subscription renews every 12 months.

### Mid-year plan changes

If you paid annually and then upgrade (for example from Professional to Scale), Cord shows and applies the **proration** for the remaining period before you confirm the change.

### Need your invoice (CFDI) for the charge?

If you need a CFDI for what you pay Cord, request it from our team at `soporte@flouvia.com` with your tax details and we'll issue it.

> Do not confuse this with charging your own clients: monthly retainers are configured from a recurring quote and have a separate cycle.
