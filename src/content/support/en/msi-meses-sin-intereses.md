---
title: "Months Without Interest (MSI)"
description: "Current availability of installment card payments in Cord."
category: "Payments & Deposits"
---

Cord Payments currently supports **a single card payment** and **SPEI transfer**. Months Without Interest are not yet available on the payment link.

### What the customer sees

The selector only shows methods Cord can reconcile end to end. Enabling MSI in an external account does not make it appear in Cord.

If you need installments today, configure scheduled installments with dates and amounts inside the quote. Each installment is collected as an independent transaction; it is not equivalent to interest-free bank financing.
