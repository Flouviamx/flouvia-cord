Cord's autonomous collections is an AI agent that chases your overdue receivables for you, with a professional tone and without your team lifting a finger. **It's off by default:** it only steps in once you enable it for your business.

### When it acts

The agent only writes when a quote's credit terms have **actually lapsed**. The due date is calculated from approval plus the days of the term (due on receipt or Net 7, 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90), with a few days of grace before the first reminder. It never bothers a client who is up to date.

### What it does

1. **Reminder with a real payment link.** Every email includes a button that goes straight to paying the exact outstanding amount (card, or SPEI for businesses billing in Mexican pesos), directly to your bank. If you don't have online payments active, the button points to the public link with your bank transfer details.
2. **Adaptive tone.** The agent adjusts its tone based on days overdue: friendly at first, firmer as time passes.
3. **Negotiates installments when needed.** If the client is several days overdue and can't pay in full, the agent can agree to a plan of 2 or 3 monthly installments that add up to the exact amount owed (no discounts), automatically creating the payable charges for each one.

### Where it lives

The agent lives inside **Collections**. In the sidebar, open **Collections** (in the **Revenue** group, shortcut `G` then `B`): **AI agent** appears right below it, indented, while you're on Collections or on the agent itself. You can also get there from the agent tab at the top of the Collections page, or by typing "agent" in the top bar search (`⌘ K` on Mac, `Ctrl K` on Windows).

**Settings › Artificial intelligence** shows whether it's off, in approval mode or automatic, with a button that takes you to its screen.

### How to enable it

1. Go to **Collections › AI agent**. The first time, it shows how many accounts it would email today and a sample of its email.
2. Click **Turn on with approval**. In this mode the agent drafts each email and leaves it in the **Approval inbox**: nothing goes out until you approve it, and before that you can edit it, ask it to rewrite it, or discard it.
3. Once you trust its wording, switch it to **Automatic** and emails go out on their own. The inbox then only shows the ones that couldn't be delivered.

With **Configure**, on the same screen, you set the mode, tone, waiting days and who not to email. You can turn it off at any time. Turning it on or configuring it requires the Settings permission; if you don't have it, ask an admin.

> [!NOTE]
> By enabling this feature you authorize Cord to contact your clients on your behalf about overdue balances. Make sure you have a legitimate commercial relationship and accurate receivables data. The agent uses AI and its wording may vary.
