Reconciling payments can be complex, especially if you receive multiple bank transfers or consolidated deposits.

### Manual transfer reconciliation

Cord does not have direct visibility into your bank statement. To reconcile transfers:
1. When you receive a deposit, search for the quote's folio or the client's tax id in Cord (the RFC in Mexico; the NIF/CIF, EIN, NIT, or other local identifier elsewhere).
2. Open the invoice (or the quote, if you have not invoiced yet) and click **Record payment** with the amount, method and reference.
3. The balance is recalculated and, once it reaches zero, the invoice becomes **Paid**. Recording a payment **does not stamp** the invoice: you issue the CFDI yourself. In **Mexico**, if the invoice is already a PPD CFDI, Cord issues that payment's complement right away. In every other country only the business status changes; nothing is submitted to any authority.

### Online payment reconciliation

If your clients pay online (card, SPEI in Mexico, SEPA or ACH direct debit, or from their portal) in any country where Cord Payments is available:
1. Cord records the payment after receiving a signed confirmation from the network.
2. Go to **Payments** to relate the quote number, charge, fee, net amount, and bank deposit.
3. Export the period report and reconcile it with your bank statement.
