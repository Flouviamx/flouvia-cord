---
title: "Cash Payment Reception"
description: "Current availability of cash payments."
category: "Payments & Deposits"
---

Cord Payments does not currently support OXXO vouchers or convenience-store payments. The public link offers card or SPEI transfer when those methods are enabled for the organization.

### Record a payment received outside Cord

If you accept cash independently, open the quote and record the payment manually after confirming receipt. Keep the receipt and apply the appropriate tax treatment. Cord does not verify or reconcile cash.
