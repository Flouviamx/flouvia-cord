A quote in Cord is designed to be a legally valid and persuasive commercial proposal.

### Quote Anatomy

A winning proposal consists of four main blocks:

1. **Header:** Contains your company logo, your tax ID (RFC in Mexico, NIF/CIF in Spain, EIN/Tax ID in the United States, and so on depending on your account's country), the issue date, and the Folio Number (e.g., `COT-2023-085`). It also shows who the proposal is addressed to.
2. **Line Items:** This is the main table where you break down your products or services. Each item can have a long description with bullet points, an image, unit price, quantity, discounts, and its own tax rate — you can mix a taxed line with an exempt one in the same quote.
3. **Legal and Credit Terms:** Here you define the rules of the game. For example: "This proposal is valid for 15 days. Payment must be made within 30 calendar days (Net 30) after acceptance."
4. **Closing Block (Signature/Payment):** This is the Call To Action (CTA). Depending on your setup, the client will see a button to electronically sign or a module to enter their card and pay the advance immediately.

**Customization:** You can change the accent colors and logo from **Settings > Branding**, and the welcome message on the public link from **Settings > Client portal**, to match your brand manual.
