---
title: "Reasons for Declined Payments"
description: "Common bank error codes and how to fix them."
category: "Payments & Deposits"
---

A payment may be declined by the issuing bank, incomplete authentication, incorrect details, or network risk controls.

### Identify the reason in Cord

If a customer reports that their card does not go through when trying to pay a quote:
1. Go to **Payments** and open the failed attempt.
2. Cord shows a safe explanation and support reference without exposing internal fraud controls.
3. Ask the customer to review their details, complete any bank authentication, and try again.

**Recommended Solution:**
If the problem continues, the customer should contact their bank. Share the Cord reference with support so the team can investigate without requesting full card numbers.
