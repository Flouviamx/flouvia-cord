The secret to closing B2B deals faster is acting at the exact moment your prospect is reading your proposal. Cord equips you with silent tracking capabilities.

### What does the system monitor?

From the moment you send the quote link via email or WhatsApp:
- **Open:** You will know the exact day and time the prospect opened the quote ("viewed" event in the timeline).
- **Viewing now:** While the client has the quote open, you see it live on the detail view with the "Viewing now" indicator (depending on your plan).
- **Decision:** Every approval, rejection, counteroffer, or comment from the client is recorded in that quote's timeline.

### Inside the app: the bell

The bell in the top bar gathers what the client did and the money that came in, for quotes and invoices: viewed the quote or the invoice, approved, rejected, made a counteroffer, sent you a message, made a partial payment or paid. A counter tells you how many updates you haven't seen (1 to 9, then "9+"). Your own actions —sending, editing, creating a version— don't show up there. Each alert opens its document. Details in [Getting around Cord](/en/support/moverse-por-cord).

### Configure email, Slack and Teams notifications

Go to **Settings > Notifications** (the gear in the top bar takes you to Settings). There you'll find an event × channel matrix (email, Slack and Microsoft Teams; the last two once you connect their channel) where you turn each alert on or off independently:
- Quote viewed for the first time.
- Quote approved.
- Quote rejected.
- Payment received.
- Quote about to expire.
- Payment overdue.
- A new member joins the team (email only; this one doesn't post to Slack or Teams).

There are no native browser-push or mobile-app notifications: besides the bell in the app, the channels available today are email, Slack and Microsoft Teams.
