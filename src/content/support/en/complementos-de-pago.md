This applies to organizations in **Mexico**. When you issue an invoice under the PPD (Payment in Installments or Deferred) payment method, the SAT requires a "Payment Receipt Supplement" (REP) for every installment you receive.

### What Cord does today

From the invoice detail page you can click **Register Payment** and enter the amount, date, and method. Cord adds that installment to the invoice balance, reflects it in the detail view and in collections, and marks the invoice as settled once the balance reaches zero.

<Callout type="warning">
Registering a payment in Cord **does not generate or stamp the REP** with the SAT. Automatic stamping of the Payment Receipt Supplement is on our roadmap and isn't available yet. If you invoice under PPD, keep generating the corresponding REP on your own (with your accountant or your usual invoicing system) every time you register an installment in Cord — correct tax treatment of each payment remains your responsibility.
</Callout>

If your sales are collected in full up front, invoice under the **PUE** method (Payment in a single instalment) instead of PPD: the REP doesn't apply there, and Cord covers the full cycle with no extra manual steps.
