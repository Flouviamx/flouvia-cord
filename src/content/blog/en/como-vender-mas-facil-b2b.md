Making a B2B sale easier means helping the buyer **understand, decide, approve, and pay
with less ambiguity**. It does not mean uncontrolled discounting, sending more reminders,
or promising that software will close a deal.

Friction usually appears at six handoffs:

| Moment | Typical friction | Operating change |
|---|---|---|
| Request | incomplete requirements | structured minimum intake |
| Quote | copied prices and data | reusable client and catalog |
| Review | several email versions | one current source and clear options |
| Approval | ambiguous acceptance | explicit action and technical evidence |
| Invoice/payment | requirements appear late | terms and data before closing |
| Collections | nobody knows what was promised | owner, status, and next action |

## Define the buyer's decision

A quote should explain the problem and scope, included options, price, currency, taxes,
delivery, deposit or credit, exclusions, acceptance method, and next step. If an
explanation needs a call, add context. If a detail does not affect the decision, move it
to terms or an appendix.

## Turn repeated information into a system

Clients, SKUs, descriptions, prices, costs, taxes, and templates should not live in each
seller's memory. Cord stores client terms, credit, and price level; catalog product,
unit, cost, and tax; and organization defaults for validity, deposit, PDF, and messages.
The quote remains editable for real exceptions.

See [how to create quotes faster](/en/blog/como-hacer-cotizaciones-rapido) for the full
workflow and checklist.

## Protect margin before sending

Record cost where useful, show margin internally, and define thresholds. Cord supports
rules for discount, amount, and minimum margin. Quotes crossing an enabled rule enter the
applicable internal flow. Approval workflows require Scale or Developer.

See [B2B price lists](/en/blog/listas-de-precios-b2b) to separate base price, client
level, and negotiated exception.

## Keep one current client version

PDF remains useful for download and archive. A Cord link shows the current document,
supports general or line-item comments, and can present options. Sent, viewed, and
approved are distinct states. Viewed only means the link was opened.

On approval, Cord stores declared identity, date, time, IP, and a fingerprint of the
version. This is technical evidence, not notarial certification or a universal legal
guarantee.

## Design payment from the quote

Define deposit, balance, credit, currency, and method before invoicing. Cord Payments
uses Stripe Connect for cards in Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain, the
United Kingdom, Germany, and France. Automated SPEI is limited to eligible Mexican
accounts and MXN. Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru can quote, invoice, and record
external payments but cannot currently enable Cord online collection.

Manual bank transfer displays instructions; marking paid requires deposit verification.
Cord is not the bank and does not move funds when you record payment.

## Treat collections as part of the experience

Before reminding, confirm delivery, purchase-order or vendor-onboarding requirements,
and open disputes. Then request a concrete date and record the promise.

Read [how to collect invoices faster](/en/blog/como-cobrar-facturas-mas-rapido) and
[how to offer Net 30 with controls](/en/blog/credito-b2b-net30).

## A 30-day friction-reduction plan

1. **Week 1 — observe:** review ten operations and mark every wait, correction, and repeated field.
2. **Week 2 — standardize:** clean clients and catalog; define defaults, templates, and authority.
3. **Week 3 — pilot:** use the workflow with a small group and test mobile, approval, invoice, and payment end to end.
4. **Week 4 — measure:** compare medians for send time, errors, views, decisions, and payment; automate only stable steps.

## Do not promise

- That opening a link proves purchase intent.
- That technical approval replaces every contractual formality.
- That online payment works in every country or currency.
- That a commercial invoice is automatically filed with every authority.
- That AI negotiates agreements or understands replies without configured review.
- That software guarantees conversion, punctual payment, or universal legal validity.

An easier commercial process is one where everyone knows what happened, which version is
current, and what action comes next. That control reduces administrative work without
discarding margin or context.
