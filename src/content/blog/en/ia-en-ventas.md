Collection is, historically, the most exhausting area of any B2B company. Sales executives hate collecting because it damages the commercial relationship with their clients. Finance departments end up doing the "dirty work", sending passive-aggressive emails that are often ignored.

When many invoices have different dates, terms, and context, manual follow-up loses consistency and priority.

AI can help organize that work, but it needs correct data, rules, and supervision.

## From "Automated Reminders" to "Autonomous Agents"

In recent years, financial software offered automated emails ("Drip Campaigns"). A basic system sent a template at 5 days overdue, another at 15, etc. They were predictable, robotic, and clients learned to ignore them by filtering them in their inboxes.

Language models can adapt a draft to available context. That does not make the system a financial adviser or give it unlimited authority.

### How does an AI Collection Agent operate?

Cord's agent reads operational receivables facts and prepares a message using the invoice, balance, due date, and available context.

1. **Facts before tone:** the message starts with stored balance, currency, and due date.
2. **Approval mode:** AI prepares the text and a person decides whether to send it.
3. **Automatic mode:** the system may send within enabled configuration and leaves a record for review.

Cord does not currently interpret email replies and create a payment promise by itself. A promise must be explicitly recorded in receivables.

## Negotiation and agreements remain outside automation

Cord does not autonomously negotiate write-offs, surcharges, or payment plans. A person must resolve those agreements under the business's policy and authority. AI must not invent legal consequences, threaten unauthorized action, or treat a dispute as acknowledged debt.

## Mitigating Human Friction

One of the biggest psychological advantages of delegating collection to an intelligent system (even signing the emails as "Finance Team" or explicitly as "Virtual Assistant") is that it removes the personal burden.

The account executive maintains their role as "good cop" and strategic ally, while the system acts as the "bad cop" applying the business rules objectively.

## Conclusion

Useful automation reduces repetitive work and leaves sensitive decisions to the team: disputes, agreements, unreconciled payments, and strategic relationships. Periodically review sent messages, errors, broken promises, and cases that should have stopped.
