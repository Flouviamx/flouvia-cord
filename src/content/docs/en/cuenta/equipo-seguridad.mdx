<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Team, permissions, and security</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Separate each user's personal security from the rules shared by the whole organization.</p>
</header>

Cord has three distinct access layers:

1. **Your account** protects personal identity: password, 2FA, passkeys, sessions, and
   connected Google or Apple accounts.
2. **Team and permissions** decides what each member can do inside an organization.
3. **Organization security** applies requirements such as 2FA, inactivity timeout, and
   allowed domains for invitations.

## Plans and seats

Inviting a team and using custom roles start on **Professional**. The current included
allowance is:

| Plan | Included users | Additional users |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | 1 | Not available |
| Starter | 1 | Not available |
| Professional | 5 | Monthly charge per user |
| Scale | 15 | Monthly charge per user |
| Developer | Unlimited | Included |

A valid invitation already reserves a seat even if it has not been accepted yet. If the
indicator is full, revoke an invitation you won't use or review your plan. Additional
user rates are in [Your subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).

## Invite a person

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings › Team & permissions › Team & roles** and select **Add member**. Shortcut: **Invite people**, at the bottom of the sidebar, opens that screen with the invitation already open (it appears if you have the Team permission and your plan includes more than one user).
2. Enter their email.
3. Choose Administrator, Sales rep, Read only, or a custom combination.
4. Review each permission and send the invitation.
</Steps>

The invitation expires after **7 days**. Cord tries to send it by email and also returns
a copyable link as a backup. **Resend** rotates the previous token and creates a new one
valid for seven days; the old link stops working.

If the organization restricts domains, the email must end exactly in one of the domains
configured under **Settings › Team & permissions › Security**.

## Roles and permissions

Roles are editable presets, not a replacement for effective permissions:

- **Administrator:** almost everything, including team; refunds are not enabled.
- **Sales rep:** quotes, clients, products, and reports; no settings or approvals.
- **Read only:** enters the app without modification permissions.
- **Custom:** a combination saved specifically for that member.
- **Owner:** full access by definition; cannot be downgraded or revoked from Team.

The independent permissions are Quotes, Approvals, Collections, Clients, Products,
Reports, Settings, Payment configuration, Refunds, and Team.

<Callout type="info">
Hiding a button is not the security boundary. The APIs validate the permission and the
active organization again before reading or modifying data.
</Callout>

## Membership states

- **Active:** accepted the invitation and keeps access.
- **Invited:** the link is still pending; it may be valid or expired.
- **Revoked:** no longer enters the organization. The record is kept to show history and
  the person can be invited again.

The screen shows last session, recent quotes, and last action when data is available.
Revoking removes access; it does not automatically reassign or delete documents that
person created.

## Require two-step verification

Under **Settings › Team & permissions › Security**, turn on **Require two-factor
authentication (2FA)** for the organization. A member who has not set up TOTP yet is sent
to **Settings › Your account** to finish activation before continuing to use protected
sections.

Personal activation happens under **Settings › Your account**: scan the QR code with
your authenticator app, confirm the code, and save the ten single-use backup codes shown
on activation. Store those codes outside Cord. Regenerating them invalidates the previous
set. Disabling 2FA requires your current password or an authenticator code, depending on
the account type.

When the organization requires 2FA, protection also applies to dashboard requests, not
only to the visible screen. Actions needed to set up 2FA, confirm your identity, or sign
out remain available.

If a sensitive action asks you to verify your identity again, complete it before
retrying. Team permissions are still required; having 2FA does not grant permission to
refund or administer the business.

## Passkeys and password

Under **Settings › Your account** each person can:

- create or change their password;
- register several passkeys compatible with the device or key manager;
- remove a passkey they no longer recognize;
- connect or disconnect Google and Apple.

An account created with OAuth can set a password later. Before disconnecting the last
working method, confirm you keep another way to sign in.

## Active sessions

The session list shows device, known IP, last activity, and which one is the current
session. You can close an individual session or all the others.

The organization can also configure an inactivity timeout under **Settings › Team &
permissions › Security**: unlimited, 1, 4, 8, or 24 hours. This rule is different from
manually closing personal sessions.

The timeout is checked when entering the app, billing, and protected internal APIs, and
also when registering a passkey. When it expires, the operation stops and you must sign
in again. Activity is measured by authenticated requests to those surfaces, not by
moving the mouse; automatic app requests may also count. Visiting a public page or using
the 2FA recovery routes does not restart the window. "Unlimited" disables the inactivity
timeout but keeps the maximum session lifetime, revocations, and account suspension.

## Enterprise SSO

SAML 2.0 connections are administered under **Settings › Team & permissions › SSO** and
require the **Scale or Developer** plan and the **Team** permission. A saved
configuration does not guarantee access after losing that plan. The technical setup of
the connection (Entity ID, sign-in URL, certificate, domains, provisioning, and role
mapping) is in [SSO & SAML](/en/docs/desarrolladores/seguridad/sso).

Members managed by SSO are flagged in Team, and their permissions are managed by your
identity provider.

### Require SSO

Once a connection is working, you can require your team to sign in only through SSO.

<Steps>
1. Under **Settings › Team & permissions › SSO**, confirm you have at least one
   **Active** connection with a verified domain. Without one, Cord rejects the change.
2. Under **Require SSO**, turn on **Require your team to sign in via SSO**.
3. Save.
</Steps>

With the option on, Cord blocks password, Google, Apple, and passkeys for every member
**except the account owner**, who always keeps their password as a fallback.

### Emergency access

If your identity provider is down and your team cannot sign in, the owner can
temporarily disable the requirement:

<Steps>
1. Under **Settings › Team & permissions › SSO**, select **Disable for 1 hour**.
2. Confirm your password (or your verification code) and select **Disable for 1 hour**.
</Steps>

During that hour the team can use their other sign-in methods again, and the screen
shows **SSO temporarily disabled until** the expiration date and time. When it ends, the
requirement comes back on its own. Only the owner can use this option, and the action is
recorded in the organization's audit log.

## Delete a personal account

The danger zone in **Your account** permanently deletes the personal identity, sessions,
passkeys, and connected accounts. It requires a credential and typing the email to
confirm. If that person is the sole owner of an organization, the deletion also affects
that organization. Transfer ownership and export data before continuing.

## Common problems

**I can't invite.** Check the Team permission, Professional plan or higher, an available
seat, and an allowed domain.

**The link expired.** Use Resend; don't try to recover the previous token.

**The person signs in but can't edit.** Check the effective permissions, not only the
role name.

**The organization requires 2FA.** The person must complete TOTP in their own account; an
administrator cannot generate the codes for them.

**I can't turn on Require SSO.** You need an active connection with at least one verified
domain.

**The identity provider is down.** The owner can sign in with their password and use
**Disable for 1 hour**.
