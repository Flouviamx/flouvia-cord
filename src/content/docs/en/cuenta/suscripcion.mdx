Your subscription pays for using Cord. It is separate from what you charge your clients:
the currency you quote in, Cord Payments fees and your client invoices are managed
separately.

## Requirements

- **Settings permission** to see the plan, change it and open Billing.
- **Outside the test environment.** In test mode the screen shows
  **Not available in test mode** and you cannot subscribe or change plans.
- **Developer** is not self-service: it is agreed with sales.

## Your subscription currency

Cord charges you in one of three currencies, based on your organization's country:

| Country | Subscription currency |
| --- | --- |
| Mexico | MXN |
| Spain, Germany and France | EUR |
| United States, Canada, Brazil, United Kingdom, Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru | USD |

**An existing contract keeps its currency.** Once you have paid an invoice in a currency,
Cord keeps charging you in it even if you change your account's country, language or
currency. The currency you choose in **Settings › General** is the one you sell in, not
the one for your subscription.

## Pricing

| Plan | MXN per month | USD per month | EUR per month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | 0 MXN | 0 USD | 0 EUR |
| Starter | 240 MXN | 12 USD | 12 EUR |
| Professional | 590 MXN | 30 USD | 30 EUR |
| Scale | 1,390 MXN | 70 USD | 70 EUR |
| Developer | Via sales | Via sales | Via sales |

The **annual cycle** costs ten monthly payments and is charged yearly (for example,
Starter annual: 2,400 MXN, 120 USD or 120 EUR). Overage is billed separately and is not
part of that saving. Prices include taxes: what you see is what you pay.

| Scale | 500 | 500 | Unlimited | 10,000 | 15 | Unlimited |
| Developer | Unlimited | 1,000 | Unlimited | 50,000 | Unlimited | Unlimited |

Check your usage for the period under **Settings › Plans & subscription › Plan usage this
period**.

## Capacity limits

Besides monthly allowances, Free and Starter cap how many resources can exist at the same
time. These are not billed as overage: to go past them you need to upgrade.

| Plan | Active quotes | Products | Clients | Active workflows |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | 5 | 50 | 50 | 1 |
| Starter | 50 | 500 | 500 | 5 |
| Professional, Scale and Developer | No cap | No cap | No cap | No cap |

An **active** quote is one that is still live: draft, sent, viewed or approved. Paid,
invoiced, rejected or expired quotes do not count.

On Free and Starter, the bottom of the sidebar shows the **Active quotes** meter with how
many you have against the cap (for example, "4 / 5"). The bar turns amber from 80% and
red when you reach the limit, and the **Upgrade plan** link opens **Settings › Plans &
subscription**. On plans without a cap the meter doesn't appear.

## Overage

**Free has hard limits**: when you reach the limit, the feature stops until next month or
until you upgrade. From Starter on, usage above the allowance is charged at the end of
the period, and only the overage is billed, never the included amount again. Free's quote
sends have no overage: the cap of five per month is fixed.

Rates are fixed per currency, not a daily exchange-rate conversion. Starter keeps a

## Change plan or billing cycle

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings › Plans & subscription** and select **Change plan**.
2. Choose the **Monthly** or **Annual** cycle (**2 months free**).
3. Select **Choose** on the plan you want. For Developer, the button takes you to sales.
4. Review the summary, currency and total, and confirm the payment.
</Steps>

<DmPlanPicker lang="en" />

What happens depending on the change:

- **Upgrading** applies immediately. Cord charges the prorated difference for the current
  period right away, and the new capabilities turn on once the payment is confirmed.
- **Downgrading** or **changing only the cycle** (for example, monthly to annual on the
  same plan) is scheduled for the end of your paid period. You keep your current plan
  until that date.
- **Canceling** is done from **Billing** and also takes effect at the end of the period.
  You can resume the subscription before that date.

## What happens when you downgrade

**Your data is not deleted.** Quotes, clients, products, invoices, members and settings
are kept. What changes is what can keep operating:

- **Resources over the cap** (active quotes, products, clients, active workflows):
  existing ones are kept, but you cannot create more (or activate more workflows) until
  you are below the new plan's limit.
- **Team:** if the new plan includes fewer users, the owner and the first members that
  fit in the plan, by seniority, keep operating. The others keep their membership but
  cannot operate until you regain seats.
- **Plan features** (collections, recurring invoices, SSO, approvals, custom domain, audit
  log...): they stop operating, with their configuration saved, until you return to an
  eligible plan.
- **Cord branding:** if you downgrade to Free, it shows on your links again.
- **Custom domain:** links with your domain redirect to `cordhq.app`. See
  [Custom domain](/en/docs/cuenta/dominio-propio).
- **Issued invoices** remain available for download and payment. Issuing a new tax
  invoice requires Starter or higher.

## Past-due payment

If a subscription charge fails, your paid plan is not guaranteed: Cord only grants paid
capabilities with an active subscription and a period covered by a paid invoice. Until
it is resolved, your account operates with Free permissions. From **Billing** you can
settle the past-due charge without leaving Cord; once paid, you get your plan back.

## Billing and receipts

In **Settings › Plans & subscription**, **Go to Billing →** opens
[billing.cordhq.app](https://billing.cordhq.app), where you manage your card, review
charges and download receipts.

A charge receipt and a tax invoice are different documents. In Mexico you can request the
CFDI for a subscription payment from Billing, with your tax details complete. Only a paid
charge with an amount is invoiced; a 0 MXN charge does not generate a CFDI. Outside
Mexico, the charge receipt is your proof of payment.

## Commercial and tax documents

Free includes **10 commercial invoices per calendar month (UTC)**, independent of its five
quote sends; from Starter on they are unlimited. Tax-valid invoices start on Starter:
**30, 200 and 500** per month on Starter, Professional and Scale, and **1,000** on
Developer. Each type has its own allowance.

In Mexico and Spain, the commercial option generates **pro forma** documents labeled as
such; they do not replace a tax invoice. Integrated tax issuing (CFDI 4.0 in Mexico)
requires Starter or higher and your issuer details configured. In Spain, invoice
registration with the AEAT (Verifactu) is not active yet: paying for a plan does not
turn it on. In the other markets, Cord issues commercial invoices without integration
with the local tax authority.

Creating drafts, downloading and resending do not consume another document. A successful
issuance consumes one unit of its allowance; test documents do not. Reaching the limit
does not disable downloads or payments of existing documents.

## Common problems

**I don't see the button to change plans.** You need the Settings permission and to be
outside the test environment.

**I paid but still see Free.** Wait for the payment to be confirmed and reload the page.
If the charge is pending, finish the payment from Billing.

**The summary currency is not what I expected.** If you already have a contract, Cord
keeps its currency. If this is your first subscription and it doesn't match your
country, reload the page before paying and check the country in **Settings › General**.

**I downgraded and a teammate can no longer work.** Your plan includes fewer users.
Upgrade or, from Professional, add extra users.
