## Two places, two different questions

Your subscription lives across two surfaces, and the split is deliberate:

- **Settings > Plans & subscription** (`/app/ajustes/plan`) answers *which plan you
  have and how much you've used*: the period's included quotas, the meters for AI,
  invoices, API and users, and plan changes.
- **[billing.cordhq.app](https://billing.cordhq.app)** answers *the money*: your
  card, your receipts, your billing details, the invoice for your payments, and
  cancellation.

You reach the second from the first, using the session you already have open. No
second sign-in.

## Which currency Cord bills you in

Your subscription currency follows **your business's country** — not the language
you browse in, and not the currency you quote your own clients in:

| Business country | Subscription currency |
| :--- | :--- |
| Mexico | Mexican pesos (MXN) |
| Everywhere else | US dollars (USD) |

<Callout type="info">
**Not the same as your sales currency.** A business in Madrid quotes its clients in
euros and pays Cord in dollars. Two separate decisions: yours lives in
**Settings > General**, the platform's is set by your country.
</Callout>

What you see published is what you're charged: nothing is added at confirmation.
Paying **annually** costs ten months, so you get two free.

If you already have an active subscription, **your currency doesn't change** even if
you later change your business's country. It's locked at your first charge and stays
there; that's a payment-processor constraint, not a preference.

## Payment method

On **billing.cordhq.app** you can store several cards, mark which one is the
default, and remove the ones you no longer use. The default is the card your next
renewal charges.

<Callout type="warning">
You can't remove your only card while a subscription is active. Add the new one
first, then delete the old one — that way no renewal is left with nothing to charge.
</Callout>

Your card details never pass through Cord. The payment processor captures them
directly in an encrypted field, and we only receive the confirmation.

## If a charge didn't go through

When a card declines, your subscription goes to **past due** and you'll see a notice
at the top of billing.cordhq.app with a button to settle it right there. Your plan
stays active while the charge is pending, but not indefinitely — resolve it so you
don't lose your plan's capabilities.

## Receipts and invoices

Two different documents, worth not confusing:

- The **receipt** is the record of the charge. It always exists, in every country,
  and downloads from the right-hand column of the charges table.
- The **invoice** is the tax document. In Mexico, Cord issues your **CFDI 4.0**
  stamped with the SAT.

### Invoicing your subscription (Mexico)

<Steps>
1. Go to **billing.cordhq.app** and scroll to **Billing details**.
2. Enter your **RFC**, and make sure your tax regime and postal code are complete
   under **Settings > Invoicing > Tax details**. Without those three fields nothing
   can be stamped.
3. In the **Charges** table, press **Get invoice** on the charge you need.
4. The row changes to **Invoiced**. Your CFDI is issued to the tax details you
   entered.
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
**A charge can only be invoiced once.** Canceling an issued CFDI requires the
recipient's authorization with the SAT, so double-check your tax details **before**
pressing Get invoice.
</Callout>

A $0 charge — fully covered by a discount, for instance — can't be invoiced: there's
no income to declare. You'll see a dash instead of the button.

Outside Mexico the invoice option doesn't appear, and that isn't a gap: your payment
receipt **is** the document you record the expense with.

## Changing plans

Done from **Settings > Plans & subscription**:

- **Upgrading** applies immediately. You're charged the prorated difference for what
  remains of your period, and your quotas adjust right away.
- **Downgrading** is scheduled for the end of the period you already paid for. You
  keep your current plan until then.

## Canceling

On billing.cordhq.app, **Cancel** schedules the end of your subscription for the
close of the period you already paid for. You keep access until that date, and you
can **resume** any time before it arrives.

When you cancel, your account returns to the Free plan:

- **Your data and settings are kept**, intact. Nothing is deleted.
- Anything above the Free plan's limits **stops working** until you upgrade again.
  If you have more active quotes or more users than Free allows, those resources are
  still there but inoperative.
