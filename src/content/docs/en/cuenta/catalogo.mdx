## Manage your SKUs easily

The heart of a B2B quote is product accuracy. Cord allows you to manage a centralized catalog where your sales team simply searches and selects, ensuring the name, SKU, and tax base are always correct.

### 1. Manual Creation

If you have a small catalog or sell services, you can add products one by one from **Products > New Product**.
You will be asked for the following data:
- **Product / Service Name**
- **SKU (Internal Code):** Unique identifier, optional.
- **Unit:** piece, service, hour, m², kg... whichever you sell by.
- **Description:** free text that accompanies the product on the quote.
- **Sale Price (Before taxes):** the list price.
- **Internal Cost (Optional):** If you enter how much the product costs you, Cord will calculate and show you the **Profit Margin** in real-time when building the quote. Your client will never see this cost.
- **Volume Pricing (Optional):** Vital in B2B. You can set up automatic rules, for example: *"From 1 to 10 pieces the price is $100, from 11 to 50 pieces the price drops to $85"*. Cord will automatically adjust the price based on the quantity the sales rep adds to the quote.
- **Status (Active / Inactive):** If you discontinue a product or run out of stock long-term, you can mark it as *Inactive* so sales reps can no longer add it to new quotes, without deleting its history.

<Callout type="info">
**Tax doesn't live on the product.** Cord calculates tax **per quote line**, not per
catalog item — the same product can be sold taxed in one deal and exempt in another
(an export, a tax-exempt client). You pick the rate from your account's tax catalog
(**Settings > Quotes**) when you add the line item; the tax name follows your country
(VAT, IVA, GST, Moms...), never a generic label. See
[Products and discounts](/en/docs/cotizacion/productos) for how this applies when
quoting.
</Callout>

### 2. Bulk Import (CSV)

For companies with hundreds or thousands of references, Cord supports bulk import.
Download our **CSV Template** (columns `sku, name, unit, price, active`), paste the
information extracted from your ERP (such as SAP, Oracle, or Microsip), and upload it
to Cord to create prices, SKUs, and descriptions in a single move.

<Callout type="info">
**Tip:** Do your prices change constantly?
With the **Cord Public API** (Developers), you can synchronize your catalog in real-time. If you update a price in your ERP, a *webhook* or API call can automatically update it in Cord, ensuring your salespeople always quote with the current price list.

</Callout>

## The product profile

The catalog (**Products** in the main menu) has quick filters (active, inactive, with
volume pricing, missing cost, low margin) so you can find what you need without
scrolling a long list.

Click any product to open its **profile**: closed amount and units sold, close rate,
price realization (the lowest, average, and highest price actually charged against the
list price), list margin vs. **realized margin** over historical sales, its volume
pricing ladder, who buys it most, its recent quotes, and which kits include it — all
computed from your real history, not just the data you entered when creating it.
