<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Import your catalog</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Products, kits and clients ready to quote in seconds, one by one or from a CSV.</p>
</header>

The catalog is what your team searches and adds when quoting. With it, the name, unit and
price of each item always come out the same, and Cord can calculate your margin live. It
lives in **Products**, in the sidebar, with two tabs: **Catalog** and **Kits**.

## Requirements

- **Products permission** to create, edit and import products and to manage kits.
- **Clients permission** to import clients.
- **Plan limits:** how many products and clients can exist at once depends on your plan
  (see [Limits by plan](#limits-by-plan)).

## Create a product

<Steps>
1. In **Products**, select **+ New product** (or **Create › Product** in the top bar).
2. Choose what you sell: **Product**, **Service**, or **Subscription or license**. It only
   changes the suggested units (unit and box, hour and project, month and license); it
   doesn't limit anything else.
3. Enter the **Name** (required) and, optionally, the **SKU**, the **Unit**, and a
   **Description**. The unit is free text: pick a suggestion or type your own.
4. Enter the **Selling price**, before taxes, in your account's currency.
5. Optional: under **Your cost** enter what it costs you. Cord shows how much you earn
   or lose per unit and calculates the margin when quoting. Your client never sees the
   cost.
6. Optional: under **Tax**, choose the rate this product brings into a quote or invoice
   (shown when your tax catalog has more than one rate).
7. Mexico only, optional: assign its **SAT keys** (see below).
8. Optional: add **Volume pricing** (see below).
9. Choose the **Status** (Active or Inactive) and select **Save product**.
</Steps>

An **inactive** product is no longer offered when quoting, but keeps its history and
earlier quotes.

<Callout type="info">
**A product's tax is a suggestion.** Cord calculates taxes per **quote line**: when you
add the product, the line comes in with the rate you assigned to it (or with your default
rate if you didn't assign one, or if that rate is no longer in your catalog under
**Settings › Quotes › Taxes**), and you can change it on the line. Changing the product's
rate later doesn't alter quotes or invoices already sent. See
[Products and discounts](/en/docs/cotizacion/productos).
</Callout>

## SAT keys (Mexico)

If your business invoices in Mexico, each product has an **E-invoicing (SAT, Mexico)**
section:

- **SAT product or service key:** type a word ("software", "consulting") and pick the key
  from the SAT catalog, or type its 8 digits.
- **SAT unit key:** derived from the product's unit ("hour" → `HUR`, "month" → `MON`,
  "kg" → `KGM`). Type another one only if your accountant asks for a different key.

Invoices read these keys when stamping. A product without a product key, or a free line,
is stamped with the generic keys `01010101` and `H87`. More in
[Invoicing](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion).

## Volume pricing

Use it for wholesale: the price drops automatically when the line quantity reaches a
tier.

<Steps>
1. In the product form, open **Volume pricing** and select **Add volume tier**.
2. For each tier, enter **From** (the minimum quantity) and the unit price from that
   quantity on.
3. Save the product.
</Steps>

For example, with a list price of 100.00 MXN and tiers **From 500 → 90.00 MXN** and
**From 2,000 → 75.00 MXN**: a 300-piece line uses 100.00 MXN, an 800-piece line uses
90.00 MXN and a 2,500-piece line uses 75.00 MXN. Below the first tier the list price
applies. The client-level discount is calculated on the price of the applicable tier.

## Kits

A kit groups items you usually quote together, for example an installation package, so
you can insert them into a quote with one click.

<Steps>
1. Open **Products › Kits** and select **+ New kit**.
2. Enter the **Name** and, optionally, a description.
3. Under **Kit line items**, search catalog products by name or SKU, or add a
   **+ Free-form line** (for example, labor) with its description. Adjust each line's
   quantity.
4. Optional: turn on **Sell the kit at a fixed price** to offer a bundle. Cord shows the
   sum of list prices and how much the bundle saves (or how much more it costs).
5. Select **Save kit**.
</Steps>

<DmKitModal lang="en" />

When quoting, use **+ Insert kit** in the editor. The lines come in as normal quote
lines: you can edit or remove them like any other, and changing the kit later does not
modify quotes already created. With a bundle price, Cord spreads that total across the
catalog lines; free lines do not take part. Only active kits appear when inserting.

## Import products from CSV

<Steps>
1. In **Products**, select **Import CSV**. If you don't know the format yet, use
   **↓ Download sample template**.
2. **File.** Drag your file or click to choose it. Only `.csv` is accepted.
3. **Columns.** Match your file's columns to Cord's fields. Cord suggests the matching
   based on the header names. Uncheck **The first row is headers** if your file has
   none.
4. **Review.** You see how many products will be imported, how many rows are skipped
   for having no name, and a preview of the first rows. Decide whether to
   **Update existing products with the same SKU**.
5. Select **Import** to load the catalog.
</Steps>

### Columns that are imported

| Cord field | Required | How it is read |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Yes | Rows without a name are skipped. |
| **SKU / Code** | No | Stored in uppercase. It is the key for updating existing products. |
| **Unit** | No | If empty, `pieza` (piece) is used. |
| **List price** | No | Use a period as the decimal separator, for example `1234.50`. A negative price is stored as 0. |
| **Active** | No | `0`, `no`, `false`, `inactive`, `off` or empty mark it inactive; any other value, active. Without the column, all are active. |

**Not imported:** cost, description and volume pricing. Enter them in each product's
form after importing.

### Update existing products

With **Update existing products with the same SKU** on (the default), a row whose SKU
already exists in your catalog **updates** that product instead of creating a new one.
Rows without a SKU always create a new product.

<Callout type="warning">
**When updating, Cord rewrites name, unit, price and status with what the file
contains.** If you don't map the price column, updated products end up with price 0; if
you don't map the unit, they end up as `pieza`. To change prices safely, first export
your catalog from **Settings › Advanced › Data & privacy** (same format as the import),
edit that file and import it again.
</Callout>

## Import clients from CSV

The **Clients** directory has the same three-step assistant: **Import CSV**, match
columns and review. It also offers its own sample template.

| Cord field | Required | How it is read |
|---|---|---|
| **Company** | Yes | Rows without a company are skipped. |
| **Contact**, **Email**, **Phone** | No | Text as provided. |
| **Tax ID** (RFC, NIF, EIN...) | No | Stored in uppercase. |
| **Payment terms** | No | A value containing "60" becomes Net 60; containing "30", "net" or "credito", Net 30; anything else, Cash. |
| **Credit limit** | No | A number without a currency symbol; empty means no limit. |

With **Update existing clients (same tax ID or name)** on, a row that matches by tax ID
or, if it has none, by company name, updates that client. As with products, empty or
unmapped fields replace what the client already had.

## Limits by plan

| Plan | Products | Clients | Kits |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Free | 50 | 50 | No separate cap |
| Starter | 500 | 500 | No separate cap |
| Professional, Scale and Developer | No cap | No cap | No separate cap |

Each import accepts **up to 2,000 rows**; if your file is larger, split it. Inactive
products also count toward the cap. If you reach the product cap in the middle of an
import, what was already imported is kept and Cord tells you how many were created and
updated.

## The product page

The **Products** list has quick filters: **All**, **Active**, **Inactive**,
**With volume pricing**, **No cost set** and **Low margin**. Opening a product shows its page
with closed revenue and units sold, win rate, price realization (minimum, average and
maximum charged against list price), list margin versus realized margin, its volume
pricing ladder, who buys it most, its latest quotes and which kits include it.

## Common problems

**"Maximum 2000 rows per import".** Split the file into parts of 2,000 rows or fewer.

**"Choose which column has the product name".** Map the **Name** field before
continuing.

**"The file is empty or couldn't be read".** Save it as CSV with UTF-8 encoding. An
Excel file (`.xlsx`) is not accepted by this assistant: export it as CSV.

**Prices were imported wrong.** Check the decimal separator: use a period and don't use
periods as thousands separators.

**I hit my plan's cap.** Deactivate or delete products you no longer use, or change plans
in [Your subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).
