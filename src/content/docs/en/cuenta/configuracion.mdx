## 1. General company data

The first step is to register your company in Cord. Go to **Settings > General** to fill in the following information:

- **Name:** This is how your clients will see your company on quotes and public links.
- **Currency:** the currency you quote and charge your clients in (not your Cord subscription's currency, which follows your country — see [Your subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion)).
- **Contact email, phone, and address.**
- **Website and WhatsApp** (shown on your public link).
- **Country of operation:** sets the regulatory rail Cord uses — which tax fields it asks for, which tax rates it seeds by default, and the invoicing vocabulary (see below).
- **Time zone:** used to calculate and display the dates on your quotes and invoices.
- **Language:** applies to the app, your public links, and the emails Cord sends on your behalf.

The tax details used for invoicing (Legal name, your tax ID, fiscal address) are
captured separately, under **Settings › Invoicing › Tax details**. That form changes
based on your country: in Mexico it asks for **RFC** and **Tax Regime** and enables the
CSD for CFDI 4.0 (see [Add tax information](/en/docs/cuenta/csd)); outside Mexico it
asks for your legal name, your local tax ID (NIF/CIF in Spain, EIN/Tax ID in the US...),
address, and invoice prefix — Cord issues a numbered invoice with an immutable copy and
a PDF. Cord does not automatically file it with the local tax authority. In Spain,
Verifactu requires a valid certificate and an enabled Cord regulatory rail; see
[Fiscal invoicing by country](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion).

## 2. Invite your sales team

Selling is usually a team effort. From the **Pro** plan, Cord lets you invite as many team members as your plan allows (you'll see your seat usage and limit right on the screen). Go to **Settings > Team and permissions** and select **Add member**, or select **Invite people** at the bottom of the sidebar, which opens that same screen with the invitation ready. See [Team, permissions, and security](/en/docs/cuenta/equipo-seguridad).

### Roles and permissions

Every invite starts from a permission **preset** you can use as-is or customize
field by field:
- **Admin:** almost all control, including the team; the Refunds permission isn't enabled by default.
- **Sales rep:** quotes, clients, products, and analytics; doesn't manage settings or approve.
- **Read only:** can enter the app without modification permissions, useful for audits.
- **Custom:** toggle individual permissions (quoting, catalog, clients, collections,
  settings...) when none of the three presets fits.

From each member's profile you can see their effective role, last session, recent
activity, edit their permissions, resend the invitation, revoke access, or re-invite
them if already revoked. If you restricted invitations to your company's email domains
(`Settings > Security`), an invite to an address outside those domains is rejected before
it sends. A member who signs in through SSO has permissions managed by your identity
provider and isn't edited here.

## 3. Quote defaults

If your business sells on credit (you don't charge upfront, but on a term of days), you don't have to configure it manually on every quote.

Go to **Settings > Quotes** to set the values every new quote starts with:
- **Default validity (days).**
- **Default terms:** *Due on receipt* or a *Net* term of 7, 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 days. From now on, every new quote will automatically inherit this payment term (you can still change it per quote when quoting).
- **Default advance (%).**
- **Numbering prefix**, and the **legal text** that accompanies the PDF.

<Callout type="info">
**The credit limit is a signal, not an automatic block.** Each client can have their own
credit limit (**Clients > [client]**). Cord uses it to warn you — on the client's
profile and in Collections — how much open balance they carry against that limit, but
it doesn't by itself stop a new quote from being created; the call to pause selling to
an over-exposed client is still yours.
</Callout>
