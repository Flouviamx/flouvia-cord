  <p class="page-subtitle">The secure link where your client reviews, discusses, approves and pays. Adjust what they see and how it looks.</p>

The **client portal** is the page that opens when your client clicks a quote link. They
need no account or password: the link is their access. From there they review the
proposal, comment, approve or reject and, when applicable, pay online.

## Requirements

- **Settings permission** to change the portal configuration.
- **All plans** can customize layout, announcement, welcome and comments.
- **Starter or higher** to remove Cord branding.
- **Professional or higher** to serve the portal from your own domain. See
  [Custom domain](/en/docs/cuenta/dominio-propio).

## What your client sees

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Header with your brand** | Your logo, colors and typography from [Identity](/en/docs/cuenta/marca). |
| **Top announcement** | A strip in your brand color, if you wrote an announcement. |
| **Welcome** | Your welcome message, if you wrote one. |
| **Line items** | Each item with quantity, price and amount, in the quote's currency. If the negotiated price is lower than the list price, the list price appears struck through. |
| **Partial approval** | While the quote is open, each line has an **Include this line in your approval** checkbox. |
| **Line comments and conversation** | If comments are on: a button to comment on each line and a general chat with **Counteroffer**. |
| **Approve or reject** | **Approve quote** records the signer's name and technical evidence; **Reject quote** accepts an optional reason. |
| **Online payment** | If you have an active payment method and the quote allows it. See [Accept a payment](/en/docs/pagos/aceptar). |
| **Download PDF** | The quote document. |
| **Contact** | Shortcuts to reach you by WhatsApp, email or phone, based on the contact details you entered in Settings › General. |
| **Activity notice** | "Whoever sent you this proposal can see when you open it and which sections you review." |
| **Cord branding** | The "Verified by Cord" seal, unless your plan allows removing it and you turned it off. |

<Callout type="info">
**Your own visit does not count as a view.** If you open the link while signed in to
Cord, you see a **Preview — this is what your client sees** strip. Cord does not record
that visit as client activity or trigger "viewed" alerts.
</Callout>

## Customize the portal

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings › Brand appearance › Client portal**.
2. Under **Layout**, choose the header style (**Essential**, **Soft** or **Contrast**),
   **Corners** (Precise, Soft or Rounded) and **Spacing** (Comfortable or Compact).
3. Under **Messages**, write the **Top announcement** (up to 200 characters; leave it
   empty to hide it) and the **Welcome message** (up to 280 characters).
4. Under **Interaction**, turn **Comments and counteroffers** and **Show Cord branding**
   on or off.
5. Check the preview on the right and save.
</Steps>

The preview shows a sample quote on **Portal**, **Quote**, **PDF** and **Email**, on
**Desktop** or **Mobile**, and lets you compare **Your draft** against **Saved**. It
generates no activity or messages.

## Behavior and edge cases

- **Layout is shared.** The header style, corners and spacing are also used in emails
  and commercial invoices. The quote PDF has its own design under
  **Settings › Quotes › PDF document**.
- **Turning comments off does not delete the conversation.** The client stops seeing the
  per-line button, the chat and the counteroffer, but earlier messages are kept on your
  side.
- **Closed quote.** Once approved or rejected, the partial-approval checkboxes and the
  option to comment on lines disappear.
- **Logo and colors** are not edited here: they come from **Identity**. The **Edit** link
  on the **Shared identity** card takes you there.
- **The contact details** your client sees come from **Settings › General**: if you
  leave WhatsApp, email or phone empty, that shortcut does not appear.

## Cord branding by plan

| Plan | Cord branding in the portal |
|---|---|
| Free | Always visible. The switch appears disabled and takes you to review your plan. |
| Starter, Professional, Scale and Developer | You can hide it with **Show Cord branding**. |

If you downgrade to Free, the branding shows on your links again even if you had turned
it off; when you return to an eligible plan your choice is respected again.

## Common problems

**I saved an announcement and don't see it.** Open the link in a new window and confirm
the text was not left empty. Remember the announcement appears on the quote link.

**The client says they can't comment.** Check that **Comments and counteroffers** is on
and that the quote is still open.

**I can't remove Cord branding.** You need the Starter plan or higher; see
[Your subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).
