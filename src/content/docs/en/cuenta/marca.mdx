## First impressions matter

When a prospect receives your quote, the first thing they see is Cord's **Interactive Public Link**. Ensuring this link carries your visual identity is key to conveying professionalism and trust.

Open **Settings › Brand appearance**. The category has three tabs: **Identity**, **Client portal**, and **Custom domain**. Changes apply when you save; until then you can **undo changes** or **reset style**.

## Identity

Your identity is shared: it's used on your portal, your quotes, and your emails.

### 1. Logo
- Upload a **PNG, JPG, SVG, or WEBP** image up to **1 MB**. Its original proportions are preserved.
- You can upload a **primary** logo and an **on color** variant, used on colored document, portal, and email headers.
- Choose the **logo size**: small, medium, or large.

### 2. Brand palette
- Start with one of the suggested palettes or enter your exact hex colors (Example: `#0071e3`).
- The **primary** color is required; the **secondary** color is optional.

### 3. Typography
- Choose **System**, **Humanist**, or **Editorial**. It applies to the portal, quotes, and emails; commercial invoices use an equivalent print font.

<Callout type="info">
**Tip:** button text automatically adapts to your primary color to stay readable.
</Callout>

### Preview

The preview shows a sample quote with your brand on **Portal**, **Quote**, **PDF**, and **Email**, on desktop or mobile, and lets you compare your draft with what's saved. It doesn't generate activity or messages.

## Client portal

Under **Settings › Brand appearance › Client portal** you adjust the layout, top
announcement, welcome, comments and counteroffers, and Cord branding (removing it requires
the **Starter plan or above**). Full guide: [Client portal](/en/docs/cuenta/portal-cliente).

## Custom domain

From the **Professional** plan you can serve your links from a subdomain of your company,
for example `quotes.yourcompany.com`. Full guide:
[Custom domain](/en/docs/cuenta/dominio-propio).

## PDF document and emails

The PDF design (template, footer message and terms) is configured under
**Settings › Quotes › PDF document**. The sender, introduction and signature of your
emails, under **Settings › Notifications › Email** (customizing them requires the Starter
plan or above).
