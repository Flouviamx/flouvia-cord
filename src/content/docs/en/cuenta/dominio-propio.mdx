<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Custom domain</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Give your quotes and invoices an address that carries your brand, such as quotes.yourcompany.com.</p>
</header>

With a custom domain, the link your client receives no longer starts with `cordhq.app`
and uses a subdomain of your company instead. Your main website does not change: Cord
only uses the address you assign to it.

## Requirements

- **Professional plan or higher.** On Free and Starter the tab shows
  **View Professional plan**.
- **Settings permission** in your role.
- **An unused subdomain** of a domain you already own, for example
  `quotes.yourcompany.com`. You do not need to buy another domain. Root domains
  (`yourcompany.com`), wildcards, IP addresses, ports and paths are not accepted.
- **Access to the DNS panel** where you manage your domain.
- **Being outside the test environment.** In test mode the tab shows
  **Unavailable in the test environment**.

An organization has one custom domain at a time.

## Connect your domain

Open **Settings › Brand appearance › Custom domain**.

<Steps>
1. **Choose your domain.** In **Subdomain** enter only the hostname, without
   `https://`, paths or ports, and select **Save domain**.
2. **Configure DNS.** Cord shows a table with **Type**, **Name** and **Value**. First
   comes an ownership **TXT** record, named `_cord.` followed by your subdomain. Add it in
   your DNS panel exactly as shown (use **Copy name** and **Copy value**).
3. Select **Verify connection**. Once Cord confirms the TXT, the **CNAME** record that
   connects your subdomain appears. Add it as well.
4. Select **Verify connection** again until the status reads **Active**. DNS changes can
   take time to propagate, and the HTTPS certificate also needs time.
</Steps>

{/* MOCKUP: Custom domain tab with the three numbered steps, the TXT and CNAME records table, and the "Waiting for DNS" status */}

<Callout type="warning">
**Keep the TXT record** after your domain is active: Cord checks it again periodically.
Only change the subdomain you are going to use, without touching your website or email
records. If your DNS panel appends the domain to the name automatically, do not type it
twice.
</Callout>

## Connection statuses

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup pending** | The domain is saved, but the connection is not active. | Add the DNS records and verify. |
| **Waiting for DNS** | Cord has not confirmed your records yet. | Check names and values; verify again later. |
| **Preparing HTTPS** | DNS is correct and the certificate is being issued. | Wait a few minutes and verify again. |
| **Active** | Your address works and is secured with HTTPS. | Nothing; you can open it with **Open domain**. |
| **Needs attention** | The connection could not be completed. | Read the message shown with the status; if it persists, contact support. |

Status is checked when you open the tab or select **Verify connection**. The screen shows
the **Last verified** date.

## What changes once it is active

- **New links use your domain.** The public link of every quote (`/q/...`) and every
  invoice (`/i/...`), including the one in the emails Cord sends on your behalf, is built
  with your subdomain.
- **The root of your subdomain** shows a minimal page with your business branding.
- **Only your documents are served.** Your domain cannot be used to enter the app,
  Settings or another organization's documents: a link that does not belong to your
  account does not open there.
- **Your links are not indexed** by search engines.

## If the domain stops being available

- **If the connection fails or a verification expires,** Cord tries to verify it again
  when someone opens a link. Until it is confirmed, new links are generated with
  `cordhq.app` so your sends keep working.
- **If you downgrade,** links your clients already have with your domain redirect them
  to the same quote or invoice on `cordhq.app`. Nothing is deleted; you get your domain
  back when you return to an eligible plan.
- **If the tab says "Custom domains are being prepared",** the connection is not yet
  available for your account. Your links keep working on `cordhq.app`; you will be able
  to set it up there when it is ready.

## Disconnect or change domain

To use a different domain, first disconnect the current one with **Disconnect domain**
and confirm. Links using that address will no longer be available at it. Cord does not
remove records from your DNS provider: delete them yourself if you no longer need them.

## Common problems

**"We could not find the ownership TXT."** Check that the name starts with `_cord.` and
that the value is complete, without extra quotes.

**"The DNS server did not respond."** Do not change your records because of this
message: wait one minute and verify again.

**"This address is already assigned or needs a review."** Contact support. Do not
delete its configuration to try connecting it again.

**"Enter a valid domain name."** Enter only the subdomain, without a protocol, paths or
ports.

**"You do not have permission to manage domains."** Ask your organization's
administrator to give you the Settings permission or to make the connection.
