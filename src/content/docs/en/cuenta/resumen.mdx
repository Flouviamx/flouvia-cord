## Your account, ready step by step

Cord is available to businesses in the 12 markets currently offered: Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru. To finish getting started, the floating **Setup Widget** organizes 11 tasks into 5 sections and updates its progress as you complete each one; you can minimize it without losing your progress.

1. **Set up your business**
   - **[Customize your brand](/en/docs/cuenta/marca):** logo, color, and contact details for your public link.
   - **Complete your tax details:** your country's invoicing identity — in Mexico, RFC, tax regime, and postal code to stamp CFDI 4.0 (and then the [CSD](/en/docs/cuenta/csd)); outside Mexico, your legal name, your local tax ID, and your address.
   - **Customize your PDF and portal:** the message, terms, and welcome copy that go on your PDFs and portal.
2. **Build your catalog**
   - **[Create your catalog](/en/docs/cuenta/catalogo):** add at least one product or service.
   - **Add your clients:** register your first client.
3. **Close your first sale**
   - **Create your first quote:** manually or with [Cord AI](/en/docs/cotizacion/ia).
   - **Send your first quote:** to the prospect's email, or by sharing the link.
4. **Get paid**
   - **Turn on online payments:** connect Cord Payments (card, plus SPEI in Mexico) or, where applicable, [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago). In Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru, where Cord Payments isn't available, the step is completed with Mercado Pago.
   - **Charge and invoice:** the task is marked once your first quote is paid —online or marked by hand— or invoiced.
5. **Grow your operation**
   - **[Automate your follow-up](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows):** turn on your first workflow.
   - **[Invite your team](/en/docs/cuenta/equipo-seguridad):** invite a second member with the right role (team invitations require Pro or above).

Every task marks itself done automatically as soon as Cord detects the change in your
account — no checkbox to tick by hand. The section open by default is always the first
one you still have pending.

<Callout type="info">
**Tip:** Do you have multiple companies?
Cord is **multi-tenant**. You can manage several companies or legal entities from a single login. The setup widget tracks progress independently per company, so one company's progress never mixes with another's.

</Callout>
