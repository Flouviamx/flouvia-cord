<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Create your account</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">From sign-up to your first screen in Cord: verification, country of operation and initial setup.</p>
</header>

Creating a Cord account has three moments: **signing up** (your personal identity),
**setting up your workspace** (the organization you will quote and collect with) and,
optionally, **setting up with AI** your brand, catalog and taxes. Every new account
starts on the **Free** plan, with no card required and no expiration date.

## Requirements

- **Country:** your business must operate in one of the 12 available countries: Mexico,
  the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain, the United Kingdom, Germany, France,
  Colombia, Argentina, Chile or Peru.
- **Plan:** none. Signing up creates a Free account; you can change plans later from
  [Your subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).
- **Email:** if you sign up with a password, you need access to the inbox to confirm the
  address before you can sign in.

## 1. Sign up

<Steps>
1. Open [cordhq.app/sign-up](https://cordhq.app/sign-up).
2. Choose how to sign up:
   - **With email:** enter your first name, last name, work email and a password of at
     least 8 characters. The meter under the field tells you how
     strong it is.
   - **With Google or Apple:** use the **Google** or **Apple** button below the form.
3. Check both legal boxes: you accept the **Terms and Conditions** and confirm you read
   the **Privacy Notice**. Without them, the sign-up buttons stay disabled. Confirming
   the privacy notice does not authorize optional analytics.
4. Select the button to create your account (or the Google or Apple button).
</Steps>

### Email verification

If you signed up with email and password, Cord **does not sign you in yet**: it takes
you to a verification screen and emails you a link. Open it from your inbox to confirm
the address and continue.

- The link expires after **24 hours**.
- The resend option on that screen sends a new link and invalidates the previous one:
  always use the most recent.
- If the link is no longer valid, the screen tells you and lets you request another.

With **Google** or **Apple** there is no verification step: the provider has already
confirmed your email.

<Callout type="info">
**Were you invited to a team?** If you arrived from an invitation, sign up with the same
email you were invited with. After you confirm your email, Cord takes you back to the
invitation so you can join that organization with the permissions you were given. You
will not see the setup wizard: only the organization owner completes it. See
[Team, permissions and security](/en/docs/cuenta/equipo-seguridad).
</Callout>

## 2. Set up your workspace

The first time an organization owner enters the app, Cord opens a full-screen
**four-step** wizard. You can move forward with **Continue** or the `Enter` key, and go
back with **Back**.

<Steps>
1. **What's your company called?** Enter the name your clients will see on every quote
   and choose the **Country you operate in**. Cord preselects a country based on your
   location; check it before continuing.
2. **What's your role?** Owner or director, Sales, Finance / Admin, Operations or Other.
3. **What does your business do?** Choose your industry and team size.
4. **What will you use Cord for?** Select all that apply (quote faster, collect payments
   online, issue invoices, follow up on quotes, control margins or automate collections)
   and select **Finish**.
</Steps>

### What your country sets up

The country is not decorative: Cord uses it so the account is ready to operate in your
market from day one.

| What is set | How |
|---|---|
| **Currency** | The country's currency, for example MXN in Mexico, EUR in Spain, Germany and France, GBP in the United Kingdom, COP in Colombia. It is the currency you quote and charge your clients in; you can quote in another currency when you need to. |
| **Time zone** | That of the country's capital or main business center. Your quotes and invoices are dated with it. |
| **Language** | Spanish if the country's language is Spanish; English everywhere else. |
| **Taxes** | Cord seeds your tax catalog with the country's standard, editable rates (see below). |
| **Tax vocabulary** | The name of your identifier (RFC, NIF / CIF, EIN / Tax ID, CUIT, NIT, RUT, RUC...) and of the tax (IVA, VAT, USt., TVA, IGV, Sales tax...). |
| **Invoicing** | In Mexico, CFDI 4.0 with your CSD. In the other countries, a commercial invoice with a number and PDF. See [Invoicing by country](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion). |

You can change all of these later in **Settings › General**, except the tax catalog,
which you edit in **Settings › Quotes › Taxes**.

### Taxes created at the start

Seeded rates are a starting point, not tax advice: review them with your accountant and
adjust the ones that do not apply to your business.

- **Mexico:** IVA 16%, IVA 8% border region, Exempt and the most common IVA and ISR
  withholdings.
- **Spain:** IVA 21%, 10% and 4%, Exempt and IRPF withholdings (15%, and 7% for new
  self-employed).
- **Canada:** GST 5%, the provincial HST rates, QST, PST and RST, and Zero-rated.
- **United Kingdom, Germany and France:** the country's VAT, USt. and TVA rates and
  Zero-rated.
- **Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru:** the country's IVA or IGV and Exempt; Colombia
  and Peru also include their usual withholdings.
- **United States:** there is no national rate. As soon as you declare your state in
  your tax details, Cord offers that state's sales tax plus the exempt option.
- **Brazil:** no suggested rate on purpose (taxes depend on the state and the
  municipality); enter your own in Settings.

### Online payments by country

| Country | How you collect online inside the link |
|---|---|
| Mexico and Brazil | Cord Payments or Mercado Pago. In Mexico, Cord Payments includes SPEI. |
| United States, Canada, Spain, United Kingdom, Germany and France | Cord Payments. |
| Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru | Mercado Pago. Cord Payments is not available in these countries and Cord tells you before you start. |

In every country you can record manual payments (bank transfer, cash) without
connecting a payment method. See [Payment methods](/en/docs/pagos/metodos).

## 3. Set up with AI (optional)

When you select **Finish**, Cord offers **Set up your account with AI**: you give it
your website, a description of your business or your price list, and it proposes your
brand, catalog, terms and taxes for you to review before applying them.

- **Build my proposal** generates the proposal; review it and select **Apply setup**.
- **I'll set it up later** takes you straight to your dashboard. The assistant stays
  available in **Settings › General › Set up with AI**.

When you finish, if you are going to connect Cord to your own system, the
**Connecting Cord to your code?** panel shows three
[CLI](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/cli) commands that check themselves off as
you run them.

Full details in [Set up your account with AI](/en/docs/cuenta/configurar-con-ia).

## 4. Finish setup with the widget

While your account is incomplete, the app shows the **Set up Cord** widget with 11 tasks
in 5 sections. Each task checks itself off when Cord detects the change in your account;
there are no boxes to tick by hand.

| Section | Task | It checks off when... |
|---|---|---|
| Set up your business | Customize your brand | You upload a logo or enter a contact email or phone. |
| | Complete your tax details | You save your tax identifier (RFC, NIF, EIN...). |
| | Customize your PDF and portal | You write the PDF message or terms, or the portal welcome. |
| Build your catalog | Create your catalog | You have at least one product. |
| | Add your clients | You have at least one client. |
| Close your first sale | Create your first quote | You save your first quote. |
| | Send your first quote | A quote leaves draft. |
| Get paid | Turn on online payments (or **Get paid with Mercado Pago** in CO, AR, CL and PE) | Any online payment method is enabled. |
| | Charge and invoice | A quote is paid or invoiced. |
| Grow your operation | Automate your follow-up | You have a published, active workflow; a draft does not count. |
| | Invite your team | There is a second active or invited member (requires the Pro plan or higher). |

<DmOnboardingWidget lang="en" />

The widget can be **minimized to the bar**: it becomes a pill with your completion
percentage (**Setup guide**) in the top bar. The section open by default is the first
one with pending tasks. Each organization tracks its own progress: if you manage
several, one does not mix with another. A test organization never completes the widget.

## Common problems

**"Check your details — email must be valid and password at least 8 characters."** Fix
the email format or use a longer password.

**An account with that email already exists.** Cord offers **Sign in instead**. If you
do not remember the password, use recovery from the sign-in screen.

**The verification email did not arrive.** Check spam and promotions, wait a couple of
minutes and use the resend option on the verification screen. Only the latest link works.

**I chose the wrong country.** Change it in **Settings › General**. Then review the
currency, the time zone and your tax catalog: Cord does not replace rates you already
had.

**I don't see the four-step wizard.** It only appears to the owner of an organization
that has not completed it yet. An invited member goes straight into the app.
