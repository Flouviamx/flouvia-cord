  <p class="page-subtitle">What each category and tab contains, who it affects, and where the detailed guide is.</p>
</header>

Open **Settings** with the gear in the top bar. The index groups categories into four
sections: **Personal settings**, **Account settings**, **Product settings** and
**Developers**. Each category opens its first tab and shows the others in a horizontal
bar.

The index search (and the **⌘K** palette) also recognizes alternative terms: IVA, VAT,
RFC, NIF, CSD, SAML, DNS, API and others.

<Callout type="info">
Organization changes affect everyone working in that workspace. Your password, 2FA,
passkeys and sessions are personal and don't change when you switch organizations.
</Callout>

## Before changing anything

- Confirm you are in the right organization, and outside the test environment if you are
  configuring something real.
- **Settings** controls most tabs; **Team**, **Payment configuration** and other
  sensitive operations have their own permissions.
- Many values are defaults for new documents: documents already sent or issued keep their
  version.
- Availability by country and plan is shown inside each screen. An option appearing on
  this map does not mean it is enabled for every account.

## Personal settings

### Your account › Profile & security

Your name, picture, emails, password, connected Google or Apple accounts, 2FA, backup
codes, passkeys, active sessions, and deleting your personal account. Guide:
[Team, permissions and security](/en/docs/cuenta/equipo-seguridad).

## Account settings

### General

| Tab | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **General** | Business name, base currency, contact email and phone, address, website, WhatsApp, country of operation, language and time zone. Guide: [Configure your account](/en/docs/cuenta/configuracion). |
| **Set up with AI** | A proposal for your brand, profile, taxes, catalog and templates based on your website and price list. Guide: [Set up your account with AI](/en/docs/cuenta/configurar-con-ia). |

Changing the base currency does not convert historical amounts.

### Brand appearance

| Tab | What it contains | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | Primary and on-color logo, size, palette and typography, with a preview on portal, quote, PDF and email. Guide: [Customize your branding](/en/docs/cuenta/marca). | All |
| **Client portal** | Layout, top announcement, welcome, comments and counteroffers, Cord branding. Guide: [Client portal](/en/docs/cuenta/portal-cliente). | All; removing Cord branding from Starter |
| **Custom domain** | A subdomain of your company for your links, with DNS records and verification. Guide: [Custom domain](/en/docs/cuenta/dominio-propio). | Professional or higher |

### Team & permissions

| Tab | What it contains | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Team & roles** | Members, invitations, roles and per-section permissions. | Inviting from Professional |
| **SSO** | SAML 2.0 connections, **Require SSO** and emergency access. Requires the Team permission. | Scale or Developer |
| **Security** | Require 2FA, allowed domains for invitations and inactivity timeout. | All |

Guide: [Team, permissions and security](/en/docs/cuenta/equipo-seguridad).

### Notifications

| Tab | What it contains | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications** | A matrix of which event alerts through each channel: email, Slack and Microsoft Teams. Slack and Teams only alert if their channel is connected; the new-member alert only arrives by email. Guide: [Notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones). | All |
| **Email** | Sender name, reply-to address, introduction and signature of the emails to your clients, with a preview. | Customizing from Starter |

### Plans & subscription

**Subscription:** your effective plan, status, cycle, usage for the period, plan changes
and access to Billing for your card, receipts and invoices. Guide:
[Your Cord subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).

### Advanced

| Tab | What it contains | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Data & privacy** | Download everything (JSON), catalog and clients as CSV, data retention, and the danger zone to delete the organization (requires recent authentication). | All |
| **Settings history** | Who changed the configuration and when, with previous and saved values (last 50). You can restore appearance and text values with no later changes; it does not restore logos, payments, permissions or tax data. | Professional or higher |
| **Audit log** | Relevant actions with actor, date, resource and context. | Professional or higher |

## Product settings

### Quotes

| Tab | What it contains | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Numbering & tax** (outside Mexico, **Numbering & taxes**) | Number prefix and series, validity, payment terms, deposit and default legal text. | All |
| **Taxes** | Rate catalog with name, type (consumption, withholding or exempt) and rate, and whether your prices include tax. Guide: [Products and pricing](/en/docs/cotizacion/productos). | All |
| **PDF document** | Minimal, Classic or Detailed template, footer message, terms and line-item listing. | All |
| **Approvals** | Internal thresholds for maximum discount, maximum amount and minimum margin. Any late-interest rate shown here is suspended: it generates no new charges. Guide: [Credit terms](/en/docs/pagos/credito). | Scale or Developer |
| **Templates** | Reference texts for email, WhatsApp or notes with variables such as `{cliente}`, `{folio}`, `{total}` and `{link}`. For now the send buttons use their own text. | All |

### Discounts

| Tab | What it contains | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Coupons** | Discount codes by percentage or fixed amount, with validity dates, currency, total and per-client usage caps, and a switch to pause them. Creating, editing, or deleting requires the Settings permission. Guide: [Discounts and coupons](/en/docs/cotizacion/descuentos). | All |

### Invoicing

**Tax details** (outside Mexico, **Tax profile**): in Mexico, RFC, legal name, tax
regime, postal code, CFDI use and your CSD to stamp CFDI 4.0; in the other countries, your
local tax identifier, address and invoice prefix. Issued invoices live in **Invoices** in
the sidebar. Guides: [Add tax information (CSD)](/en/docs/cuenta/csd) and
[Invoicing by country](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion).

### Payments

**Payments:** connect Cord Payments and complete your account verification, choose
methods (card; SPEI only in Mexico; SEPA debit in Spain, Germany and France, and ACH in
the United States), decide whether your clients can turn on **automatic payments**, review fees and manage the payout account (changing
it requires recent authentication and the Payment configuration permission). In Mexico
and Brazil you can also connect Mercado Pago; in Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru it is
the way to collect online. Guides: [Connect account (KYC)](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding),
[Payment methods](/en/docs/pagos/metodos), [Automatic payments](/en/docs/pagos/cobro-automatico) and [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago).

### Integrations

**Integrations:** a directory of 13 apps —HubSpot, Shopify, Google Sheets, Microsoft
Excel, Gmail, QuickBooks Online, Xero, Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp Business, Make,
Zapier and n8n— with one page per app to connect it, configure it and see the workflows
that use it. Apps marked as upcoming have no committed date. Guide:
[Integrations](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones).

## Developers

This group appears in the index only when you turn on **Developer mode** at the bottom of
Settings. The same switch turns on the **Cord Workbench** dock across the app. Each tab's
direct URL works even when the mode is off.

| Category | What it contains | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **MCP** | Configuration to connect an AI assistant to Cord through the MCP server. Guide: [MCP Server](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/mcp). | All |
| **Artificial intelligence** | Status of **AI collections** (Off, On · with your approval or On · automatic) and connections to your other systems that the AI can look up when quoting. Guide: [Autonomous collections (AI)](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia). | AI collections from Professional; agent governance on Scale or Developer |
| **Embeddable quote builder** | Preview, allowed domains and code to embed the quote builder on your site. Guide: [Cord Elements](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/elements/resumen). | All |

API keys, webhooks, logs and events are not in Settings: they live in
[Cord Workbench](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/workbench).

## If a change doesn't show up

<Steps>
1. Confirm the save finished without an error.
2. Check the effective organization, permission, country and plan.
3. Open a new document if the option only works as a default.
4. Leave test mode if you are validating an external provider.
5. If it persists, note the route, time, user, expected result and visible message
   before contacting [support](https://cordhq.app/soporte).
</Steps>
