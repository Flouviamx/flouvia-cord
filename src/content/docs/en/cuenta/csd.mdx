## What is a CSD and why do you need it?

The **Digital Seal Certificate (CSD)** is an electronic document issued by the SAT that allows you to digitally sign (stamp) your electronic invoices. Unlike the FIEL (e.firma), the CSD is used exclusively for issuing tax receipts.

For Cord to convert your approved quotes or orders directly into an official invoice with the SAT without having to re-enter data on another portal, **it is mandatory** to upload your CSD. CFDI issuance requires the **Starter plan or above**; see tax invoice allowances in [Your subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).

## Before you start

- **Starter plan or above.** The CSD can't be uploaded on Free.
- **Settings permission** in your organization role.
- **Complete tax details:** RFC, legal name, tax regime, and issuing postal code, entered on the same screen. Without them Cord rejects the upload and asks you to complete them first.
- **Recent sign-in:** because this is a sensitive action, Cord may ask you to confirm your identity again before accepting the files.

## Steps to upload your CSD in Cord

1. Go to **Settings › Invoicing › Tax details**.
2. In the **Digital Seal Certificate (CSD)** section, choose your files and enter the password:
   - **`.cer` file**: The public certificate.
   - **`.key` file**: Your private key.
   - **Key password**: The password you chose when processing your CSD with the SAT (Note: this is usually different from your FIEL password). It is only used to decrypt your `.key` and is not stored in Cord.
3. Click **Upload CSD and connect**.

<Callout type="warning">
**Warning:** the file and password travel over HTTPS/TLS to the server and fiscal provider. Cord keeps only the secret reference required to operate the integration encrypted at rest; do not describe this as end-to-end encryption. We never use your FIEL: upload only the CSD and never your FIEL (e.firma) for invoicing.
</Callout>

## Validations and Common Errors

When you upload your CSD, the fiscal provider Cord stamps with reviews the certificate. If it is rejected, you'll see the reason on screen. The most common cases:
- **Validity:** an expired CSD (they last 4 years) is rejected; renew it on the SAT portal and upload it again.
- **RFC:** Cord stamps under the certificate's RFC, so the CSD must belong to the RFC you entered in your tax details.
- **Incorrect password:** if the password can't open the `.key` file, the upload is rejected.

Once accepted, the section shows **CSD uploaded**, and any closed quote will be able to issue its CFDI 4.0 with a single click.
