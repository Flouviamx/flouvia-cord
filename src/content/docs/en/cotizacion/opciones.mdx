## What it is

Beyond client and lines, each quote carries its commercial and financial conditions.
Payment terms are chosen with the client, in step 1 (**Who are you quoting?**).
Validity, deposit, currency, and retainer live in step 3, **Terms** ("How long it's
valid, how it's paid and in which currency."), below the lines. In the **Review and
send** summary on the right of the editor, only the tax-inclusive switch remains, next
to the totals. The values a new quote starts with are set once in **Settings › Quotes**.

## Requirements

- **Permission:** Quotes to use the options; Settings to change the defaults.
- **Plan:** every option is on every plan. The monthly retainer and paying the deposit online require an active payment rail.
- **Country:** the invoice series is Mexico only; SPEI is Mexico and MXN only.

## Payment terms

In **Who are you quoting?** you choose a chip: **Due on receipt**, **Net 15**,
**Net 30**, or **Net 60**. The **Other term** chip offers **Net 7**, **Net 45**, and
**Net 90**. When you pick a client, Cord preselects the terms from their profile. Below
the summary total you read the term together with the validity, for example "Net 30 ·
valid for 30 days".

- **Due on receipt:** payment is available as soon as the client approves.
- **Net N (credit):** the pay button doesn't appear on approval. The client sees that the order is confirmed on credit and the date it's due; online payment is enabled on that date, counted from approval.

## Deposit

In the **Terms** step, the **Deposit (%)** field (optional) takes a percentage between 1
and 99. Below it, the editor tells you how much the client pays on approval and how
much later, for example "Upon approval, your client pays MXN 3,000.00 as a deposit and
the rest (MXN 7,000.00) per the terms." On approval, Cord splits the
charge into deposit and balance: the client pays the deposit immediately (even if the
terms are on credit) and the balance keeps its due date. Even before approval, the
link shows what they'll pay once approved. Details in [Upfront and
balance](/en/docs/pagos/anticipos).

## Monthly retainer

In the **Terms** step, turn on **Monthly recurring charge (retainer)** to charge the
total every month by card.

- It only exists with **Due on receipt** terms: if you choose any Net term, the switch disappears.
- It excludes a deposit: when on, the deposit field is hidden.
- The client authorizes their card once from the link on approval; they pay the first month then, and the same amount is charged every month until it's canceled.

Details in [Monthly retainers](/en/docs/pagos/igualas).

## Tax-inclusive prices and withholding

- **Prices include VAT** (with your country's tax name: IVA, GST, Sales tax…): it's the switch in the **Review and send** summary, above the total. With it on, the price you enter already includes tax and Cord breaks it out instead of adding it on top. To have it on for every new quote, turn it on in **Settings › Quotes › Taxes**, under **Behavior in quotes**.
- **Tax per line:** each line carries its rate, taken from your tax catalog. See [Products and discounts](/en/docs/cotizacion/productos).
- **Withholding:** if your catalog has active withholding entries marked as default, Cord applies them to every quote and **subtracts** them from the total; they show as rows in the summary, on the link, and in the PDF. They aren't toggled per quote: they're managed in **Settings › Quotes › Taxes**. Where your country has no withholding, no row appears.

## Sales currency and accounting currency

These are two different pieces of data and Cord doesn't mix them:

- **Sales currency:** the one you choose in **Which currency are you quoting in?**, inside the **Terms** step. It's what the client sees on the link, what they're charged in, and what the invoice is issued in. You can choose MXN, USD, CAD, BRL, EUR, GBP, COP, ARS, CLP, PEN, JPY, CNY, CHF, or AUD.
- **Accounting currency:** your business's currency, set in **Settings › General**. It's the one you keep your books in.

When both are the same there's no exchange rate. When they differ, Cord gets a real,
dated rate from an external provider and adds a coverage cushion:

| Option | Cushion |
|---|---|
| **Light** | +1% |
| **Normal** (default) | +2% |
| **Cautious** | +5% |

If you change the currency of a document that already has lines, Cord warns you that
prices are not converted: the lines keep the same numbers, now in the new currency, and
you need to review each price.

When the sales currency differs from your business's, the **Terms** step shows a panel
with **Rate today**, **Your protected rate**, and the question "How cautious do you want
to be?" to choose the cushion. The rate is
locked when you save the quote (or when you change the currency pair on a draft) and
isn't recalculated: the invoice declares it as the exchange rate even if the market moves.

<Callout type="warning">
**No provable rate, no save.** If no source publishes that pair's rate at that moment,
Cord doesn't save the quote in that currency and asks you to try again or quote in your
business currency. It never uses a made-up rate. The "Rate locked for 30 days" label is
informational: the rate is kept as long as you don't change the pair.
</Callout>

More in [Treasury and FX Lock](/en/docs/pagos/tesoreria-fx).

## Validity

In the **Terms** step, the **Valid for** field sets how many days the proposal is valid:
15, 30, 60, or your organization's default. A daily process marks as *Expired* every sent or viewed quote
whose date has passed, and three days earlier it can alert you (see
[Notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones)). An expired quote no longer accepts the
client's approval.

## Notes and conditions

- **Note for your client** (optional): the block at the end of the editor, with free text for the quote (for example, "on-site delivery included"). It appears on the link and in the PDF.
- **PDF conditions:** your business policies (cancellation, delivery, warranty) are set once in **Settings › Quotes › PDF document**, one per line, and are printed only in the PDF.

## Defaults (Settings › Quotes)

In **Settings › Quotes › Numbering & tax** you define what every quote starts with:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Quote number prefix** | Up to 6 letters or numbers (Cord uppercases it). The quote number is prefix, hyphen, and a 4-digit sequence, for example COT-0149. Without a prefix, Cord uses COT. |
| **Series (optional)** | Mexico only: series for CFDI numbering. |
| **Default rate (%)** | The rate new lines get when your catalog has no profile marked as default. |
| **Withholding applied** | Read only: lists your catalog's default withholding, with the **Manage taxes** link. |
| **Default validity (days)** | Between 1 and 365. The editor starts with this value. |
| **Default payment terms** | Due on receipt or a Net term (7, 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 days). Preselected on creation; the client's profile wins when you pick one. |
| **Default deposit (%)** | Between 1 and 99, or empty for no deposit. Prefills the editor field. |
| **Default legal text** | Saved on your organization, but today it isn't printed on the link or in the PDF. For text that is printed, use **PDF document**. |

Changing a default doesn't modify quotes already created.

## Limits by plan

The options on this page have no cap by plan. Online payment, retainers, and SPEI
depend on having an active payment rail in your country (see [Payment
methods](/en/docs/pagos/metodos)).

## Common problems

**I don't see the retainer switch.** You chose a Net term; retainers only exist with Due on receipt terms.

**I can't change validity, deposit, currency, or retainer.** You're building a new version of a quote that was already sent: the client, currency, and terms stay as they were sent. If you need different terms, duplicate the quote.

**I don't see the deposit field.** You have the retainer turned on.

**Cord couldn't get the exchange rate.** No source published that pair's rate at that moment. Try again shortly or quote in your business currency.

**I changed the default validity and my quotes didn't change.** That's expected: it applies to new ones.
