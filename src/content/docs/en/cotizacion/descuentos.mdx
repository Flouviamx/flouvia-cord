## What it is

Cord has two ways to lower the price of a sale, and both can live on the same document:

- **Negotiated price per line.** You lower the price of one specific line. It's the
  difference between the list price and the negotiated price, and the panel shows it as
  **You discounted them**. See [Products and pricing](/en/docs/cotizacion/productos).
- **Document discount.** A reduction on the **whole** quote or invoice: a
  **percentage** or an **amount** in the document's currency. Cord applies it **before
  tax** and spreads it across the lines in proportion to their amount. You can enter it
  by hand or with a **coupon**.

A **coupon** is a reusable code (for example, `WELCOME10`) that you create under
**Settings › Discounts › Coupons**, with its value, its validity dates, and how many
times it can be used. Your team applies it in the editor, and your buyers can enter it
when they quote from your website through the API or Cord Elements.

## Apply a discount

The control lives in the editor's **Review and send** panel, between the subtotal and
the taxes. It's in the new-quote editor, when editing a quote, and in the invoice
editor.

<Steps>
1. In **Review and send**, click **+ Add discount or coupon**.
2. Choose the type: **Percent** (0 to 100), **Amount** (in the document's currency), or
   **Coupon**.
3. Type the value or the code and click **Apply**. A coupon is validated right then: if
   it doesn't apply, the panel tells you why (see [Coupon messages](#coupon-messages)).
4. The summary shows the row **Discount (10%)** or **Discount (WELCOME10)** with the
   amount in green, and the taxes and total already calculated on the discounted base.
   To take it off, click **Remove** next to the label.
</Steps>

A document carries **one** document discount at a time. If you send a coupon and a
manual discount together (for example, from the API), the coupon wins.

## How it's calculated

1. **Gross subtotal:** the sum of the lines at their negotiated price, before tax.
2. **Discount:** the percentage of that subtotal, or the amount you entered. An amount
   larger than the subtotal is capped at the subtotal: the discount never makes the sale
   negative.
3. **Spread per line:** each line gets a share proportional to its gross amount. The
   spread is done in the currency's smallest unit (cents in MXN, cents in EUR, whole
   units in CLP or JPY), so the shares add up to exactly the discount and no line goes
   negative.
4. **Taxes:** each line calculates its tax on its already-discounted base, with its own
   rate. That's why an exempt line and a taxed line each get their share of the
   discount and the tax only drops where it should.
5. **Withholdings:** if your catalog has withholdings (for example, ISR or IRPF), they're
   calculated on the discounted bases.

| Example (Mexico, 16% VAT) | No discount | With 10% |
|---|---:|---:|
| Subtotal | 169,370.00 MXN | 169,370.00 MXN |
| Discount (WELCOME10) | — | −16,937.00 MXN |
| IVA 16% | 27,099.20 MXN | 24,389.28 MXN |
| **Total** | **196,469.20 MXN** | **176,822.28 MXN** |

**Tax-inclusive prices.** If you turned on **Prices include IVA** (or your country's
tax), an **amount** discount lowers what your client pays, and the pre-tax base is
broken out afterwards. A percentage works the same with or without tax-inclusive prices.

<Callout type="warning">
The browser and the API never send the discount amount: they send the **definition**
(percentage or amount) or the coupon **code**. Cord recalculates on the server with the
same engine the editor uses, so what you see is what's saved, charged, and invoiced.
</Callout>

## Create and manage coupons

<DmCouponsSettings lang="en" />

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings › Discounts › Coupons** with the **Settings** permission.
2. Click **New coupon**.
3. Type the **Code**: 3 to 32 letters, numbers, hyphens, or underscores. Cord turns it
   into uppercase and doesn't allow two coupons with the same code on your account.
4. Optional: an internal **Name** (for example, "New client promotion").
5. Choose the **Discount**: **Percentage** or **Fixed amount**, and its **Value**. A
   fixed amount also asks for its **Currency**, among the ones your account offers when
   quoting.
6. Optional: **Valid from** and **Valid until**. The dates are read in your business's
   time zone.
7. Optional: **Total uses** and **Uses per client**. Empty means unlimited.
8. Click **Create coupon**.
</Steps>

| Rule | Details |
|---|---|
| **Currency** | A percentage coupon works in any currency. A fixed-amount coupon only applies to documents in **its** currency: a 2,500 MXN coupon can't be applied to a quote in USD. |
| **What you can edit** | The name, the validity dates, the usage limits, and whether it's active. The **code, type, value, and currency don't change** once it's created: a document that already carries it keeps what it discounted. For a different value, create another coupon. |
| **Deactivate** | The toggle on each row. A deactivated coupon can no longer be applied to new documents; documents that already carry it keep it. |
| **Delete** | Only a coupon that was never used. One with uses is deactivated instead, to keep its history. |
| **Per-client limit** | A coupon with **Uses per client** requires choosing the client before applying it. |

## When a use is counted

A coupon is **applied** to the document when you validate it, but its use is
**counted** when the document becomes binding:

| Document | The use is counted | The use is returned |
|---|---|---|
| **Quote** | When it's **approved**: the client clicks **Approve quote** on the link, or you use **Mark approved**. | If the quote is **rejected** or **expires**. |
| **Invoice** | When it's **issued**, before it gets its number. | If the invoice is **voided**, or if you delete a draft whose issuance had failed. |
| **Invoice from a quote** | Not counted again: it reuses the quote's use. | Along with the quote. |

- The **validity dates** and whether the coupon is **active** are checked when it's
  **applied**. A document built while the coupon was valid keeps it even if the coupon
  later expires or you deactivate it.
- The **usage limits** are checked when applying and again when approving or issuing,
  because they're the coupon's budget. If the uses ran out between the draft and that
  moment:
  - **Invoice:** it isn't issued and no number is used up. Cord tells you the coupon has
    no uses left and to remove it from the draft to issue the invoice.
  - **Quote approved by the client:** the approval doesn't go through. Your client reads
    that the quote needs an update from you, and the activity shows that they tried to
    approve with an exhausted coupon. Remove the coupon and send the quote again.

## Coupon messages

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| That coupon does not exist. | Check the code; spaces and lowercase don't matter. |
| That coupon is deactivated. | Turn it back on in **Settings › Discounts › Coupons** if you want to keep using it. |
| That coupon is not valid yet. / That coupon has expired. | Check **Valid from** and **Valid until**. |
| That coupon is in a different currency than this document. | Use a percentage coupon or a fixed-amount one in the document's currency. |
| That coupon has no uses left. | Raise **Total uses** or create another coupon. |
| This client has already used that coupon as many times as allowed. | The per-client limit was reached. |
| That coupon is limited per client: choose a client first. | Choose the client in **Who are you quoting?** and apply it again. |

## Where the discount shows up

- **Quote link and PDF:** the row **Discount (WELCOME10)** or **Discount (10%)** between
  the subtotal and the taxes. Each line keeps its amount before the discount.
- **Quote and invoice detail**, and the **invoice link**: the same row.
- **Google Sheets and Excel:** the **discount** column of the quotes sheet, with the
  subtotal before the discount.
- **QuickBooks Online and Xero:** each line arrives with its already-discounted price.

## On your tax invoice

The discount travels, spread per line, to every tax document, in the shape each
authority requires:

| Document | How it's declared |
|---|---|
| **CFDI 4.0 (Mexico)** | Each line carries its unit value **before** the discount and its share of the discount in the **Descuento** attribute; the VAT base is the amount minus the discount. A line that the discount brings to zero can't be stamped (the SAT requires a base greater than zero for a transferred tax): Cord rejects it before stamping and tells you which one. In the [global invoice](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion#global-invoice), each sale carries its share of the discount. |
| **Verifactu (Spain)** | Each line declares its **already-discounted** base. |
| **Factur-X, XRechnung, and Peppol** | Each line with its amount before the discount, and the discount as a **document allowance**, one per VAT category and rate, so that each group in the breakdown matches the invoice to the cent. |
| **Facturae (Spain)** | Each line with its own discount. |
| **Authorized invoices in Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, and Peru** | Each line with its share of the discount, in the field each country's authority requires. |

A **credit note** spreads its amount in the same proportion, discount included.

## Copies, recurring invoices, and partial approval

| Case | What happens to the discount |
|---|---|
| **Partial approval** | Cord reapplies the definition to the approved lines: a percentage is recalculated; an amount is capped at the new subtotal. |
| **Invoice from a quote** | Inherits the definition and applies it to what's invoiced. |
| **Duplicate or repeat an invoice** | Copies each line's **gross** price and the discount separately. |
| **Recurring invoices** | Only carry a **manual** discount: a coupon isn't repeated every month. |
| **Duplicate a quote** | The copy doesn't carry the document discount: add it again if it applies. |
| **Replace a CFDI (Mexico)** | The replacement inherits the original's discount, with its coupon, without counting another use. |
| **Shopify order** | The order Cord creates in your store carries each line's negotiated price, but **not yet** the document discount. Check the order total in Shopify. |

## Discounts and internal approvals

If your organization uses [internal approvals](/en/docs/cotizacion/aprobaciones-internas):

- A **manual** document discount counts as a discount on each line for the **maximum
  discount** threshold and the **minimum margin** threshold. Moving the reduction to
  the footer doesn't get around the rule.
- A **coupon** doesn't count toward those thresholds: it was created by whoever manages
  Settings and is already an authorized reduction.

## From the API and Cord Elements

- `POST /api/v1/cotizaciones` and `POST /api/v1/facturas` accept `descuento`
  (`{ "tipo": "porcentaje" | "monto", "valor": 10 }`) or `cupon` (`"WELCOME10"`). See
  the [Quotes API](/en/docs/desarrolladores/funciones/cotizaciones) and the [Invoices
  API](/en/docs/desarrolladores/funciones/facturas).
- With a **publishable key** (your website's), the only discount accepted is a `cupon`:
  a buyer can't set a percentage for themselves.
- A coupon that doesn't exist responds `404` with `code: "coupon_no_existe"`; an
  expired, exhausted, or wrong-currency one responds `409` with `coupon_vencido`,
  `coupon_agotado`, `coupon_moneda`, and so on.

## Limits

- One document discount per document.
- Percentage from 0 to 100; an amount is capped at the gross subtotal.
- Coupon codes of 3 to 32 characters (`A-Z`, `0-9`, `-`, `_`), unique on your account.
- Uses are counted per binding document: approving and then invoicing the same sale
  counts a single use.

## Troubleshooting

**The coupon says it's exhausted, but there are documents that weren't approved.** A use
is counted when approving or issuing. Check which approved quotes or issued invoices
carry it; when an invoice is voided or a quote is rejected, the use comes back.

**I can't change a coupon's value.** The value isn't editable so it doesn't change what
it already discounted on existing documents. Deactivate it and create another.

**The CFDI is rejected because of a line at zero.** The discount left a line without a
base. Lower the discount or remove that line before stamping.

**The coupon isn't on the duplicated quote.** Duplicating doesn't copy the document
discount: apply it again.
