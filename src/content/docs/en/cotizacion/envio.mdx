## The client experience (the link)

One of Cord's biggest advantages is that you don't send a dead file (PDF) attached to an email. You send a **link** to a dynamic web experience with your brand (your colors and logo) where the client can interact, approve, and pay.

Before sending, open the draft's details and click **Preview** to review the link exactly as your client will see it. While you're signed in, Cord shows you a preview notice and your visit isn't counted as a client view. Check:
- A clean and responsive design (mobile-optimized).
- The breakdown of what the client will pay (if you requested a deposit, the deposit amount and the balance).
- The **Approve quote** button.

## Pay from the link

Once the client approves, the quote turns into an active checkout point, as long as your account has an active online payment rail.

The client can pay the total (or the agreed deposit) with:

1. **Credit / Debit Card:** processed via Cord Payments, inside the same link (see [Payment methods](/en/docs/pagos/metodos)).
2. **SPEI bank transfer (Mexico, MXN only):** the system generates a dedicated interbank CLABE for that charge. When the client makes the transfer from their banking app, Cord detects the deposit, marks the charge as paid, and alerts you according to your [notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones). On quotes in another currency, SPEI isn't offered.
3. **Mercado Pago:** in Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru, if you connected your Mercado Pago account, the client can pay through that rail; the payment is completed in Mercado Pago's checkout (see [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago)). Where you also have Cord Payments, Cord Payments is the primary rail and Mercado Pago is the alternative.

<Callout type="info">
**Money straight to your account:** with direct charges to your connected bank account, card or SPEI funds never pass through a Cord account. Today Cord charges a platform fee only on payments in MXN, calculated transparently and shown before you charge; in other currencies Cord charges no fee of its own (see [Fees and terms](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones)).
</Callout>

## Sending methods

**Send to client** (on the draft's details) or **Create and send** / **Save and send** (in the editor) change the quote's status to *Sent*, generate the public link and, if the client has an email on file, automatically send them an email with the link and record the send in the activity. If the client has no email, the quote is still marked as sent and you share the link yourself.

Once it's sent, the details' actions panel gives you more ways to deliver it:

1. **Copy link:** paste it into Slack, your personal email, or wherever you talk to your client.
2. **WhatsApp:** opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message that includes the link.
3. **PDF:** download or print the PDF version of the quote.
4. **Open link:** opens the public view so you can review it.

<Callout type="info">
**Limits and customization:** the Free plan includes 5 quote sends per month. From the Starter plan you can customize the sender name, reply-to address, introduction, and signature of the email in **Settings › Notifications › Email**. If your organization has internal approval rules (Scale plan) and a new quote exceeds them when you use **Create and send**, it stays as a draft waiting for a manager to approve it.
</Callout>
