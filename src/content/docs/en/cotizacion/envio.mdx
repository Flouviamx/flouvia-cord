## What it is

Sending a quote activates its **public link**: a page with your brand where the client
reviews, comments, signs, and pays. It isn't an attached PDF; it's the same document
your team keeps seeing in Cord, and any new version arrives at the same link.

## Requirements

- **Permission:** Quotes.
- **Plan:** all. Free has a cap of 5 sends per month.
- **Client email:** optional. Without an email, the quote is still marked as sent and you share the link.
- **Online payment:** optional. For the client to pay from the link you need an active payment rail (see [Payment methods](/en/docs/pagos/metodos)).

## Review before sending

<Steps>
1. Open the draft from **Quotes** and, in the actions panel, click **Preview**.
2. The link opens in a new tab with the notice "Preview — this is what your client sees. Your visit is not counted as a view." While you're signed in to the organization that owns the quote, your visit doesn't mark it as *Viewed* or trigger notifications.
3. Check the total, the lines, the struck-through prices where you negotiated, the payment plan if you asked for a deposit, and the notes. What the client sees is explained in [What your client sees](/en/docs/interaccion/vista-del-cliente).
</Steps>

## Send the quote

There are three paths and all three do the same thing: they change the status to
*Sent*, activate the link and, if the client has an email, send them an email with the
link and log the email in the activity.

| From | Button |
|---|---|
| The editor of a new quote | **Create and send** |
| The editor of a draft | **Save and send** |
| The detail of a draft | **Send to client** |

You can also drag the card from *Draft* to *Sent* on the board (see [Home and
views](/en/docs/gestion/dashboard)); it performs exactly the same send.

<Callout type="info">
**Internal approval.** If your organization uses [internal
approvals](/en/docs/cotizacion/aprobaciones-internas) and a new quote exceeds your caps
on **Create and send**, it stays a draft **Pending approval** until a manager approves it.
</Callout>

## Share the link

Once sent, the detail's actions panel gives you more ways to deliver it:

- **Copy link:** to paste it into your email, Slack, or the chat where you talk with your client.
- **WhatsApp:** opens WhatsApp with a ready message: "Hi! Here's your quote COT-0149 for … You can review and approve it here:" followed by the link.
- **PDF:** opens the printable version to download or print it.
- **Open link:** opens the public view to review it.

The link doesn't change when you create a new version with **Modify and resend**: the
client keeps using the same one.

## Paying from the link

Once the client approves, the quote becomes a payment point, as long as your account
has an active online rail:

1. **Credit or debit card** with Cord Payments, inside the same link.
2. **SPEI transfer** (Mexico, MXN only): Cord generates a dedicated CLABE for that charge and marks the quote as paid when the deposit arrives. In other currencies, SPEI isn't offered.
3. **Mercado Pago** in Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru, if you connected your account. Where you also have Cord Payments, Cord Payments is the primary rail.

If you enabled bank transfer details, the link also shows them with the note "After
transferring, send your receipt to the seller to confirm payment." You record that
payment yourself with **Record payment**.

<Callout type="info">
**Money straight to your account.** Card and SPEI funds go to your connected account,
not to a Cord account. Today Cord charges a platform fee only on MXN charges; in other
currencies it charges no fee of its own (see [Fees and advantages](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones)).
</Callout>

## Behavior and edge cases

- **No client email:** the quote moves to *Sent* and no email goes out. Share the link.
- **Resending doesn't use sends.** **Modify and resend** creates a new version and sends the email again, but doesn't count against Free's monthly cap.
- **The email carries your brand.** From Starter you can customize the sender, reply-to address, introduction, and signature in **Settings › Notifications › Email**.
- **A test quote** is marked on the link with the ribbon "Test quote — no commercial or fiscal validity".

## Limits by plan

| | Free | Starter | Pro | Scale | Developer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sends per month | 5 | No cap | No cap | No cap | No cap |
| Custom email | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

When you hit Free's cap, **Send to client** tells you that you reached your plan's
monthly limit and suggests upgrading.

## Common problems

**The client didn't get the email.** Check that their profile has an email and look for the email entry in the activity. If it isn't there, share the link manually.

**I don't see the pay button on the link.** Payment shows after approval and only if you have an active rail. With a credit term (Net 7 to Net 90) it's enabled on the due date (see [Credit terms](/en/docs/pagos/credito)).

**My own visit marked the quote as viewed.** It shouldn't: open the link while signed in to the same organization. From another browser without a session, Cord can't tell you apart from the client.
