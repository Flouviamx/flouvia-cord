## Ways to build your Catalog

Before adding products to a quote, Cord allows you to nourish your base catalog in different ways:

1. **Manual Creation:** Add products one by one from **Products › + New product**, defining their SKU, name, description, unit, and List Price. If you also capture the cost, Cord calculates the list margin automatically and compares it against the margin you actually charge on each sale.
2. **Bulk Import via CSV:** With **Import CSV**, download the template, paste the inventory exported from your current system, and upload it as a `.csv` file (if it's in Excel, save it as CSV first). Cord proposes which column holds the name, SKU, unit, price, and status based on the headers, and you confirm the mapping before loading. Each import accepts up to **2,000 rows**; if a SKU already exists, that product is updated.
3. **Product Kits (Bundles):** If you usually sell the same things together (e.g., *Point of Sale Kit: Computer + Barcode Scanner + Thermal Printer*), you can create a **Kit** in Cord. When quoting, use **+ Insert kit** and the system will automatically add all its parts, saving you from searching for and adding the same items multiple times. A kit can carry a fixed combo price, different from the sum of its parts — Cord prorates that total across the lines when it's inserted.

### Volume pricing

From each product's detail page you can define price tiers by minimum quantity: each tier is a **fixed unit price** that applies from a given quantity on (e.g., *$90 per unit from 500 units; $75 from 2,000*). When quoting, Cord automatically applies the tier that matches the quantity you entered, so you never have to remember the table. If the client has a tier with a discount, that discount is calculated on top of the volume price.

## Adding line items to the quote

In the **What are you quoting them?** step of the quoter, you have these options:

1. **Search the Catalog:** Use the search bar (by name or SKU) to find the products or services you already configured in your catalog, and **+ Insert kit** for your kits.
2. **Free line:** If you need to quote something unique on the fly (e.g., *A special weekend installation service*), use **+ Free line**. You simply type any name you want, set the quantity, and assign whatever price you want right there, without having to go create a product in the catalog first.

For each item you add, you can customize:
- **Quantity:** Number of units.
- **Negotiated price:** The system shows your catalog's **List** price and, next to it, the **Negotiated** price, which you can edit freely (e.g., lower it from $500 to $450) for that specific quote without affecting the general catalog.
- **Tax:** if your tax catalog has more than one rate, each line shows its own tax selector (for example, one taxed item and one exempt item on the same quote).

Line items don't carry their own notes. For clarifications aimed at the client (scope, delivery times, special conditions), use the quote's **Notes for the client** field, which appears on the link and in the PDF.

### How discounts are applied

Business-to-business sales are all about negotiation. In Cord, a discount isn't a separate field: it's the difference between the list price and the negotiated price of each line, and it builds up like this:

1. **Client tier (automatic):** if the client has a discount configured on their price tier, Cord already lowered the negotiated price of catalog products by that percentage when adding them.
2. **Volume pricing (automatic):** if the quantity reaches a tier, that line's list price becomes the tier price.
3. **Per-line negotiated price (manual):** you can lower the negotiated price of any line by hand.

In the **Review and send** summary, the **You discounted them** line shows the total difference between list and negotiated prices across the quote. Cord has no global discount on the subtotal and no per-line discount percentage: to give a discount, adjust the lines' negotiated price.

<Callout type="warning">
Discounts are always calculated **before** taxes are applied — that's a rule of Cord's calculation engine, not an optional setting. The editor and the server use the same engine, so what the seller sees is what gets saved and invoiced.
</Callout>

## Real-Time Calculation

As you add products or change prices, Cord instantly updates the **Review and send** panel, to the right of the editor:
- **Subtotal:** Sum of all line items without taxes.
- **You discounted them:** Total difference between list and negotiated prices (only shown when there is a discount).
- **Taxes:** Each line item carries its own rate, taken from your country's tax catalog when you enter it — the name printed is your country's own (VAT in the UK, GST in Canada, Sales tax by state in the United States, IVA in Mexico and Spain), never a generic label.
- **Total:** What the client will ultimately pay.
