## What it is

The **What are you quoting them?** step of the editor is where you add lines. Each line
keeps its **List** price (from your catalog) and its **Negotiated** price (what the
client will actually pay). The discount isn't a separate field: it's the difference
between the two.

<DmCotLines lang="en" />

## Requirements

- **Permission:** Quotes to quote; **Products** to create, edit, or import the catalog.
- **Plan:** all. The price suggested by history is available from Starter. Free allows up to 50 products and Starter up to 500; from Pro there's no cap.
- **Per-quote limit:** 200 lines.

## Build your catalog

1. **Manually:** in **Products › + New product** you set SKU, name, description, unit, and list price. If you capture the **cost**, Cord calculates the margin.
2. **CSV import:** with **Import CSV**, Cord proposes which column is name, SKU, unit, price, and status; you confirm the mapping. Up to 2,000 rows per file; if a SKU already exists, it's updated.
3. **Kits:** group products you sell together. A kit can have a fixed **combo price**, different from the sum of its parts.

See [Import your catalog](/en/docs/cuenta/catalogo) for the import details.

### Volume pricing

On each product's profile you can define tiers: a fixed unit price from a minimum
quantity (for example, 90 per piece from 500 units). When quoting, Cord applies the
tier that matches the quantity entered.

## Add lines

<Steps>
1. **Search the catalog** by name or SKU in the section's search box. Press Enter to add the result and keep typing to add several in a row.
2. **Insert a kit** with **+ Insert kit**: choose the kit, type how many times to insert it, and click **Add**. Cord adds all its parts multiplying the quantities; if the kit has a combo price, it spreads it across its lines and tells you how much the client saves.
3. **Add a free line** with **+ Free line** for something that isn't in your catalog: you type the description, quantity, and price.
4. **Adjust each line:** quantity, **Negotiated** price and, if your catalog has more than one rate, the line's tax.
</Steps>

Lines don't carry their own notes. For clarifications to the client use **Notes for the
client** (see [Advanced options](/en/docs/cotizacion/opciones)).

## How the discount is built

1. **Client tier (automatic):** if the client has a discount on their price tier, Cord lowers the negotiated price of catalog products by that percentage and notes that the client's tier discount was applied to list prices.
2. **Shopify B2B price list (automatic):** if you connected [Shopify](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/shopify) and the client buys through a company with a B2B price list, that list wins over the tier discount, product by product, and only when the quote is in the list's currency. If you already changed the negotiated price by hand, Cord doesn't overwrite it.
3. **Volume price (automatic):** if the quantity reaches a tier, the line's list price becomes the tier's.
4. **Manual negotiated price:** you can lower the negotiated price of any line.

In **Review and send**, **You discounted them** adds up the difference between list and
negotiated across the whole quote. You can also apply a **document discount** to the
whole sale (percentage, amount, or coupon), which Cord spreads across the lines before
tax: see [Discounts and coupons](/en/docs/cotizacion/descuentos).

<Callout type="warning">
Discounts are always calculated **before** taxes. The editor and the server use the
same calculation engine: what you see is what's saved, charged, and invoiced.
</Callout>

## Price suggested by your history

From Starter, when you add a catalog product Cord looks at your already decided quotes
(approved, paid, invoiced, rejected, or expired) and suggests a price. It groups history
into discount bands of 5 points and picks the lowest band that closes at least 60% of
the time; if none does, it picks the band with the best close rate.

- Cord looks first at the history of **that product**, then at **that client's**, and finally at **your whole organization's**, and uses the first with at least 3 decided lines.
- The suggestion appears under the name as "Suggested: … (68% close)". Click it to apply it to the negotiated price.
- If the negotiated price already matches the suggestion, or there isn't enough history, nothing appears.

## Margin

The **Margin** column shows your profit on the negotiated price after cost:
(negotiated − cost) ÷ negotiated. Lines without a cost show a dash. If your organization
has a minimum margin in [internal approvals](/en/docs/cotizacion/aprobaciones-internas),
lines below it turn red.

The margin column is internal: it doesn't appear on the link or in the PDF.

## Shopify stock

If your products come from a connected Shopify store, the editor shows "N in stock",
"Only N in stock" if the quantity exceeds what's available, or "Out of stock" next to
each one. It's a warning: Cord doesn't block the quote.

## Real-time calculation

The **Review and send** panel updates instantly:

- **Subtotal:** the sum of the lines before taxes.
- **You discounted them:** total difference between list and negotiated (only if there's a discount).
- **Discount:** the document discount or the coupon, if you applied one, with its label (for example, "Discount (WELCOME10)").
- **Taxes:** one row per rate, with your country's name for it (IVA in Mexico and Spain, VAT in the United Kingdom, GST in Canada, Sales tax by state in the United States).
- **Withholding:** if your catalog has any, it's subtracted.
- **Total:** what the client will pay.

## Common problems

**"Suggested" doesn't appear.** You're on Free, the product doesn't have enough history, or the negotiated price is already the suggested one.

**The margin shows a dash.** The product has no cost captured, or it's a free line.

**I don't see the per-line tax column.** Your catalog has a single rate; every line uses it.

**The Shopify list wasn't applied.** The quote is in a different currency from the list's, or you already edited that line's negotiated price.
