## Digital Approval

When the client reviews the link and clicks **Approve quote** (see [Approval and technical evidence](/en/docs/interaccion/firma)), several things happen:
1. The status changes from *Sent* or *Viewed* to *Approved*.
2. The quote is commercially frozen: it can no longer be modified or resent.
3. If you configured a deposit or cash payment and your account collects online, **payment on the link is enabled** automatically.

If the client approved through another channel, you can use **Mark approved** from the quote's details.

## Invoicing: a different document depending on your country

Cord doesn't issue the same kind of receipt everywhere — your business's country and your plan decide the rail. The button appears in the actions panel of an *Approved* or *Paid* quote:

- **Mexico:** on the Starter plan or higher, the button is **Stamp CFDI 4.0** and it stamps with the SAT through a certified electronic-invoicing provider. On the Free plan the button is **Issue pro forma**: a numbered pro forma that does not replace a tax invoice.
- **Spain:** on the Starter plan or higher, with your organization's certificate uploaded and the Verifactu integration enabled, Cord issues the invoice with its chained Verifactu record. Until those conditions are met, the button is **Issue pro forma**: a numbered pro forma that does not replace a tax invoice.
- **Other supported markets** (United States, Canada, Brazil, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru): the button is **Issue invoice** and it creates a commercial invoice with sequential numbering and taxes configured by the business, without automatic filing with the local tax authority.

See the full country-by-country detail in [Fiscal invoicing by country](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion).

### CFDI 4.0 stamping (Mexico)

1. Go to the approved (or paid) quote and click **Stamp CFDI 4.0**. Cord asks you to confirm before issuing.
2. Cord builds the CFDI with the data the client already has: the name entered under **Company**, their RFC, tax regime, postal code, and CFDI use. There's no extra step to fill them in: if the client has no RFC on file, the CFDI is issued to **"general public"** (público en general), so review their data on their profile before stamping (see [Client data](/en/docs/cotizacion/cliente)).
3. If the client approved only some line items, only the approved lines are invoiced.
4. When it's done, the receipt appears in the **Tax documents** section of the details, with its number, its UUID, and links to download the **PDF** and the **XML**, and the quote moves to *Invoiced*. Cord doesn't email the receipt to the client automatically from this step: download it and share it, or email it from **Invoices**.

<Callout type="info">
**Active stamping:** to stamp on behalf of your business you need the Starter plan or higher and your CSD uploaded (see [CSD](/en/docs/cuenta/csd)). If tax stamping isn't active on your account yet, the document is marked as **Simulated** or **Tax test mode**: it's recorded for audit purposes, but it is not a valid CFDI before the SAT.
</Callout>

## Collections and Treasury

For quotes with credit terms (Net 30/60) or those where the client only paid the advance, Cord has a **Collections** and **Treasury** module — the full picture lives in [Treasury and FX Lock](/en/docs/pagos/tesoreria-fx) and [Autonomous Collections (AI)](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia); here's a summary of how it connects to a closed quote.

### Late interest

Automatic application is suspended while the country-by-country legal review is
completed. Saved rates do not generate new charges during this period.

### Autonomous Collections Agent (AI)

Available from the **Scale** plan, Cord's AI Agent monitors your overdue portfolio and, depending on the mode you choose (Approval or Automatic), drafts personalized collection emails and can negotiate a monthly installment plan with the debtor. The full behavior lives in [Autonomous Collections (AI)](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia).

### Predictive Treasury

In `/app/informes`, Cord crosses the credit terms of your open quotes with each client's actual payment behavior to give you a more realistic estimate of the money that will actually come in over the next 90 days.
