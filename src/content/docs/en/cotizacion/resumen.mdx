A quote in Cord is a living document with its own link. Your team builds it in the
internal editor; your client reviews, negotiates, signs, and pays from the link without
creating an account. The quoting engine is the same in the 12 countries where Cord
operates (Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain, the United Kingdom,
Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru); what changes between countries
is the fiscal document that closes the cycle (see [Approval and
invoicing](/en/docs/cotizacion/timbrado)).

<DmCotLifecycle lang="en" />

## Requirements

- **Permission:** creating, editing, sending, and resending requires the **Quotes** permission. Marking a quote as approved or rejected, and deciding internal approvals, requires **Approvals**. The account owner has every permission.
- **Plan:** every plan can quote. Limits change by plan (see below).
- **Country:** any of the 12 markets. CFDI 4.0 stamping is exclusive to Mexico.

## Quote statuses

Cord uses eight statuses. They are the same in the app, the public link, the API, and
webhooks.

| Status | What it means | Who triggers it |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved, but the client doesn't have an active link yet. | The seller, with **Save draft**. A quote waiting for an [internal approval](/en/docs/cotizacion/aprobaciones-internas) also stays a draft. |
| **Sent** | The link is active and the client can review it. | The seller, with **Create and send**, **Send to client**, **Modify and resend**, or by dragging the card on the board. Also a manager with **Approve and send**. |
| **Viewed** | The client opened the link for the first time. | The client. Only a real open with the tab visible counts; your own preview doesn't (see [What your client sees](/en/docs/interaccion/vista-del-cliente)). |
| **Approved** | The client signed the full proposal or only some lines. | The client, with **Approve quote** and **Confirm and sign**. The seller can use **Mark approved** if approval came another way. |
| **Rejected** | The proposal closed without a sale. | The client, with **Reject** and **Confirm rejection**. The seller with **Mark rejected**. |
| **Expired** | The validity date passed without a decision. | Cord's daily process, which checks sent or viewed quotes whose validity date has passed. A [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/acciones) can also expire it earlier. |
| **Paid** | The charge is settled. | Cord, when it confirms an online payment; or the seller with **Record payment**. |
| **Invoiced** | The fiscal or commercial document was issued. | The seller, with **Stamp CFDI 4.0**, **Issue invoice**, or **Issue pro forma**. |

Valid transitions are strict. For example, only a *Sent* or *Viewed* quote can be
approved or rejected, and only an *Approved* or *Invoiced* quote can move to *Paid*. If
two people act on the same quote at the same time, the first one wins and the second
is asked to reload and try again.

<Callout type="info">
**Expired isn't final.** An *Expired* quote still allows **Modify and resend**, but
resending doesn't change its validity date, so the daily process expires it again. To
propose it again with a new date, use **Duplicate quote** (see [Quote
management](/en/docs/gestion/cotizaciones)).
</Callout>

## The workflow, step by step

<Steps>
1. **[Build with AI (optional)](/en/docs/cotizacion/ia).** Paste the client's order or upload a photo or PDF and Cord fills the lines from your catalog.
2. **[Choose the client](/en/docs/cotizacion/cliente).** In step 1, **Who are you quoting?**, you search for or create the client and choose their payment terms.
3. **[Add products and negotiate](/en/docs/cotizacion/productos).** In step 2, **What are you quoting?**, you add products, kits, or free lines and adjust each line's **Price** without touching your list price.
4. **[Set the terms](/en/docs/cotizacion/opciones).** In step 3, **Terms**, you choose validity, deposit, currency, and monthly retainer.
5. **[Review and send](/en/docs/cotizacion/envio).** In the **Review and send** summary you confirm the total, open the **Preview**, and send the link by email, WhatsApp, or wherever you talk to your client.
6. **[Negotiate on the link](/en/docs/interaccion/negociacion).** The client asks, comments on lines, or proposes a counteroffer; you reply with a new version.
7. **[The client signs](/en/docs/interaccion/firma).** Cord keeps the technical evidence of the approval.
8. **[Collect and invoice](/en/docs/cotizacion/timbrado).** Payment is enabled on the link and you issue the document that applies to your country.
</Steps>

## Limits by plan

| Limit | Free | Starter | Pro | Scale | Developer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active quotes (draft, sent, viewed, or approved) | 5 | 50 | No cap | No cap | No cap |
| Quote sends per month | 5 | No cap | No cap | No cap | No cap |
| AI builds included per month | 3 | 20 | 50 | 500 | No cap |
| Lines per quote | 200 | 200 | 200 | 200 | 200 |
| Live presence and client attention | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Internal approvals | No | No | No | Yes | Yes |

Paid, invoiced, rejected, and expired quotes don't count as active. On Free, the AI cap
is hard; from Starter up, AI overage is billed. When you hit the active-quote cap,
Cord tells you how many active quotes your plan allows and asks you to free up space or
upgrade. Plan details live in [Your Cord subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).

## Common problems

**I don't see "New quote".** Your role doesn't have the Quotes permission. Ask whoever manages the team.

**I can't create more quotes.** You reached your plan's active-quote cap. Close the ones that won't move forward (reject them or delete drafts) or change plans.

**The client says they can't approve.** Check the status: if it's *Expired*, *Rejected*, or already *Approved*, the link no longer accepts a signature.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Cord work for businesses outside Mexico?**
Yes. A quote can use any currency Cord offers (MXN, USD, CAD, BRL, EUR, GBP, COP, ARS, CLP, and PEN, plus JPY, CNY, CHF, and AUD for international trade). Online payment is available with Cord Payments in Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain, the United Kingdom, Germany, and France, and with Mercado Pago in Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru. Without a connected rail you can quote, invoice, and record payments you receive outside Cord.

**Does CFDI 4.0 stamping work in any country?**
No: stamping with the SAT is exclusive to Mexico. In Spain, Cord issues the invoice with a Verifactu record only when the company certificate and the integration are active; until then it issues a pro forma. In the other markets it issues a commercial invoice. See [Approval and invoicing](/en/docs/cotizacion/timbrado).

**Do I need technical knowledge?**
No. Create your account, upload your catalog, and send your first quote. The [API](/en/docs/desarrolladores/funciones/cotizaciones) is optional.
