<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Internal approvals</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Protect your margins: a quote that exceeds your caps doesn't reach the client until someone with authority approves it.</p>
</header>

## What it is

Internal approvals are a rule for your organization. You define how far a seller can
go without asking (maximum discount, maximum amount, and minimum margin). When a new
quote exceeds any of those caps on **Create and send**, Cord doesn't send it: it keeps
it as a draft marked **Pending approval** and notifies whoever can decide.

This is not the client's approval. That's the [signature on the
link](/en/docs/interaccion/firma); this is an internal control for your team.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Scale or Developer. On other plans the caps can't be saved with a value above 0 and Cord doesn't evaluate them.
- **Permission to configure:** **Settings**.
- **Permission to decide:** **Approvals**. The account owner can always decide.
- **Country:** available in all 12 markets.

## Set up the caps

<Steps>
1. Go to **Settings › Quotes › Approvals**.
2. Under **Approval thresholds**, enter the caps you want to use. Each one is independent; a value of **0** means "no cap".
3. Save your changes. They apply to quotes created from then on.
</Steps>

{/* MOCKUP: Settings › Quotes › Approvals with the "Approval thresholds" section: Max discount without approval (%) = 15, Max amount without approval = 250,000, Minimum gross margin without approval (%) = 20, and below it the Treasury section with late interest disabled. */}

| Cap | What Cord compares | When it asks for approval |
|---|---|---|
| **Max discount without approval (%)** | The largest discount on any line: the difference between its list price and its negotiated price. | If any line has a discount above the cap. |
| **Max amount without approval** | The quote total. | If the total is above the cap. |
| **Minimum gross margin without approval (%)** | The margin of each line with a captured cost: (negotiated price − cost) ÷ negotiated price. | If any line with a cost falls below the minimum. Lines without a cost don't count. |

<Callout type="warning">
**The amount is compared without converting currencies.** Cord compares the cap number
against the quote total in the currency you're quoting in. If your cap is 250,000 and
you quote in USD, the rule triggers from USD 250,000, not its equivalent in your
accounting currency. If you quote in several currencies, set the cap with the
highest-value currency in mind.
</Callout>

## What happens on send

When the seller clicks **Create and send** and the quote exceeds at least one cap:

1. The quote is saved as a **Draft** with the internal status **Pending approval**. The client gets no email and the link isn't activated.
2. Cord writes the reason: which cap was exceeded and by how much (for example, a 22% discount against an allowed 15%, or a USD 310,000 total against a USD 250,000 cap). The reason shows on the quote detail and in its activity.
3. The quote appears under **Quotes** with the **Needs approval** filter, and above the list you see how many are **awaiting your approval**.
4. Cord emits the approval-request event, which feeds Slack, Teams, webhooks, and [workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/disparadores).

With **Save draft**, Cord doesn't evaluate the caps: the rule is checked when the quote
is created with **Create and send**.

## Who approves and how

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="From Cord">

The quote detail shows the **Pending approval** notice with the reason. Anyone with
the Approvals permission sees two buttons:

- **Approve and send:** the quote moves to *Sent*, the link becomes active, and the activity logs that management approved it and sent it to the client.
- **Reject:** Cord asks you to confirm ("Reject this approval request?"). The quote stays a draft and the detail shows **Approval request rejected by management**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="From Slack">

If you connected Cord's Slack app, the request arrives in your channel with the
number, client, total, reason, and **Approve** and **Reject** buttons.

For the button to decide, the email of your Slack account must match an active Cord
member with the Approvals permission. If it doesn't, Slack replies that your account
doesn't match a member with that permission and that you should decide from Cord. Once
decided, the message updates with who approved or rejected.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="From Teams">

If you connected Microsoft Teams, the request arrives in the channel as a card with
the number, client, total, and reason, and a **Decide in Cord** button that opens the
detail. Teams doesn't allow deciding from the card: the decision is made in Cord.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="With a workflow">

The **Approve the internal request** action in
[Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/acciones) approves the pending request and
sends the quote. Use it with care: a workflow that approves without conditions defeats
the purpose of the cap.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Behavior and edge cases

- **Approve and send doesn't email the client.** It activates the link and marks the quote as sent, but the email with the link doesn't go out at this step. Share it from the detail with **Copy link**, **WhatsApp**, or your own email.
- **A draft sent later isn't re-evaluated.** If the quote was saved as a draft and later sent with **Send to client**, from the board, or through the actions API, Cord doesn't check the caps again. If you need everything to go through the rule, ask your team to use **Create and send**.
- **A request is decided only once.** If two managers decide at the same time, the second is told there's no pending approval request.
- **The seller sees the margin before sending.** In the editor, the **Margin** column turns red when a line falls below your minimum margin.
- **If your plan drops below Scale,** Cord stops evaluating the caps on new quotes, and requests left pending can only be decided once the plan is restored.

## Common problems

**I can't save a cap above 0.** Your plan doesn't include internal approvals.

**The margin cap doesn't trigger.** The lines have no cost. Capture the cost on each product's profile; free lines have no cost.

**I approved and the client got nothing.** That's expected: share the link from the detail.

**The Slack button says I don't have permission.** Your Slack email doesn't match your Cord user, or your role doesn't have Approvals.
