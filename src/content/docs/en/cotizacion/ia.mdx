Cord AI is the AI engine built into Cord that generates a quote draft from text, a PDF file, or an image. It extracts products and quantities and matches them against your real Cord catalog, instead of you keying in every line item by hand.

*Last updated: August 2026*

## The end of manual data entry

Many sales start chaotically: a client sends you a massive WhatsApp message asking for 15 products, or forwards a scanned PDF with a bill of materials, or even sends a photo of handwritten notes.

Traditionally, you'd have to open that file and manually type product by product into the system, searching for prices one by one. **Cord AI removes that data entry: it reads the file and hands you back a draft already built from your catalog.**

## How does Cord AI work?

On your Dashboard's main screen, next to the "New quote" button, you'll find the **Generate with AI** button.

Clicking it opens an assistant where you can provide input in three different ways:

### 1. Paste text (messages or emails)
If a client sends an email asking: *"Hi, I need 5 annual software licenses, 2 high-end laptops, and 10 hours of training"*, simply copy and paste that text into Cord AI.
- The AI reads the text and searches **your Cord catalog** for matches (licenses, laptops, training).
- It extracts the requested quantities and applies the base prices from your catalog automatically.

### 2. Upload a PDF file
If you take part in tenders or a client sends a scanned purchase order or requisition:
- Upload the PDF file (it can have multiple pages).
- Cord AI reads it with the same vision model it uses for a photo — no separate OCR engine —, maps the concepts to your SKUs, and pre-fills the line items.

### 3. Images (AI vision)
Did they send you a photo of physical inventory or notes from a napkin after a business lunch?
- Upload the photo directly (JPG, PNG, WEBP, or GIF).
- The same model reads the image and builds the draft proposal for you.

## Human review

Cord AI is designed to be your copilot, not your replacement.

Once it processes your input, it doesn't send anything to the client automatically. Instead, it generates a **quote draft** already structured from your catalog. You get the chance to:

- Review that the selected products are correct.
- Adjust quantities if there was any interpretation error.
- Apply discounts and set the validity period before sending the link.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Cord AI replace the sales rep?**
No. Cord AI generates a draft that always requires human review before sending. The sales rep keeps full control over prices, discounts, and commercial terms.

**What file types can Cord AI process?**
Plain text (copied from emails or messages), a PDF file, and photos in JPG, PNG, WEBP, or GIF format. Spreadsheets aren't processed by this assistant.

**Does Cord AI work in any language?**
Yes. The model can interpret requests in Spanish, English, and other languages, mapping them to your catalog's products regardless of the input language.

**How accurate is Cord AI?**
It depends on how well-structured your catalog is: with clear names and defined SKUs, the AI matches products with more confidence than against a messy catalog or ambiguous names. The draft always goes through your review before sending, precisely because no automatic match is infallible.
