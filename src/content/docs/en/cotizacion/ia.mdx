## What it is

Cord AI is the artificial intelligence engine in the quote editor. It reads an order as
text, a PDF, or an image, extracts products and quantities, and matches them against
**your real catalog**. It doesn't save or send anything on its own: it fills the
editor and you decide.

<DmCotAiBlock lang="en" />

## Requirements

- **Permission:** Quotes, to open **New quote**.
- **Plan:** all, with a monthly allowance per plan (see below).
- **Catalog:** Cord AI matches against your products. Without a catalog, everything comes in as free lines.

## How to use it

<Steps>
1. Open **Quotes › New quote**. The first block is **Build the quote with AI**. You can also get there from the command palette with **Build with AI**.
2. Paste the order into the text box, attach a file with the clip, or both.
3. Click **Build quote with AI**.
4. Review the lines that were added, pick the client, adjust prices and validity, and save or send.
</Steps>

## What it can read

| Input | Limit | What Cord AI does |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** (email, WhatsApp, notes) | 2,000 characters | Finds matches in your catalog, takes the list price and, if the client mentions a lower price, proposes it as negotiated. |
| **PDF** (purchase order, requisition, tender) | 3 MB, can have several pages | Reads it with the same vision model as a photo, with no separate OCR. |
| **Image** (JPG, PNG, or WEBP) | Cord compresses it before uploading | Reads the photo and builds the lines. |

Anything it can't find in your catalog comes in as a **free line** so you decide what
to do with it. The list price and product data always come from your catalog, not
from the text.

## Human review

Cord AI is a copilot. You:

- confirm the products are the right ones;
- adjust quantities if something was misread;
- choose the client, negotiated prices, and validity before saving or sending.

## Limits by plan

Each build that identifies products uses one unit of the monthly allowance. If it
identifies none, nothing is used.

| | Free | Starter | Pro | Scale | Developer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Builds included per month | 3 | 20 | 50 | 500 | No cap |
| Beyond the allowance | Stops | Overage billed | Overage billed | Overage billed | Not applicable |

See your usage in **Settings › Plans & subscription › Subscription**.

## Common problems

**Cord asks for the order.** You clicked the button with no text and no file: paste the client's order or attach a photo or PDF.

**"The PDF is over 3MB".** Scan it at a lower resolution or upload a photo instead.

**Unsupported format.** Upload a photo (JPG, PNG, or WEBP) or a PDF. Spreadsheets aren't processed by this assistant; to load many products use the [CSV import](/en/docs/cuenta/catalogo).

**It matched a product wrong.** Clear names and defined SKUs in your catalog improve matching. Fix the line before sending.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Cord AI replace the seller?**
No. It fills a draft that a person always reviews before it's sent.

**Does it work in any language?**
Yes. It understands orders in Spanish, English, and other languages and matches them against your catalog.
