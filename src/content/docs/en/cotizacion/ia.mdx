Cord AI is the AI engine built into Cord that fills in a quote's line items from text, a PDF file, or an image. It extracts products and quantities and matches them against your real Cord catalog, instead of you keying in every line item by hand.

*Last updated: October 2026*

## The end of manual data entry

Many sales start chaotically: a client sends you a massive WhatsApp message asking for 15 products, or forwards a scanned PDF with a bill of materials, or even sends a photo of handwritten notes.

Traditionally, you'd have to open that file and manually type product by product into the system, searching for prices one by one. **Cord AI removes that data entry: it reads the order and hands you the line items already built from your catalog.**

## How does Cord AI work?

Cord AI lives inside the **New quote** editor: the first block, **Build the quote with AI**, sits above the client step. You can also get there from the command palette with **Build with AI**.

Paste the order into the text box, attach a file with the paperclip, or both, and click **Build quote with AI**. The input can come in three ways:

### 1. Paste text (messages or emails)
If a client sends an email asking: *"Hi, I need 5 annual software licenses, 2 high-end laptops, and 10 hours of training"*, simply copy and paste that text. The text accepts up to **2,000 characters**.
- The AI reads the text and searches **your Cord catalog** for matches (licenses, laptops, training).
- It extracts the quantities and takes the list price from your catalog. If the client mentions a price lower than the list price, it's proposed as the negotiated price.
- Anything it can't find in your catalog comes in as a free line, so you can decide what to do with it.

### 2. Upload a PDF file
If you take part in tenders or a client sends a scanned purchase order or requisition:
- Upload the PDF file (it can have multiple pages, up to **3 MB**).
- Cord AI reads it with the same vision model it uses for a photo — no separate OCR engine —, maps the concepts to your products, and fills in the line items.

### 3. Images (AI vision)
Did they send you a photo of physical inventory or notes from a napkin after a business lunch?
- Upload the photo directly (JPG, PNG, or WEBP). Cord compresses it automatically before sending it, so a heavy phone photo is not a problem.
- The same model reads the image and builds the proposal's line items for you.

## Monthly allowance

Each AI build that identifies products uses one unit of your plan's monthly allowance: **3** on Free, **20** on Starter, **50** on Pro, and **500** on Scale; Developer has no cap. On Free the allowance is a hard limit; on paid plans overage is billed. If the AI doesn't identify any product, no allowance is used. You can see your usage in **Settings › Plans & subscription › Subscription**.

## Human review

Cord AI is designed to be your copilot, not your replacement.

Once it processes your order, it doesn't save or send anything automatically: it adds the line items to the editor of the quote you're building. You get the chance to:

- Review that the selected products are correct.
- Adjust quantities if there was any interpretation error.
- Pick the client, adjust negotiated prices, and set the validity period before saving or sending the link.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Cord AI replace the sales rep?**
No. Cord AI fills in a draft that always requires human review before sending. The sales rep keeps full control over prices, discounts, and commercial terms.

**What file types can Cord AI process?**
Plain text (copied from emails or messages, up to 2,000 characters), a PDF file up to 3 MB, and photos in JPG, PNG, or WEBP format. Spreadsheets aren't processed by this assistant.

**Does Cord AI work in any language?**
Yes. The model can interpret requests in Spanish, English, and other languages, mapping them to your catalog's products regardless of the input language.

**How accurate is Cord AI?**
It depends on how well-structured your catalog is: with clear names and defined SKUs, the AI matches products with more confidence than against a messy catalog or ambiguous names. The result always goes through your review before sending, precisely because no automatic match is infallible.
