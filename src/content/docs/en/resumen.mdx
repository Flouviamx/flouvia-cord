<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Get started</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">From proposal to payment. All in one link.</p>
</header>

Cord is the deal-closing platform that covers the sales cycle from proposal to payment.
You build a quote —by hand, from a kit or with AI— and your client receives it in a link
where they discuss it, counteroffer, approve with technical evidence and pay online. Then
Cord issues the invoice, tracks receivables, follows up on overdue balances and shows you
how your business is doing. All with your brand, in each sale's currency and with
per-person permissions.

Cord is available in 12 countries: Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain, the
United Kingdom, Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru. In the first eight
you collect online with Cord Payments; in Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru, with
[Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago), which also works in Mexico and Brazil. In
Mexico, Cord stamps CFDI 4.0 with your CSD; in the other countries it issues commercial
invoices. See what changes in each market in
[Invoicing by country](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion).

## First steps

This is the actual order in which Cord guides you. Each step links to its full guide.

<Steps>
1. **Sign up and verify your email.** With email and password, Cord sends you a
   verification link; with Google or Apple you go straight in.
   [Create your account](/en/docs/cuenta/crear-cuenta).
2. **Set up your workspace.** A four-step wizard asks for your company name, country,
   role, industry and what you will use Cord for. The country sets your currency, time
   zone and the taxes your account starts with.
3. **Set up with AI (optional).** Give Cord your website or price list and review the
   proposed brand, catalog and taxes. [Set up with AI](/en/docs/cuenta/configurar-con-ia).
4. **Prepare your business.** Your [brand](/en/docs/cuenta/marca), your tax details (in
   Mexico, your [CSD](/en/docs/cuenta/csd)) and the message of your PDF and
   [portal](/en/docs/cuenta/portal-cliente).
5. **Build your catalog.** Products, kits and clients, one by one or by CSV.
   [Import your catalog](/en/docs/cuenta/catalogo).
6. **Create and send your first quote.** [Create a quote](/en/docs/cotizacion/resumen)
   and [send it](/en/docs/cotizacion/envio).
7. **Turn on online payments.** Connect [Cord Payments](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding) or
   [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago), depending on your country.
8. **Collect and invoice.** [Accept a payment](/en/docs/pagos/aceptar) and issue the
   [invoice](/en/docs/pagos/facturas-emitidas).
9. **Automate your follow-up.** Publish your
   [first workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/primer-workflow).
10. **Invite your team.** From the Professional plan.
    [Team, permissions and security](/en/docs/cuenta/equipo-seguridad).
</Steps>
