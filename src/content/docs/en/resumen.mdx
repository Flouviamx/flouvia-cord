<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Get started</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Create an account and learn how to build on Cord.</p>
</header>

Cord is standalone software for quoting, payments, and collections — with CFDI 4.0 invoicing for businesses in Mexico and Verifactu for businesses in Spain. It lets sales teams build proposals with negotiated pricing, accept online card payments (or SPEI in Mexico), and automate collections with AI. Built by the Flouvia team, Cord operates in 12 countries — Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru; in the last four, the account can quote, invoice, and track collections, but online payments aren't available yet.

*Last updated: August 2026*
