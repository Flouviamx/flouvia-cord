  <p class="page-subtitle">Create an account and learn how to configure and use Cord.</p>
</header>

<Callout type="info">
This guide includes the September improvements. Their combined publication and integrated acceptance still need verification. See [scope and availability](/en/docs/pagos/mejoras-confiabilidad).
</Callout>

Cord is standalone software for quoting, payments, invoicing, and collections. It lets teams build proposals with negotiated pricing, record approvals with technical evidence, collect online payments where Cord Payments is available, and automate collections with AI. Built by the Flouvia team, Cord offers signup in 12 countries — Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru; in the last four, accounts can quote, issue commercial invoices, and track collections, but online payments aren't available yet. In Mexico, Cord can stamp CFDI 4.0; see the fiscal guide for Verifactu's operating status in Spain.
