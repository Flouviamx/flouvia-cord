<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">All products</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Everything Cord does from proposal to payment, with the minimum plan for each capability.</p>
</header>

Each card shows the **minimum plan** where the capability is available. Higher plans
include everything in the lower ones. When a feature depends on the country, the card
says so. Full caps and allowances are in
[Your Cord subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).
