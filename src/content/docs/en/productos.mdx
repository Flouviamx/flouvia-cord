<div class="product-area">
  <h2 class="product-area-title">Closing, Payments, and Audit</h2>
  <div class="product-grid">
    <a href="/en/docs/interaccion/firma" class="product-link">
      <h4>E-Signature and Audit Trail</h4>
      <p>The client enters their name and email; Cord stores the IP address, time, and fingerprint of the approved version as technical evidence.</p>
    </a>
    <a href="/en/docs/pagos/condiciones" class="product-link">
      <h4>Dynamic SPEI Payments (Mexico)</h4>
      <p>Cord Payments assigns an interbank CLABE to each charge for automatic reconciliation. Fee of 1% + MXN 7 + VAT per charge, with a cap; manual bank transfer has no fee.</p>
    </a>
    <a href="/en/docs/pagos/aceptar" class="product-link">
      <h4>Advances and Balances</h4>
      <p>Charge a deposit percentage today and the balance upon project completion via dedicated links.</p>
    </a>
    <a href="/en/docs/cotizacion/timbrado" class="product-link">
      <h4>CFDI 4.0 Invoicing</h4>
      <p>Generate the official SAT invoice (PUE payment method) with a single click after approval.</p>
    </a>
    <a href="/en/docs/cotizacion/timbrado" class="product-link">
      <h4>Verifactu (Spain)</h4>
      <p>When the company's certificate and Cord's regulatory rail are active, the invoice creates a chained record; submission to the Spanish tax authority is shown as a separate status.</p>
    </a>
