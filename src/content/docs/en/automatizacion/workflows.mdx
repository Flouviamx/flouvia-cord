## What a workflow is

A workflow listens to an event in your account — a quote sent, a deposit received, an overdue invoice, a dispute — and runs the steps you defined. You'll find it under **Workflows**, in the Intelligence group of the sidebar. Creating and publishing workflows requires the **Settings** permission.

## Start from an idea

When you create your first workflow, Cord opens a gallery of ready-made examples. Each one is created as a draft so you can review it before publishing:

- **Follow up if the client does not open:** three days after sending a quote, if it is still unopened, create a task to call.
- **Large deal alert:** when a quote of 100,000 or more is approved, email the owner.
- **Slack when a payment arrives:** post to your channel every time a quote is paid.
- **HubSpot note when the client approves:** add a note to the Deal and post to Slack.
- **Broken payment promise:** create a collections task for today and notify the team.
- **Kick off when the deposit arrives:** create the task to start the work and report the remaining balance.
- **Collect overdue invoices:** create a collection task and, if it is still open a week later, tell the owner.
- **Win back a rejected quote:** create a task to call the client tomorrow.
- **Alert when a quote needs approval:** email the whole team with the reason.

## Triggers

The trigger is the event that starts the workflow. They are grouped by category:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Quotes | Created, sent, opened by the client, approved, rejected, expired, a message arrives. |
| Approvals | A quote requests internal approval, or that approval is decided. |
| Payments | Paid in full, partial payment (deposit, balance, or installment), failed recurring charge, dispute opened or closed, refund succeeded or failed, payout paid or failed, a change in your payments account. |
| Invoices | Issued, sent, paid, payment failed, overdue, voided, or marked uncollectible. |
| Clients and products | A client or product is created, updated, or deleted. |
| Tasks and promises | A task is created or completed; a payment promise is recorded, kept, or broken. |

## Steps

Add steps with **Add a step**. There are three kinds:

- **Action:** create a task, email your team, send a Slack message, or add a note in HubSpot.
- **Condition:** splits the flow into two branches, **If the condition is met** and **If the condition isn't met**, based on the event data. For example, "the total is greater than or equal to 50,000" or "the payment type is Deposit". Fields marked as *current status* are read at evaluation time, so they work after a wait.
- **Wait:** pauses the flow for 1 to 30 days, then continues. Waits are checked once a day.

In any text you can insert event data with double braces, such as `{{cliente}}`, `{{folio}}`, `{{total}}`, or `{{monto}}`. The editor only offers the fields that trigger provides.

## Publish and pause

A workflow has a **draft** you can edit freely and a **published version** that actually runs. Click **Publish** to activate your changes; until then the workflow keeps running the previous version. You can pause and resume it from the **⋯** menu in the editor.

## Runs

The **Runs** tab records every time the workflow fired: when, which steps ran, and the result. A step that fails for a temporary reason is retried right away and, if it keeps failing, up to three attempts in total. If it does not recover, the run is marked with an error and the message tells you what happened.

## Limits

| Plan | Active workflows |
|---|---|
| Free | 1 |
| Starter | 5 |
| Pro, Scale, and Developer | Unlimited |

Drafts do not count toward the limit. Each workflow supports up to 30 steps and up to 4 levels of nested conditions.

<Callout type="info">
**What a workflow never does:** it never writes to your client, charges, issues invoices, or marks payments. Those stay human decisions. It also never triggers on its own events, so two workflows cannot loop.
</Callout>

## Actions that need a connection

- **Send a Slack message** requires Slack connected in Settings › Integrations.
- **Add a note in HubSpot** requires HubSpot connected. The note goes on the quote's Deal or, when there is no Deal, on the client's Company.

If the connection is missing, the run ends with an error telling you what to connect. See [Integrations](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones).
