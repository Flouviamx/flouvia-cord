## The app directory

Open **Settings › Integrations**. Each app shows as a card with its logo, category and status. **Connected** apps sit at the top and **Available** ones below, so the page stays short as more apps are added.

Tap a card to open that app's page. Everything about the integration lives there: the connection, its settings, a link to the guide and, where it applies, **Use it in Workflows** with workflow ideas that use that app and that you can create with one tap.

## What each status means

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Connected** | The integration is active and working. |
| **Needs reconnecting** | The connection exists but lost its authorization (for example, someone removed the app in HubSpot). Open the app and click **Reconnect**. |
| **Not connected** | Available and not set up yet. |
| **Direct connection** | Connected from the other tool and authorized with one click on a Cord screen, without keys. This is the case for Zapier and Make. |
| **With an API key** | Connected from the other tool by pasting a Cord API key. |
| **Coming soon** | On the way and not usable yet. |

## What fits each case

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| Your sales team to see Cord clients and sales in HubSpot | **HubSpot** |
| To hear in a channel when the client opens, approves or pays | **Slack** with the Settings › Notifications alerts |
| A Slack message with your own text, or only in certain cases | **Slack** with a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows) |
| To create tasks or email the team when something happens in Cord | [Cord Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows), no connection needed |
| To send Cord data to a spreadsheet, an ERP or any other app | **Make**, **n8n** (or Zapier) |
| To build your own integration | The [developer guide](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/integraciones) |

## Who can connect integrations

Connecting, configuring and disconnecting integrations requires the **Settings** permission. If someone without it tries to connect HubSpot, they see "You need Settings permission to connect integrations."

Integrations belong to the **organization**, not a person: if whoever connected HubSpot leaves the team, the connection keeps working. Every connect, configure, sync or disconnect action is recorded in **Settings › Audit log**.

## Security

- **Cord never asks for another tool's password.** HubSpot and Slack are authorized on their own page; Teams and n8n use URLs or keys you generate and can revoke, and Zapier and Make are authorized on a Cord screen that you can also revoke.
- **Access credentials are stored encrypted** and only used for what each integration describes.
- **Disconnecting really cuts access.** For HubSpot, Cord also revokes its authorization in your account.
- **Webhook URLs are secrets.** Anyone with your Slack or Make webhook URL can post to it; do not share them.

## If your tool is not listed

With Cord's webhooks and API you can connect today any system that receives or makes HTTP calls. If you want a direct integration with another app, write to contacto@cordhq.app.
