## The app directory

Open **Settings › Integrations**. Each app shows as a card with its logo, its category and its status. **Connected** apps are at the top and **Available** ones below, so the page does not grow as apps are added.

Tap a card to open that app's page. Everything about the integration lives there: the connection, its settings, a link to the guide and, where it applies, **Use it in Workflows** with workflow ideas that use that app and that you can create with one tap.

No integration requires a paid plan to connect. What does depend on the plan:

- **Zapier, Make and n8n** work with the Cord API, and every call counts against the [monthly API quota](#integrations-and-the-api-quota).
- Slack, Teams, WhatsApp and HubSpot actions inside a workflow count like any workflow: 1 active on Free, 5 on Starter and no limit from Pro. See [Workflow limits](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/limites).
- **Products and customers** that come in from Shopify count against your plan's limit, like the ones you create by hand.
- Slack's **approval buttons** and the Teams approval card only appear with internal approvals, which come with the **Scale** plan or higher.

## What each status means

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Connected** | The integration is active and working. |
| **Needs reconnecting** | The connection exists but lost its authorization (for example, someone removed the app in HubSpot). Open the app and click **Reconnect**. |
| **Not connected** | Available and not set up yet. |
| **Direct connection** | Connected from the other tool and authorized with one click on a Cord screen, without keys. This is the case for Zapier and Make. |
| **With an API key** | Connected from the other tool by pasting a Cord API key. This is the case for n8n. |
| **Coming soon** | On the way and not usable yet. |

## Integrations and the API quota

Zapier, Make and n8n work with the Cord API. Every action or search they run in Cord (create a client, find a quote, mark it paid) is at least one API call, and so is registering or removing their webhook when you turn a Zap, scenario or flow on or off. Receiving Cord events does not use calls.

| Plan | API calls included per month | When you go over |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 100 | Calls are rejected until the next month |
| **Starter** | 1,000 | Billed as overage |
| **Pro** | 5,000 | Billed as overage |
| **Scale** | 10,000 | Billed as overage |
| **Developer** | 50,000 | Billed as overage |

- Only calls in **live mode** count. With an `sk_test_…` key (n8n or your own requests) you work against your test environment without using quota.
- On plans with overage there is a safety cap of 10 times what is included: if a connection runs away, Cord stops it there and asks you to write to us.
- Besides the monthly quota, each key has a per-minute limit; if you exceed it, the call returns **429** until the minute is over.
- Webhooks created by your integrations have their own allowance of **100 per organization**, separate from your plan's webhook endpoints.

Your usage for the month is in **Settings › Plans & subscription › Subscription**, in the **API calls** row. Overage pricing and plans are in [Your Cord subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).

## Who can connect integrations

Connecting, configuring and disconnecting integrations requires the **Settings** permission. If someone without it tries to connect HubSpot, they see "You need Settings permission to connect integrations."

Integrations belong to the **organization**, not a person: if whoever connected HubSpot leaves the team, the connection keeps working. Every connect, configure, sync or disconnect action is recorded in **Settings › Audit log**.

## Security

- **Cord never asks for another tool's password.** HubSpot, Slack, Teams, Shopify, Google Sheets, Excel, Gmail, QuickBooks and Xero are authorized on the provider's own page. n8n uses a key you generate and can revoke, and Zapier and Make are authorized on a Cord screen that you can also revoke.
- **Each integration asks for the narrowest permission it needs.** Google Sheets can only open the spreadsheet Cord created, not the rest of your Drive; sending from Gmail can only send, not read your inbox; Xero gets access to invoices, contacts and payments, plus reading your accounting settings, but not bank transactions or manual journals.
- **Access credentials are stored encrypted** and only used for what each integration describes.
- **Disconnecting really cuts access.** For HubSpot, Cord also revokes its authorization in your account.
- **Webhook URLs are secrets.** Anyone with the URL of your Slack webhook, your Teams flow or Make can post to it; do not share them.

## If your tool is not listed

With Cord's webhooks and API you can connect today any system that receives or makes HTTP calls, or use Zapier, Make or n8n as a bridge. If you want a direct integration with another app, write to contacto@cordhq.app.
