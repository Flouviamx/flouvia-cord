## The apps directory

Open **Settings › Integrations**. Each app appears as a card with its status: **Connected**, **Needs reconnecting**, **Not connected**, **Via webhooks**, or **Coming soon**. The ones you already connected come first. Opening an app shows its connection, its settings, and the workflows that use it.

## HubSpot

Keeps your clients and quotes up to date in HubSpot without entering them twice.

**What syncs:**
- **Clients ↔ Companies and Contacts**, both ways. HubSpot sends back to Cord the name, contact, email, and phone of clients that are already linked.
- **Quotes → Deals.** Every sent quote creates its Deal and moves it to the stage you choose for each status: sent, viewed, approved, rejected, expired, paid, and invoiced.
- **Moving a Deal in HubSpot changes nothing in Cord.** Cord is the source of the sale's status.

**Connect:**
1. In **Settings › Integrations › HubSpot**, click **Connect HubSpot**.
2. Authorize Cord in your HubSpot account. A HubSpot account can only be connected to one Cord organization.
3. Choose the HubSpot **pipeline** and the **stage** for each quote status, and save.
4. If you already had clients and quotes, use **Send existing data** to send them too (up to 500 of each). New changes are sent automatically.

The page shows the last sync, pending items, and failed ones. **Disconnect** stops syncing and removes Cord's access to your account; what is already in HubSpot is not deleted.

## Slack

Posts to a channel every time a quote is sent, opened, approved, or paid. Create an **Incoming Webhook** in Slack, paste the URL in **Settings › Integrations › Slack**, and use **Send test**. Which events trigger an alert is chosen in **Settings › Notifications**. Step by step in the [support guide](https://cordhq.app/en/support/conectar-slack).

## Make

Connect Cord with your scenarios using the modules Make already includes: **Webhooks** to receive Cord events and **HTTP** to create or update data through the API. The Make page in Settings takes you to Webhooks and the API, and the [support guide](https://cordhq.app/en/support/conectar-make) explains each module. Treat the Make webhook URL as a secret: Make does not verify Cord's signature.

## Zapier

The Cord app for Zapier is on its way. In the meantime you can connect Zapier with Cord's webhooks and API, just like Make.

## Workflows with integrations

The Slack and HubSpot actions are also available in [Cord Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows). For example: when the client approves, add a note to their HubSpot Deal and post to Slack.

## For developers

If you are building your own integration, the [developer integrations guide](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/integraciones) explains when to use the API, webhooks, or MCP.
