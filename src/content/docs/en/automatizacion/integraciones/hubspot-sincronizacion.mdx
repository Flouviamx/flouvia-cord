## At a glance

| In Cord | In HubSpot | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Client (company name) | Company | Cord → HubSpot, and the name comes back |
| Client (contact, email, phone) | Contact associated with the Company | Cord → HubSpot, and changes come back |
| Sent quote | Deal associated with the Company and Contact | Cord → HubSpot only |
| Quote status | Deal stage | Cord → HubSpot only |

## Clients → Companies and Contacts

### The Company

| Field in Cord | HubSpot property |
|---|---|
| Company | Company name |

Every Cord client creates **its own Company** in HubSpot. Cord does not look for existing Companies by name: if you already had that company in HubSpot before connecting, you may end up with two. Review your Companies after using **Send existing data**.

### The Contact

| Field in Cord | HubSpot property |
|---|---|
| Contact (name) | First name and Last name. The first word goes to First name and the rest to Last name. |
| Email | Email |
| Phone | Phone number |

- The Contact is only created if the client has an **email or contact name**. A client with neither stays as a Company only.
- **No duplicate contacts:** before creating a Contact, Cord looks in HubSpot for one with the same email. If it exists, Cord uses it and updates its data instead of creating another.
- The Contact is **associated with the client's Company**.
- If two Cord clients share the same email, only the first is linked to that Contact, and the panel shows a warning to fix the duplicate email in Cord.

### When they are sent

A client is sent to HubSpot when **created** and every time it is **updated** in Cord. It is also sent before its first quote, so the Deal is created already associated. If a change does not touch any field that goes to HubSpot (for example, you changed payment terms), Cord makes no call.

## Quotes → Deals

| Data in Cord | Deal property |
|---|---|
| Number and client | Deal name, like "COT-128 · Northwind" |
| Total | Amount |
| Sale currency | Currency |
| Chosen pipeline | Pipeline |
| Quote status | Deal stage, per your [stage settings](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/hubspot-etapas) |
| Actual close date | Close date, only when reaching Rejected, Expired, Paid or Invoiced ([details](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/hubspot-etapas)) |

- **Drafts are not sent.** The Deal is created when the quote is sent to the client.
- On creation, the Deal is **associated with the client's Company and Contact**.
- Every status change **moves the Deal** to the matching stage: Sent, Viewed by client, Approved, Rejected, Expired, Paid and Invoiced.
- If the total changes (for example, you edit and resend), the Deal amount is updated.
- **A status without a stage is not sent.** If you left a status without a stage, quotes in that status do not reach HubSpot until you assign one.

## From HubSpot to Cord

When someone corrects data in HubSpot, Cord receives it right away and applies it to the linked client:

| If in HubSpot you change | In Cord it changes |
|---|---|
| Company name of the Company | Company |
| First name or Last name of the Contact | Contact |
| Email of the Contact | Email |
| Phone number of the Contact | Phone |

Rules:

- **Linked clients only.** Creating a new Company or Contact in HubSpot does **not** create a client in Cord.
- **An empty field does not erase anything.** If you clear the phone in HubSpot, Cord keeps the one it had.
- **An invalid email is ignored.**
- Nothing else comes back: credit, payment terms, tax data and address are edited only in Cord.
- The change is recorded in **Settings › Audit log** as coming from HubSpot with the fields that changed, and fires the **A client is updated** event for your workflows and webhooks.

**Deals do not come back.** Moving a Deal's stage, changing its amount or deleting it in HubSpot changes nothing in Cord. The sale status is decided by the client's approval and payment in Cord.

## No echoes

When a change arrives from HubSpot, Cord applies it but **does not send it back** to HubSpot. And when Cord sends something to HubSpot, it recognizes the notification HubSpot sends back and does not process it as a new change. No data bounces between the two tools.

## Deletions

| If you delete… | Then |
|---|---|
| A client in Cord | Cord forgets its HubSpot link. The Company and Contact **stay in HubSpot**. |
| A draft quote in Cord | Nothing; drafts are not sent. |
| A Company or Contact in HubSpot | Cord forgets the link. The client stays in Cord; if you edit it again in Cord, it is created again in HubSpot. |
| A Deal in HubSpot | Cord detects it on the next change to that quote and creates a new Deal. |

Cord never deletes anything in HubSpot.

## When it happens

- **Right away.** Every change in Cord is sent as soon as it happens, usually within seconds.
- **With retries.** If HubSpot does not respond or asks to slow down, Cord retries automatically, up to 5 times per send, with more spacing each time.
- **A daily check** picks up any pending send and clears send history older than 14 days.

The panel shows how many sends are **Queued** and how many failed in **Errors (7 days)**.

## Existing data

Connecting HubSpot does not send anything from the past on its own. To send what already existed:

<Steps>
1. In **Settings › Integrations › HubSpot**, find **Existing data**.
2. Click **Send existing data**.
3. You see "N records queued for HubSpot". It runs in the background; you can leave the page.
</Steps>

What is sent:

- Up to **500 clients**, the most recent.
- Up to **500 quotes** that are not drafts, the most recent.
- Anything already linked and unchanged is not created or updated again, so you can click the button more than once without duplicating.

## What does not sync

- Products and catalog.
- Quote line items.
- Cord Invoicing invoices, payments, CFDI and documents.
- Tasks, payment promises and chat conversations.
- Notes, except those created by a workflow with the [Add a note in HubSpot](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/acciones) action.
- HubSpot owners: Deals and Companies are created without an assigned owner.
