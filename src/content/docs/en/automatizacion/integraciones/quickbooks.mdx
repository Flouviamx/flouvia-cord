## How it works

Every invoice you issue in Cord shows up in your accounting with its customer, lines and currency. You stop entering the same thing twice.

**QuickBooks has no drafts**: what goes in is recorded in your books. That is why Cord only sends invoices that are already **final** on its side.

## Connect

<Steps>
1. Go to **Settings › Integrations › QuickBooks Online**. You need the **Settings** permission.
2. Click **Connect QuickBooks** and authorize with the account that manages your company.
3. When you come back you see the connected company's name. From then on, each new invoice travels on its own.
4. If you already had invoices, click **Send pending invoices** to send the latest 50 missing ones.
</Steps>

## What it creates in your accounting

| Record | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | If it does not exist. Cord looks it up by email first, then by name, so it does not duplicate it, and remembers the link for later invoices. |
| **Invoice** | Date, due date, currency and one line per item with the negotiated price and its discount applied. |
| **"Cord" service** | Created once. QuickBooks requires every sales line to hang from a product; Cord uses one instead of filling your catalog. |
| **Private note** | Holds the Cord number. The invoice number belongs to your accounting and Cord does not overwrite it. |

## What it does not do yet

- **It does not send payments.** You record the payment in your accounting as usual.
- **It does not map your tax codes.** Check the tax on the first invoice before relying on it.
- **It brings nothing from your accounting into Cord.** It flows one way.

## Guarantees

- **An invoice is never sent twice.** Cord remembers which invoice was recorded and does not repeat it, even if the event is retried.
- **Only final invoices travel.** Never a quote or a Cord draft.
- **Each line amount is computed on the already-rounded price**, so the total matches Cord to the cent.

## Disconnect

With **Disconnect** on the card. What was already recorded stays in your accounting. If you connect a different company or organization, Cord starts from scratch with it instead of reusing the previous one.

## Common issues

| Message or symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| **The card asks you to reconnect** | QuickBooks expired or revoked access. Reconnect; missed invoices go out with **Send pending invoices**. |
| **"0 invoices sent"** | There are no pending final invoices, or they were all already recorded. |
| **The tax does not match** | QuickBooks applies its own tax codes; adjust it on the first invoice. |
