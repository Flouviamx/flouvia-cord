## How it works

Cord creates an Excel workbook in your OneDrive and writes your quotes and invoices into it: one row per document, updated on its own when the document changes status.

Data goes into a real **Excel table**, not a loose range: you get filters, banding, a header that stays visible while scrolling and formulas by column name, such as `=SUM(CordCotizaciones[total])`. The tables are named `CordCotizaciones` and `CordFacturas`.

**Cord works only with the workbook it creates.** When you authorize, Microsoft asks for permission to read and write your OneDrive files; Cord only opens and writes the workbook it created. Connecting Excel does not give Cord access to your Teams channels.

## Connect

<Steps>
1. Go to **Settings › Integrations › Microsoft Excel**. You need the **Settings** permission.
2. Click **Connect Excel** and authorize with your Microsoft work or school account.
3. When you come back, Cord creates the workbook in your OneDrive and starts filling it with what you already had.
4. Click **Open the sheet** to see it.
</Steps>

## What the sheet contains

Two tabs, **Quotes** and **Invoices**, with one row per document:

| Tab | Columns |
|---|---|
| **Quotes** | Number, client, status, date, valid until, currency, subtotal, discount, taxes, total, collected and public link. |
| **Invoices** | Number, client, status, tax status, date, due date, currency, total, paid, balance and public link. |

- **Amounts are numbers, not text.** You can sum, average and chart them without cleaning anything.
- **Currency has its own column.** If you sell in two currencies, the sheet says so instead of hiding it inside the number, so you never add pesos to dollars.
- **Dates use your account's time zone**, in `YYYY-MM-DD` format, which sorts correctly and both apps recognize as a date.
- **The header row arrives frozen and filtered**, with columns sized to their content.

## How it stays current

When a quote is sent, approved, rejected, expires or is paid —and when an invoice is issued, sent, paid, becomes overdue or is canceled— Cord looks for its **number in column A** and replaces that row. If it is not there, it adds it at the end.

That is why you can sort, filter and add your own columns to the right: Cord matches by number, not by row position. The only thing you should not touch is **column A**.

**Update now** rewrites your latest 500 documents in each tab. Use it right after connecting or whenever you are unsure.

## Disconnect

With **Disconnect** on the card. The file stays in your account with what it had; it simply stops updating. If you connect again, Cord creates a new file.

## Common issues

| Message or symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| **"The connection to your sheet stopped working"** | Access was revoked or the account password changed. Reconnect from the card. |
| **You deleted the file** | Disconnect and connect again; Cord creates and fills a new one. |
| **You deleted the first row** | No problem: Cord writes it again on the next update. |
| **"The workbook could not be opened"** right after connecting | OneDrive sometimes takes a few seconds to have a new file ready. Click **Update now** in a minute. |
| **A row got duplicated** | Check that nobody edited column A; Cord matches by number. |
