## How it works

Cord sends WhatsApp messages with **Meta's Cloud API** and **your** account: messages come from your number and Meta bills the conversation to your account, not Cord.

Meta does not allow starting a conversation with free text, only with an **approved template**. That is why Cord asks for your template name and fills its variables; there is no free-text field because Meta would reject it.

## Connect

<Steps>
1. At [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com), create a **WhatsApp Business** app and add your sending number.
2. In **WhatsApp Manager**, create a message template and submit it for approval. Example: `Hi {{1}}, your quote {{2}} expires on {{3}}. You can see it here: {{4}}`.
3. Copy the **phone number ID** (digits only) and generate a **permanent access token**.
4. In Cord, go to **Settings › Integrations › WhatsApp Business**, paste both, plus the template name and language (for example `en_US`).
5. Click **Save**, then **Send test** to your own number, with country code.
</Steps>

The token is stored encrypted and never shown again. To change the template, leave the token field empty.

## Use it in a workflow

The **Send the client a WhatsApp** action in [Cord Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows) sends your template to the phone of the document's client. Each step variable fills `{{1}}`, `{{2}}`… in order, and accepts event data: `{{cliente}}`, `{{folio}}`, `{{total}}`, `{{vence}}`.

The phone comes from the client, never from a step field, and must have its **country code** (`+1 512 555 0100`). Cord does not guess the country: the same number exists in several.

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Messages from workflows | 60 per hour per account |
| Tests from Settings | 5 per hour |
| Cost per conversation | Billed by Meta to your account, by country |

## Common issues

| Message or symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| **"The client has no phone with country code"** | Edit the client and save the number with `+` and its country code. |
| **"WhatsApp did not accept your template"** | Check it is approved, the name matches and the number of variables is the same. |
| **"WhatsApp did not accept the message"** | Usually the token expired or the sending number was disabled in Meta. |
