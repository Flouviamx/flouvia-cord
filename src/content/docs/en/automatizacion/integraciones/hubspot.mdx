  <p class="page-subtitle">Your Cord clients and quotes, up to date in HubSpot, without entering them twice.</p>

## What the integration does

- **Every Cord client** is created in HubSpot as a **Company** and, if it has an email or contact name, also as a **Contact** associated with that Company.
- **Every sent quote** is created as a **Deal** with its amount and currency, associated with the client's Company and Contact.
- **The Deal moves on its own**: Cord updates its stage when the client opens the quote, approves it, rejects it, when it expires, is paid or invoiced.
- **Contact corrections flow back to Cord**: if you fix the Company name, or the Contact's name, email or phone in HubSpot, they change in Cord too.

Field-by-field detail in [What syncs](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/hubspot-sincronizacion).

<Callout type="info">
**Cord owns the sale status.** Moving a Deal to another stage in HubSpot does not change the quote in Cord: its status is decided by the client's approval and payment.
</Callout>

## Before connecting

- **In Cord:** you need the **Settings** permission.
- **In HubSpot:** you need a user who can install apps in the account. If you do not have one, ask a HubSpot Super Admin to connect.
- **One account per organization:** a HubSpot account can only be connected to one Cord organization, and each Cord organization connects to a single HubSpot account.
- **Currencies:** if you quote in several currencies, enable them in your HubSpot account's currency settings. A Deal in a currency HubSpot has not enabled cannot be created.

## Connect step by step

<Steps>
1. **Open the app.** Go to **Settings › Integrations** and tap **HubSpot**.
2. **Click Connect HubSpot.** Cord takes you to HubSpot.
3. **Choose the HubSpot account**, if you have several, and **accept the permissions**. You have 10 minutes to complete this; if it takes longer, click Connect again.
4. **Return to Cord.** You see "HubSpot is connected." and the **Connected** status, with the account name.
5. **Review the stages.** In **HubSpot stage for each quote status**, confirm the pipeline and the stage for each status and click **Save stages**. If you use HubSpot's default pipeline, Cord already suggests a stage for each status. See [Pipeline and stages](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/hubspot-etapas).
6. **Send what you already had (optional).** In **Existing data**, click **Send existing data** to send your earlier clients and quotes. See [What syncs](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/hubspot-sincronizacion).
</Steps>

From then on, every new or updated client and every quote that moves forward is sent automatically.

<Callout type="warning">
If the button does not take you to HubSpot or you come back to an error, **click Connect HubSpot again** from Cord. Do not reuse an old authorization link saved in your browser: every attempt creates a one-time link.
</Callout>

## Permissions Cord requests

When authorizing, HubSpot shows the permissions Cord requests. Only what this integration needs:

| Permission | What for |
|---|---|
| Read and write **Companies** | Create each client's Company and read name corrections. |
| Read and write **Contacts** | Create each client's Contact, find existing ones by email and read corrections. |
| Read and write **Deals** | Create each quote's Deal, move its stage and read your pipelines. |

Cord does not read your emails, calls, tickets, lists, forms or any other HubSpot object.

## Connection messages

| Message | What happened | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| HubSpot is connected. | All good. | Review the stages. |
| You canceled the HubSpot connection. | You closed or declined the authorization in HubSpot. | Click **Connect HubSpot** again whenever you want. |
| The connection request expired or is not valid. Try again. | More than 10 minutes passed, an old link was used or it was opened from another session. | Click **Connect HubSpot** again from Cord. |
| That HubSpot account is already connected to another Cord organization. | The account is in use in another organization. | Disconnect it there or choose another account. |
| Couldn't connect HubSpot. Try again. | HubSpot did not complete the authorization. | Try again; if it persists, write to contacto@cordhq.app. |
| You need Settings permission to connect integrations. | Your role lacks the Settings permission. | Ask the account owner to connect or grant you the permission. |

## The connection panel

Once connected, the HubSpot page shows:

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **HubSpot account** | The number and domain of the connected account. |
| **Last sync** | When something was last sent or received. "Not yet" if nothing has happened. |
| **Queued** | Pending sends. |
| **Errors (7 days)** | Sends that failed in the last week. |
| **Last error** | The most recent error message, if any. |

What to do with each error: [HubSpot troubleshooting](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/hubspot-problemas).

## Switch to another HubSpot account

Disconnect the current account and connect the new one. When you connect a **different** account, Cord forgets the links to the previous account and resets the stage settings, because the other account's pipelines do not exist in the new one. Then use **Send existing data** to populate the new account.

If you reconnect **the same** account, Cord keeps the links and your stage settings: Companies and Deals are not duplicated. Changes you made in Cord while disconnected were not sent; use **Send existing data** to catch up.

## Disconnect

<Steps>
1. In **Settings › Integrations › HubSpot**, click **Disconnect**.
2. Confirm in **Disconnect HubSpot?**.
3. You see "HubSpot disconnected".
</Steps>

When you disconnect:

- Cord **stops syncing** and discards queued sends.
- Cord **revokes its access** to your HubSpot account.
- **What is already in HubSpot is not deleted**: Companies, Contacts, Deals and notes stay as they are.
- Workflows with the **Add a note in HubSpot** action start failing with "Connect HubSpot in Settings › Integrations to use this action."
