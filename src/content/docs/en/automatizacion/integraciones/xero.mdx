## How it works

Every invoice you issue in Cord shows up in your Xero with its contact, lines and currency.

**It arrives as a draft and you approve it.** An outside system should not post to anyone's books on its own: Cord prepares the invoice and your accountant reviews it before approving.

## Connect

<Steps>
1. Go to **Settings › Integrations › Xero**. You need the **Settings** permission.
2. Click **Connect Xero** and authorize. Xero asks which organization to grant access to: choose your company's.
3. When you come back you see the organization's name. From then on, each new invoice travels on its own.
4. If you already had invoices, click **Send pending invoices** to send the latest 50 missing ones.
</Steps>

## What it creates in your accounting

| Record | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Contact** | If it does not exist, looked up by email first. Cord remembers the link. |
| **Sales invoice** | Type `ACCREC`, in **draft** status, with date, due date, currency, the Cord number as the reference and one line per item. |
| **Amounts** | Tax exclusive (`Exclusive`), and each line with the Xero rate that matches Cord's and the tax Cord already computed. |

Cord asks for Xero's granular permissions for invoices and contacts (`accounting.invoices` and `accounting.contacts`), plus reading your tax rates (`accounting.settings.read`), not access to your whole ledger. If you connected Xero before Cord asked for that permission, the card will ask you to reconnect once.

## What it does not do yet

- **It does not approve the invoice.** That is deliberate: you approve it in Xero.
- **It does not send payments.** You record the payment in your accounting as usual.
- **Your tax arrives with its rate.** Each line carries the tax rate in your accounting that exactly matches Cord's. If that rate does not exist there, the invoice is not sent and the card tells you which one is missing: create it and click **Send the pending invoices**.
- **Invoices with withholdings are not sent.** Your accounting does not record them on a sales invoice; post them by hand.
- **It brings nothing from your accounting into Cord.** It flows one way.

## Guarantees

- **An invoice is never sent twice.** Cord remembers which invoice was recorded and does not repeat it, even if the event is retried.
- **Only final invoices travel.** Never a quote or a Cord draft.
- **Each line amount is computed on the already-rounded price**, so the total matches Cord to the cent.

## Disconnect

With **Disconnect** on the card. What was already recorded stays in your accounting. If you connect a different company or organization, Cord starts from scratch with it instead of reusing the previous one.

## Common issues

| Message or symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| **The card asks you to reconnect** | Xero renews access every 30 minutes and revokes the previous one; if renewal fails, reconnect. |
| **I cannot see the invoice** | Look for it in Xero under **Drafts**: it stays there until you approve it. |
