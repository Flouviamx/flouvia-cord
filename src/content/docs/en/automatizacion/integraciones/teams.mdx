## How it works

Cord posts a card in the Teams channel you choose when a quote moves forward. There are two ways to connect it, and only one can be active:

| | Connect with Microsoft | Power Automate flow |
|---|---|---|
| **How** | Sign in with your work account and pick team and channel. | Create a flow in the channel and paste its URL. |
| **Who posts** | The card is posted in the name of whoever connected; Cord's card says so. | The channel's flow. |
| **Requirement** | A Microsoft 365 work or school account (channels do not exist in personal accounts). | Being able to create flows in the channel. |

## Connect with Microsoft

<Callout type="info">
This feature is new and may not be enabled on your account yet. If you do not see it, write to us.
</Callout>

<Steps>
1. Go to **Settings › Integrations › Microsoft Teams**.
2. Click **Connect with Microsoft** and sign in with your work account.
3. Pick the **team** and **channel** from the lists and click **Use this channel**.
4. Click **Send test**: a sample card appears in the channel.
</Steps>

Cord asks to read your profile, the teams and channels you belong to, and to send channel messages. Microsoft may show you a notice that the app's publisher is unverified. If your organization does not let each person authorize apps on their own, a Microsoft 365 administrator will need to approve it.

## With a Power Automate flow

Teams' old connectors (Office 365 "Incoming Webhook") are retired; the URL comes from a flow.

<Steps>
1. In the channel, open **Workflows** from the three-dot menu.
2. Use the template **"Post to a channel when a webhook request is received"** and pick team and channel.
3. Copy the flow URL (it starts with `https://prod-` and ends in `logic.azure.com`).
4. In Cord, open **Use a Power Automate flow** on the Teams card, paste the URL, save and click **Send test**.
</Steps>

The URL is a credential: if it leaks, delete the flow and create another.

## Turn on alerts

Connecting turns on no alerts. In **Settings › Notifications**, check the **Teams** column for the events you want:

| Event | When it fires |
|---|---|
| **Your client viewed the quote** | When the client opens the public link. |
| **Quote approved** | The client accepted the proposal. |
| **Quote rejected** | The client rejected it. |
| **Payment received** | An online payment was recorded. |
| **Quote expiring** | 3 days before the valid-until date. |
| **Payment overdue** | A receivable passed its due date. |

Email, Slack and Teams are checked separately.

## Teams in a workflow

The **Send a Teams message** action in [Cord Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows) posts your own text with event data (`{{cliente}}`, `{{folio}}`, `{{total}}`), even if alerts are off.

## Approval requests

When a quote requests internal approval, Cord posts a card in the connected channel with the quote number, client, total and a **Decide in Cord** button. The decision is made in Cord: the Teams card has no buttons to approve or reject. Internal approvals require the **Scale** plan or higher.

## Disconnect

With **Disconnect** on the card. Microsoft does not allow revoking a single access from outside: to remove the app from your account, do it at myapps.microsoft.com.

## Common issues

| Message or symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| **"Microsoft no longer accepts the connection"** | Access expired or was revoked. Click **Reconnect**. |
| **No teams appear** | Your account does not belong to any Teams team, or it is a personal account. |
| **"The Teams URL must be the flow's URL"** | You pasted another address; it must end in a `logic.azure.com` domain. |
| **"Teams did not accept the card"** | The flow was turned off or deleted. Turn it on in Workflows or paste a new URL. |
