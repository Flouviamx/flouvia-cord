<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Lookups</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">A step that asks before deciding. It brings a number from your account and leaves it available to the steps that follow.</p>
</header>

## What it is for

A workflow only knows what the event carried. "Quote COT-7 for 150,000 was approved" says nothing about how much that client owes you or how your receivables are doing, so an alert could not include it and a condition could not decide on it.

A lookup closes that gap: you place it earlier, Cord runs it, and its results become variables for the rest of the workflow.

<Callout type="info">
A lookup only **reads**. It never changes anything in your account, so you can use it as often as you want.
</Callout>

## How to add one

<Steps>
1. Click **Add a step › Action or lookup**.
2. In the finder, filter by **Lookup** or type what you are after ("overdue", "pipeline", "balance").
3. Pick the lookup. If it has a days parameter, set it.
4. The panel shows **Makes available**: those are the variables you can use afterwards.
</Steps>

## The lookups

| Lookup | What it brings | Variables |
|---|---|---|
| **Overdue receivables** | How much you are owed past the due date. | `{{vencido_total}}`, `{{vencido_cantidad}}`, `{{vencido_dias_max}}` |
| **Open pipeline** | How much sits in sent or viewed quotes that are undecided. | `{{pipeline_total}}`, `{{pipeline_cantidad}}` |
| **What was collected** | How much was paid over the last days you choose (1 to 90). | `{{cobrado_total}}`, `{{cobrado_cantidad}}` |
| **What is about to be due** | How much is due over the next days you choose (1 to 90). | `{{por_vencer_total}}`, `{{por_vencer_cantidad}}` |
| **Client balance** | How much the event's client owes and how much is overdue. | `{{cliente_saldo}}`, `{{cliente_vencido}}`, `{{cliente_documentos}}` |

Amounts come formatted with your account's currency and number format, like the rest of the event data.

## Order matters

A lookup variable exists **from the step that brings it onward**. If you use it before, the workflow will not publish and the editor says so: "The variable {{vencido_total}} comes from a lookup that runs after this step."

The same applies inside a branch: what the **If met** branch looks up does not exist in the **If not met** branch. If both need it, put the lookup **before** the condition.

## Client balance needs a client

**Client balance** uses the event's client, so it only works with triggers that carry one: quotes, invoices, payments and clients. A scheduled or payout trigger has no client to ask about, and the workflow cannot be published with that lookup.

## A full example

"Month-end summary for the team", one of the ideas Cord ships with:

1. **When this happens:** on the 1st of each month at 8.
2. **Lookup:** what was collected in 30 days.
3. **Lookup:** the open pipeline.
4. **Lookup:** overdue receivables.
5. **Action:** email the whole team with the three numbers together.
6. **Action:** post the summary to Slack.

## Limits

Each lookup is a step, so it counts toward the 30-step maximum per workflow. They have no hourly cap of their own: they only read.
