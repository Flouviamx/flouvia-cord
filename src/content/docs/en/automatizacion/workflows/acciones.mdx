## Summary

| Action | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Create a task** | Adds a follow-up task in **Tasks**, linked to the quote when the event has one. | Nothing |
| **Email the team** | Emails the account owner or the whole team. | Nothing |
| **Send a Slack message** | Posts a message to your Slack channel. | Slack connected |
| **Add a note in HubSpot** | Adds a note to the quote's Deal or the client's Company. | HubSpot connected |
| **Email the client** | Sends a branded email to the client's contact, with the button to view and pay the document. | A quote or invoice trigger, and a client with an email |
| **Expire the quote** | Marks the quote as expired so the client can no longer approve it. | A quote trigger |
| **Approve the internal request** | Approves the pending internal approval and sends the quote to the client. | Approvals in your plan |
| **Void the invoice** | Voids the invoice if it has no payments applied. | An invoice trigger |
| **Send the data to a URL** | POSTs the event data to the URL you paste. | A public https URL |
| **Send a Teams message** | Posts a card to your Microsoft Teams channel. | Teams connected |
| **Send the client a WhatsApp** | Sends your approved WhatsApp template to the client's phone. | WhatsApp connected and a client with a phone |

To add an action, click **Add a step › Action or lookup**: a finder opens with every action and every [lookup](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/consultas), filtered by family (your team, your client, the document, other tools). Pick one and fill in its fields in the panel. Every text field accepts [event data](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/datos-del-evento) like `{{cliente}}`.

<Callout type="info">
Step error messages from runs currently appear in Spanish in the app. This page gives the English meaning of each one.
</Callout>

## Create a task

Adds a task for your team in **Tasks**, just as if you had created it by hand.

| Field | Required | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Task title** | Yes | Up to 200 characters. Accepts event data. Line breaks become spaces. |
| **Due in (days)** | No | 0 to 365. **0** means due today; **1**, tomorrow. Leave empty for a task without a date. |

**What it is linked to:**

- If the trigger is about a quote (sent, approved, paid, partial payment, invoiced, etc.), the task is linked to that quote and shows in its detail.
- If the trigger is a dispute or refund on a quote, it is also linked to that quote.
- On **A draft is deleted** it is not linked, because the quote no longer exists.
- On other triggers (invoices, clients, products, payouts, tasks, promises) the task is standalone.

**When it fails:**

- "The task title ended up empty": the title only had event data and all of it arrived empty. Add fixed text, for example "Follow-up: `{{cliente}}`".
- "Hourly task limit for workflows reached": your workflows created 120 tasks in the last hour. See [Limits](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/limites).

<Callout type="info">
Creating a task is also an event. If you have a workflow with the **A task is created** trigger, it will react to tasks created by your other workflows. Use the condition "Who caused it is not Another workflow" if you do not want that.
</Callout>

## Email the team

Sends an email to people in your organization. **It never writes to the client.**

| Field | Required | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **To** | Yes | **The account owner**, or **The whole team**: the active members of your organization, up to 25. Pending invitations do not receive it. |
| **Subject** | Yes | Up to 150 characters. Accepts event data. If it ends up empty when running, the workflow name is used. |
| **Message** | Yes | Up to 2,000 characters. Accepts event data and line breaks. |

**What the email looks like:** your message exactly as you wrote it, and at the bottom a line "Sent by the *Name* workflow in Cord." linking to the workflow. The footer uses your account's language. Event data is inserted as text: if a client's name contains symbols or tags, they are shown literally and do not alter the email.

Each person receives their own email; nobody sees the others' addresses.

**When it fails:**

- "There is nobody to send the email to": you chose **The account owner** and the account has no owner with an email, or there are no active members.
- "Email is not available right now": it could not be delivered to anyone. It is retried automatically.
- "Hourly email limit for workflows reached": your workflows sent 30 emails in the last hour.

## Email the client

Emails the contact of the client on the event's document, with the business's branding and the button to the public link: approve, if the event is about a quote; pay, if it is about an invoice.

| Field | Required | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Subject** | Yes | Up to 150 characters. Accepts event data. |
| **Message** | Yes | Up to 2,000 characters. Accepts event data and line breaks. Your client reads this. |

**Who receives it:** the email of the document's client, never an address typed into the step. If the client has no email on file, the step fails and says so.

**What Cord adds:** your logo and brand color, the button to the document, the link in plain text in case the button does not open, and your business footer. In the test environment the subject is marked as a test.

**It counts against your plan:** each client email uses one send from your plan's monthly quota, the same as sending a quote by hand. If the quota is gone, the step fails and nothing is sent.

<Callout type="info">
Cord already sends its own invoice reminders on the cadence set in **Settings › Collections**. If you also build a workflow that emails the client, check that two notices do not land on the same day for the same money.
</Callout>

## Expire the quote

Marks a quote that is still sent or viewed as **Expired**. The client can no longer approve or reject it from the link, the event is recorded in its history, and **A quote expires** fires for the rest of your automations.

It only works with triggers that carry a quote; if the quote was already approved, rejected or expired, the step fails with "The quote is no longer in a state that allows this action".

## Approve the internal request

Approves the quote's pending internal approval request and sends the quote to the client, exactly as if someone approved it in the app. Use it behind a condition — a total below a certain amount, for example — so small approvals do not wait on a person.

It requires internal approvals in your plan. If the quote no longer has a pending request, the step says so and nothing changes.

## Void the invoice

Voids the event's invoice. It follows the same rules as voiding by hand from **Invoices**: an invoice with payments applied is **not** voided — that is what the credit note is for — and canceling a CFDI with the SAT may stay pending a response. When it cannot be voided, the step fails and the invoice stays as it was.

| Field | Required | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Reason** | No | Up to 200 characters. It is kept in the invoice history. |

## Send the data to a URL

Makes a `POST` with the event as JSON to the address you paste: a Zapier Zap, a Make scenario, an n8n flow or your own server.

| Field | Required | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Destination URL** | Yes | It must be `https` and on a public server. Cord rejects internal addresses both when publishing and when running. |
| **Message** | No | Up to 1,000 characters. Accepts event data; it travels in the `mensaje` field. |

The body looks like this:

```json
{
  "workflow": "Large deal alert",
  "workflow_id": "…",
  "evento": "quote.approved",
  "objeto": "quote",
  "objeto_id": "…",
  "enviado_en": "2026-09-20T18:00:00.000Z",
  "mensaje": "ACME approved COT-7",
  "datos": { "folio": "COT-7", "cliente": "ACME", "total": "150,000", "moneda": "MXN" }
}
```

**When it fails:** if the URL is not valid or points to an internal server, and if the destination responds with an error. It retries within the run's 3 attempts.

<Callout type="info">
This action does not sign the request. If you need to verify that the request comes from Cord, use the [webhooks](/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/webhooks) in the Developers dock, which are signed.
</Callout>

## Send a Slack message

Posts a message to the channel you connected in **Settings › Integrations › Slack**.

| Field | Required | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Message** | Yes | Up to 1,000 characters. Accepts event data and line breaks. |

**What it looks like:** the text appears in the channel with the name and icon of the webhook's Slack app. Event data is inserted as plain text; a `<` or `&` in a client's name does not break the message formatting.

This message is independent from the **Settings › Notifications** alerts: a workflow posts even if those alerts are off, and with the text you write.

**When it fails:**

- "Connect Slack in Settings › Integrations to use this action": no Slack URL is saved.
- "Slack did not accept the message. Check the connection in Settings › Integrations": Slack rejected it, usually because the webhook was deleted or the app was removed from the channel. Create a new webhook and paste the URL. Meanwhile, Cord retries.
- "The message ended up empty": the message only had event data and it arrived empty.
- "Hourly message limit for workflows reached": 120 messages in the last hour.

How to connect Slack: [Slack](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/slack).

## Send a Teams message

Posts a card to the channel you connected in **Settings › Integrations › Microsoft Teams**.

| Field | Required | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Message** | Yes | Up to 1,000 characters. Accepts event data and line breaks. |

**How it looks:** a card with your text in the channel. Teams does not use Slack's formatting, so asterisks and `<url|text>` links show up literally; write plain text.

It is independent from **Settings › Notifications**: the workflow posts even when those notices are off.

**When it fails:**

- "Connect Microsoft Teams in Settings › Integrations to use this action.": there is no flow URL saved.
- "Teams did not accept the message.": the Power Automate flow was turned off or deleted. Create a new one and paste the new URL.
- "Workflows reached their hourly limit of Teams messages.": 120 messages in the last hour.

How to connect Teams: [Connect Microsoft Teams](https://cordhq.app/en/support/conectar-teams).

## Send the client a WhatsApp

Sends the template you set up in **Settings › Integrations › WhatsApp Business** to the phone of the document's client.

| Field | Required | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Variable 1 to 4** | No | Fill `{{1}}`, `{{2}}`… of your template, in order. They accept event data. |

**Why variables and not a message:** Meta does not allow starting a conversation with free text, only with an approved template. A free-text field here would promise something the provider rejects.

**The phone comes from the client**, never from a field in the step, and it must carry a country code. Cord does not guess the country: the same number exists in several.

**When it fails:**

- "The client has no phone with a country code on file.": edit the client and save the number with `+`.
- "WhatsApp did not accept your template.": it is not approved, the name does not match, or the number of variables differs.
- "Workflows reached their hourly limit of WhatsApp messages.": 60 messages in the last hour.

How to connect it: [Connect WhatsApp Business](https://cordhq.app/en/support/conectar-whatsapp).

## Add a note in HubSpot

Adds a note in HubSpot so your sales team sees what happened in Cord.

| Field | Required | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Note** | Yes | Up to 2,000 characters. Accepts event data and line breaks. |

**Where the note goes:**

1. On the **Deal** of the event's quote, if that quote is already in HubSpot.
2. If there is no Deal, on the client's **Company**, if the client is already in HubSpot.
3. If neither is in HubSpot yet, the step waits and retries: usually the sync finishes within moments.

The note is timestamped when created and appears on the record's timeline in HubSpot.

**Which triggers have somewhere to put the note:** quote triggers, quote payments, disputes, refunds and client triggers. On triggers without a quote or client (for example **A payout is paid to your bank** or **A product is created**) the note has no record to attach to: the step retries and the run ends as **Failed** with "This record is not in HubSpot yet". Use this action only with triggers that have a quote or client.

**When it fails:**

- "Connect HubSpot in Settings › Integrations to use this action": HubSpot is not connected.
- "HubSpot needs to be reconnected in Settings › Integrations": the connection lost its authorization.
- "This record is not in HubSpot yet. It will retry once it finishes syncing": see point 3 above. If it persists, check that the pipeline and stages are complete; quotes in a status without a stage are not sent to HubSpot.
- "The record no longer exists in HubSpot": the Deal or Company was deleted in HubSpot.
- "Hourly note limit in HubSpot reached": 120 notes in the last hour.

How to connect HubSpot: [HubSpot](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/hubspot).

## Several actions in a row

Actions at the same level run in order, one after another. If one fails, the following ones do not run until it is resolved: the run is retried **from the step that failed**, without repeating the ones already done. So a task is not created twice because the Slack message after it failed.
