## How to use an idea

There are three ways:

- In the **Workflows** list, under **Ideas to get started**, click **Use** on the idea.
- Inside the editor of an empty workflow, in **Start with an example**, click **Customize this workflow**. If the draft already had steps, Cord asks **Replace what you have?** before replacing them.
- In **Settings › Integrations**, the Slack, Teams and HubSpot pages show the ideas that use that app.

Every way creates a **draft** with the idea's name, trigger and steps, with the texts in your account's language. Nothing runs until you click **Publish**, and before that you can click **Test** to see what it would do with the latest real event. Change whatever you want: amounts, days, texts or recipients.

## 1. Quote follow-up ladder

So no quote goes cold in silence.

**Which business:** agencies, firms, industrial suppliers or any team that sends many quotes a week and has no time to check one by one who opened them.

<Steps>
1. **Trigger:** a quote is sent.
2. **Wait until** the **Current quote status** is **Viewed** or **Approved** (any is met), with a **2-day** limit.
3. **If it happens:** create the task "Call `{{cliente}}` while `{{folio}}` is warm", due in 1 day. The client has seen it: it is time to call.
4. **If the time runs out:** email the client with the subject "Any questions about `{{folio}}`?" and the message "Hi, we are following up on quote `{{folio}}` for `{{total}}` `{{moneda}}`. If anything needs adjusting, reply to this email and we will sort it out."
5. **Then wait until** the status is **Approved** or **Rejected**, with a **5-day** limit. If the client decides, the run ends there.
6. **If there is still no decision:** look up the client's balance and tell the owner, with the subject "`{{folio}}` went a week without a decision" and the open balance with you (`{{cliente_saldo}}`, of which `{{cliente_vencido}}` is overdue).
</Steps>

- **Needs:** the client with an email on file. The email to the client uses your plan's sending quota.
- **Adapt it:** change the time limits, or remove the client email if you want the follow-up to stay within the team.

## 2. Auto-approve only small quotes

So an 8,000 internal approval does not wait for someone to open the app.

**Which business:** sales teams where a discount or a large amount needs a manager's sign-off, but most requests are small and always get approved.

<Steps>
1. **Trigger:** a quote requests internal approval.
2. **Condition:** the **Total** is less than **50,000**.
3. **If the condition is met:** approve the internal request; the quote goes out to the client.
4. **If the condition isn't met:** email the whole team with the subject "`{{folio}}` needs a person: `{{total}}` `{{moneda}}`" and the reason for the request (`{{motivo}}`).
</Steps>

- **Needs:** internal approvals, which come with the **Scale** plan or higher. Without them, the approve step fails with "Your current plan does not include internal approvals."
- **Adapt it:** the amount is yours and is compared in the quote's currency. If you sell in several currencies, add a rule on **Currency** so 50,000 pesos and 50,000 dollars are not treated the same.

## 3. Teams when the client approves

So the team hears about it in the channel where it already works.

**Which business:** companies that use Microsoft 365 and coordinate production, deliveries or purchasing in a Teams channel.

<Steps>
1. **Trigger:** a quote is approved.
2. **Action:** send a Teams message: "`{{cliente}}` approved `{{folio}}`: `{{total}}` `{{moneda}}`".
</Steps>

- **Needs:** [Microsoft Teams](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/teams) connected in Settings › Integrations.
- **Adapt it:** swap it for **Send a Slack message** if your team lives there, or add a condition on **Total** to post only large sales.

## 4. HubSpot note when the client approves

So the CRM has the history without anyone copying it.

**Which business:** sales teams that run their pipeline in HubSpot and quote in Cord.

<Steps>
1. **Trigger:** a quote is approved.
2. **Action:** add a note to the quote's Deal (or, if there is no Deal, to the client's Company): "`{{cliente}}` approved `{{folio}}` for `{{total}}` `{{moneda}}` in Cord."
3. **Action:** post "`{{cliente}}` approved `{{folio}}`" to Slack.
</Steps>

- **Needs:** [HubSpot](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/hubspot) and [Slack](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/slack) connected.
- **Adapt it:** remove the Slack step if you only want the record in HubSpot.

## 5. Staged collection of an overdue invoice

To chase the money with judgment, not with a single email.

**Which business:** distributors, wholesalers and services that invoice on 30 or 60-day credit and need an even collections process.

<Steps>
1. **Trigger:** an invoice has been past due for days. It happens every day while the invoice stays open with a balance, for up to 90 days.
2. **Condition:** **Days past due** equals **3**. If met, it emails the client with the subject "Invoice `{{numero}}` is past due" and the balance (`{{saldo}}` `{{moneda}}`); Cord adds the button to pay.
3. **Condition:** **Days past due** equals **15**. If met, it looks up the client's balance and tells the owner, with the subject "`{{cliente}}`: 15 days past due", the total open, the overdue amount and across how many documents, to decide whether to stop credit.
</Steps>

Since the trigger happens every day, each condition picks the exact day it acts on; on the other days the run ends without doing anything.

- **Needs:** invoices with a due date and the client with an email.
- **Adapt it:** change the days, or add a third stage at 30 with a collections task.

## 6. Kick off when the deposit arrives

So production does not wait for someone to say so.

**Which business:** workshops, made-to-order manufacturing, construction, events or consulting: anything that charges a deposit before starting.

<Steps>
1. **Trigger:** a partial payment arrives.
2. **Condition:** the **Payment type** is **Deposit**.
3. **If the condition is met:** create the task "Start the work for `{{folio}}` (`{{cliente}}`)" due in 1 day, and tell the owner: "A deposit of `{{monto}}` `{{moneda}}` arrived. Remaining balance: `{{saldo_pendiente}}` `{{moneda}}`."
</Steps>

- **Needs:** quotes with a deposit. See [Deposits](/en/docs/pagos/anticipos).
- **Adapt it:** send the notice to the whole team instead of only the owner, or post it to Slack or Teams.

## 7. Collections summary every Monday

To start the week knowing whom to chase.

**Which business:** owners and finance leads who want the week's number in their inbox without opening the app.

<Steps>
1. **Trigger:** on a fixed schedule, every week, on Monday at 9 (in your account's time zone).
2. **Look up** overdue receivables.
3. **Look up** the open pipeline.
4. **Email the owner** with the subject "Your week in Cord (`{{fecha}}`)": what is overdue, across how many documents, how many days the oldest one is past due, and what sits in open quotes.
</Steps>

- **Needs:** nothing extra.
- **Adapt it:** make it monthly on day 1, add the **what was collected** or **what is about to be due** lookup, and also send it to Slack.

## 8. Remind the client 3 days before the due date

To collect before the date, not after.

**Which business:** recurring services, retainers and any business with receivables where the client pays on time if reminded.

<Steps>
1. **Trigger:** an invoice is about to be due. It happens every day while there are 1 to 30 days left and the invoice is still open with a balance.
2. **Condition:** **Days until due** equals **3**.
3. **If the condition is met:** email the client with the subject "Invoice `{{numero}}` is due on `{{vence}}`" and the balance. Cord adds the button to pay.
</Steps>

- **Needs:** invoices with a due date and the client with an email.
- **Adapt it:** change the 3 to the days you want, or swap the email for **Send the client a WhatsApp** if you connected [WhatsApp Business](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/whatsapp).

## 9. Last call and quote close-out

To close the loop instead of leaving quotes open forever.

**Which business:** anyone quoting prices that change (materials, imports, exchange rates, seasons) who cannot honor an old quote.

<Steps>
1. **Trigger:** a quote is about to expire. It happens every day while there are 1 to 30 days left and the quote is still sent or viewed.
2. **Condition:** **Days until it expires** equals **2**.
3. **If the condition is met:** email the client with the subject "Quote `{{folio}}` expires on `{{vence}}`" inviting them to approve it from the link.
4. **Wait until** the status is **Approved** or **Rejected**, with a **2-day** limit.
5. **If the time runs out:** expire the quote (the client can no longer approve it from the link) and tell the owner: "`{{folio}}` expired without an answer".
</Steps>

- **Needs:** quotes with a validity date and the client with an email.
- **Adapt it:** remove the **Expire the quote** step if you prefer to close it by hand.

## Combining ideas

An idea is a starting point, not a limit. For example, you can merge "HubSpot note when the client approves" and "Teams when the client approves" into one workflow: always add the HubSpot note and, with a condition on **Total**, post to Teams only the large sales. On the Free and Starter plans, merging ideas into one workflow also saves active-workflow slots.
