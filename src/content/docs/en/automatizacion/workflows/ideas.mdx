## How to use an idea

There are two ways:

- In the **Workflows** list, under **Ideas to get started**, click **Use** on the idea.
- Inside the editor of an empty workflow, in **Start with an example**, click **Customize this workflow**.

Both create a **draft** with the idea's name, trigger and steps, in your account's language. Nothing runs until you click **Publish**. Change whatever you want first: amounts, days, texts or recipients.

<Callout type="info">
Ideas that use Slack or HubSpot can be created even if those apps are not connected, but those steps will fail when they run until you connect them in **Settings › Integrations**.
</Callout>

## 1. Quote follow-up ladder

So no quote goes cold in silence.

- **Trigger:** a quote is sent.
- **Steps:**
  1. Waits until the client opens or approves it, with a 2-day limit.
  2. If it happens: creates the task to call while the deal is warm.
  3. If the time runs out: emails the client asking if anything needs clearing up, and waits another 5 days for a decision. If none comes, it looks up the client's balance and tells the owner what they already owe.
- **Needs:** a client with an email on file.
- **Adapt it:** change the limits, or drop the client email if you want follow-up to stay internal.

## 2. Auto-approve only small quotes

So an internal approval for 8,000 does not wait for someone to open the app.

- **Trigger:** a quote requests internal approval.
- **Steps:**
  1. Condition: **Total** is under 50,000.
  2. If met: approves the internal request and the quote goes out to the client.
  3. If not: emails the whole team with the reason, so a person reviews it.
- **Needs:** internal approvals in your plan.
- **Adapt it:** the amount is yours. You can add a condition by client or by currency.

## 3. Teams when the client approves

So the team finds out in the channel where they already work.

- **Trigger:** a quote is approved.
- **Steps:**
  1. Sends a Teams message: "`{{cliente}}` approved `{{folio}}`: `{{total}}` `{{moneda}}`".
- **Needs:** Microsoft Teams connected in Settings › Integrations.
- **Adapt it:** switch it to **Send a Slack message** if your team lives there, or add a **Total** condition to post only the large deals.

## 4. HubSpot note when the client approves

So the CRM has the story without anyone copying it over.

- **Trigger:** a quote is approved.
- **Steps:**
  1. Adds a note to the quote's Deal (or the client's Company).
  2. Posts the alert to Slack.
- **Needs:** HubSpot and Slack connected.
- **Adapt it:** drop the Slack step if you only want the HubSpot record.

## 5. Staged collection of an overdue invoice

To chase the money with judgment, not with a single email.

- **Trigger:** an invoice has been past due for days.
- **Steps:**
  1. At exactly 3 days: emails the client with the pay button.
  2. At exactly 15: looks up the client's total balance and tells the owner, to decide whether to stop credit.
- **Needs:** invoices with a due date and a client with an email.
- **Adapt it:** change the days, or add a third stage at 30 with a collections task.

## 6. Kick off when the deposit arrives

So production does not wait for someone to pass the word.

- **Trigger:** a partial payment arrives.
- **Steps:**
  1. Condition: the payment type is **Deposit**.
  2. If met: creates the task to start the work and tells the owner the remaining balance.
- **Needs:** nothing extra.
- **Adapt it:** send the alert to the whole team instead of only the owner.

## 7. Collections summary every Monday

To start the week knowing who needs chasing.

- **Trigger:** every Monday at 9 (your account's time).
- **Steps:**
  1. Looks up overdue receivables.
  2. Looks up the open pipeline.
  3. Emails the summary to the account owner.
- **Needs:** nothing extra.
- **Adapt it:** switch it to monthly on the 1st, add the **what was collected** lookup and send it to Slack too.

## 8. Remind the client 3 days before the due date

To collect before the date, not after it.

- **Trigger:** an invoice is about to be due.
- **Steps:**
  1. Condition: **Days until due** equals **3**.
  2. If met: emails the client with their balance. Cord adds the pay button.
- **Needs:** invoices with a due date and a client with an email.
- **Adapt it:** change the 3 to whatever notice you want.

## 9. Last call and quote close-out

To close the loop instead of leaving quotes alive forever.

- **Trigger:** a quote is about to expire.
- **Steps:**
  1. Condition: **Days until it expires** equals **2**.
  2. If met: emails the client and waits 2 days for a decision.
  3. If none comes: expires the quote and tells the owner.
- **Needs:** quotes with an expiration date and a client with an email.
- **Adapt it:** remove the **Expire the quote** step if you prefer to close it by hand.

## Combining ideas

An idea is a starting point, not a limit. For example, you can merge "HubSpot note when the client approves" and "Teams when the client approves" into one workflow: always add the HubSpot note and, with a **Total** condition, post to Teams only for the large deals.
