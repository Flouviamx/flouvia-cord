Cord organizes work around a single sales cycle: **register the client, prepare the
offer, negotiate, approve, collect, invoice and follow up**. The sections are not isolated
databases; they show different stages of the same clients, documents and transactions.

## The main flow

<Steps>
1. Create or import the client in **Clients**.
2. Create reusable products, services or kits in **Products**.
3. Prepare a quote by hand, from a kit or with an AI draft.
4. Send the link. Cord records your client's first view and keeps the document's
   activity.
5. The client discusses, approves or rejects and, when applicable, pays online.
6. Cord records the online payment or the payment you mark manually.
7. The deal moves to **Invoices** and **Collections** based on the document issued, the
   balance and the due date.
8. **Reports** and **Performance** aggregate the results without creating a second copy of the
   transaction.
</Steps>

## Sidebar

<DmAppMap lang="en" />

At the top of the sidebar is the **organization switcher**. From it you switch
workspaces, create a new one (**Create workspace**), open **Team settings**, turn the
**Test environment** on or off, and sign out.

| Group | Item | Route | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | **Home** | `/app` | KPIs, pipeline, recent activity and pending follow-ups. Guide: [Home and views](/en/docs/gestion/dashboard). |
| — | **Quotes** | `/app/cotizaciones` | Create, search, filter, edit, duplicate, send and print. The counter shows quotes that need follow-up. Guide: [Quote management](/en/docs/gestion/cotizaciones). |
| — | **Clients** | `/app/clientes` | Directory, CSV import, commercial terms, tax details, credit and statement. Guide: [Clients and statements](/en/docs/gestion/clientes). |
| — | **Products** | `/app/productos` | Catalog, volume pricing, CSV import and the **Kits** tab. Guide: [Import your catalog](/en/docs/cuenta/catalogo). |
| — | **Tasks** | `/app/tareas` | Team to-dos by assignee and due date, with filters, editing and completed tasks. The counter shows your tasks (or unassigned ones) due today or overdue; it turns red when some are overdue. Guide: [Tasks and follow-up](/en/docs/gestion/tareas). |
| Revenue | **Invoices** | `/app/facturas` | Issue, review, export and follow up on invoices; includes recurring ones (Professional or higher). Guide: [Invoices](/en/docs/pagos/facturas-emitidas). |
| Revenue | **Payments** | `/app/cobros` | Charges, payouts, balances, refunds and disputes. Guide: [Payments dashboard](/en/docs/pagos/dashboard). |
| Revenue | **Collections** | `/app/cobranza` | Receivables, aging and exposure by client (Professional or higher). The red counter shows overdue documents. Guide: [Receivables and follow-up](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza). |
| Revenue | **AI agent** (under Collections) | `/app/cobranza/agente` | Configure and supervise AI collections (Pro, Scale, or Developer). Guide: [Autonomous collections (AI)](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia). |
| Insights | **Reports** | `/app/informes` | 15 reports in seven groups: Overview, Sales, Clients, Products, Finance, Team, and Custom. Seven are widget boards, seven are tables with comparison and CSV export, and the Custom report builds your own, saves it for the team, and emails it. Guides: [Reports and performance](/en/docs/gestion/informes) and [Custom reports](/en/docs/gestion/informes-personalizados). |
| Insights | **Performance** | `/app/desempeno` | Team performance across your whole history: results attributed to whoever created or duplicated each quote. For a range with comparison, use the **Sales by team member** report. |
| Automation | **Workflows** | `/app/workflows` | "When this happens, do this" automations, ready-made ideas and run history. Guide: [Cord Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows). |

Also:

- **Pinned.** The pin button next to any page title (or the `X` key) adds it to the
  **Pinned** group at the top of the sidebar.
- **Collapsible groups.** **Revenue**, **Insights** and **Automation** collapse with a click on their
  title.
- **Collapse the sidebar.** The button at the bottom of the sidebar shrinks it to icons.
- **Settings** (`/app/ajustes`) opens with the gear in the top bar. See the
  [Settings map](/en/docs/cuenta/mapa-de-ajustes).
- **Set up with AI** (`/app/setup`) opens from **Settings › General**.

## Top bar

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search…** (`⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`) | Opens the palette to search quotes, clients, products and Settings tabs, or run a command. |
| **Setup guide** | While your account is incomplete, shows your completion percentage and opens the **Set up Cord** widget. See [Create your account](/en/docs/cuenta/crear-cuenta). |
| **Create** | Menu to create a **Quote**, **Invoice**, **Client**, **Product** or **Task**. It only shows what your permissions allow. |
| **Apps** | For people with the Settings permission: which apps are connected and which you can connect, without leaving the screen. |
| **Notifications** | Recent activity panel with **Mark as read** and **View all activity**. A dot means there is something new. Guide: [Notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones). |
| **Help** | **Help center** with search, keyboard shortcuts, user guide, contact support, frequently asked questions and the Cord AI assistant. |
| **Theme** | Switches between light and dark mode. |
| **Settings** | Opens the Settings index. |

### Keyboard shortcuts

On desktop you can use these shortcuts when you are not typing in a field. On screens
narrower than 880 px they are disabled and everything is done with visible buttons.

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` or `/` | Open search |
| `C` | New quote |
| `F` | New invoice |
| `T` | New task |
| `G` then `D`, `C`, `L`, `P`, `T`, `F`, `O`, `B`, `A`, `E` or `W` | Go to Home, Quotes, Clients, Products, Tasks, Invoices, Payments, Collections, Reports, Performance or Workflows |
| `X` | Pin or unpin the current page in the sidebar |
| `?` | Show or hide the shortcuts help |

The quote and invoice editor (`/app/cotizaciones/nueva` and `/app/facturas/nueva`, also
for drafts with `?draft=` and new versions with `?version=`) has its own shortcuts:
there `/` jumps to the catalog search, `⌘ Enter` sends or issues, and `⌘ S` saves the
draft; `⌘K` still opens search. See [Preview and sending](/en/docs/cotizacion/envio).

On a phone, the sidebar opens as a panel and includes shortcuts to **Help** and
**Settings**.

<Callout type="info">
The options you see depend on your **permissions**, your **effective plan**, your
**country of operation** and the account's setup state. A page existing does not mean
any member can change it.
</Callout>

## Test environment

The **Test environment** is an isolated copy of your organization for rehearsing without
touching your real data: quotes, charges with test data, webhooks and integrations.

<Steps>
1. Open the organization switcher at the top of the sidebar.
2. Turn on **Test environment**.
3. The app shows a **Test mode** strip with the notice "Everything you do here is a test —
   your real data is untouched".
4. To go back, select **Exit test mode** on the strip or turn the switch off.
</Steps>

From the strip you can also **Clear test data** to start over.

Keep in mind:

- Test data does not mix with production: when you leave, you won't see what you created
  inside.
- Some real actions are not available in test mode: subscribing or changing plans,
  connecting a custom domain, and applying a **Set up with AI** proposal.
- Test documents do not consume your plan's allowance.

Guide for integrations: [Test environments](/en/docs/desarrolladores/empezar/pruebas).

## Cord Workbench

**Cord Workbench** is the developer panel. It appears as a dock across the app when you
turn on **Developer mode** at the bottom of **Settings**, and requires the Settings
permission.

Its tabs are **Overview**, **Webhooks**, **Events**, **API**, **Logs** and **Health**: API
keys, webhook endpoints and their deliveries, emitted events, request logs and the status
of your integration. Guide: [Cord Workbench](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/workbench).

## Quote statuses

| Status | Operational meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | Editable draft not yet sent to the client. |
| `sent` | Sent; the link is ready to be viewed. |
| `viewed` | The client opened the quote for the first time. Your own visit doesn't count. |
| `approved` | The client recorded their approval. A balance may remain. |
| `rejected` | The client rejected the proposal. |
| `expired` | Its validity ended without closing. |
| `paid` | The non-recurring flow was paid according to its recorded payments. |
| `invoiced` | The deal already has an associated invoice. |

A recurring quote does not become `paid` forever: each installment creates its own charge
and the subscription keeps a separate status.

## Active organization

Cord is multi-company. Every client, product, quote, invoice, member and setting belongs
to the **active organization**. If you have access to more than one, check the
organization switcher before importing, issuing or changing configuration.

## Permissions that change the experience

The owner always has full access. Other members get presets or per-section permissions:

- **Quotes:** create, edit, send and duplicate.
- **Approvals:** approve discounts, amounts or controlled changes.
- **Collections:** review and manage receivables.
- **Clients** and **Products:** change their directories.
- **Reports:** open Reports and Performance, see Home's analytics widgets, and save custom reports.
- **Settings:** change the business configuration.
- **Payment settings:** change Cord Payments and the payout account.
- **Refunds:** start refunds of reconciled charges.
- **Team:** invite people and change permissions.

If an action doesn't appear, check the member's permission first; then the plan and the
configuration that feature requires.

## What changes by country

- Sign-up is available in 12 markets: Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain,
  the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru.
- Cord Payments is available in the first eight. In Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru,
  online payments work with [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago), which is also
  offered in Mexico and Brazil as an alternative.
- SPEI is exclusive to Mexico.
- Mexico issues CFDI 4.0 with a valid CSD.
- Outside Mexico, Cord issues commercial invoices. In Spain, registration with the AEAT
  (Verifactu) is not active yet.

## Before looking for an error

When something isn't available, check in this order:

1. active organization and test environment;
2. the member's permission;
3. the plan's limit or capability;
4. country of operation and compatible method;
5. required fields of the client, issuer or document;
6. the previous status of the quote, invoice or payment.

Then read the section's guide. The [support center](https://cordhq.app/soporte) is for
specific incidents; these guides explain the full model.
