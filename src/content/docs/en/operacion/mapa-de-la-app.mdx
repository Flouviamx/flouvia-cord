Cord organizes work around a single sales cycle: **register the client, prepare the
offer, negotiate, approve, collect, invoice and follow up**. The sections are not isolated
databases; they show different stages of the same clients, documents and transactions.

## The main flow

<Steps>
1. Create or import the client in **Clients**.
2. Create reusable products, services or kits in **Products**.
3. Prepare a quote by hand, from a kit or with an AI draft.
4. Send the link. Cord records your client's first view and keeps the document's
   activity.
5. The client discusses, approves or rejects and, when applicable, pays online.
6. Cord records the online payment or the payment you mark manually.
7. The deal moves to **Invoices** and **Collections** based on the document issued, the
   balance and the due date.
8. **Reports** and **Performance** aggregate the results without creating a second copy
   of the transaction.
</Steps>

## Sidebar

<DmAppMap lang="en" />

The sidebar is the app's main navigation. From top to bottom it has: the organization
switcher, your **Pinned** pages (if you have any), an untitled list of daily shortcuts,
the collapsible **Revenue**, **Insights** and **Automation** groups, and the footer with
your plan usage, the team invitation and the button to collapse the sidebar.

**Settings is not in the sidebar.** It opens with the gear in the top bar (on a phone,
from the menu; see [On a phone](#on-a-phone)).

### Organization switcher

At the very top is your workspace: a square with the organization's initial (or its
logo, if you uploaded one), the **Workspace** label and the name of the active
organization. Selecting it opens a menu to:

- **switch workspaces** among the organizations you belong to;
- **Create workspace**;
- open **Team settings** (Settings › Team & roles);
- turn the **Test environment** on or off (see [Test environment](#test-environment));
- **Sign out**.

Everything below it (counters, pinned pages, data) belongs to the organization the
switcher shows.

### Pinned

**Pinned** is its own group, above the sections, with the pages you chose to keep one
click away: a client you work with every day, a specific report, or an invoice you are
chasing.

<Steps>
1. Open the page you want to pin.
2. Select the pin button next to the page title (**Pin to menu**) or press <kbd>X</kbd>.
   Cord confirms with "Pinned to the menu" and the page appears at the end of the
   **Pinned** group.
3. To remove it, select the pin again (or press <kbd>X</kbd>) on that same page, or hover
   over the pinned item and select the **X** that appears on its right.
</Steps>

- **Name and icon.** The pinned item takes the page title and the icon of the section it
  belongs to: a pinned client carries the Clients icon; an invoice, the Invoices icon. If
  the page doesn't belong to any sidebar section (for example, a Settings tab), it
  carries a pin.
- **Each report counts separately.** In **Reports**, each report (Overview, Finance, Cash
  flow…) is pinned as a separate page.
- **Reorder.** On desktop, drag a pinned item above or below another.
- **Up to 12.** If you pin one more, the first one in the list is removed.
- **They follow you.** They are saved per user and per organization: you see the same
  ones on another computer, another browser or your phone, but each organization has its
  own and your teammates don't see yours.
- The **Pinned** group only appears when you have at least one.

On desktop, if you rest the cursor on a section for half a second, its shortcut appears
on the right (for example <kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd>), unless a counter is taking that
spot.

The section you are in is marked with a tinted background and navy text; hovering over
the others shows a fainter tint that never covers or moves the active one. When you are
inside a page of a section (a client's detail, a specific invoice), that section stays
highlighted.

### Counters

Three sections show a number on the right when something needs attention. They don't
count history: they count what is pending right now, in the active organization.

| Section | What it counts | Color |
|---|---|---|
| **Quotes** | Quotes your client already **viewed** and hasn't approved or rejected yet (*Viewed* status). It's the same number as the **Needs follow-up** KPI on Home. | Gray |
| **Tasks** | Open tasks that are **yours or unassigned** and are due **today** or already **overdue**. "Today" is the day in your business's time zone. Other members' tasks don't count. | Gray; orange if any is overdue |
| **Collections** | **Overdue** receivables: open invoices and approved quotes whose payment date has passed. A quote that already has its open invoice is counted once. | Orange |

- When nothing is pending, the counter doesn't show.
- Above 99 it shows **99+**.
- On hover, the counter explains what it counts, for example "3 quotes viewed, no
  reply", "2 overdue receivables" or "4 tasks due today or overdue".
- Counters are recalculated every time you open a page.

### AI agent, under Collections

**AI agent** is a sub-page of **Collections**, not a separate section. It appears under
Collections, indented with a vertical guide, only when you are in **Collections** or in
the agent itself; everywhere else in the app, the Collections row takes a single line.
The agent's page is titled **Collections agent**.

To get there from anywhere: open **Collections** (<kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>) and select
**AI agent**, or type "collections" in the [command menu](#command-menu).

### Collapsible groups

**Revenue**, **Insights** and **Automation** collapse and expand with a click on their
title. The title is gray and in regular weight; the arrow appears on hover or when the
group is collapsed.

- **They are remembered.** The groups you collapse are saved to your user: they stay
  collapsed when you change pages, when you come back another day and on another device.
- **They open on their own if you are inside.** If you open a page of a collapsed group
  (for example, with <kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> while **Revenue** is collapsed), the group
  shows open so you can see where you are.
- The daily-shortcuts list (Home through Tasks) has no title and doesn't collapse.

### Sidebar footer

| Item | What it does | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Active quotes** | A meter with your active quotes against your plan's cap (for example, "4 / 5"), a bar and the **Upgrade plan** link, which opens **Settings › Plans & subscription**. Draft, sent, viewed and approved quotes count as active. The bar is navy, turns amber from 80% and red when you reach the limit. | Only on plans with an active-quote cap: Free (5) and Starter (50). |
| **Invite people** | Opens **Settings › Team & roles** with the invitation form already open. | Only if you have the **Team** permission and your plan includes more than one user (Pro or higher). |
| Collapse button | The panel icon on the right. Collapses the sidebar to a column of icons or expands it again. Shortcut: <kbd>[</kbd>. | Always, on desktop. |

### Collapsed sidebar

With the footer button or the <kbd>[</kbd> key, the sidebar shrinks to a narrow column of
icons and leaves more room for the content. Select the button again (or press
<kbd>[</kbd>) to expand it.

- Every section shows, even if you collapsed groups; groups are separated by a short
  line.
- **Counters become a dot.** Each counter turns into a dot on the icon: gray for Quotes
  and Tasks, red for Collections or for Tasks with overdue items.
- **Label on hover.** Each icon shows, on its right, the section name, the counter's
  explanation (if any) and its shortcut, for example "Quotes · 3 quotes viewed, no reply
  · G C". It also appears when you move through the sidebar with <kbd>Tab</kbd>.
- The organization switcher stays as the centered initial square.
- The active-quotes meter is hidden and **Invite people** becomes an icon.
- Collapsed or expanded is remembered **in that browser**.

### When you change pages

- In Chrome, Edge and Safari 18.2 or later, the sidebar stays still and only the content
  fades when you change pages. In other browsers the page changes without a transition.
- When you hover over a sidebar section, Cord starts loading it, so it opens faster when
  you click.
- The sidebar keeps its scroll position between pages.

### On a phone

On screens 880 px wide or narrower, the sidebar opens with the menu button (three lines)
on the left of the top bar, as a panel over the page. It closes when you pick a page,
tap outside it or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

At the top it has three large rows that live in the top bar on desktop:

- **Help**: opens the Help center.
- **Theme**: shows the current mode (**Light**, **Dark** or **System**) and switches to
  the next one with each tap.
- **Settings**: opens **Settings**.

Below come your pinned pages and the same sections, with 44 px rows for your finger and
a single scroll. On a phone there is no collapsed sidebar, no shortcuts or floating
labels are shown, and the **X** to remove a pinned page is always visible. To pin a new
page, do it on desktop: the pin button doesn't appear on a phone.

## Top bar

The top bar floats over the content, in the same material as the sidebar. From left to
right:

| Item | What it does | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Menu button | Opens the sidebar as a panel. | Only on a phone. |
| **Search…** | Opens the [command menu](#command-menu). Shows <kbd>⌘K</kbd> on Mac and <kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> on Windows and Linux. | Always. On a phone it shrinks to a magnifier. |
| **Setup guide** | A pill with a ring and your completion percentage. Selecting it reopens the **Set up Cord** widget. See [Create your account](/en/docs/cuenta/crear-cuenta). | While your initial setup is incomplete and you minimized the widget. On a phone only the ring shows. |
| **Create** | Navy button with **+**: opens the menu to create (see [Create](#create)). | If you have at least one permission to create something. On a phone it's a circle with **+**. |
| **Apps** | Your integrations and the ones you can connect (see [Apps](#apps)). | Only with the **Settings** permission. |
| **Notifications** | The bell, with the number of unread updates (see [Notifications](#notifications)). | Always. |
| **Help** | Opens the **Help center**. | On desktop. On a phone, from the sidebar. |
| **Theme** | Switches between light, dark and system (see [Theme](#theme)). | On desktop. On a phone, from the sidebar. |
| **Settings** | The gear opens the **Settings** index and stays tinted while you are in Settings. See the [Settings map](/en/docs/cuenta/mapa-de-ajustes). | On desktop. On a phone, **Settings** in the sidebar. |

The top bar icons don't animate: on hover only their background changes.

Below the bar is each page's header: its title, the pin button to pin it and, on the
right, that page's own actions (for example, **Customize** on Home).

### Create

The **Create** menu has up to five options, each with its key:

| Option | Key | What it opens | Permission needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quote** | <kbd>C</kbd> | The editor for a new quote. | Quotes |
| **Invoice** | <kbd>F</kbd> | The editor for a new invoice. | Quotes |
| **Client** | <kbd>L</kbd> | Clients, with the new-client form open. | Clients |
| **Product** | <kbd>P</kbd> | Products, with the new-product form open. | Products |
| **Task** | <kbd>T</kbd> | **New task**, a quick window with the title and due date, without leaving the page. | Quotes, Collections or Clients |

- Each option only appears if your role has its permission, and its key only works if
  the option appears. If you have none, the **Create** button isn't shown.
- The letters are the same ones <kbd>G</kbd> plus letter uses to go to that section:
  <kbd>C</kbd> creates a quote and <kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd> goes to Quotes.
- The keys work on any desktop screen even when the menu is closed.

### Apps

The **Apps** button (a grid icon with a **+**) shows the logos of up to two connected
integrations next to it. When you open it you see **Your integrations**: connected ones
first, then available ones, with one action per row: **Open**, **Review** (if a
connection needs attention), **Connect** or **Coming soon**. At the bottom,
**Explore integrations** opens **Settings › Integrations**. Only people with the
**Settings** permission see it, because they are the ones who can connect something.

### Notifications

The bell tells you what **your client** did and the money that **came in**, on quotes
and invoices: views, approvals, rejections, counteroffers, messages, partial payments and
payments. Your own actions don't light it up. It shows a number with the unread updates
(1 to 9; from 10 on, **9+**), and when you open it you see the 15 most recent ones,
**Mark as read** and **View all activity**. What you read is saved to your user, so it
doesn't come back on another device. Full details in
[Notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones#the-bell-in-the-top-bar).

### Help

The help icon opens the **Help center**, a side panel with an article search,
**Keyboard shortcuts**, **User guide**, **Contact support**,
**Frequently asked questions** and the **Cord AI** assistant, which answers questions
about using the platform.

### Theme

The theme button shows the icon of the **current** mode: sun for **Light**, moon for
**Dark** and monitor for **System**. Each click moves to the next one: Light → Dark →
System → Light.

- **System** follows your operating system's preference live: if your computer switches
  to dark mode at night, Cord switches with it.
- The default mode is **Light**.
- Your choice is remembered **in that browser**; on a phone you change it from the
  **Theme** row in the sidebar.

### Keyboard use

The top bar menus open one at a time: opening one closes whichever was open. **Create**
and **Apps** can be used with the keyboard: <kbd>Enter</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd> or
<kbd>↓</kbd> on the button move into the menu; <kbd>↑</kbd>, <kbd>↓</kbd>,
<kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> move between options; <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the menu
and returns focus to the button. A click outside the menu also closes it.

## Command menu

The command menu searches your organization and takes you to any section without the
mouse. Open it with **Search…** in the top bar, with <kbd>⌘K</kbd> on Mac or
<kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> on Windows and Linux, or with <kbd>/</kbd>. On a phone, tap the
magnifier.

**With an empty search** it shows:

- **Recent:** the last 5 pages you opened from the command menu, saved in that browser.
- **Go to:** the app's sections, each report separately and each Settings tab (for
  example "Settings · Taxes"). Sections that have a shortcut show it on the right
  (<kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>C</kbd>, <kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd>…).
- **Actions:** **Build with AI** (opens a new quote, <kbd>C</kbd>), **New invoice**
  (<kbd>F</kbd>), **New client** (<kbd>L</kbd>), **New product** (<kbd>P</kbd>),
  **New task** (<kbd>T</kbd>, opens the same quick window as **Create › Task**) and
  **View public site**. If your role doesn't have the permission to create something,
  the corresponding action can't be completed.

**As you type** it filters sections and actions, and from two letters on it also
searches your data:

| Group | Searches by | Opens | Context it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quotes** | Quote number or client name | The quote's detail | Status and total in the quote's currency, for example "Viewed · MX$1,200.00". |
| **Invoices** | Invoice number or client name | The invoice's detail | Status and total in the invoice's currency, for example "Open · €3,480.00". Statuses: Draft, Open, Paid, Void and Uncollectible. |
| **Clients** | Name or tax ID | The client's record | — |
| **Products** | Name or SKU | The product's record | The SKU next to the name. |

- Each group shows up to 6 results, newest first (clients and products, alphabetically).
- **Invoices** only appears if you have the **Collections** permission, the same one the
  invoice list requires.
- Settings tabs can also be found by synonyms: "VAT", "IVA", "NIF", "RFC", "CSD", "SAML"
  or "DNS" take you to the tab that configures them.

**To move around:** <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> go through the results,
<kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the selected one and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the menu and returns
focus to where you were.

## Test environment

The **Test environment** is an isolated copy of your organization for rehearsing without
touching your real data: quotes, charges with test data, webhooks and integrations.

<Steps>
1. Open the organization switcher at the top of the sidebar.
2. Turn on **Test environment**.
3. The app shows a **Test mode** strip with the notice "Everything you do here is a test —
   your real data is untouched".
4. To go back, select **Exit test mode** on the strip or turn the switch off.
</Steps>

From the strip you can also **Clear test data** to start over.

Keep in mind:

- Test data does not mix with production: when you leave, you won't see what you created
  inside.
- Some real actions are not available in test mode: subscribing or changing plans,
  connecting a custom domain, and applying a **Set up with AI** proposal.
- Test documents do not consume your plan's allowance.
- The notifications bell doesn't show an unread counter inside the test environment.

Guide for integrations: [Test environments](/en/docs/desarrolladores/empezar/pruebas).

## Cord Workbench

**Cord Workbench** is the developer panel. It appears as a dock across the app when you
turn on **Developer mode** at the bottom of **Settings**, and requires the Settings
permission.

Its tabs are **Overview**, **Webhooks**, **Events**, **API**, **Logs** and **Health**: API
keys, webhook endpoints and their deliveries, emitted events, request logs and the status
of your integration. Guide: [Cord Workbench](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/workbench).

## Quote statuses

| Status | Operational meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | Editable draft not yet sent to the client. |
| `sent` | Sent; the link is ready to be viewed. |
| `viewed` | The client opened the quote for the first time. Your own visit doesn't count. |
| `approved` | The client recorded their approval. A balance may remain. |
| `rejected` | The client rejected the proposal. |
| `expired` | Its validity ended without closing. |
| `paid` | The non-recurring flow was paid according to its recorded payments. |
| `invoiced` | The deal already has an associated invoice. |

A recurring quote does not become `paid` forever: each installment creates its own charge
and the subscription keeps a separate status.

## Active organization

Cord is multi-company. Every client, product, quote, invoice, member and setting belongs
to the **active organization**. If you have access to more than one, check the
organization switcher before importing, issuing or changing configuration.

## Permissions that change the experience

The owner always has full access. Other members get presets or per-section permissions:

- **Quotes:** create, edit, send and duplicate. Shows **Quote** and **Invoice** in the
  **Create** menu.
- **Approvals:** approve discounts, amounts or controlled changes.
- **Collections:** review and manage receivables. It also decides whether the command
  menu finds invoices.
- **Clients** and **Products:** change their directories. Each one shows its option in
  the **Create** menu.
- **Reports:** open Reports and Performance.
- **Settings:** change the business configuration. Shows the **Apps** button in the top
  bar.
- **Payment configuration:** change Cord Payments and the payout account.
- **Refunds:** start refunds of reconciled charges.
- **Team:** invite people and change permissions. On Pro or higher it shows
  **Invite people** at the bottom of the sidebar.

Creating tasks (**Create › Task** and the <kbd>T</kbd> key) requires Quotes, Collections
or Clients, any of the three.

If an action doesn't appear, check the member's permission first; then the plan and the
configuration that feature requires.

## What changes by country

- Sign-up is available in 12 markets: Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil, Spain,
  the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru.
- Cord Payments is available in the first eight. In Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru,
  online payments work with [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago), which is also
  offered in Mexico and Brazil as an alternative.
- SPEI is exclusive to Mexico.
- Mexico issues CFDI 4.0 with a valid CSD.
- Outside Mexico, Cord issues commercial invoices. In Spain, registration with the AEAT
  (Verifactu) is not active yet.

## Before looking for an error

When something isn't available, check in this order:

1. active organization and test environment;
2. the member's permission;
3. the plan's limit or capability;
4. country of operation and compatible method;
5. required fields of the client, issuer or document;
6. the previous status of the quote, invoice or payment.

Then read the section's guide. The [support center](https://cordhq.app/soporte) is for
specific incidents; these guides explain the full model.
