Cord organizes work around one commercial cycle: **record the client, prepare the
offer, negotiate, approve, collect, invoice, and follow up**. Sections aren't
isolated databases; they expose different stages of the same clients, documents,
and money movements.

## The primary workflow

1. Create or import the client under **Clients**.
2. Create reusable products, services, or kits under **Products**.
3. Prepare a quote manually, from a kit, or with an AI draft.
4. Send the public link. Cord records the first view and keeps document activity.
5. The client can chat, approve or reject, and choose a payment method when one is
   available.
6. Cord records an online payment or a manually marked payment.
7. The operation appears under **Invoices** and **Collections** according to its
   document, outstanding balance, and due date.
8. **Reports** and **Performance** aggregate those results without creating another
   copy of the operation.

## Where each task lives

| Section | Route | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `/app` | KPIs, pipeline, recent activity, and follow-up items. |
| Quotes | `/app/cotizaciones` | Create, search, filter, open, edit, duplicate, send, and print quotes. |
| Clients | `/app/clientes` | Directory, CSV import, commercial terms, tax data, credit, and statements. |
| Products | `/app/productos` | Catalog, prices, taxes, volume pricing, import, and export. |
| Kits | `/app/productos/kits` | Bundles of line items inserted together into a quote. |
| Collections | `/app/cobranza` | Receivables, overdue balances, aging, client exposure, and follow-up. |
| Collections agent | `/app/cobranza/agente` | Configure and supervise AI collections when included in the plan. |
| My money | `/app/cobros` | Payments, payouts, balances, refunds, and dispute access. |
| Invoices | `/app/facturas` | Issue, inspect, export, and follow up on fiscal or commercial documents. |
| Recurring invoices | `/app/facturas/recurrentes` | Schedule repeated issues; requires Pro or above. |
| Reports | `/app/informes` | Overview, commercial diagnosis, finance, cash flow, collections, clients, and products. |
| Performance | `/app/desempeno` | Results attributed to the person who created or duplicated each quote. |
| Settings | `/app/ajustes` | Account, business, branding, fiscal, payments, team, security, integrations, and plan. |

<Callout type="info">
Visible options depend on **permissions**, the **effective plan**, the **country of
operation**, and account setup. A page existing doesn't mean every member can change it.
</Callout>

## Quote states

| State | Operational meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | Editable draft that hasn't been sent. |
| `sent` | Sent; the public link is available to the client. |
| `viewed` | The client opened the quote for the first time. |
| `approved` | The client recorded approval. A balance may remain outstanding. |
| `rejected` | The client rejected the proposal. |
| `expired` | Its validity period ended without closing. |
| `paid` | A non-recurring flow is paid according to its recorded charges. |
| `invoiced` | An invoice document is associated with the operation. |

A recurring quote doesn't become permanently `paid`: each installment creates its
own payment and the subscription keeps a separate status.

## Active organization and test mode

Cord is multi-company. Every client, product, quote, invoice, member, and setting
belongs to the **active organization**. If you can access more than one, verify the
organization switcher before importing, issuing, or configuring anything.

**Test mode** uses a separate sandbox organization. Its data doesn't mix with
production, and its keys, webhooks, and operations are test assets. Records created
inside the sandbox won't appear after returning to live mode.

## Permissions that change the experience

The owner always has full access. Other members receive presets or section-level
permissions for quotes, approvals, collections, clients, products, analytics,
settings, payment configuration, refunds, and team administration.

If an action is missing, first check the member's permission, then the plan and the
setup required by that capability.

## What changes by country

- Signup is available in 12 markets: Mexico, the United States, Canada, Brazil,
  Spain, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru.
- Online payments are available in the first eight. The last four can quote, issue
  commercial invoices, and manage collections, but can't activate Cord Payments yet.
- Dynamic SPEI is Mexico-only.
- Mexico can issue CFDI 4.0 with a valid CSD.
- Outside Mexico, Cord issues a commercial invoice. Spain uses the Verifactu rail
  only when both the account and Cord's regulatory integration are enabled.

## Before treating something as an error

Check these in order:

1. active organization and test mode;
2. member permission;
3. plan limit or capability;
4. operating country and compatible method;
5. required client, issuer, or document fields;
6. previous quote, invoice, or payment state.

Then open the guide for that section. The [Support Center](https://cordhq.app/en/support)
handles specific incidents; these guides explain the complete operating model.
