  <h1 class="page-title">Cord Payments Overview</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">The collection engine of your business. Card, SPEI, Mercado Pago, or manual transfer, with the money in your own account.</p>
</header>

**Cord Payments** is the system you use to accept card, SPEI (Mexico and pesos only), or manual-transfer payments directly from a quote. The money lands in your own payments account and from there it is paid out to your bank account at the frequency you choose in **Settings › Payments**: daily, weekly, monthly, or manual.

On payments in Mexican pesos (MXN), Cord charges a per-transaction fee that you see and accept before you start collecting. On payments in any other currency, Cord applies no platform fee today. See the [full fee schedule](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones).

In Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru you can also collect with [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago), using your own Mercado Pago account.

## Goodbye manual reconciliations

In many businesses, receiving a payment means: asking the client to email the transfer receipt, sales forwarding it to finance, and finance searching for the exact deposit in the bank portal the next day to cross-reference it against the quote.

**Cord Payments eliminates this process for online payments.** Every card or SPEI payment is tied to its quote and changes its status as soon as it is confirmed, with no receipts to cross-check by hand.

## Direct charges to your own account

Cord Payments uses **direct charges**: when your customer pays, the payment is recorded in your own payments account, in your business's name — it never passes through a Cord account. From there, the money is paid out to your bank account at the payout frequency you choose. Cord isn't a middleman for the money — it's the software layer that orchestrates the transaction, verifies your identity, and calculates the fee — in Mexico, it also issues the CFDI for that fee (see [Fiscal invoicing by country](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion)).

<Callout type="info">
This also means you're the merchant of record for every payment — not Cord. You're responsible to your customer for the goods or services sold, and to the bank for any chargeback. See [Fees and advantages](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones) for the details.
</Callout>
