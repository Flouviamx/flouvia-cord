## Goodbye manual reconciliations

In traditional B2B, receiving a payment means: asking the client to email the transfer receipt, sales forwarding it to finance, and finance searching for the exact deposit in the bank portal the next day to cross-reference it against the quote.

**Cord Payments completely eliminates this process.** Our payment infrastructure is natively designed for B2B, automating reconciliation and sending the money straight to your bank account.

## A "Direct-to-Bank" architecture

Unlike generic payment gateways that hold your money and then do periodic "payouts", Cord Payments uses **direct charges**: when your customer pays, the money travels straight to your own connected bank account — it never passes through a Cord account. Cord isn't a middleman for the money — it's the software layer that orchestrates the transaction, verifies your identity, calculates the fee, and issues the CFDI for that fee.

<Callout type="info">
This also means you're the merchant of record for every payment — not Cord. You're responsible to your customer for the goods or services sold, and to the bank for any chargeback. See [Fees and advantages](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones) for the details.
</Callout>
