## Where to find invoices

Open **Revenue › Invoices**. The inbox shows the invoice number, client, creation date, due date, total, balance, and operational status.

Drafts do not have an official number yet. Cord labels them **No number** and adds a temporary `BOR-...` reference so you can recognize them. The sequential number is reserved only when you issue the invoice. Opening a draft from the inbox takes you straight to the editor.

## Create an invoice

**New invoice** opens the same editor as quotes (see [Quote creation
overview](/en/docs/cotizacion/resumen)), with invoice wording and its own step 3.

<Steps>
1. **Build with AI (optional).** The **Build the invoice with AI** block takes the order as text, a photo, or a PDF and fills the line items from your catalog (see [Cord AI](/en/docs/cotizacion/ia)).
2. **Who are you invoicing?** Search the client by name, contact, email, or tax ID, or choose **+ Create new client…**. Cord reads their email, payment terms, and tax details from their record. Terms are chosen with chips (Due on receipt, Net 15, Net 30, Net 60, or **Other term**: Net 7, Net 45, or Net 90) and recalculate the due date.
3. **What are you charging for?** Add catalog products, kits, or a free line. Each item carries its quantity, its price, and its own tax rate. You can duplicate, reorder, and remove lines with **Undo**, just like in a quote (see [Products and discounts](/en/docs/cotizacion/productos)).
4. **Terms.** Set the **Due date** (a date; changing the payment terms recalculates it, and you can pick another by hand), the **Document type**, and the **Invoice currency**. The document type offers **Pro forma** in Mexico and Spain or **Commercial invoice** in the other markets, plus **CFDI 4.0** (Mexico) or **VERI\*FACTU** (Spain) when your plan and tax setup allow it (see [Fiscal invoicing by country](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion)). If the currency differs from your business's, the exchange-rate panel appears: "The rate is stated on the invoice."
5. **Note for your client** (optional). It's shown on the PDF and on the invoice page.
6. **Review and issue.** The summary shows subtotal, taxes per rate, withholdings, the **Total** and, below it, the terms and due date (for example "Net 30 · due Nov 7"). The **Delivery** block shows the email it will go to: "The email includes the PDF and the payment link." Click **Preview** to see the approximate sheet your client will receive.
7. **Issue.** **Issue and send** assigns the number and emails the invoice in the same flow; **Issue without sending** issues it and leaves delivery for later; **Save and exit** saves the draft without issuing. Before issuing, Cord opens a **Final review** ("Issue this invoice?") with the client, total, due date, and delivery; confirm with **Issue and send** or **Issue invoice**, or go back with **Go back**.
</Steps>

Each line item is rounded on its own and the total is the sum of the already-rounded
lines, so the invoice total matches the sum of its rows.

### Draft saving and shortcuts

- **A draft that's already saved saves itself** a few seconds after your last change (the summary says "Saved at …"). A new invoice is created as a draft the first time you click **Save and exit** or issue; until then, your browser keeps a local copy that Cord offers to **Restore** if you close without saving (it expires after 7 days).
- On desktop, **⌘ Enter** (Ctrl Enter) opens the final review to issue and send, **⌘ S** (Ctrl S) saves the draft and exits, and **/** jumps to the catalog search. On a phone, a bar pinned to the bottom shows the **Total** and **Issue and send**.
- **Issue and send** needs the client to have an email. If they don't, Cord tells you ("This client has no email. Add one to their record or choose "Issue without sending".").
- If an issuance failed with certainty, **Fix and retry** on the detail opens the same editor as **Fix** with the reserved number: you correct it and issue again keeping that number.

<Callout type="warning">
Issuing is irreversible: it assigns the official number and locks the fiscal document. To correct an issued invoice, use a void or credit note according to its payment state.
</Callout>

## What your client receives

The email includes the PDF and the public invoice link. From that page, the client can review the balance and payment history; if your payments account is enabled, they can also pay by card, and with [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago) if you connected it. They can pay the full balance or choose **Pay another amount** to pay part of it.

The public page presents your business brand, invoice number, balance, parties, line items, and downloads in one view. If the document was created in test mode or without fiscal issuance, Cord displays a clear warning and disables online payment.

If issuance succeeds but email delivery fails, Cord keeps the issued invoice and marks only delivery as pending. Open the invoice detail and use **Send invoice** again without generating another number.

## Inbox statuses

- **Draft**: editable and without an official number.
- **Open**: issued with a remaining balance.
- **Overdue**: open and past its due date.
- **Paid**: fully settled.
- **Issue error**: the fiscal provider did not confirm issuance; open the detail and use **Retry issuing**.
- **Void** or **Uncollectible**: no longer in the normal collection flow.

The primary badge always represents financial status. Cord shows delivery and
environment context separately underneath it, for example **Sent · Test**. This
keeps **Test** from replacing or contradicting states such as **Open** or **Paid**.

## Actions from the invoice detail

You can resend the email, record manual or partial payments, copy the public link, download PDF and XML when available, void, create a credit note, use **Duplicate invoice** to open a new copy in the editor, or start a monthly recurrence with **Repeat monthly**.

The progress bar separates five moments: **Draft**, **Issued**, **Sent**, **Viewed**, and **Paid**. The action column provides direct access to open or copy the client link, download the PDF, and share through WhatsApp. Exceptional actions, such as voiding or marking an invoice uncollectible, stay under **More actions**.

After the first delivery, the inbox shows **Sent** and removes that invoice from bulk selection. Resending remains available in the invoice detail next to the recipient's email, which helps prevent accidental duplicate delivery.

<Callout type="warning">
**Test document** means there was no valid fiscal issuance. In Mexico, it is not a CFDI before the SAT. Configure fiscal issuance and create a new invoice; Cord preserves the previous document without rewriting its history.
</Callout>

The **Activity** section records issuance, delivery, views, reminders, payments, and status changes, so you can understand what happened without relying on the seller's inbox.

## Bulk delivery and export

Select issued invoices that have not been sent yet to deliver them in a batch. Drafts, previously sent invoices, and non-deliverable documents keep a visible but disabled checkbox; hovering it explains why the invoice is excluded from that delivery. **Export CSV** downloads the current view and respects the active status filter and search.

To repeat issuance, open **View recurring** and see [Recurring invoices](/en/docs/pagos/facturas-recurrentes).
To review the available document —CFDI 4.0 in Mexico, a commercial invoice in other markets, and Verifactu's operating conditions in Spain—, see [Fiscal invoicing by country](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion).

## Payments, credits and refunds are different

Cord keeps payments, issued credit notes and confirmed refunds separate. This
helps prevent counting the same movement twice and explains balance changes.

**Amount due = total − issued credits − recorded payments + confirmed refunds**,
with a minimum of zero. A draft credit note does not reduce the balance. A pending
or failed refund does not count as money returned.

| Example in MXN | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1,000 invoice; 400 payment | 600 remains due |
| 1,000 invoice; 200 issued credit; 800 payment | Zero balance |
| Paid 1,000 invoice; 200 issued credit | Zero balance and 200 to return |
| Same case, with a confirmed 200 refund | Zero balance and nothing to return |
| Paid 1,000 invoice; 200 refund without a credit | 200 becomes due again |

The last case requires reviewing the agreement with the customer. Returning money
does not itself reduce the document amount. Issuing a credit does not send money.

## Partial payments and confirmation

After a partial payment is applied, a new payment is prepared for the current
balance. An attempt still in progress must be resolved before opening another.
A repeated notification for the same payment must not add the amount again.

After returning from payment, use **Refresh balance**. Returning to the link is
not confirmation: status depends on the payment record. If the bank shows a charge
but the balance has not changed, contact the business before paying again.

## When the balance does not match

Open the invoice detail and compare payments, issued credits, confirmed refunds
and activity. Check currency and the relevant document. If a movement is pending,
wait for confirmation or request a review; do not add another manual payment to
force the balance to zero.

Cord does not silently recalculate older documents. If an older invoice does not
match, review it individually.

Commercial documents are included in Free (10 per month); integrated fiscal issuance starts at Starter where enabled. The commercial option in Mexico and Spain is a pro forma, not a test invoice. It has no fiscal XML and does not replace a tax invoice.
