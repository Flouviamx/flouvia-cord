<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Issued invoices</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">All your CFDI together — folio, client, amount, date, status, and their files — without opening each quote one by one.</p>
</header>

## Where to find it

**Settings › Invoicing › Issued invoices.** It's the second tab, next to "Tax details" (where you upload your CSD).

## What each row shows

- **Folio** — links straight to that quote's detail page.
- **Client** and **Total**.
- **Date** it was stamped.
- **Status**: Stamped, Pending, Error, or Cancelled.
- **PDF** and **XML** of the CFDI, ready to download or forward.

<Callout type="info">
An invoice only shows up here after you stamp it from the quote's detail page (the **"Stamp CFDI 4.0"** button). This isn't a list of quotes — it's exclusively the record of tax documents already issued.
</Callout>

## Context badges

If an invoice was generated in a test environment, the row marks it explicitly (so it's never confused with a real CFDI filed with the SAT). See [Test Environments](/docs/desarrolladores/empezar/pruebas) for more on how Cord separates test data from real data.
