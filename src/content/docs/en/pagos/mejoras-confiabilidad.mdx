## Partial invoice payments

On an invoice link, your customer can pay the full balance or choose **Pay another
amount** and type how much they want to pay. Cord caps that amount at the invoice's
real balance. After a partial payment, the next payment is prepared for the current
balance, not the original total, and a repeated notice for the same payment is not
added twice.

## Credit notes and the invoice balance

From the detail of an issued invoice you can issue a credit note. In Mexico it is
stamped as an expense CFDI related to the original invoice's fiscal folio.

Cord keeps payments, issued credit notes, and confirmed refunds separate, and
calculates the balance as: **total − issued credits − recorded payments + confirmed
refunds**, with a minimum of zero. Details and examples are in
[Create, issue, and collect invoices](/en/docs/pagos/facturas-emitidas).

## Downloads from the invoice link

The invoice's public page includes the PDF download and, for invoices stamped in
Mexico, the XML. Your customer no longer needs to ask you for them by email.

## Commercial invoices on the Free plan

The Free plan includes **10 commercial invoices per month**, separately from quote
sends. From Starter they are unlimited. Fiscal issuance (CFDI in Mexico) starts on
Starter, with its own monthly allowance.

## Prices in euros

In Spain, Germany, and France, the Cord subscription is purchased at fixed prices in
euros (EUR) for the self-service plans. If you already have a contract in another
currency, Cord keeps it. Developer has no self-service euro price; it is purchased
through sales. See [Subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).

## Mercado Pago as a second payment rail

You can connect your own Mercado Pago account in **Settings › Payments**. Your
customer sees it on the quote's payment page and on the invoice link, including
partial payments. Refunds you issue from Mercado Pago are reflected in the balance
automatically. See [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago).

## Safe switching between SPEI and card

If your customer starts paying with one method and switches to another, Cord first
checks the previous attempt. If it has already received money or has a payment in
progress, it isn't replaced and the customer is asked to wait for confirmation. See
[Payment methods](/en/docs/pagos/metodos).

## Payout frequency

In **Settings › Payments** you choose how often your money goes out from Cord Payments
to your bank account: daily, weekly (choosing the day), monthly (from the 1st to the
28th), or manual. If your account's verification has pending requirements, that same
screen tells you payouts are paused.

## Refunds with a fee notice

When you refund a payment from **My Payments**, the dialog asks you to confirm that
you understand Cord's platform fee is not refunded automatically. See
[Refunds](/en/docs/pagos/reembolsos).
