<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Invoicing by country</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">One invoicing engine, a different document depending on where your business operates and which plan you have.</p>
</header>

## What it is

Cord doesn't treat an invoice as a generic PDF with a number. The **document type** issued depends on your business country, your plan, and the tax setup you have ready. Once issued, a document keeps its type even if you later change plan or setup: a proforma doesn't become a CFDI or vice versa.

For the full life of an invoice (draft, issue, delivery, payment, credit notes, and voiding), see [Create, issue, and collect invoices](/en/docs/pagos/facturas-emitidas).

## Which document is issued

| Country | Free plan | Starter or higher |
|---|---|---|
| **Mexico** | Proforma (number with the `PRO` series) | **CFDI 4.0** stamped with the SAT, with your CSD uploaded. You can also choose a proforma. |
| **Spain** | Proforma (`PRO` series) | Proforma today. Spanish tax registration will be enabled later; see [Spain](#spain-verifactu). |
| **Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, and Peru** | Commercial invoice | Commercial invoice. When issuing with the country's authority is active on your account, the authorized e-invoice (new feature; see [Other markets](#other-markets-commercial-invoice)); the commercial option then becomes a proforma (`PRO` series). |
| **United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, and France** | Commercial invoice | Commercial invoice |

- **Proforma:** a commercial document that explicitly states it doesn't replace a tax invoice. It's for collecting and for your customer to process payment, not for tax deductions.
- **Commercial invoice:** an invoice with a sequential number, per-line taxes and withholdings, and both parties' tax details. Cord doesn't file it with any authority.
- **CFDI 4.0:** Mexico's digital tax receipt, stamped with the SAT using your business's certificate.
- **Authorized invoice:** in Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, and Peru, the e-invoice authorized by the country's tax authority, with its legal number.

In all three cases, tax is calculated **per line**: you can mix a taxed item with an exempt one, and each prints your country's tax name (IVA in Mexico and Spain, VAT in the United Kingdom, GST in Canada, Sales tax per state in the United States).

In the United States, from the Starter plan, you can turn on sales tax **by the client's address** in **Settings › Taxes**: each line takes the combined state, county, city and special district rate for that address, and the document prints one row per jurisdiction. It needs your full business address, the states where you collect, and your payments account set up. In a state where you don't collect, the line carries 0 % and the document says so; mark an exempt client in its profile with its certificate. If something is missing (for example, the client's ZIP) or the calculation isn't available, the document isn't saved and tells you what's missing: it never uses an estimated rate. Each plan includes a number of invoices with automatic sales tax per month; see [Your Cord subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).

<Callout type="info" title="Test documents">
If a document shows as a **Test document**, no tax provider issued it: it has no validity with the authority, the link warns your customer, and it can't be paid online. A test issue doesn't become valid by changing the setup.
</Callout>

## Quotas per plan

Invoices have their own monthly quotas, separate from quote sends:

| Plan | Commercial invoices and proformas per month | Tax documents (CFDI) included per month |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 10 | No tax issuing |
| Starter | Unlimited | 30 |
| Pro | Unlimited | 200 |
| Scale | Unlimited | 500 |
| Developer | Unlimited | 1,000 |

- Beyond the included tax quota, from Starter up, additional documents are billed as usage overage on your subscription. See [Subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).
- In Mexico, each **payment complement** Cord stamps counts as one more tax document.
- On Free, after 10 commercial documents in a month, issuing stops until the next month or until you change plans.
- Drafts and test documents don't consume quota.
- Running out of quota or downgrading does **not** block downloading, sending, or collecting documents already issued.

Subscribing to a plan doesn't turn on tax issuing by itself: in Mexico you also need your CSD uploaded.

## Mexico: CFDI 4.0

With Starter or higher, Cord stamps with the SAT through a certified provider, **under your business's RFC and Digital Seal Certificate (CSD)**. You upload the CSD once in **Settings › Tax details** and never attach it again. See [Digital Seal Certificate](/en/docs/cuenta/csd).

### Recipient details

Cord takes the recipient's tax details from the customer record:

| Item | Source | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| RFC | Customer record | Issued with the generic RFC XAXX010101000, in the customer's name and with your business's tax postal code. The name "PUBLICO EN GENERAL" is reserved for the global invoice. |
| Tax regime | Customer record | 601 (General corporate regime) if the customer has an RFC; 616 (No tax obligations) whenever it is issued with the generic RFC. |
| CFDI use | Customer record, or your account's default use | G03 (General expenses). |
| Tax postal code | Customer record | There's no value that can safely replace it: CFDI 4.0 requires the postal code on the recipient's tax certificate, and stamping may be rejected. |

The defaults may not match your customer's tax certificate (constancia de situación fiscal). Cord doesn't invent them from other data: complete them in the record before issuing so the CFDI is useful to whoever receives it.

### Customers abroad

If the customer's country in their record isn't Mexico, the CFDI is issued to a **foreign resident**: with the SAT generic foreign RFC (XEXX010101000), their **tax residence** (the country in the record), use **S01** and, if you entered their tax ID (EIN, NIF, VAT…), that number as the **foreign tax registration number**. The tax regime and CFDI use in the record don't apply to a foreign customer and aren't sent. The VAT rate on each item is your call with your accountant; for example, exported services usually go at 0%.

### SAT keys for each item

Each product can carry its **product or service key** (c_ClaveProdServ, 8 digits) and its **unit key** (c_ClaveUnidad) under **Products › E-invoicing (SAT)**. You search the product key by description in the SAT catalog; if you leave the unit key empty, it's derived from the product's unit ("hour" → HUR, "month" → MON, "kg" → KGM). In the quote and invoice editors, each item can carry **its own keys**, which win over the product's; duplicating a document keeps them. Items with no keys of their own and no classified product are stamped with the SAT generic keys: 01010101 and H87, and the editor tells you how many are left that way before issuing. Keys are read when stamping and saved in the document.

### Taxes and issuing

Cord sends each item's taxes as you entered them: 16% IVA, 8% IVA, or exempt, plus IVA and ISR withholdings. It doesn't collapse them into a single rate. If the quote is in a currency different from your accounting currency, the CFDI declares the exchange rate locked at quoting time. A [document discount](/en/docs/cotizacion/descuentos) travels spread per item in the **Descuento** attribute.

In the invoice editor, the **CFDI details** section lets you set that invoice's **CFDI use** (by default, **From the client's record**) and **Payment method** (by default, **Automatic from the payments**). If you replace the invoice, the replacement inherits them.

A confirmed payment and an issued invoice are different states. Paying doesn't create, stamp, or send an invoice by itself; check each document's tax and delivery status. If stamping succeeds and the email fails, the number is kept and you can resend from the detail page.

Credit notes for a Mexican invoice are an expense CFDI (egreso) related to the original UUID. A cancellation request doesn't void the CFDI until the SAT confirms it; meanwhile you can click **Check cancellation status** on the detail page.

### Payment method: PUE or PPD

<Callout type="info">
This feature is new and may not be enabled on your account yet. If you don't see it, write to us.
</Callout>

When stamping, Cord sets the CFDI's **payment method** from what actually happened with
the money:

| Situation when stamping | Method | Payment form |
|---|---|---|
| The invoice is already **fully paid** (on its link, on the source quote, or with payments you recorded) | **PUE**, single payment | The payment's: 04 card, 03 transfer (including SPEI), 01 cash, 02 check. If you set one under **CFDI details**, that one. |
| The invoice will be collected **later**, in full or in installments (for example, Net 30) | **PPD**, installments or deferred | 99, to be defined |
| The recipient is the **general public** (generic RFC XAXX010101000) | Always **PUE**: that recipient doesn't accept payment complements | The payment's, or 03 if there's none yet |

Cord used to stamp everything as PUE even when the invoice was on credit. Not anymore: an
invoice that isn't paid when issued goes out as PPD, as the SAT requires.

### Automatic payment complement

For each payment a **PPD** invoice receives, the SAT requires a **payment receipt
complement** (type P CFDI, also called REP) no later than the 5th of the following month.
Cord stamps it **by itself**, as soon as the payment is recorded:

- **However the payment is recorded:** a card, SPEI, or Mercado Pago payment on the
  invoice link, a payment from the [client portal](/en/docs/pagos/portal-facturas) or
  [automatic payments](/en/docs/pagos/cobro-automatico), a payment you record by hand, or
  the source quote's payments applied to the invoice.
- **One complement per payment.** Each payment gets its own, related to the invoice's UUID
  and with its installment number and previous balance. A retry never stamps the same
  payment twice.
- **Complement payment form:** the payment's (04 card, 03 transfer, 01 cash, 02 check);
  if it can't be known, 03.
- **It uses your tax quota:** each complement counts as one tax document for the month.
- **It's recorded in the invoice history** (in Spanish): "Complemento de pago emitido"
  with its tax folio (UUID). If it couldn't be stamped, the history says "Complemento de
  pago pendiente" with the reason.

Cord does **not** issue it by itself, and says so in the invoice history, in two cases the
SAT documents differently:

| Case | What to do |
|---|---|
| The payment is in **a currency other than MXN**. | The complement needs the official exchange rate for the payment date, which Cord doesn't have. Issue it with your accountant. |
| The payment is **earlier than the invoice** (an advance). | It's documented with the SAT's advance-payment procedure, not with a payment complement. |

<Callout type="warning">
Today Cord doesn't send the complement to your client or offer it for download from the
invoice detail, and a complement that's left pending isn't retried on its own. If your
client needs a complement's XML or PDF, or one is pending, write to us.
</Callout>

### Cancellation and replacement

When you void an issued CFDI, Cord asks for the **SAT reason**: 02 (with errors, without a replacement), 03 (the transaction did not take place) or 04 (named transaction in a global invoice, only on the global invoice). Reason 01 is used through **Replace CFDI**: Cord creates a draft with the original's data, relates it to the original when you issue it (relationship 04), moves the original's payments to it and requests the original's cancellation under reason 01 with the replacement's UUID. An invoice with valid credit notes or issued payment complements can't be replaced: the SAT doesn't allow cancelling a CFDI with valid related documents.

### Global invoice

Sales collected without the customer asking for an invoice are documented under **Invoices › Global invoice**: you choose the frequency, month and year, Cord lists the quotes collected in the period with no invoice of their own and no other global invoice, and issues a CFDI to PUBLICO EN GENERAL (XAXX010101000, regime 616, use S01, PUE) with one item per sale (key 01010101, unit ACT and the quote number as identification number). A sale in a valid global invoice can't be invoiced separately; if the customer asks for their invoice, the global invoice is cancelled under reason 04 and issued again without that sale.

### What Cord doesn't do in Mexico today

- **Payment complement for a payment in another currency or an advance.** See [Automatic payment complement](#automatic-payment-complement).
- **Foreign trade complement (Comercio Exterior).** Definitive export of goods (key A1) requires it; Cord stamps the operation as "not applicable" for export and doesn't issue that complement.

## Spain: Verifactu

Verifactu is the invoicing system required by Spanish law: each invoice generates a record chained to the previous one and sent to the Spanish Tax Agency.

### What Cord does today

- **It issues proformas.** While Cord completes its enablement as an invoicing system with the Tax Agency, accounts in Spain issue **proformas** with the `PRO` series, even with a paid plan and a certificate uploaded.
- **It doesn't register or send anything to the Tax Agency.** A proforma has no fingerprint or verification QR, and its PDF states it doesn't replace a tax invoice.
- **It doesn't fake tax issuing.** If you explicitly request a tax invoice, Cord replies that integrated tax issuing isn't enabled yet for your account and country, instead of issuing a document that pretends to have a record.

Meanwhile, if your business must issue registered invoices, use your current invoicing system for the tax document and Cord to quote, collect, and follow up.

### What you can prepare now

In **Settings › Tax profile** (Spain accounts only) you can upload your **electronic certificate** (`.p12` or `.pfx`) with its password. Cord validates the password, reads the expiry date, and stores the certificate encrypted. Also make sure your NIF is in your tax details.

### What will change once enabled

When enablement is complete, on accounts with Starter or higher and a valid certificate:

- Each invoice will generate a **chained record** with a digital fingerprint that links it to your business's previous invoice.
- The PDF will include the Tax Agency's **verification QR code**, the **VERI\*FACTU** legend, and the fingerprint.
- The record will be sent to the Tax Agency after issuing. Issuing doesn't wait for that response: check the submission status on the document.
- A registered invoice isn't edited: it's corrected with a new correction or cancellation record.

We'll announce it in [what's new in payments and invoicing](/en/docs/pagos/mejoras-confiabilidad).

## Other markets: commercial invoice

In the United States, Canada, Brazil, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru, Cord issues a **commercial invoice** with a sequential number per organization, per-line taxes and withholdings, and both parties' tax details. Cord doesn't file it with the local tax authority. Whether you must issue, report, or complement that document depends on your country and regime; confirm with your tax advisor.

<Callout type="info">
This feature is new and may not be enabled on your account yet. If you don't see it, write to us.
</Callout>

- **Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, and Peru.** Cord can issue the e-invoice
  **authorized by each country's tax authority** (ARCA in Argentina; NFS-e and NF-e in
  Brazil; SII in Chile; DIAN in Colombia; SUNAT in Peru), with Starter or higher and your
  business's credentials in **Settings › Tax profile**. When issuing is active on your
  account, the invoice carries the legal number the authority assigns and the commercial
  option goes out as a proforma. Until then, you keep issuing commercial invoices.
- **Germany, France, and Spain.** Each invoice can also be downloaded as a European
  e-invoice (Factur-X, XRechnung, Peppol, and, in Spain, Facturae). In France, issuing
  through an approved platform is built and coming soon. See [European
  e-invoicing](/en/docs/pagos/factura-electronica).

## The tax receipt for Cord's fee (Mexico)

Besides the CFDIs you issue to your customers, in Mexico Cord issues its **own income CFDI** for the fee it charges on your MXN Cord Payments payments (see [Fees](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones)). It's the receipt for the processing service Cord provides you, not for your sale.

- **It's monthly and consolidated:** one CFDI per month with the sum of that month's fees, not one per payment.
- **Cord reviews it before stamping:** at the start of each month the previous month's draft is prepared and recipient, base, IVA, and total are checked before issuing.
- **It's issued to your tax details:** RFC, tax regime, and postal code from **Settings › Tax details**, with your account's CFDI use or G03 if there's none, and the PUE payment method.
- **Mexico only.** Outside Mexico, the payment receipt is your document.

The CFDI for your Cord **subscription** is requested separately, from your subscription billing, for each payment already settled. See [Subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).

<Callout type="warning">
If your RFC, tax regime, or postal code aren't complete in **Settings › Tax details**, the fee CFDI can't be issued. If you didn't receive it or need a month's XML and PDF, write to us.
</Callout>

## Limits

- CFDI: Mexico only, from Starter, with a CSD uploaded.
- Spain: proformas only today.
- Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, and Peru: authorized invoice only when its issuing is active on your account; otherwise, commercial invoice.
- Other markets: commercial invoice not filed with the authority.
- Free: 10 commercial documents per month and no tax issuing.
- Mexico: the payment complement is automatic for MXN payments after the invoice; payments in another currency and advances are documented outside Cord.

## Common problems

**My invoice came out as a proforma and I expected a CFDI.** Check your plan (Starter or higher), that your CSD is uploaded and valid, and that you didn't choose the commercial option when issuing.

**My CFDI came out PPD and I expected PUE.** When it was stamped, the invoice wasn't fully paid. Cord will issue a payment complement for each payment it receives. If the invoice was already paid, check that the payment was recorded before issuing it.

**My customer says their CFDI is unusable.** Check their tax regime, postal code, and CFDI use in their record; if they were missing, Cord used defaults.

**I'm in Spain and uploaded my certificate, but I still issue proformas.** That's expected while Cord completes its enablement with the Tax Agency.

**I hit the document limit on Free.** Issuing resumes next month or when you change plans. Your issued documents remain available.
