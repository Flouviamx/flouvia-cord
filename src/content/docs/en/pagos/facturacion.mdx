## Global Fiscal Abstraction

Cord's payments system is designed to scale internationally thanks to the **FiscalFactory** design pattern.

Depending on the country of your client and your own business, the fiscal engine decides which stamping provider to use and what type of receipt to generate (for example, *US Commercial Invoices* for transactions in the United States).

## Issuing in Mexico: Nominative CFDI 4.0

For users in Mexico, Cord uses the **Facturapi** API (`MexicoSatProvider`) under the hood to achieve 100% compliance with SAT requirements, completely invisibly and automatically.

### Extracting data from your Catalog

Unlike other systems that issue generic invoices to the "General Public" and then force the client to log into a self-invoicing portal, Cord is proactive.

Cord automatically extracts the information from the individual client's profile (CRM):

- **Tax Regime:** Extracted directly from the SAT Catalog assigned to that client.
- **CFDI Use:** Determined based on the type of service or product, or explicitly defined in the client's profile.
- **Zip Code (C.P.):** Validated against the tax status certificate registered on their record.

### Automatic and Transparent Stamping

1. As soon as the payment is confirmed (by Card or dynamic SPEI through Cord Payments), the system notifies Cord's server.
2. The system assembles the payload with the quote's line items and the client's exact fiscal data (Regime, Zip Code, CFDI Use).
3. The `FiscalFactory` is invoked and stamps the document with the SAT (nominative CFDI 4.0).
4. The final XML and PDF files are sent directly to the client's email along with their payment receipt, closing the commercial cycle without any manual intervention from your team.

## The CFDI for Cord Payments' fee

In addition to the CFDI you stamp for your own customers, Cord issues its **own income CFDI** for the fee it charges on every Cord Payments transaction (see [Fees and advantages](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones)) — this is the tax receipt for the payment-processing service Cord provides you, not for your own sale.

This receipt is issued **consolidated, in periodic batches** (not one per individual transaction) against the RFC and fiscal data registered on your account.

<Callout type="warning">
If your RFC, tax regime, or zip code aren't up to date on your profile, Cord's commission CFDI cannot be issued. Keep that information current in **Settings › Fiscal**.
</Callout>
