## Where to refund

Refunds live in **My Payments** (`/app/cobros`), not in the quote's detail page. In the "Recent payments" widget, every row that corresponds to a payment **reconciled through Cord Payments** has a **Refund** button. A payment recorded manually (a transfer outside Cord Payments) doesn't have this button — for that case, you handle the refund directly with your customer outside Cord.

The button appears on **quote** payments, including the monthly charges of a [retainer](/en/docs/pagos/igualas). A payment made from an **invoice** link, from the [client portal](/en/docs/pagos/portal-facturas), or by [automatic payments](/en/docs/pagos/cobro-automatico) doesn't have a refund button in Cord today; if you need to return it, contact us. Once a refund of that payment is confirmed, Cord reflects it in the balance of each invoice it paid (see [Effect on a linked invoice](#effect-on-a-linked-invoice) and [Refunding a charge that paid several invoices](#refunding-a-charge-that-paid-several-invoices)). A payment collected through Mercado Pago is refunded from your Mercado Pago account (see [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago)).

<Callout type="warning">
Refunding requires the **Refunds** permission. This permission is **not granted automatically** to team administrators — the account owner has to explicitly grant it to each member who needs to issue refunds, from Settings › Team. This is intentional: moving money out of the account is a more sensitive action than administering it.
</Callout>

## How it works

1. Click **Refund** on the payment's row.
2. A dialog opens with the amount available to refund (it may be less than the total, if you already issued a partial refund before) and an optional internal reason field.
3. Enter the amount — the full total or just a portion.
4. Tick the box confirming you understand that the refund returns the stated amount to the customer but **does not automatically refund Cord's platform fee**. You can't confirm without it.
5. Since this moves money, Cord asks you to **re-confirm your identity** (your password or your 2FA code) before executing the refund, even if your session was already active. It's the same "re-authentication" mechanism that protects other sensitive account actions.
6. The refund is logged in your organization's audit trail, with the amount, the reason (if given), and who authorized it.

You can refund the same payment **more than once**, as long as the accumulated total doesn't exceed what was originally charged — the dialog always shows you how much is still available.

## Card vs. SPEI: two different flows

### Card

The refund is processed **automatically** as soon as you confirm it — no manual step required on your part. The money returns to the customer's original card on whatever timeline their bank handles.

### SPEI

In Cord, the SPEI refund flow requires a manual transfer by the business. Requesting it in the dashboard does not itself return the money:

1. Gets recorded in Cord as "pending, manual refund".
2. Cord automatically creates a task on your to-do list reminding you to transfer the amount back to the customer.
3. You make the transfer yourself through your normal bank account, and mark the task as resolved.

## What happens to your money

- The refunded amount is deducted from your net revenue across all of [My Payments](/en/docs/pagos/dashboard)' reports — it never stays "hidden" in the total collected figure.
- **The platform fee Cord charged on the original payment is not refunded automatically**, and the payment processor's fees are governed by its own terms. It's a processing cost that already occurred when the card was charged or the SPEI transfer was received — refunding the customer doesn't reverse that cost. See the details in [Fees and advantages](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones).
- If the refunded payment was part of a quote with an [upfront deposit and balance](/en/docs/pagos/anticipos), the refund only affects the specific payment you chose — not the rest of that quote's slices.

<Callout type="info">
A refund **does not revert the quote's status** — it stays marked as paid. If you also need to cancel the quote itself (for example, because the entire order was called off), do that from the quote's detail page separately.
</Callout>

## Effect on a linked invoice

Cord distinguishes a requested refund from a confirmed one. Only confirmed refunds
count toward money returned on the invoice; a repeated event doesn't subtract it again.
A refund can arrive before the payment record and await that link.

A confirmed refund **reopens** that amount in the invoice balance: the invoice is no
longer paid for that amount. If the refund is for something that won't be collected
anymore (a returned product, a service that wasn't delivered), also issue a **credit
note** for that amount: a credit note reduces the document amount while a refund
returns money, and they're separate actions. Review [balance
calculation](/en/docs/pagos/facturas-emitidas).

## Refunding a charge that paid several invoices

<Callout type="info">
This feature is new and may not be enabled on your account yet. If you don't see it, write to us.
</Callout>

From the [client portal](/en/docs/pagos/portal-facturas) and with [automatic
payments](/en/docs/pagos/cobro-automatico), a single charge can pay several invoices. If
part of that charge is later refunded, Cord allocates the refund among the invoices it
paid:

1. **From the last one to the first.** The refund is assigned starting with the last
   invoice the charge was applied to, and works backwards.
2. **Up to what each invoice received.** Each invoice is assigned at most what it
   received from that charge, minus what other refunds of the same charge already
   assigned to it.
3. **All or nothing.** If the refund doesn't fit entirely in what the charge applied
   (for example, because the refund notice arrived before the payment notice), it isn't
   split halfway: it waits and is allocated as soon as the payment is recorded.

| Example | F-0231 | F-0236 | Charge total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Payment applied (in this order) | 48,940.00 MXN | 52,316.40 MXN | 101,256.40 MXN |
| Refund of 60,000.00 MXN | −7,683.60 MXN | −52,316.40 MXN | −60,000.00 MXN |
| Balance that reopens | 7,683.60 MXN | 52,316.40 MXN | |

- A charge that paid **a single** invoice assigns the whole refund to that invoice.
- Refunds of charges from before October 2026 have no allocation: they only count in the
  balance if their charge paid a single invoice.
- An **ACH** bank debit only supports **full** refunds.
