## What it is

In Brazil the document depends on **what you sell**, and the two are not mixed:

- **Services → national-standard NFS-e** (NFS-e de Padrão Nacional), generated by the national NFS-e system from a declaration (DPS) signed with your certificate.
- **Goods → NF-e model 55**, authorized by your state's **SEFAZ** before the goods leave, with its DANFE.

Cord talks directly to each authority, without an intermediary, using **your** ICP-Brasil certificate. An invoice that mixes products and services **is not issued**: Cord asks you to split it in two.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Starter or higher to issue NFS-e or NF-e. On Free, commercial invoice (10 per month).
- An **ICP-Brasil A1 digital certificate** for your CNPJ (e-CNPJ, `.pfx`/`.p12`), or the e-CPF if you issue as an individual. You buy it from an ICP-Brasil certification authority and pay its cost directly to them. A headquarters e-CNPJ signs for its branches (same root CNPJ), and the same certificate works for the NFS-e and the NF-e.
- **NFS-e:** your municipality must have joined the national issuer.
- **NF-e:** you must be **enabled (credenciado) as an NF-e issuer** with your state's SEFAZ and have your Inscrição Estadual.
- **Permissions:** **Settings** for the tax profile; **Quotes** to issue; **Collections** to void.

## Taxes

Brazil **starts without a national rate** on purpose: state ICMS, municipal ISS, and PIS/COFINS don't fit in a single rate, and suggesting one would be worse than letting you choose. The catalog only has **Isento**; each document's taxes are declared by its tax data (Settings and product), not by a line percentage.

<DmTaxCatalog lang="en" country="BR" />

The tax reform (CBS 0.9% and IBS 0.1% as a 2026 trial) is not seeded either: it is not charged to those who meet the ancillary obligations.

## NFS-e: services

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › Tax profile**, complete your **CNPJ** under **Invoice identity**.
2. In the **NFS-e** section, complete your establishment's **municipality** (seven-digit IBGE code), the municipal registration if your municipality requires it, your **Simples Nacional status** (not opted in, MEI, or ME/EPP; for ME/EPP, the assessment regime and the approximate tax percentage), the special municipal regime if you have one, and the **service** from the national list (LC 116/2003).
3. Choose a **DPS series** exclusive to Cord (1 to 49999) and, if you want, the first number.
4. If your clients withhold ISS from you, choose the **withholding profile** from **Settings › Taxes** that represents it.
5. Upload your **ICP-Brasil A1 certificate** with its password. Cord tests the connection with the national NFS-e system when you upload it, with no effects.

</Steps>

<DmRailSettings lang="en" rail="nfse" />

**How it is built.** Cord declares **one service per NFS-e**: all the invoice's line items under the configured code, and the description lists them with quantity and price. The service is provided in your municipality and **ISS is included in the price**: line items cannot add tax on top. Your municipality sets the ISS rate. The document discount travels as an unconditional discount.

**Not issued yet:** services taxed where they are provided or at the client, construction works and events, exports and foreign currency, base deductions, municipal benefits, immunity or non-incidence, federal withholdings (IRRF, CSLL, PIS/COFINS, INSS), intermediaries, and the reform group (IBS/CBS). Cord rejects them before sending, with the reason.

**Cancel.** **Void invoice** asks the national system to cancel the NFS-e. Outside the period or the amount your municipality allows, the cancellation is rejected with the reason and the invoice stays valid.

**No credit note.** The NFS-e has none: a credit note on an NFS-e is rejected and Cord asks you to void and issue again.

**Printing.** Cord's PDF accompanies the NFS-e: it prints its number, competence period, DPS, service, base, rate, and ISSQN, the full access key, and the public lookup QR code. The NFS-e XML is kept.

## NF-e: goods

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › Tax profile › NF-e**, complete the **tax regime** (Simples Nacional, MEI, or Regime Normal), your **Inscrição Estadual**, the establishment address with its **IBGE municipality** (your state and its SEFAZ come from it), and the CEP.
2. Under **Operation and taxes**, choose an **NF-e series** exclusive to Cord (0 to 889), the nature of the operation, how you sell, freight, and the default **PIS and COFINS CST**.
3. On each catalog product, complete the **NF-e (sale of goods)** section: NCM, CEST if applicable, CFOP, origin, unit, GTIN, your regime's ICMS (CSOSN in Simples, or rate and FCP in Regime Normal), IPI, and the IBS/CBS classification.
4. On each client, complete number, district, IBGE municipality, whether they are an **ICMS contributor** (with their IE), exempt, or a non-contributor, and whether they buy as an end consumer.
5. If you already uploaded your e-CNPJ for the NFS-e, the NF-e uses it. You can upload another one of the same CNPJ just for the NF-e. Cord checks your SEFAZ's service status when you upload it, with no effects.

</Steps>

<DmRailSettings lang="en" rail="nfe" />

**What it covers.** A sale in reais to a Brazilian recipient with a CNPJ or CPF: **Simples Nacional and MEI** within the state and to other states; **Regime Normal** within the state with its own ICMS, IPI, PIS/COFINS, and IBS/CBS (mandatory for Regime Normal since August 3, 2026). ICMS, PIS, COFINS, IBS, and CBS are included in the price; **the only tax added to the line is the product's IPI**, and the NF-e total equals Cord's, to the cent.

**Technical contact.** The SEFAZ of **AM, MS, PE, PR, SC, and TO** require the system developer's details. Cord does not have them yet: if your business is in one of those states, Settings tells you and the NF-e is not issued.

**Contingency.** If your state's SEFAZ does not respond, Cord authorizes your NF-e on the virtual contingency service assigned to your state and returns to your SEFAZ by itself when it is back. Settings shows the notice while it lasts.

**Cancellation (24 hours).** **Void invoice** cancels the NF-e within 24 hours of its authorization; the invoice panel says until when. After that, the SEFAZ rejects it and the invoice stays valid.

**Correction letter.** From the invoice panel you can issue up to 20 CC-e. Each letter replaces the previous one and must repeat everything still to be corrected. A CC-e **cannot** change amounts, quantities, rates, or bases, the issuer or recipient, or the issue or shipping date.

**Inutilização.** A number that Cord reserved and that never became an authorized NF-e leaves a gap in the series. Settings lists the gaps and whoever manages the tax details declares the range to the SEFAZ, with a reason. Cord never declares them by itself.

**DANFE and XML.** Cord prints the A4 DANFE with the access key as a barcode and keeps the authorized XML (nfeProc), which is downloaded from the invoice and its public link.

**Not issued yet:** tax substitution (ST), DIFAL to non-contributors in another state, exports, fuels, withholdings, Regime Normal to another state, returns of goods (another NF-e with purpose 4), and Simples Nacional from January 4, 2027, when its IBS/CBS group becomes mandatory without published rules yet.

## Status before the authority

<DmInvoiceAuthority lang="en" rail="nfe" />

Each invoice's side panel says where it stands with the authority:

| Status | NFS-e | NF-e |
|---|---|---|
| Authorized | NFS-e issued, with its number and access key | NF-e authorized by the SEFAZ, with number, series, access key, and protocol |
| Waiting | Waiting for the national NFS-e system to confirm | Waiting for the SEFAZ to authorize (or confirm) |
| Rejected | Did not issue the NFS-e, with the reason | The SEFAZ rejected the NF-e, with the reason; or denied its use |
| Test | Test environment: no legal validity | Homologação: no legal validity |

If a response is lost, Cord **checks** with the authority before doing anything: it never resends a document that may have arrived.

## Common problems

**My invoice is not issued and says it mixes products and services.** Split it in two: services go on the NFS-e and goods on the NF-e.

**Settings says my state requires data Cord does not have.** Your business is in AM, MS, PE, PR, SC, or TO, which require the technical contact. Contact us.

**I can't cancel an NF-e.** More than 24 hours have passed since its authorization. Correct it with a correction letter if what changes allows it.

**The municipality rejected the NFS-e.** Your municipality has not joined or has no active agreement, or the service is taxed where it is provided. The message says which.
