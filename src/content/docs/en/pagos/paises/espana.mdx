## What it is

In Spain Cord is built for the invoicing system the law requires (**Verifactu**, RD 1007/2023): each invoice generates a record chained to your business's previous one, which is sent to the Tax Agency, and its PDF carries the verification QR code. While Verifactu is being activated, Cord issues **pro formas** with the `PRO` series and registers nothing with the AEAT.

Once Verifactu is active, each invoice will also be downloadable as **Facturae 3.2.2** (with IRPF, IGIC, or IPSI) and as Factur-X, XRechnung, and Peppol; today, since your account issues pro formas, there is no electronic version yet. Cord has also built **e-invoicing between businesses** through the AEAT public solution, which will be activated when the AEAT publishes its service.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Starter or higher for Verifactu. On Free you issue pro formas (10 per month).
- A valid **NIF** for your business in **Settings › Tax profile**.
- A **qualified electronic certificate** (`.p12` or `.pfx`) for your business or its representative, with its password. You get it from a certification service provider (for example, the FNMT); its cost, if any, you pay directly to them.
- **Permissions:** **Settings** for the tax profile and the certificate; **Quotes** to issue and correct records; **Collections** to void.

## Set up your account

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › Tax profile**, complete your legal name, **NIF**, address, city, province, postal code, and **invoice prefix**. If your business is in the Canary Islands, Ceuta, or Melilla, write the province (or its code: 35, 38, 51, or 52): it decides which tax is seeded.
2. Review the catalog in **Settings › Taxes**: IVA 21, 10, and 4%, exemptions with their reason, and IRPF withholdings.
3. In the **Verifactu** section, upload your electronic certificate with its password. Cord validates it, reads its expiration, and stores it encrypted.
4. Under **European e-invoicing**, complete your billing contact and, if you want, turn on **Sign the Facturae with my electronic certificate**.

</Steps>

## Taxes

<DmTaxCatalog lang="en" country="ES" />

- **IVA** at 21, 10, and 4%, per line item.
- **Exemptions with a reason**: Exportación (art. 21, E2), Entrega intracomunitaria (art. 25, E5), Exenta art. 20 (E1), and Inversión del sujeto pasivo (S2). The line item **freezes** the reason when you choose it, Verifactu declares it, and the PDF cites the legal provision. Plain **Exento** lets Cord derive the reason from the client: not subject outside Spain or in an intra-community transaction with an EU VAT number, exempt inside.
- **IRPF withholding** at 15% or 7% (new self-employed): it is subtracted from what you collect. It is not a tax you pass on to your client.
- **Canary Islands:** the catalog starts with **IGIC** at 7, 3, and 0%. **Ceuta and Melilla:** only **Exento**, because each city sets its IPSI rates; add them by hand.

## Series per issuer and fiscal year

A Spanish invoice is identified by **issuer's NIF + number**, so numbering belongs to the business, not to the Cord account:

- The series carries the **fiscal year** attached to the prefix and numbering starts over every year. With the prefix `F`, the first invoice of 2026 is `F2026-000001`.
- If another Cord organization has your same NIF, it cannot use your same series: when you save your tax details Cord asks you to choose another one (for example `F2`) so the two never repeat a number.

## Verifactu

### What it does once activated

- Each invoice generates a **registration record** with a **hash** that chains it to your business's previous invoice. The chain is never edited: not even Cord can modify a record once generated.
- The PDF carries the AEAT **verification QR code** and the **VERI\*FACTU** legend at the top. With IRPF, "Importe total factura" (the QR's amount) and "Total a pagar" are shown separately.
- Issuing **does not wait** for the AEAT: the record is generated when you issue and the submission runs afterwards, in the background. The invoice detail says how it is going.
- A client without a tax ID is registered as a simplified invoice (no recipient).
- An invoice in another currency is converted with the document's exchange rate if your accounting currency is EUR.

### Status before the AEAT

<DmInvoiceAuthority lang="en" rail="verifactu" />

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Waiting to be sent to the AEAT | The record exists and will go out in the next submission. |
| Registered with the AEAT | The AEAT accepted it. |
| Registered with errors | The AEAT accepted it with warnings: it is worth correcting. |
| Rejected by the AEAT | The AEAT did not accept it, with its code and reason. |
| Could not be sent to the AEAT | The record does not pass the AEAT schema and was set aside without being sent. |

### Correct and void

- **Correct and resend:** an invoice that was rejected, set aside, or registered with errors is corrected from its detail with a **correction record**. You can set the exemption reason of 0% line items; amounts never change. **Settings › Tax profile** lists the invoices to correct.
- **Void:** generates a chained **cancellation record**. If the invoice never reached the AEAT, it is voided "without a previous record".
- **Credit note:** a corrective invoice by differences (R1, or R5 if the original was simplified) that cites the corrected invoice.

### Responsible declaration

The **responsible declaration of the invoicing system** is Cord's, as the software producer (Orden HAC/1177/2024, art. 15): you do not sign it. When Verifactu is active, **Settings › Tax profile** links to it so you can read and print it.

### What you can prepare today

Upload your certificate: while registration is not active, the section says **Certificate saved, registration not active yet**. If uploading it shows "Verifactu todavía no está disponible en Cord" (Verifactu is not available in Cord yet), activation has not finished: contact us and we will let you know.

## Facturae, IRPF, IGIC, and IPSI

Today Spanish accounts issue pro formas, which have no electronic version. Once Verifactu is active, each invoice in euros will be downloadable as **Facturae 3.2.2** from its detail and from its public link, with the same amounts as the PDF:

- **IRPF** as a withholding (the base and total do not change);
- **IVA**, **IGIC**, or **IPSI** with its rate, and exempt or non-subject transactions with their reason;
- **reverse charge** with its legal mention;
- the **credit note** as a corrective invoice by differences.

Signed with your certificate (XAdES) if you turn signing on; unsigned otherwise. Cord does not submit it to FACe and does not capture the DIR3 codes FACe requires yet. Factur-X, XRechnung, and Peppol do not support withholdings: an invoice with IRPF can only be downloaded as Facturae. Details in [European e-invoicing](/en/docs/pagos/factura-electronica).

## E-invoicing between businesses (AEAT)

The law requires issuing and receiving e-invoices between businesses and professionals (Ley 18/2022 and RD 238/2026). Cord will send each invoice to a client established in Spain through the **free AEAT public solution** and report when you collected it.

<DmRailSettings lang="en" rail="spfe" />

- **Status: Coming soon.** The AEAT has not published its service's technical specification yet. When it does, Cord will activate it. All you need is your certificate uploaded (the same one as Verifactu).
- **Obligation dates:** October 6, 2027 for businesses invoicing more than 8 million euros a year; October 6, 2028 for everyone else; October 6, 2029 for self-employed people up to 8 million to report payment of the invoices they receive.
- **Meanwhile**, each invoice's detail says whether it already has the data the AEAT requires or what is missing (for example, the client's NIF or full address).
- **Does not apply** to foreign clients, and invoices with **IRPF** will be sent once the AEAT publishes how they are declared: until then, download them as Facturae. The Basque Country and Navarre depend on their regional tax authorities.

## Client and product data

- **Client:** validated NIF and full address (municipality, postal code, and province) for e-invoicing between businesses; their EU VAT number if they are in another EU country (for reverse charge); their electronic address and buyer reference if they ask for them for the e-invoice.
- **Line item:** its rate and, at 0%, its exemption reason.

## Get paid online

- **Card** with Cord Payments.
- **SEPA Direct Debit** in EUR, which you turn on in **Settings › Payments**: each debit takes up to six business days to confirm and the account holder can return it for eight weeks. This feature is new.
- Your payouts arrive at your **IBAN**, validated with its check digits. See [Payouts](/en/docs/pagos/depositos).

## What Cord does not cover yet

- **Verifactu with IGIC, IPSI, or equivalence surcharge:** today it registers invoices with IVA; an invoice from the Canary Islands or Ceuta and Melilla cannot be registered.
- **FACe** and the public administration's DIR3 codes.
- **E-invoicing between businesses** until the AEAT publishes its service, and with IRPF until it publishes how it is declared.

## Common problems

**I uploaded my certificate and still issue pro formas.** That is expected while Verifactu is being activated.

**Cord asks me for another series when saving my tax details.** Another organization with your same NIF already uses that series. Choose a different one so numbers are not repeated.

**My Canary Islands invoice shows IGIC and cannot be registered.** Verifactu in Cord registers invoices with IVA today. Download it as Facturae.

**The AEAT rejected an invoice.** Open it and click **Correct and resend**: Cord generates the correction.
