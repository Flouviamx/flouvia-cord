## What it is

In Argentina each invoice must be authorized by **ARCA**, which assigns its number and its **CAE** (electronic authorization code). Cord talks directly to ARCA's web services, without an intermediary, using **your** CUIT's certificate: it builds the document, requests authorization, and stores the response. The PDF prints the CAE, its expiration, and ARCA's QR code.

<Callout type="info">
Electronic invoicing with ARCA is built and being activated: Cord turns it on country by country. If you don't see it, contact us.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Starter or higher to issue with ARCA. On Free, commercial invoice (10 per month).
- A **point of sale** created in ARCA as **"Factura electrónica - Web services"**.
- Your CUIT's **ARCA certificate** associated with the electronic invoicing service (`wsfe`): the `.crt` that ARCA issues and the private key you used to request it, or a `.p12`/`.pfx` file. You generate it in ARCA with your tax password (clave fiscal).
- Your **VAT status**: Responsable Inscripto, Monotributo, or Exento.
- **Permissions:** **Settings** for the tax profile; **Quotes** to issue; **Collections** for the credit note.

## Set up your account

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › Tax profile**, complete your legal name, your **CUIT**, and your address under **Invoice identity**.
2. Under **Electronic invoicing with ARCA**, complete the **point of sale (web service)**, your **VAT status**, **what you sell** (products, services, or both) and, if you want, your gross income tax number and the start of activities.
3. Under **ARCA certificate**, upload the `.crt` and `.key` (or the `.p12`) with the key password if it has one. Cord validates that it belongs to your CUIT and shows its validity.
4. On each Argentine client, choose their **VAT status** on their profile: it decides the invoice type.

</Steps>

## Taxes

<DmTaxCatalog lang="en" country="AR" />

- **IVA 21%** (default), **10.5%**, **27%**, and **Exento**, per line item. ARCA receives IVA by rate; a 0% line item is reported as exempt.
- **Monotributo and exempt** issuers issue type C, without itemized IVA.
- The document discount (bonificación) travels already split across line items, so the net amount, the bases, and IVA match the invoice.

## Invoice type

The type comes from ARCA's table using your status and your client's:

| Your status | Client | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Responsable Inscripto | Responsable Inscripto or Monotributo | A |
| Responsable Inscripto | End consumer, exempt, or others | B |
| Monotributo or Exento | Any | C |

- **Recipient:** with CUIT, with DNI, or an unidentified end consumer (types B and C only, and below 10,000,000 ARS). From that amount, the end consumer must be identified with their DNI or CUIT.
- If your client has a CUIT and you did not capture their status, Cord asks for it before issuing.
- **Services:** the invoice reports the billed period (from the service dates) and the payment due date.
- **Foreign currency:** Cord reports the document's frozen exchange rate if your accounting currency is ARS; otherwise, ARCA's official rate. Without an exchange rate it is not issued.

## Status before ARCA

<DmInvoiceAuthority lang="en" rail="arca" />

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Authorized by ARCA | It has a CAE; the panel shows the CAE and its expiration. |
| Waiting for ARCA to confirm | It was sent and Cord checks the result. |
| ARCA did not authorize the invoice | Rejected, with the reason. Correct it and issue again. |
| ARCA did not register this attempt | It never arrived: the number is free. |

If a response is lost, Cord **checks** ARCA's last authorized document before doing anything: it never requests two CAEs for the same invoice. In ARCA's test environment the number carries the `H-` prefix and the invoice says it has no tax validity.

## Void or correct

A document with a CAE **is not voided**: it is offset with a **credit note** of the same type, to the same recipient and in the same currency, associated with the invoice it adjusts. **Void invoice** takes you down that path.

## Get paid online

Cord Payments does not open accounts in Argentina: online collection is with **[Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago)**, on the quote link and the invoice link, with the methods Mercado Pago has enabled on your account. You can also record a transfer by hand.

## What Cord does not cover yet

Rejected with a message **before** requesting the CAE:

- Export **type E invoice** (client outside Argentina).
- **MiPyME Electronic Credit Invoice**.
- **Not categorized** recipient (requires a perception).
- **Withholdings** and **taxes or perceptions**.
- **CAEA** and type **M** documents.

## Common problems

**Settings says Coming soon.** Electronic invoicing with ARCA is being activated. Your invoices come out as commercial invoices.

**The client's VAT status is missing.** Choose it on their profile: it decides whether the invoice is A or B.

**ARCA requires identifying the end consumer.** The amount exceeds 10,000,000 ARS. Add the client's DNI or CUIT.

**The certificate is not accepted.** It must belong to your CUIT, be valid, and be associated with the electronic invoicing service in ARCA.
