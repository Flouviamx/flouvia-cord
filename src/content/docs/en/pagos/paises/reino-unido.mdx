## What it is

In the United Kingdom Cord issues a **commercial invoice** with a sequential number, each line item's VAT, your **VAT registration number**, and your client's details. Today the United Kingdom has no authority that authorizes or registers invoices, so Cord's document **is** your invoice: there is no registration with HMRC to make from Cord.

| | In the United Kingdom |
|---|---|
| Default currency | GBP |
| Tax | VAT |
| Tax ID | VAT reg. no., validated (HMRC modulus 97); also the Company Number |
| Document | Commercial invoice and commercial credit note |
| Authority | Not applicable to the invoice |
| PDF language | English |
| Online payments | Card with Cord Payments |

## Requirements

- **Plan:** any (Free: 10 invoices per month).
- Your **VAT registration number** if you are registered: it is what the law requires on the invoice, not your UTR.
- **Permissions:** **Settings** for the tax profile and taxes; **Quotes** to issue; **Collections** to void and credit.

## Set up your account

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › Tax profile**, complete your legal name, your **VAT reg. no.** (with or without the GB prefix; XI for Northern Ireland), address, city, postal code, and invoice prefix.
2. Review your catalog in **Settings › Taxes**.
3. Activate your payments account in **Settings › Payments** to get paid by card.

</Steps>

## Taxes

<DmTaxCatalog lang="en" country="GB" />

- **VAT 20%** (standard, default) and **VAT 5%** (reduced).
- **Zero-rated** and **Exempt** are two different profiles on purpose: in the United Kingdom they are different regimes (the first lets you recover input VAT; the second does not), and your return treats them differently.
- Each line item carries its own rate; the document prints one row per rate.

## Tax ID and vocabulary

- The tax ID label is **VAT reg. no.** Cord validates the number with HMRC modulus 97 (including the variant for new numbers) and accepts government department numbers. It also accepts the Companies House **Company Number**, which is what payments onboarding asks a company for.
- The tax is called **VAT** throughout the app, the PDF, and your client's link.

## Client and product data

- **Client:** their country and, if a registered business, their VAT number, validated with the same rule.
- **Product:** its default rate from the catalog.

## Void or correct

An invoice is **voided** from its detail if it has no payments applied; with payments, it is corrected with a full or partial **credit note**.

## Get paid online

- **Card** with Cord Payments, on the quote link and the invoice link.
- Your payouts arrive at your account with a **sort code** and an eight-digit account number. See [Payouts](/en/docs/pagos/depositos).

## What is coming: mandatory e-invoicing

The UK government confirmed that **e-invoicing between businesses** will be mandatory for VAT invoices from **April 2029**, with the Peppol network as the exchange framework. The technical specifications have not been published yet.

<Callout type="warning" title="Cord does not cover it yet">
Today Cord does not generate structured e-invoices (Peppol) for businesses in the United Kingdom: the European formats it generates are for issuers established in the European Union. Your Cord commercial invoice remains valid until the obligation takes effect.
</Callout>

## What Cord does not cover yet

- **Peppol e-invoicing** for issuers in the United Kingdom.
- **Bank debit (Bacs Direct Debit)**: online collection is by card.
- **Making Tax Digital**: Cord does not file your VAT return; export your invoices to your accounting software or connect [QuickBooks Online](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/quickbooks) or [Xero](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/xero).

## Common problems

**Cord rejects my VAT number.** The check digits do not match. Check the number on your VAT registration certificate.

**Should I enter my UTR or my VAT number?** Your VAT registration number. Whatever you enter in **VAT reg. no.** is printed on the invoice, and the UTR should never go on an invoice.

**My client needs the invoice in an electronic format.** Today Cord does not generate it for the United Kingdom; send them the PDF.
