## What it is

In France Cord issues an invoice in **French**, with each line item's TVA and **the mentions the e-invoicing reform requires**, downloadable as **Factur-X** (the PDF with the XML inside), which is also what the email carries by default.

The reform also requires invoices between businesses established in France to travel through an **approved platform** (PA) and sales to consumers and with other countries to be **reported** (e-reporting). Cord has built issuing through a platform: it issues, reports, and communicates your payments from the invoices you already issue. It is being activated.

| | In France |
|---|---|
| Default currency | EUR |
| Tax | TVA |
| Tax ID | SIREN / N° TVA (SIREN, SIRET, and TVA validated) |
| Document | Invoice with Factur-X; transmitted through an approved platform once activated |
| Authority | Approved platform and the tax administration (DGFiP) |
| PDF language | French |
| Online payments | Card and SEPA Direct Debit (EUR) with Cord Payments |

<Callout type="info">
Issuing through an approved platform is built and being activated: meanwhile, Settings says **Coming soon** and your invoices already carry the reform's mentions. European e-invoicing is also new. This feature is new and may not be enabled on your account yet. If you don't see it, contact us.
</Callout>

## Requirements

- **Plan:** any to invoice with Factur-X (Free: 10 invoices per month). **Starter or higher** to register with the platform.
- Your **SIREN** (or your TVA number, which contains it) in **Settings › Tax profile**.
- Your **reception platform**: since September 1, 2026 every business established in France must be able to receive e-invoices, and Cord **only issues**.
- **Permissions:** **Settings** for the tax profile and registration; **Quotes** to issue.

## Set up your account

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › Tax profile**, complete your legal name, your **SIREN / N° TVA**, address, and invoice prefix. If you apply the franchise, turn on **VAT small-business exemption (art. 293 B du CGI)**.
2. Under **E-invoicing in France**, choose your **VAT regime**, the **nature** of hand-typed line items (goods or services), and, if you have it, the **option to pay VAT on debits**.
3. In the catalog, choose each product's **type**: a product is a supply of goods; a service, subscription, or license is a supply of services.
4. On each French business client, enter their **SIREN** (or SIRET).
5. When issuing through the platform is active, click **Register with the platform** and follow the mandate step.

</Steps>

## Taxes

<DmTaxCatalog lang="en" country="FR" />

- **TVA 20%** (default), **10%**, **5.5%**, and **2.1%**, per line item.
- **Exonéré de TVA** for exempt transactions; on the exempt profile you can choose its e-invoice classification.
- **Franchise (art. 293 B du CGI):** with the option on, your tax-free invoices carry the legal notice that explains it.

## The reform's mentions

Every French invoice from Cord carries them, whether or not the platform is active:

- **Transaction category:** supply of goods, supply of services, or mixed, according to each product's type or, for hand-typed line items, your profile's default nature. Without it, the invoice is written as before the reform and cannot be transmitted.
- **Option for debits:** if you have it, the invoice prints "Option pour le paiement de la taxe d'après les débits" (services only).
- **Client SIREN:** a SIRET travels as the identifier and its SIREN as the legal registration.
- **Delivery address:** in the invoice editor (France only), when goods are delivered somewhere other than the client's address. It is not declared for a supply of services.
- **Business-to-business conditions:** late payment penalties, the flat collection cost indemnity, and early payment discount conditions (art. L441-9 and L441-10 of the Commercial Code).

## Issuing through an approved platform

### What Cord does

| Transaction | What Cord does | Payments |
|---|---|---|
| Between businesses established in France, client with SIREN | Transmits the Factur-X through the platform | If TVA is due on payment, reports each services payment as status 212 "Encaissée" |
| With a business established outside France | Reports the invoice (e-reporting), one by one | Reports its payments |
| To consumers | Reports the summary of each closed day, by currency and category | Reports what was collected that day |

Only payments for **services** are reported, without the option for debits. A refund is reported as a negative amount with its reason.

### Register

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › Tax profile › E-invoicing in France**, check your SIREN, regime, email, and address: Cord creates your registration with that data.
2. If you want, enter the **legal representative** (first name, last name, and position) and click **Register with the platform**.
3. Click **Verify identity and sign the mandate with Iopole**, the approved platform Cord issues through, and complete the identity verification and the mandate there.
4. Come back to Cord and click **Refresh status**. When registration is complete, the section says **Registration complete: Cord issues and reports for you**.

</Steps>

An invoice issued before registration is complete is not transmitted; the invoice detail says so.

### E-reporting deadlines

| VAT regime | Transactions | Payments |
|---|---|---|
| Standard regime, monthly | By ten-day periods: 1–10, on the 20th; 11–20, on the last day of the month; 21–end, on the 10th of the next month | Month, on the 10th of the next month |
| Standard regime, quarterly | Month, on the 10th of the next month | Month, on the 10th of the next month |
| Simplified | Month, on the last day of the next month | Same |
| Franchise | Two months, on the last day of the next month | Same |

Cord sends each day as soon as it closes; Settings counts what is queued, unconfirmed, or past its deadline.

### Invoice statuses

<DmInvoiceAuthority lang="en" rail="frpa" />

Each invoice's **E-invoicing platform** panel says how the reform treats it (transmitted or reported), how its submission is going, and each status the platform reported, with its date and reason:

| Code | Status |
|---|---|
| 200 | Submitted to the platform |
| 201 | Issued by the platform |
| 202 | Received by the client's platform |
| 203 | Made available to the client |
| 204 | Acknowledged by the client |
| 205 / 206 | Approved by the client (fully or partially) |
| 207 | Disputed by the client |
| 208 / 209 | Suspended: the client asks for supporting documents / Completed |
| 210 | Refused by the client |
| 211 / 212 | Payment sent / Payment received |
| 213 | Rejected by a platform |
| 501 | File not accepted by the platform |

**A rejected invoice (210 or 213) is not corrected:** cancel it with a credit note (it is not transmitted: it is an accounting cancellation) and issue a new one with the right data. Nothing is corrected automatically.

## Client and product data

- **Client:** SIREN or SIRET (validated with its check digit), their VAT number if they are in another EU country, and their electronic address if they have one.
- **Product:** its **type** (product, service, subscription, or license) decides the transaction category.
- **Invoice:** the delivery address when applicable.

## Get paid online

- **Card** with Cord Payments.
- **SEPA Direct Debit** in EUR, which you turn on in **Settings › Payments**. This feature is new.
- Your payouts arrive at your **IBAN**. See [Payouts](/en/docs/pagos/depositos).

## What Cord does not cover yet

- **Receiving** invoices from your suppliers: you need your reception platform.
- **Correcting a report** (e-reporting) already sent: not possible from Cord.
- **Voiding with the platform** an invoice already transmitted: it is corrected with a transmitted credit note.
- Invoices with withholdings, self-billing, multi-vendor, down payments, and the margin scheme: not issued through the platform.

## Common problems

**The invoice says the transaction category is missing.** Choose the product's type or the default nature of hand-typed line items in **Settings › Tax profile**.

**My invoice says "Not transmitted by Cord".** It was issued before your registration with the platform was complete.

**The platform rejected an invoice.** Cancel it with a credit note and issue a new one with the right data.
