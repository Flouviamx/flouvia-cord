  <h1 class="page-title">Accepting a payment</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Your customer pays inside the same quote or invoice link, with your branding and without leaving the document.</p>
</header>

## What it is

The public link of a quote or invoice includes the **payment checkout**: a screen with your logo and colors where your customer pays with the methods you have active. There's no second payment link to send: the same link is used to review, approve, pay, and check what's left.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Plan | All plans. |
| Payment rail | [Cord Payments](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding) active (MX, US, CA, BR, ES, GB, DE, FR) or [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago) connected (MX, BR, CO, AR, CL, PE). |
| Methods | At least one turned on in **Settings › Payments**. See [Payment methods](/en/docs/pagos/metodos). |
| Document | An approved quote (and signed, if you required it) or an issued invoice with a balance. A test document can't be paid online. |
| Currency | The document's sale currency. SPEI only in MXN. |

Without an online payment rail, the link shows your manual transfer details (if you set them up) and you record the payment once you verify it.

## When the pay button appears

| Situation | What your customer sees |
|---|---|
| Approved **upfront** quote | The pay button, from approval onward. |
| Quote with a **credit term** (Net 30 or Net 60) | A confirmation that the order is on credit with its due date. Online payment opens on that date. See [Credit terms](/en/docs/pagos/credito). |
| Quote with a **deposit** | From approval, the deposit amount. The balance opens according to the term. See [Upfront and balance](/en/docs/pagos/anticipos). |
| Quote with an **installment plan** | The next installment that's already due. See [Installment payment plans](/en/docs/pagos/cuotas). |
| **Recurring** quote | The card authorization for the monthly retainer. See [Monthly retainers](/en/docs/pagos/igualas). |
| Issued **invoice** | The full balance, with the **Pay another amount** option to pay part of it. |
| Paid document | The **Paid** status and the payment history. |

## How your customer pays

<Steps>
1. Opens the link and, if it's a quote, approves it.
2. Clicks the pay button. If you have both card and SPEI active, they first **choose how to pay**; with only one, they see the form directly.
3. Pays by card inside the document, follows the SPEI instructions, or clicks **Pay with Mercado Pago**.
4. Once confirmed, the link shows the payment and the document changes status.
</Steps>

### By card

- The form lives inside the document, with no redirects to another site.
- It accepts the credit and debit cards your payments account supports. Your customer's bank may ask for additional verification.
- If the payment fails, your customer sees a clear message with a reference they can share with you; they never see technical errors.
- Once confirmed, the document moves to **Paid** (or that part's charge does, if it's a deposit, an installment, or a partial payment).

### By SPEI

Only on **MXN** quotes from businesses in Mexico.

- Cord generates instructions with a **CLABE tied to that charge**, bank, beneficiary, reference, and amount, with copy buttons.
- Your customer can **Print or save PDF** or click **Email these to me** to receive them at the email registered on the quote.
- They transfer from their online banking. When the money arrives, Cord reconciles it on its own and changes the status: no receipts to cross-check.

SPEI confirmation depends on the bank's notice and isn't always instant. See [SPEI](/en/docs/pagos/metodos#automatic-spei).

### With Mercado Pago

Your customer goes to Mercado Pago's checkout, pays with the methods available in their country, and returns to your link. The payment is marked once Cord reads the approved payment in your Mercado Pago account.

## After payment

- **Your customer** sees the payment reflected on the link. If they return from a payment still being confirmed, the link waits a few seconds for the notice and lets them refresh.
- **You** see the payment in [Revenue › Payments](/en/docs/pagos/dashboard), on the document timeline, and, if you set up alerts, in your [notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones).
- **Your integrations** receive the payment event. A partial payment (deposit, installment, or partial invoice payment) doesn't trigger the quote-paid event: that one only goes out when no balance is left.
- **The money** lands in your payments account balance and reaches your bank at your [payout frequency](/en/docs/pagos/depositos).

<Callout type="info">
Fees depend on the method and the currency. On MXN payments, each collected part (deposit, balance, installment, or partial payment) carries its own fee. See [Cord Payments fees](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones).
</Callout>

## Edge cases

- **The quote amount changed after the link was opened.** If there are no payments yet, your customer sees a notice asking you to review the breakdown before paying.
- **Your customer switched methods mid-payment.** Cord checks the previous attempt before opening another and doesn't replace it if it already received money. See [Switch between SPEI and card](/en/docs/pagos/metodos#switch-between-spei-and-card).
- **The same charge was paid through Cord Payments and Mercado Pago.** Cord notes it in the history so you can decide whether to refund one.
- **The invoice balance changed while your customer was paying.** Cord asks them to refresh the invoice before continuing.

## Common problems

**My customer doesn't see the pay button.** Check that your account is active, that a method is turned on, and that payment is already open according to the credit term.

**My customer paid, but the document is still pending.** If it was SPEI, wait for the bank's confirmation. If it was a card, check **Recent payments** on the dashboard before recording anything by hand.

**The link says the document is a test.** That document has no valid fiscal issue and can't be paid online. See [Invoicing by country](/en/docs/pagos/facturacion).
