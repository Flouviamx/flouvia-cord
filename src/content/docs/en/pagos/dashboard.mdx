## What it is

**Payments** (sidebar › **Revenue › Payments**; on desktop, **G** then **O**) is the dashboard to **see** money that already came in: collected, net of fees, payouts, disputes, and the health of your payments account. It's different from **Settings › Payments**, where you **configure** Cord Payments (verification, methods, fee, and payouts).

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Plan | All plans. |
| Permission | **Collections**. Without it, the page shows "No access". |
| Refunds | Also the **Refunds** permission, which the owner grants explicitly. |
| Payments account | Needed for Available balance, Deposits, Net deposited, Open disputes, and Where every peso goes. Without it, those widgets don't appear and the rest works with your recorded payments. |

## Date range and customization

- **Date range:** the selector at the top applies to every period widget (up to one year back). Changing it updates the whole dashboard at once. Balance, Deposits, and Account status always show the current state.
- **Customize:** hide, reorder, or resize any widget; **Reset** restores the original layout. The configuration is saved per user. On a phone, widgets are reordered with up and down buttons.
- **Library:** besides the 12 widgets, you can add **Recurring contracts** (current recurring revenue from your retainers).

## The widgets

### Your money today

**Available balance.** How much is available for payout, how much is still **on the way** (collected but not released yet), and how much is **reserved**.

**Deposits.** Transfers to your bank account with arrival date, amount, currency, and status: **Scheduled**, **In transit**, **Deposited**, **Failed**, or **Canceled**. The history is stored in Cord and survives frequency changes. If there are none yet, the widget says so. See [Payouts to your bank](/en/docs/pagos/depositos).

**Account status.** Three independent indicators:

| Indicator | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Payments** | Whether your account can process card and SPEI right now (Active / Inactive). |
| **Payouts** | Whether it can transfer to your bank, and to which account ("Payouts to [bank] ••••1234"). |
| **Verification** | Whether it's complete, how many requirements are missing, how many are under review, and their deadline. |

When something is pending, **Manage** appears and takes you to complete it in Settings › Payments. If you haven't activated Cord Payments yet, the widget explains it: you can quote and record manual payments, but not charge cards or SPEI.

### Key indicators

**Collected.** The amount collected in the period, the number of transactions, the change versus the previous period, and a daily mini chart. It includes manually recorded payments.

**Net deposited.** What you collected **after the Cord Payments fee**, with the period's fee and the effective rate (which varies with your card and SPEI mix). If the period has more than 1,000 transactions, it warns that it's a partial sample.

**Active retainers.** How many [recurring payment](/en/docs/pagos/igualas) contracts are active and how many have a failed charge that needs attention.

**Open disputes.** How many chargebacks there are in the period and the amount at stake, with the response deadline for each. Each dispute opens its [evidence page](/en/docs/pagos/disputas). With no disputes, it says so.

### Movement and reconciliation

**Collection over time.** Money that came in day by day, against the same number of days in the previous period (in a lighter tone).

**Monthly volume.** What you collected month by month within the period.

**Where every peso goes.** From the gross collected: how much lands net in your bank, how much went to fees, how much was deducted for refunds, and how much is tied up in disputes.

**Breakdown by method.** How much you collected by card, SPEI, and manual transfer, with the number of transactions. Tap a method to filter the recent payments table.

**Recent payments.** Number, customer, method, date, and amount of the latest payments. With the **Refunds** permission, every reconciled payment has a **Refund** button. See [Refunds](/en/docs/pagos/reembolsos).

## Where the data comes from

- **Collected, Retainers, Monthly volume, Breakdown by method, and Recent payments** come from payments recorded in Cord, so they load with the page.
- **Balance, Deposits, Net, Where every peso goes, and Disputes** fill in a few seconds later with your payments account's information. If that request fails, you'll see "Could not refresh the dashboard" instead of made-up numbers.
- Mercado Pago payments are recorded as payments, but their balance and payouts live in your Mercado Pago account.

## Limits

- The dashboard doesn't show which payments make up each payout.
- It doesn't include invoices pending collection: for what you're owed, use [Collections](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza).
- It doesn't read Mercado Pago chargebacks.

## Common problems

**Balance and Deposits don't appear.** Your payments account isn't connected yet.

**Net deposited says "We couldn't reconcile the fees".** The request to your payments account failed. Reload later.

**I don't see the Refund button.** You need the **Refunds** permission, and the payment must have been reconciled through Cord Payments.
