## Choose how to pay

The quote link offers methods enabled by the business. Cord has two online payment
rails:

- **Cord Payments:** card and, in Mexico, automatic SPEI bank transfer. It requires a
  verified, active [payments account](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding).
- **[Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago):** the customer pays into your own
  Mercado Pago account. It is offered in Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Chile,
  and Peru; where you also have Cord Payments, it appears as an alternative on the
  same page.

The public invoice page accepts card through Cord Payments and, if you connected it,
Mercado Pago; it does not offer SPEI. Do not assume it offers every quote payment method.

### Card

The customer reviews the amount and completes the payment fields. Their bank may
request additional verification. The payment is reflected when confirmation arrives;
closing the payment screen or returning to the link is not proof of payment.

### SPEI

Automatic SPEI is available for MXN payments for eligible businesses in Mexico.
Instructions show the CLABE, bank, reference and remaining amount. Use the current
instructions for that payment, not a saved screenshot from another document.

Each payment keeps a bank-customer reference to identify its funds. Reconciliation
depends on confirmation; it is not guaranteed to be instant. If you already sent
a transfer, check its status before trying a card. Do not assume a previous CLABE
is unusable merely because you changed screens or methods. Contact the seller if
you sent money to previous instructions.

### Manual bank transfer

When a business offers its own bank details, the seller verifies receipt and
records the payment in Cord. An uploaded receipt does not replace that verification.
Refunding a manual transfer is handled with the business.

## Switching between SPEI and card

Before offering another method, Cord checks the previous payment attempt. If that
attempt has already received money or has a payment in progress — for example, a
partial SPEI transfer or an authorized card — it is not replaced: the customer sees
that a payment is waiting for confirmation. A concurrent retry reuses the same
attempt instead of creating another one. This prevents duplicate charges caused by
switching methods or by a lost response.

While a payment is unresolved, do not repeat the transfer or open another method
just to try it. Refresh the link or contact the business. Cord does not automatically
return a transfer that arrives late to earlier instructions; that case is resolved
with the business.

See [method-specific fees](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones). Sale currency is separate from
[your Cord subscription currency](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).

## Integrations that use the previous checkout

If an integration still requests a quote's previous checkout, it receives a link to
Cord's current payment screen, with the same payment-attempt registry and the balance
that is due. The response keeps its `url` field and accepted fees remain unchanged.
