## What it is

Cord has **two online payment rails** and one manual option:

- **Cord Payments:** card; in Mexico, automatic SPEI transfer; and in the euro area and the United States, bank debit (SEPA and ACH). The money lands in your own [payments account](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding) and from there reaches your bank at the [payout frequency](/en/docs/pagos/depositos) you choose.
- **[Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago):** your customer pays with your own Mercado Pago account, using the methods Mercado Pago offers in your country.
- **Manual transfer:** you share your bank details and record the payment yourself once you verify it.

## Method by country and currency

| Method | Countries | Payment currency | Quote | Invoice | Client portal | Retainer | Automatic payments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Card** (Cord Payments) | MX, US, CA, BR, ES, GB, DE, FR | The document's sale currency, if the processor supports it | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Automatic SPEI** (Cord Payments) | MX only | MXN only | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| **SEPA Direct Debit** (Cord Payments) | ES, DE, FR | EUR only | No | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| **ACH bank debit** (Cord Payments) | US only | USD only | No | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| **Mercado Pago** | MX, BR, CO, AR, CL, PE | Whatever Mercado Pago supports on your account | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Manual transfer** | All 12 Cord countries | Any | Yes | Yes (manual record) | No | No | No |

<Callout type="info">
In Mexico and Brazil both rails coexist: Cord Payments is the primary one and Mercado Pago appears as an alternative on the same page. In Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru, Mercado Pago is the way to collect online. See [Cord Payments fees](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones) for the cost of each method.
</Callout>

## Turn methods on

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings › Payments** with the **Payment settings** permission.
2. Under **Payment methods**, turn on **Credit and debit card** and, depending on your country, **SPEI** (Mexico), **SEPA Direct Debit** (Spain, Germany, and France), or **ACH bank debit** (United States). They become available once your payments account is **Active**; before that they appear locked.
3. Leave **Automatic payments** on if you want your clients to be able to save a method in their portal. See [Automatic payments](/en/docs/pagos/cobro-automatico).
4. If the screen shows the current fee schedule with an acceptance checkbox, review and accept it. See [Cord Payments fees](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones#how-your-fee-schedule-is-activated).
5. To offer manual transfer, turn it on and enter the **bank**, the account in your country's format, and the **beneficiary**.
6. For Mercado Pago, click **Connect Mercado Pago** on its card. See [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago).
</Steps>

## Card

Your customer reviews the amount and enters their card details in a form inside the link, without leaving for another page. Their bank may ask for additional verification. The payment shows up once confirmation arrives; closing the screen or returning to the link doesn't prove it was paid.

Card is the only method for [recurring retainers](/en/docs/pagos/igualas), because it's the only one that can charge itself every month.

## Apple Pay and Google Pay

<Callout type="info">
This feature is new and is being enabled: it doesn't appear on your customer's payment page yet. This page will be updated when it's available.
</Callout>

Once it's active, your customer will be able to pay with **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay** on the same screen as the card: on the quote's payment page, on the invoice link, and in the client portal.

- **They aren't turned on separately:** they come with **Credit and debit card** in **Settings › Payments**. If you turn the card off, they aren't offered either.
- **They're charged as a card:** the same [fee](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones), the same confirmation, and the same refunds.
- **Only on compatible devices:** Apple Pay on an iPhone, iPad, or Mac with a card in Wallet; Google Pay in a browser with a card saved to the Google account. If the device doesn't have them, your customer sees the usual card form.

## Automatic SPEI

SPEI is Mexico's interbank transfer system and only settles in pesos. It appears only on **MXN** payments of businesses in Mexico with SPEI turned on; if the quote is in another currency, your customer pays by card even if SPEI is on.

When choosing **SPEI transfer**, your customer sees the **SPEI instructions**:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Amount | The outstanding amount of that charge, with its currency. |
| CLABE | A CLABE tied to that charge, with a **Copy** button. |
| Bank | The receiving bank. |
| Beneficiary | Your business name. |
| Reference | The charge reference, with a **Copy** button. |
| Valid until | If the instructions have a deadline, it appears here. |

From that screen they can **Print or save PDF**, or click **Email these to me**.

### SPEI instructions by email

**Email these to me** sends the same instructions (amount, CLABE, bank, beneficiary, and reference), with your business branding, to **the customer email registered on the quote**. No other recipient can be typed: if your customer needs them at another address, update their record. If the quote has no customer email, the button returns an error and your customer can print or save the PDF. For security, sending is limited to three emails every ten minutes per link.

### How it's confirmed

Each charge keeps its own bank reference, so Cord recognizes the funds without anyone cross-checking receipts. When the money arrives, the charge is marked paid and the quote changes status. Confirmation depends on the bank's notice and **isn't always instant**: if your customer already transferred, they should check the status before trying a card.

## Bank debit (SEPA and ACH)

<Callout type="info">
This feature is new and may not be enabled on your account yet. If you don't see it, write to us.
</Callout>

With bank debit, your client pays with a **direct debit from their bank account**
instead of a card. Cord offers the scheme that matches your business's country:

| | SEPA Direct Debit | ACH bank debit |
|---|---|---|
| **Businesses in** | Spain, Germany, and France | United States |
| **Currency** | EUR only | USD only |
| **Client's account** | IBAN in the SEPA area | United States bank account |
| **Confirmation** | Up to 6 business days | Up to 4 business days |
| **Refunds** | Full or partial | Full only |
| **Notice to the account holder** | The processor notifies them before each debit | The processor confirms the authorization |

<DmPaymentSettings lang="en" />

### Turn it on

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings › Payments** with the **Payment settings** permission. Your payments
   account must be **Active**.
2. Under **Payment methods**, turn on **SEPA Direct Debit** or **ACH bank debit**.
3. Cord requests the capability for your payments account. While the processor reviews
   it, the method shows **Under review** and isn't offered to any client yet.
4. Once approved, it changes to **Active** and shows up as an option on the link of your
   invoices in EUR (SEPA) or in USD (ACH), in the [client
   portal](/en/docs/pagos/portal-facturas), and in [automatic
   payments](/en/docs/pagos/cobro-automatico).
</Steps>

If it shows **Unavailable: check your verification**, the processor didn't enable the
capability: review the pending requirements of your [payments
account](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding).

### How a debit works

- **Where it's offered:** on the invoice link, in the client portal, and as the saved
  method for automatic payments, only on invoices in its currency. It isn't offered on
  the quote link or for retainers.
- **It takes days.** While the bank confirms, the invoice shows **Payment processing**:
  it can't be charged again or voided, and the invoice detail says so. When the debit is
  confirmed, the payment is applied like any other; if the bank declines it, the invoice
  goes back to being payable.
- **Where Cord charges a fee (today, in MXN), it isn't offered.** Cord's fee is a card
  fee and there's no approved rate for debits. Outside MXN, Cord charges no fee; see
  [Fees](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones).
- **Turning it off** stops offering it on new charges; debits already in progress carry
  on.
- **With SEPA, the account holder can return a debit** without giving a reason for 8
  weeks. Keep it in mind before handing over something of high value against a debit
  that was just confirmed.

## Mercado Pago

The quote's payment page and the invoice link show **Pay with Mercado Pago**. Your customer goes to Mercado Pago's checkout and returns to your link when done. Cord marks the charge paid only when it **reads the approved payment** in your Mercado Pago account. Details in [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago).

## Manual transfer

Your customer sees your bank details and transfers from their bank. When you verify the deposit in your bank, you record the payment in Cord: on the quote, on the invoice, or with **Record payment** from [receivables](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza). A receipt sent by the customer doesn't replace that verification. Refunding a manual transfer is handled directly with your customer.

## What the quote, the invoice, and the portal accept

| | Quote link | Invoice link | Client portal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Card | Yes | Yes, if **Card** is turned on | Yes |
| Automatic SPEI | Yes, in MXN | No | No |
| SEPA or ACH debit | No | Yes, in EUR or USD | Yes, in EUR or USD |
| Mercado Pago | Yes | Yes | No |
| Partial payment | In defined parts: deposit, balance, or installments | Free amount: your customer chooses how much to pay | No: each invoice is paid at its balance |
| Several invoices in one charge | No | No | Yes, in the same currency |
| Retainers | Yes, by card | No | No |

### Partial payment of an invoice

On the invoice link, your customer can pay the full balance or click **Pay another amount** and enter how much they want to pay, by card or with Mercado Pago:

- Cord **caps the payment at the real balance**: if they enter more than they owe, only the balance is charged.
- There's a **minimum that can be charged online**: 0.50 in the invoice currency (for example, 0.50 USD or 0.50 EUR), or 50 units in currencies without decimals. If the amount is lower, Cord asks them to pay a bit more or settle the balance.
- Each payment is recorded in the invoice's payment history and the balance is recalculated. The invoice becomes **Paid** when the balance reaches zero.
- If the balance changes while your customer is paying (for example, because you recorded another payment), Cord asks them to refresh before continuing.

See [Create, issue, and collect invoices](/en/docs/pagos/facturas-emitidas).

## Switch between SPEI and card

Your customer can choose another method on the same page. Before offering the new one, Cord checks the previous attempt:

- If the previous attempt **received no money**, it's canceled and the new method opens.
- If it **already received money or has a payment in progress** (a partial SPEI transfer or a card being authorized), it isn't replaced: your customer sees there's a payment pending confirmation.
- If two attempts arrive at the same time, the same one is reused instead of creating another.
- If Cord can't confirm the previous attempt's status, it **doesn't open another one**: it prefers a review over risking a double charge.

While a payment is pending clarification, your customer shouldn't repeat the transfer or try another method: they should refresh the link or contact you. Cord doesn't automatically return a transfer that arrives late at old instructions; you resolve that case with your customer.

## Limits

- SPEI: Mexico only, MXN only, quotes only.
- Bank debit: SEPA only in EUR for businesses in Spain, Germany, and France; ACH only in USD for businesses in the United States. Only on invoices, the portal, and automatic payments.
- The invoice doesn't offer SPEI or retainers.
- Mercado Pago doesn't run recurring charges and Cord doesn't read its chargebacks today.
- In Colombia, Argentina, Chile, and Peru there's no Cord Payments: online collection is through Mercado Pago.
- A test document (without a valid fiscal issue) can't be paid online.

## Common problems

**My customer doesn't see the SPEI option.** The quote isn't in MXN, SPEI is off in **Settings › Payments**, or your account isn't active.

**My customer doesn't see a pay button on the invoice.** Your payments account isn't active, **Card** is off and Mercado Pago isn't connected, or the invoice is a test document.

**My customer doesn't see bank debit.** The invoice isn't in the method's currency (EUR for SEPA, USD for ACH), the method isn't **Active** in **Settings › Payments**, or it's a charge in MXN.

**"Email these to me" fails.** The quote has no customer email or the sending limit was reached. Your customer can **Print or save PDF**.

**My customer transferred to another charge's CLABE.** Each charge has its own reference. Contact your customer and review the quote's charges before recording anything by hand.

## Integrations using the previous checkout

If an integration still requests a quote's previous checkout, it receives a link to Cord's current payment screen, with the same attempt record and the balance due. The response keeps the `url` field and doesn't change the accepted fees.
