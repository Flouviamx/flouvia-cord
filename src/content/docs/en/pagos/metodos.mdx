## Payment architecture

Cord Payments is designed so that charging isn't a point of friction at the end of the project. Once you activate Cord Payments (see [Connect your account](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding)), your business can accept three methods — card, dynamic SPEI, and manual transfer — each with its own fee (see [Fees and advantages](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones)) and its own level of automation.

On the public cotización link, the customer **picks the method explicitly before continuing** — one is never assigned to them by default without their knowledge. If your business only activated one method, that's the only one shown.

### 1. Dynamic SPEI

This is the method built for B2B in Mexico: it eliminates manual invoice reconciliation.

**How it works:**
Instead of giving every customer the same CLABE for your company, Cord Payments generates **a unique, temporary CLABE for each individual payment**.

When the customer picks "SPEI Transfer":
1. Cord Payments generates a virtual CLABE assigned exclusively to that specific payment, and shows it on a Cord-native instructions screen — without leaving the cotización link or redirecting to an external site.
2. The customer transfers from their banking app to that CLABE.
3. As soon as the funds enter the interbank network, Cord Payments identifies them automatically (because the CLABE was unique to that payment).
4. The system marks the cotización as **Paid** in real time and sends you the confirmation.

<Callout type="info">
**Zero collisions:** unlike the traditional model where you get a "$15,000" deposit and have to investigate which client sent it, dynamic SPEI makes it impossible for two payments to be confused — every payment has its own CLABE.
</Callout>

Fee: 1% + $7.00 MXN + VAT, capped at $588.12 MXN per payment (see the full detail in [Fees and advantages](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones)).

### 2. Credit and debit card

The fastest, lowest-friction method. It shows up directly in the checkout embedded in the cotización (without ever taking the customer out of the document).

**Key features:**
- Supports Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.
- Native interface with your brand's color and name — no third-party logos or references to any external processor.
- **Near-instant authorization:** as soon as the customer confirms the payment, the cotización flips to Paid within seconds.
- The only method that supports [recurring retainers](/en/docs/pagos/igualas) — a charge that gets set up once and repeats every month without the customer having to intervene again.

Fee: 4% + $3.00 MXN + VAT per transaction.

### 3. Manual bank transfer

If you'd rather not use Cord Payments' automated collection — or the cotización is a large amount where you'd prefer to avoid any fee — you can leave manual transfer as the only active method.

**The manual flow:**
1. The customer sees your traditional banking details (Bank, CLABE, Beneficiary) configured in Settings.
2. The customer transfers outside of Cord.
3. You, the seller, go into the cotización and press "Mark as paid" manually once you've verified the money landed in your account.

Fee: $0.00 — it doesn't go through Cord Payments, so it doesn't generate any fee, but it also doesn't have automatic reconciliation or chargeback protection.

<Callout type="info">
You can activate all three methods at once, or just the ones that make sense for you, from **Settings › Payments**. The customer only sees the options you have turned on.
</Callout>
