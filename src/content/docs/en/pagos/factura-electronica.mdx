<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">European e-invoicing</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">The same invoice, in the structured formats your clients' accounts payable systems read across Europe.</p>
</header>

If your business is established in the European Union, every invoice issued in Cord is
also available as an **e-invoice** under the European standard **EN 16931**. It is not a
different document: it has the same number, the same date, and the same amounts as the
PDF, written so your client's system can process it without keying it in.

<Callout type="info">
This feature is new and may not be enabled on your account yet. If you don't see it, contact us.
</Callout>

## Formats

- **Factur-X (PDF):** the invoice PDF with the XML inside (EN 16931 profile). It opens
  like any PDF, and systems that accept Factur-X or ZUGFeRD also read it. It is the usual
  format in France.
- **XRechnung (UBL) and XRechnung (CII):** the format required by the German public
  sector and accepted by many companies in Germany. UBL is the most common; CII is there
  in case your client asks for it.
- **Peppol BIS 3.0 (UBL):** the format of the Peppol network, used across much of Europe.
- **Facturae 3.2.2 (XML):** the Spanish format, only for businesses established in Spain
  and invoices in euros. It is the only one that declares the **IRPF withholding**.

<Callout type="warning">
Cord **generates** these files, lets you download them, and can attach them to the
invoice email. It **does not send them** over the Peppol network or to FACe. If your
client asks to receive them over Peppol, download them and upload them to your access
provider. In France, Cord does transmit them through an approved platform: see
[E-invoicing in France](#e-invoicing-in-france).
</Callout>

## Set up your data once

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › Tax profile**, **European e-invoicing** section, fill in the **billing
   contact** and the **contact phone**. XRechnung requires a contact with name, phone, and
   email (your business email is used).
2. Add your **electronic address**: the scheme (for example GLN, SIRET, or your VAT number)
   and the identifier. Peppol requires it; XRechnung uses your email if there is none.
3. If you have them, add your **company registration number** and your bank's **BIC**.
   The IBAN on the invoice is the one in **Settings › Payments**; XRechnung requires it to
   state how to pay.
4. On each client that needs it, open the **E-invoicing** section and enter their
   **electronic address** and **buyer reference**. In Germany, for public bodies, the
   reference is the **Leitweg-ID** and it is mandatory (see [Leitweg-ID](#leitweg-id)).

</Steps>

When you create an invoice you can also enter the **buyer reference** and the **purchase
order** in the editor summary. If you leave the reference empty, the client's is used.
Peppol requires at least one of the two.

## Download

Open the issued invoice. In the right column, the **E-invoice** section lists every format
that invoice can already produce, ready to download. For the others, it says which data is
missing and links to the place where you fix it.

Your client can also download the available formats from the invoice's public link, next
to the PDF.

<Callout type="info">
An issued invoice keeps the data it was issued with. If you correct your contact, your
electronic address, or the client's, the change applies to the **next** invoices; the
issued one is not rewritten.
</Callout>

## Attach to the email

In **Settings › Tax profile › Attach to invoice emails** you choose what goes with the email:

- **Automatic:** Factur-X in France, PDF plus XRechnung XML in Germany, and the PDF only
  elsewhere.
- **PDF only.**
- **Factur-X PDF:** replaces the regular PDF (it looks the same and carries the XML inside).
- **PDF + XRechnung XML.**
- **PDF + Facturae XML** (Spain only): signed if you turned signing on, unsigned
  otherwise. In Spain the automatic value is the PDF only: your client does not expect an
  XML they did not ask for.

If an invoice is missing data for the chosen format, the email goes out with the regular
PDF and the invoice tells you what is missing. Sending never stops because of this.

## Taxes and exemptions

Each line is classified with the European VAT category:

- with a rate above zero, **standard or reduced rate**;
- at 0%, the **reason** you chose on the exempt profile in **Settings › Taxes** (reverse
  charge, intra-community supply, export, not subject to VAT, zero rated, or an exemption
  of the VAT Directive). In Spain, the Verifactu reason is used;
- at 0% without a reason, Cord infers it: the issuer's small-business scheme, a client in
  another EU country with a VAT number (reverse charge), a client outside the EU (export),
  or a domestic exemption.

**Canary Islands, Ceuta, and Melilla.** If your business is in the Canary Islands, each
line is declared with **IGIC** (category L of the European standard); in Ceuta or Melilla,
with **IPSI** (category M), always with its rate, 0% included. Those territories are
outside the EU VAT area, so your NIF travels as a tax registration and not as a VAT
number, and a line cannot carry an exemption reason: remove it from the exempt profile
and keep the rate. **IGIC at 0%** can be downloaded as XRechnung (UBL), Peppol, and
Facturae, but not as Factur-X or XRechnung (CII): the official validator of that syntax
requires a rate above zero.

A [document discount or a coupon](/en/docs/cotizacion/descuentos) appears as it does on
the PDF: each line with its amount before the discount and the discount subtracted in the
totals, split by VAT category and rate, so the total matches the invoice to the cent. In
the Facturae, each line carries its share of the discount.

## Facturae (Spain)

If your business is in Spain, today you issue pro formas, which have no electronic version.
Once Verifactu is active, every invoice in euros will also be downloadable as **Facturae
3.2.2**, from the invoice detail and from its public link. It will come from the same issued
invoice, with the same amounts:

- **IRPF** as a withholding (not as a discount: the invoice base and total do not change);
- **VAT**, **IGIC**, or **IPSI** with its rate, and exempt or non-taxable supplies with
  their reason;
- **reverse charge** with its legal wording;
- a **credit note** as a corrective invoice by differences, with the invoice it corrects.

<Steps>

1. To **sign it**, upload your electronic certificate in **Settings › Tax profile ›
   Verifactu**.
2. In the **European e-invoicing** section, turn on **Sign the Facturae with my electronic
   certificate**. The signature is made in your name, so it only applies if you turn it on.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Without a signature, the Facturae is downloaded as an unsigned XML and the invoice detail
says so: it works for your client, but **to submit it to FACe it must be signed**. FACe,
the Spanish public sector's invoice entry point, also requires the DIR3 codes of the
public body, which Cord does not collect yet. Cord does not submit invoices to FACe.
</Callout>

## E-invoicing between businesses in Spain

Spanish law requires businesses and professionals to issue and receive e-invoices when the
client is another business or professional established in Spain (Law 18/2022 and Royal
Decree 238/2026). Order HAC/1028/2026 started the clock on 6 October 2026:

- **6 October 2027:** businesses invoicing more than 8 million euros a year.
- **6 October 2028:** all other businesses and professionals.
- **6 October 2029:** self-employed people invoicing up to 8 million euros start reporting
  payment of the invoices they receive.

Cord will send each invoice to a client established in Spain through the **AEAT public
e-invoicing solution**, which is free, and will tell it when you were paid. Only full
payment is reported: a partial payment is not. If the invoice was already paid when you
issued it, the invoice itself says so and your client does not have to report the
payment.

<Callout type="info">
**Coming soon.** The AEAT has not yet published the technical specification of its
service. Cord will turn sending on as soon as it does. Until then nothing is sent, and the
invoice detail and **Settings › Tax profile** say so.
</Callout>

Get ready now:

<Steps>

1. Upload your electronic certificate in **Settings › Tax profile › Verifactu**: it is the
   same one your business will use to identify itself to the AEAT.
2. On each Spanish client, enter their **NIF** and full address: street and number, town,
   postal code, and province. The AEAT does not accept the invoice without them.
3. Open an invoice issued to a client in Spain. The **E-invoice (AEAT)** section says
   whether it already has what the AEAT requires or what is missing.

</Steps>

These will not go through the public solution:

- invoices to clients outside Spain, which are outside the obligation;
- invoices with **IRPF withholding**, until the AEAT publishes how its format declares
  them. Until then, download them as **Facturae**, one of the syntaxes the law accepts;
- invoices from businesses in the Basque Country and Navarre, which depend on agreements
  with their regional tax authorities that have not been published yet;
- credit notes for a simplified invoice.

## E-invoicing in France

Since **1 September 2026** every business established in France must be able to
**receive** e-invoices through an approved platform (PA), and large and mid-sized
companies must also **issue** them and **report** their other transactions
(e-reporting). Small and micro businesses issue and report from **1 September 2027**.

Cord issues and reports for you, from the invoices you already issue:

- an invoice to another business established in France (with a SIREN) travels as
  **Factur-X** through the platform, and Cord shows on the invoice each status the
  platform reports: submitted, received by your client's platform, approved, rejected…;
- a sale to a **consumer** is reported in the daily summary, and an invoice to a
  **business in another country**, one by one;
- if your VAT is due on payment (services without the option to pay VAT on debits), Cord
  reports **each payment** you record.

<Callout type="info">
**Coming soon.** Issuing through an approved platform is not active in Cord yet. Your
invoices already carry the mentions the reform requires, and **Settings › Tax profile**
and each invoice's detail say whether it has everything or what is missing.
</Callout>

French invoices already carry the four new mentions: the **client's SIREN**, the
**transaction category** (goods, services or both), the **option to pay VAT on debits**
if you took it, and the **delivery address** when it is not the client's.

<Steps>

1. In **Settings › Tax profile**, fill in your **SIREN** (or your VAT number, which
   contains it) and, under **E-invoicing in France**, your **VAT regime**, whether you
   opted to pay VAT **on debits**, and whether lines typed by hand are **goods or
   services**.
2. In the catalog, choose each product's **type**: a product is a supply of goods; a
   service, subscription or license is a supply of services.
3. If you deliver somewhere other than the client's address, enter the **delivery
   address** in the invoice editor.
4. Once the feature is active, click **Register with the platform** and, on its page,
   verify your identity and sign the mandate. Cord only transmits what you issue after
   the registration is complete.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Cord **only issues**. To receive your suppliers' invoices you need your own reception
platform, mandatory since 1 September 2026; registering in Cord does not change it.
</Callout>

### Statuses of a transmitted invoice

Each status the platform reports is kept on the invoice detail, with its date and reason,
and in its history:

| Code | Status | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | Submitted to the platform | Nothing: the invoice went out. |
| 201 | Issued by the platform | Nothing. |
| 202 | Received by the client's platform | Nothing. |
| 203 | Made available to the client | Nothing. |
| 204 | Acknowledged by the client | Nothing. |
| 205 | Approved by the client | Nothing. |
| 206 | Partially approved by the client | Talk to your client about the part that wasn't approved. |
| 207 | Disputed by the client | Resolve the disagreement with your client. |
| 208 | Suspended: the client asks for supporting documents | Send them the documents they ask for. |
| 209 | Completed with supporting documents | Nothing. |
| 210 | Refused by the client | Cancel it with a credit note and issue a new invoice. |
| 211 | Payment sent | Nothing: your client reported that they paid. |
| 212 | Payment received | Nothing: the payment was reported. |
| 213 | Rejected by a platform | Cancel it with a credit note and issue a new invoice. |
| 501 | File not accepted by the platform | Check the reason, cancel it, and issue a new invoice. |

If your client or a platform **rejects** an invoice (210, 213, or 501), the invoice says
so with the reason. It is not corrected: cancel it with a **credit note** (it is an
accounting cancellation and is not transmitted) and issue a new invoice with the right
data.

### What's reported about your sales and payments

| Sale | How Cord reports it | Its payments |
|---|---|---|
| To a business established in France, with a SIREN | Transmits the invoice (Factur-X) through the platform. | If VAT is due on payment, each payment as status **212 Payment received**. |
| To a business in another country, with a tax ID | Reports it one by one in the e-reporting. | In the payments e-reporting. |
| To a consumer, French or not | The day's total, per currency and per type (goods, services, or transactions outside the scope of French VAT). | The day's collected amount. |

- **Which payments are reported:** only those for **services**, when you didn't opt to
  pay VAT on debits. On a mixed invoice only the services part is sent, split by VAT
  rate.
- **Refunds:** on a transmitted invoice, a refund is reported as a negative payment with
  its reason. In the payments e-reporting the platform doesn't accept negative amounts:
  Cord leaves it flagged so you can regularize it in the platform's console.
- **Currency:** the payments e-reporting goes in euros, with the invoice's locked
  exchange rate.
- **Deadlines:** Cord sends every day as soon as it closes. The platform groups what was
  sent into the period that matches your **VAT regime**: by ten-day periods in the
  monthly standard regime, by month in the quarterly and simplified ones, and every two
  months under the franchise. **Settings › Tax profile** counts what's overdue.

## Leitweg-ID

The Leitweg-ID identifies a German public body and ends with **two check digits**. Cord
checks them on any electronic address with scheme 0204 (yours or a client's) and on the
buyer reference of a client with that address: on the client, in the invoice editor, and
in the API. If they do not match, it is not saved and Cord says why; copy it again,
complete, from the purchase order or the public body.

A Leitweg-ID saved before this check is not changed on its own: the client page flags it
as **Leitweg-ID to correct**, and their e-invoices are not generated until you fix it.

## When it cannot be generated

Cord does not produce an e-invoice that differs from the real invoice. There is no
electronic version for:

- drafts, voided invoices, pro formas, and test documents;
- in Factur-X, XRechnung, and Peppol, invoices with **withholdings** (for example Spanish
  IRPF): the European standard has no place to declare them, and subtracting them as a
  discount or prepayment would change the base or the amount due. In Spain, download it
  as **Facturae**;
- an IGIC or IPSI line with an exemption reason;
- a Leitweg-ID whose check digits do not match;
- older invoices whose totals are not the exact sum of their lines;
- a supply **not subject** to VAT mixed with lines of another category: issue them
  separately.

## Common problems

- **"XRechnung requires the buyer reference":** enter it on the client or on the invoice
  before issuing it.
- **"Peppol requires your participant identifier":** add your electronic address in
  **Settings › Tax profile** with a scheme other than email.
- **"XRechnung requires payment instructions":** add your IBAN in **Settings › Payments**.
- **"The Leitweg-ID check digits do not match":** copy it again, complete, from the
  purchase order or the public body, and correct it on the client.
- **"Facturae requires the client's tax ID":** enter it on the client; the Facturae is not
  generated without it.
- **"Each line must say whether it is goods or a service"** (France): choose the
  product's type or, for lines typed by hand, the option in **Settings › Tax profile**.
  An issued invoice keeps its lines: it applies to the next ones.
