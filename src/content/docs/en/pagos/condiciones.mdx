## What it is

The Cord Payments fee is the per-transaction fee Cord deducts from each online payment processed through your payments account. **It already includes the processing cost**: there's no second third-party fee added later. It's separate from your [Cord subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion): it applies the same on every plan.

## Fee by currency

The fee depends on the **currency the payment is collected in**, not on the country or the plan.

| Payment currency | Cord platform fee today |
|---|---|
| **MXN** (Mexican pesos) | Yes: the per-method table below. |
| **USD** (US dollars) | Doesn't apply. |
| **EUR** (euros) | Doesn't apply. |
| **GBP** (pounds sterling) | Doesn't apply. |
| **CAD** (Canadian dollars) | Doesn't apply. |
| **BRL** (Brazilian reais) | Doesn't apply. |

In currencies without a platform fee you only pay your payments account's processing cost and your Cord subscription. **Settings › Payments** tells you so, with your account's currency. If a fee is ever activated in another currency, it will be published here and you'll have to accept it before it applies.

<Callout type="info">
Payments through [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago) carry no Cord fee: you pay your Mercado Pago account's fee, under your terms with them. Manual transfer carries no fee from anyone.
</Callout>

## Fee table in MXN

| Method | Total fee | Example on a $10,000.00 MXN payment |
|---|---|---|
| **Card** (credit/debit) | 4% + $3.00 MXN + VAT | $467.48 MXN |
| **SPEI** (automatic transfer) | 1% + $7.00 MXN + VAT, with a **maximum of $588.12 MXN** per payment | $124.12 MXN |
| **Retainers** (monthly recurring card charge) | 4% + $3.00 MXN + VAT on each monthly charge | $467.48 MXN per charge |
| **Manual transfer** | $0.00 MXN: doesn't go through Cord Payments | $0.00 MXN |

<DmFeeSummary lang="en" />

<Callout type="info">
VAT (16%, Mexico's IVA) is calculated on the fee (the percentage component plus the fixed one), never on the full payment amount. By card: (4% of $10,000.00 MXN + $3.00 MXN) × 1.16 = $467.48 MXN. By SPEI: (1% of $10,000.00 MXN + $7.00 MXN) × 1.16 = $124.12 MXN.
</Callout>

### Why SPEI has a cap and card doesn't

Processing a SPEI costs practically the same regardless of the amount. That's why the percentage part of the SPEI fee (the 1%) is capped at **$500.00 MXN** before VAT, reached on a **$50,000.00 MXN** payment. From there, a SPEI's total fee stays at **$588.12 MXN**: ($500.00 MXN + $7.00 MXN) × 1.16.

| Payment amount | Total card fee | Total SPEI fee |
|---|---|---|
| $50,000.00 MXN | $2,323.48 MXN (4.65%) | **$588.12 MXN** (1.18% effective) |
| $100,000.00 MXN | $4,643.48 MXN (4.64%) | **$588.12 MXN** (0.59% effective) |
| $500,000.00 MXN | $23,203.48 MXN (4.64%) | **$588.12 MXN** (0.12% effective) |

For large payments in pesos, SPEI is by far the cheapest online method.

### Split payments

Each part of a quote is a separate transaction: the [deposit and the balance](/en/docs/pagos/anticipos), each [installment](/en/docs/pagos/cuotas), and each partial payment of an invoice carries its own fee, calculated with the same table.

### Retainers

A [retainer](/en/docs/pagos/igualas) is always charged by card, because it's the only way for the charge to go out on its own every month. The fee is the same as a regular card payment, applied to each monthly charge.

## How your fee schedule is activated

Cord's fee doesn't apply until you **explicitly accept the current version** of the schedule:

<Steps>
1. Connect your payments account. See [Connect your account](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding).
2. Open **Settings › Payments** with the **Payment settings** permission. If there's a version you haven't accepted, the **Cord Payments fee** section appears with the notice "The current version isn't accepted yet. Until you accept it, Cord won't add its fee".
3. Review the fees that apply to you. If your account collects in a currency without a platform fee, the section says so instead of showing the Mexican table.
4. Check the box accepting the Cord Payments fee terms.
5. Click **Activate current fee** and confirm your identity when Cord asks.
</Steps>

The acceptance is recorded in the audit log with the terms version, date, and IP address. If we change the schedule, the change is **not retroactive**: the section appears again and the new version doesn't apply until you accept it.

<Callout type="warning" title="Until you accept, there's no fee">
A connected account that hasn't accepted the current version keeps collecting by card and SPEI, and Cord doesn't add its fee to those payments. This protects accounts that were collecting before this schedule existed: nobody changes your fee without your confirmation. Accepting the fee also turns on the payment screen where your customer first chooses between card and SPEI.
</Callout>

The full legal text is in the [Terms and conditions, Cord Payments section](https://cordhq.app/terminos#cord-pagos).

## What happens to the fee if you refund

If you return money to a customer from the [payments dashboard](/en/docs/pagos/reembolsos), the refunded amount is deducted from your net revenue, but **the original payment's fee isn't returned**: it's a cost incurred when the payment was processed.

## What happens to the fee in a dispute

If a customer disputes the charge with their bank (chargeback), the amount is held while it's resolved. If you lose the dispute, that money leaves your balance along with a processor dispute fee, and Cord's original fee isn't returned either. See [Disputes and chargebacks](/en/docs/pagos/disputas).

## Advantages of collecting with Cord Payments

1. **Automatic reconciliation.** Each payment is tied to its quote or invoice and the status changes to paid as soon as it's confirmed.
2. **The money is yours, straight to your account.** Cord doesn't hold your funds or act as a middleman for the money: you're the merchant of record of every payment.
3. **Checkout with your branding.** Your customer pays inside the document, with your name and colors.
4. **Split payments without double work.** Deposit and balance, installments, partial payments, and retainers all come from the same link.

## Before you activate

1. **Payout timing.** Money doesn't reach your bank instantly: it first lands in your payments account balance and is paid out at the [frequency you choose](/en/docs/pagos/depositos).
2. **Identity verification.** To activate Cord Payments you go through a regulatory verification of the business and the people who control it. See [Connect your account](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding).
3. **Chargebacks.** If a customer disputes a card charge, the business that collected is responsible. Cord gives you the tools to defend yourself with evidence.

<Callout type="info">
**Tip:** on very large quotes in pesos, manual transfer is still the no-cost option, at the price of losing automatic reconciliation and verifying each deposit by hand.
</Callout>

## Limits

- The platform fee only applies to MXN payments today.
- The fee isn't returned on refunds or disputes.
- The fee can't be negotiated per account from the app.

## Common problems

**I don't see the section to accept the fee.** You already accepted the current version, you don't have the **Payment settings** permission, or your payments account isn't connected yet.

**My USD payment shows no Cord fee.** That's expected: there's no platform fee outside MXN today.

**Net deposited doesn't match what I collected.** Check the **Where every peso goes** widget on the [payments dashboard](/en/docs/pagos/dashboard): it separates fees, refunds, and disputes.
