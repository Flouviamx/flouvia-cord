## The fee by method

Cord Payments charges a per-transaction fee that **already includes** the cost of processing — there's no second, hidden fee from a third party added on top later. The fee depends on the collection method.

<Callout type="info">
**This table is the current fee schedule for payments in Mexican pesos (MXN)** — today the only rail where Cord Payments has a published, active fee schedule. If you charge in any other currency (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, BRL), **Cord applies no platform fee at all on that payment today**: you pay only your processor's cost and your Cord subscription. Settings › Payments says the same thing there, in your account's currency.
</Callout>

| Method | Total fee | Example on a $10,000.00 MXN payment |
|---|---|---|
| **Card** (credit/debit) | 4% + $3.00 MXN + VAT | $467.48 MXN |
| **SPEI** (automatic bank transfer) | 1% + $7.00 MXN + VAT, up to a **maximum of $588.12 MXN** per payment | $124.12 MXN |
| **Retainers** (monthly recurring charge) | 4% + $3.00 MXN + VAT per monthly charge | — |
| **Manual transfer** | $0.00 MXN — doesn't go through Cord Payments | $0.00 MXN |

<Callout type="info">
VAT (Mexican IVA, 16%) is calculated over the fee (its percentage component plus its fixed component), never over the total payment amount. The examples already include it: by card, (4% of $10,000.00 + $3.00) × 1.16 = $467.48 MXN; by SPEI, (1% of $10,000.00 + $7.00) × 1.16 = $124.12 MXN.
</Callout>

### Why SPEI has a cap and cards don't

The cost of processing a SPEI transfer is nearly the same regardless of the amount. That's why the percentage part of the SPEI fee (the 1%) is capped at **$500.00 MXN** before VAT, a cap reached on a **$50,000.00 MXN** payment. From that amount on, the total fee for a SPEI payment stops growing and stays at **$588.12 MXN**: ($500.00 + the $7.00 fixed component) × 1.16 VAT, no matter how large the payment gets:

| Payment amount | Total card fee | Total SPEI fee |
|---|---|---|
| $50,000.00 MXN | $2,323.48 MXN (4.65%) | **$588.12 MXN** (1.18% effective) |
| $100,000.00 MXN | $4,643.48 MXN (4.64%) | **$588.12 MXN** (0.59% effective) |
| $500,000.00 MXN | $23,203.48 MXN (4.64%) | **$588.12 MXN** (0.12% effective) |

For large payments in pesos, this makes SPEI by far the cheapest online method in Cord Payments.

### Retainers and recurring charges

A retainer (monthly recurring quote) is always charged by card — it's the only way for the charge to go out automatically each month without the customer having to do anything. The fee is the **same as a regular card payment**: 4% + $3.00 MXN + VAT, applied to each individual monthly charge. There is no special reduced rate for retainers: the cost of processing a card is the same whether it's charged once or twelve times.

### Manual transfer — still free

If you'd rather share your bank details directly and have the customer transfer outside Cord Payments (uploading proof of payment for you to mark the charge by hand), that option still carries no Cord fee. It doesn't go through Cord Payments, so it doesn't generate any fee — but it also doesn't have automatic reconciliation or chargeback protection.

## How your fee schedule gets activated

Cord Payments requires you to explicitly accept the current fee schedule before you can start charging. This happens in **Settings › Payments**:

- **New account:** acceptance is the last step of the connection wizard (see [Connect your account](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding), the "Terms" step) — you can't finish connecting your account without checking the box that accepts the current fee schedule.
- **Already-connected account, or if the schedule changes:** go to **Settings › Payments** (you need the *Configure payments* permission), review the current fee schedule and check the acceptance box.

Until you accept, your account can stay connected and keep receiving manual payments, but it **cannot process card or SPEI** through Cord Payments.

<Callout type="warning">
**Accounts from before Cord Payments launched:** if your business was already accepting payments online before this fee schedule existed, your existing payments stay protected at **0% commission** until you, voluntarily, accept the current schedule in Settings › Payments. Nobody changes your rate without you confirming it first.
</Callout>

If we ever change the fee schedule, it does not apply retroactively to an already-given acceptance — you'll need to accept the new version again before it charges you differently. The full legal text lives in [Terms and Conditions, Cord Payments section](https://cordhq.app/en/terminos#cord-pagos).

## What happens to the fee if you refund a payment

If you refund a customer from **My Payments › Refund**, the refunded amount is deducted from your net revenue in the reports — but **the processing fee on the original payment is not refunded**. It's a cost already incurred at the moment the card or SPEI transfer was processed, whether the payment stays or you refund it later.

## What happens to the fee in a dispute (chargeback)

If a customer disputes a charge with their bank (a chargeback), you'll see the case in **My Payments › Disputes**. While the dispute is open, the amount is held; if you lose it, that money (plus a dispute fee) comes out of your balance — the original Cord Payments fee is not refunded in this case either. See [Disputes and chargebacks](/en/docs/pagos/disputas) for the full defense process.

## Advantages of collecting with Cord Payments

1. **100% automatic reconciliation.** Every payment gets tied to its cotización and the status flips to paid in real time — no more chasing bank deposits.
2. **The money is yours, straight to your account.** Cord Payments never holds your funds or acts as a middleman for the money — you're the merchant of record for every payment.
3. **Checkout with your brand.** Your customer pays on a screen embedded in the document, with your name and colors — no redirects to external sites, no third-party logos.
4. **Down payments, balances, and retainers.** The same system collects a down payment today and the balance later (Net 30/60), or a monthly retainer automatically, without you having to chase the payment by hand.

## Things to consider before activating

1. **Payout timing.** Money doesn't land in your bank instantly. Each payment first goes to the balance of your payments account and is paid out at the frequency you choose in **Settings › Payments** (daily, weekly, monthly, or manual). While your account's verification has pending requirements, payouts may be paused; that same screen tells you so.
2. **Identity verification (KYC).** Activating Cord Payments requires you to pass a verification with your business's tax identifier, the details of the people who control it, and their identity document. Exactly what is requested depends on your country and entity type. It's a regulatory requirement, not a Cord decision. See [Connect your account](/en/docs/pagos/onboarding).
3. **Chargebacks.** If a customer disputes a charge, the money can be held while the dispute is resolved. Cord gives you the tools to defend yourself with evidence, but responsibility for the chargeback belongs to the business that collected the payment.

<Callout type="info">
**Recommendation:** for very large cotizaciones where the SPEI cap no longer offers a clear advantage, Manual Transfer (0% fee) remains the cheapest option — in exchange for losing automatic reconciliation and dispute protection.
</Callout>
