<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Automatic payment reminders</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Cord reminds your customer of what's coming due and what's overdue, once per stage. You choose when, or turn them off.</p>
</header>

## What they are

Automatic reminders are emails Cord sends to your customer when a document is about to fall due or is already overdue. They work on every plan and come turned on: they run once a day for every open invoice and every approved quote that meets the conditions on this page.

You choose the invoice schedule in **Settings › Invoicing › Reminders**. If you never change it, Cord uses the usual ladder.

They're different from two other collection tools:

- **[AI collections](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia)** (Pro, Scale, and Developer) writes personalized emails for overdue accounts and can negotiate a payment plan.
- **The WhatsApp reminder** in [receivables](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza) is one you prepare and send yourself, whenever you want.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Plan | All plans. |
| Permission | None for them to go out: Cord sends them on its own. To change the schedule or turn them off, the **Settings** permission; to pause a customer, the **Collections** permission. |
| Customer | Must have an email on file. No email, no reminder. |
| Invoice | Issued (not a draft), open, with a due date and an outstanding balance. |
| Quote | Approved or invoiced, with a balance, not a recurring retainer, and whose balance isn't already carried by an open invoice (in that case the invoice is reminded). |
| Account | Doesn't apply to test accounts or the demo account. |

## Invoices: the reminder ladder

Every open invoice goes through a ladder of stages, counted in days from its due date. The **default** ladder has six:

| Stage | When | What your customer receives |
|---|---|---|
| 7 days before | One week before the due date | **Invoice [number] is coming due** |
| 1 day before | The day before the due date | **Invoice [number] is coming due** |
| 3 days after | Three days overdue | **Past due: invoice [number]** |
| 7 days after | One week overdue | **Past due: invoice [number]** |
| 14 days after | Two weeks overdue | **Past due: invoice [number]** |
| 30 days after | One month overdue | **Past due: invoice [number]** |

After the last stage of your schedule, Cord stops sending automatic reminders for that invoice.

Days are counted on **your business's calendar day**, using the time zone in **Settings › General**: a stage doesn't go out a day early or a day late because your customer or the server is in another time zone.

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings › Invoicing › Reminders**.
2. In **Invoice schedule**, select the moments when you want your customer to get a reminder: before the due date, on the due date, or after.
3. Check the **Your client receives** line: it spells out the full schedule as it will go out.
4. Click **Save changes**.
</Steps>

You can choose from:

| Moment | Options |
|---|---|
| Before the due date | 14, 7, 3, or 1 day before |
| On the due date | **Due today** |
| After the due date | 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, or 90 days after |

Up to **8 reminders** per invoice, and at least one. **Restore the default schedule** brings back the usual ladder.

### What happens to invoices already in progress

The change applies from the next daily run, including invoices that already received reminders:

- **A stage that already went out is never repeated**, even if it stays in your schedule.
- **A stage that falls before one already sent is skipped.** If the 7-days-before notice already went out and you add 14 days before, that invoice doesn't get a late notice; new invoices do.
- **A new later stage does go out** when the invoice reaches it. For example, if you add 60 days after, an invoice that already went through the 30-day stage gets the 60-day one when it's 60 days overdue.

### Turn reminders off

The **Send automatic reminders** switch stops the emails to your customers: invoice reminders and quote notices. Everything that doesn't go to the customer keeps working: your **Overdue payment** alert for quotes (if it's on in [Notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones)) and the **An invoice becomes overdue** event. [AI collections](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia) has its own switch.

If you turn them back on, each invoice gets only the most recent stage it already reached, not the ones skipped while reminders were off.

## Pause a customer

If a customer asked you not to write to them, pause their reminders from their record:

{/* MOCKUP: customer record in Clients (copy of the "Automatic reminders" block in the side column of src/pages/app/clientes/[id].astro): "On." state with the text "Cord reminds this client of invoices and quotes coming due and overdue." and the "Pause reminders" button; and the same block paused: "Paused." with "Cord sends this client no reminders and no AI collections emails." and the "Resume reminders" button. */}

<Steps>
1. Open **Clients** and go to the customer.
2. In **Automatic reminders**, click **Pause reminders** and confirm.
</Steps>

The pause is the same **Never email** list used by [AI collections](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia): a paused customer gets no automatic reminders and no emails from the agent. The other way around, a customer, quote, or invoice you add to **Never email** from the agent doesn't get reminders either. Paused customers appear in **Settings › Invoicing › Reminders › Paused clients**, with a **Resume** button.

## What your customer receives

<DmInvoiceReminderEmail lang="en" />
- **Subject:** the matching notice followed by your business name: **Invoice [number] is coming due** before the due date, **Invoice [number] is due today** on the due date, and **Past due: invoice [number]** after.
- **Language:** your account's language, the one you choose in **Settings › General**.
- **Body:** the invoice number, the due date, and the **outstanding balance with its currency**. The balance already deducts partial payments and credit notes, so you never remind your customer about money they've already paid.
- **Pay invoice button:** opens the invoice link, where they can pay the full balance or pay part of it. See [Create, issue, and collect invoices](/en/docs/pagos/facturas-emitidas).
- **Your signature, sender, and reply-to:** from Starter, the reminder carries the signature, display name, and reply-to address from **Settings › Email**. The introduction from that screen doesn't: it's written for the email you send the document with, and the reminder keeps its due-date message.
- **Branding:** the email uses your logo and color. If you connected your [business Gmail](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/gmail), it goes out from your Gmail account; otherwise it goes out from Cord with your business name.

Each reminder sent appears on the **invoice timeline** (for example, a due-date notice 7 days before, a due-date notice on the due date, or a collection reminder 3 days overdue) and in the audit log.

### One stage, one email

Cord records the stage **before** sending the email, and each stage can be recorded only once per invoice. In practice:

- **No duplicates.** Even if the daily process runs twice on the same day, your customer receives a single email per stage.
- **A failed send is retried.** The stage is released and tried again the next day.
- **No bursts.** If an invoice enters the ladder late (for example, you issued it already 20 days overdue), Cord sends only the most recent stage that applies, not every earlier one at once.
- **The same money isn't reminded twice.** If the invoice comes from a quote that received its own reminder that same day, the invoice waits until the next day.

### The "An invoice becomes overdue" event

The day after an invoice with a balance falls due, Cord emits the **An invoice becomes overdue** event (`invoice.overdue`) once. It reaches your [webhooks](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/webhooks) and can trigger a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/disparadores), for example to alert your team in Slack or create a follow-up task. It goes out even if you turned reminders off or paused the customer: it's for you, not for them.

## Quotes: due-date notice

Approved quotes with an outstanding balance get a simpler notice: **two emails, one three days before the due date and another on the day it's due**. These two moments are fixed; they follow the reminders switch and the per-customer pause. The due date is the approval day plus the payment term's days:

| Term | Due |
|---|---|
| Due on receipt | On the approval date |
| Net 7, 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 | That many calendar days after approval |

The email, with your logo and color, shows the quote number, the quote's **balance** with its currency (the total minus what was already collected as a deposit or installments), and the due date, with a **View and pay [quote number]** button that opens the quote link. The subject is **Payment reminder — [quote number]**; it goes out in your account's language and, from Starter, with your signature. See [Credit terms](/en/docs/pagos/credito).

<Callout type="info">
With **Due on receipt** terms, the due date matches the approval, so only the due-day notice goes out, and it may arrive the same day your customer approves.
</Callout>

## When no reminder is sent

- You turned automatic reminders off, or the customer is paused.
- The customer has no email on file.
- The invoice is already paid, voided, marked uncollectible, or still a draft.
- The invoice has no due date or its balance is zero.
- The invoice has gone through every stage of your schedule, or is more than 120 days overdue.
- The stage that would apply falls before one that already went out (see **What happens to invoices already in progress**, above).
- The quote is fully paid, isn't approved, or is a [recurring retainer](/en/docs/pagos/igualas): retainers charge themselves every month and are never treated as overdue receivables.
- The account is a test account or the demo account.

## Limits

- One invoice reminder per stage, up to 8 stages, chosen from the menu on this page.
- The reminder text can't be edited; from Starter it carries your signature. If you need a different message or channel, create a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows) with the **An invoice becomes overdue** trigger and a [wait](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/esperas).
- Quote notices have two fixed moments and no ladder after the due date. To chase overdue quotes, use [receivables](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza) or [AI collections](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia).
- Reminders don't attach the PDF; the customer downloads it from the invoice link.

## Common problems

**My customer says they didn't get the reminder.** Check that their record has an email, that they aren't paused, and that reminders are on. Then look for the event on the invoice timeline. If it isn't there, the send failed and is retried the next day.

**They got a reminder for an invoice they already paid by transfer.** The reminder goes out while the invoice has a balance. Record the payment on the invoice as soon as you verify it.

**I added a stage and an invoice didn't get it.** If that invoice had already received a later stage, the new one falls behind and isn't sent, so no late notice goes out. New invoices do get it.

**I don't want a customer to get reminders.** Pause their reminders from their record in **Clients** (see [Pause a customer](#pause-a-customer)).
