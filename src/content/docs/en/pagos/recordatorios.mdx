<header class="content-header">
  <h1 class="page-title">Automatic payment reminders</h1>
  <p class="page-subtitle">Cord reminds your customer of what's coming due and what's overdue, once per stage, without you scheduling anything.</p>
</header>

## What they are

Automatic reminders are emails Cord sends to your customer when a document is about to fall due or is already overdue. They work on every plan and don't need to be turned on: they run once a day for every open invoice and every approved quote that meets the conditions on this page.

They're different from two other collection tools:

- **[AI collections](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia)** (Pro, Scale, and Developer) writes personalized emails for overdue accounts and can negotiate a payment plan.
- **The WhatsApp reminder** in [receivables](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza) is one you prepare and send yourself, whenever you want.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Plan | All plans. |
| Permission | None: Cord sends them on its own. |
| Customer | Must have an email on file. No email, no reminder. |
| Invoice | Issued (not a draft), open, with a due date and an outstanding balance. |
| Quote | Approved or invoiced, with a balance, not a recurring retainer, and whose balance isn't already carried by an open invoice (in that case the invoice is reminded). |
| Account | Doesn't apply to test accounts or the demo account. |

## Invoices: the reminder ladder

Every open invoice goes through a ladder of **six stages**, counted in days from its due date:

| Stage | When | What your customer receives |
|---|---|---|
| 7 days before | One week before the due date | Coming-due notice |
| 1 day before | The day before the due date | Coming-due notice |
| 3 days after | Three days overdue | Past-due notice |
| 7 days after | One week overdue | Past-due notice |
| 14 days after | Two weeks overdue | Past-due notice |
| 30 days after | One month overdue | Past-due notice |

After the 30-day stage, Cord stops sending automatic reminders for that invoice.

### What your customer receives

<DmInvoiceReminderEmail lang="en" />
- **Subject:** "Tu factura [number] está por vencer" (coming due) or "Factura vencida [number]" (past due), followed by your business name.
- **Body:** the invoice number, the due date, and the **outstanding balance with its currency**. The balance already deducts partial payments and credit notes, so you never remind your customer about money they've already paid.
- **Pay invoice button:** opens the invoice link, where they can pay the full balance or pay part of it. See [Create, issue, and collect invoices](/en/docs/pagos/facturas-emitidas).
- **Branding:** the email uses your logo and color. If you connected your [business Gmail](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/gmail), it goes out from your Gmail account; otherwise it goes out from Cord with your business name.

Each reminder sent appears on the **invoice timeline** (for example, a due-date notice 7 days before, or a collection reminder 3 days overdue) and in the audit log.

### One stage, one email

Cord records the stage **before** sending the email, and each stage can be recorded only once per invoice. In practice:

- **No duplicates.** Even if the daily process runs twice on the same day, your customer receives a single email per stage.
- **A failed send is retried.** The stage is released and tried again the next day.
- **No bursts.** If an invoice enters the ladder late (for example, you issued it already 20 days overdue), Cord sends only the most recent stage that applies, not every earlier one at once.
- **The same money isn't reminded twice.** If the invoice comes from a quote that received its own reminder that same day, the invoice waits until the next day.

### The "An invoice becomes overdue" event

The day after an invoice with a balance falls due, Cord emits the **An invoice becomes overdue** event (`invoice.overdue`) once. It reaches your [webhooks](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/webhooks) and can trigger a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/disparadores), for example to alert your team in Slack or create a follow-up task.

## Quotes: due-date notice

Approved quotes with an outstanding balance get a simpler notice: **two emails, one three days before the due date and another on the day it's due**. The due date is the approval date plus the payment term's days:

| Term | Due |
|---|---|
| Due on receipt | On the approval date |
| Net 7, 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 | That many calendar days after approval |

The email, with your logo and color, shows the quote number, the quote's **balance** with its currency (the total minus what was already collected as a deposit or installments), and the due date, with a **View and pay [quote number]** button that opens the quote link. The subject is **Payment reminder — [quote number]**, and it goes out in your account's language. See [Credit terms](/en/docs/pagos/credito).

<Callout type="info">
With **Due on receipt** terms, the due date matches the approval, so only the due-day notice goes out, and it may arrive the same day your customer approves.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Invoice reminders are sent in Spanish today">
Automatic **invoice** reminders currently go out in Spanish even if your account is set to English; **quote** notices already go out in your account's language. If your customers need English invoice reminders, use a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows) with your own message, as described below.
</Callout>

## When no reminder is sent

- The customer has no email on file.
- The invoice is already paid, voided, marked uncollectible, or still a draft.
- The invoice has no due date or its balance is zero.
- The invoice is more than 30 days overdue and has gone through every stage.
- The quote is fully paid, isn't approved, or is a [recurring retainer](/en/docs/pagos/igualas): retainers charge themselves every month and are never treated as overdue receivables.
- The account is a test account or the demo account.

## Can I change the cadence?

Not from the app today. Cord stores the ladder per organization, but there's no screen yet to edit it or to turn reminders off: every account uses the 7 days before, 1 day before, and 3, 7, 14, and 30 days after ladder.

If you need a different pace or your own message, create a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows) with the **An invoice becomes overdue** trigger and a [wait](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/esperas) before sending the email. The custom content in **Settings › Email** is used when sending quotes and invoices, not in reminders: they keep their own message.

## Limits

- One invoice reminder per stage, six stages at most.
- The cadence and text can't be edited from the app.
- Invoice reminders are sent in Spanish, regardless of the account language; quote notices follow your account's language.
- Reminders don't attach the PDF; the customer downloads it from the invoice link.
- The quote notice has no ladder after the due date. To chase overdue quotes, use [receivables](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza) or [AI collections](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia).

## Common problems

**My customer says they didn't get the reminder.** Check that their record has an email and look for the event on the invoice timeline. If it isn't there, the send failed and is retried the next day.

**They got a reminder for an invoice they already paid by transfer.** The reminder goes out while the invoice has a balance. Record the payment on the invoice as soon as you verify it.

**I don't want a customer to get reminders.** There's no exclusion for automatic reminders today. The **Never email** list in AI collections only applies to the agent.
