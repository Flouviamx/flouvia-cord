## What a dispute is

A dispute (chargeback) happens when a cardholder asks their bank to reverse a charge: because they don't recognize it, because they say they didn't receive what they paid for, or for another reason. The bank holds the amount while it's resolved, and you have a limited time to present evidence that the charge was legitimate.

It applies to **card** payments processed by Cord Payments. SPEI or a manual transfer has no card chargeback, and [Mercado Pago](/en/docs/pagos/mercado-pago) chargebacks are handled from your Mercado Pago account: Cord doesn't read them today.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Plan | All plans. |
| Permission | **Collections** or **Payment settings**. Without them, the dispute page shows "No access". |
| Submission | Also confirming your identity with your password or your two-step verification code. |

## Where it appears

<Steps>
1. Cord records the dispute as soon as the processor reports it, with amount, currency, reason, status, and deadline.
2. In [My money › Payments](/en/docs/pagos/dashboard), the **Open disputes** widget shows the total at stake and, for each dispute, "respond before [date]".
3. Opening a dispute takes you to its evidence page, **Build a clear response**, with the amount, the status, and the **due date** at the top.
</Steps>

If you use the API or Workflows, Cord emits **A dispute is opened** (`dispute.created`) and **A dispute is closed** (`dispute.closed`, with the outcome). See [Workflow triggers](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/disparadores).

<Callout type="warning" title="The deadline rules">
If you don't submit evidence before the deadline, you normally lose the dispute. The deadline is set by the card network, not by Cord: start working on it as soon as you see it.
</Callout>

## Statuses

The status appears next to the amount with the processor's name for it:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| `needs response` | Evidence must be submitted before the deadline. |
| `under review` | You already submitted evidence and the customer's bank is reviewing it. |
| `won` | You won: the held money returns to your balance. |
| `lost` | You lost: the amount leaves your balance. |
| `warning needs response`, `warning under review`, `warning closed` | An early inquiry from the bank that isn't a chargeback yet. Respond anyway: it can become one. |

## The evidence

The page arrives **prefilled** with what Cord already knows about the transaction. Review and correct each field:

| Field | What Cord proposes |
|---|---|
| **Customer name** and **Customer email** | Those on the quote's customer record. |
| **Service date** | The quote's approval date. Change it to the actual delivery date. |
| **Approval IP** | The IP address from the quote's electronic signature, if there is one. |
| **What the customer purchased** | The quote's items with their quantities. |
| **Customer communication** | Empty: paste the relevant messages here (acknowledgments, delivery confirmations). |
| **Additional context** | Empty: explain the case in your own words. |
| **Receipt or supporting file** | Optional: your own PDF, JPG, or PNG. If you don't choose one, Cord prepares an automatic receipt. |

### What Cord builds for you

On submission, Cord generates two PDFs:

1. **Transaction receipt:** your business, the customer, the amount, the method, the transaction, the items and, when available, **who signed the quote, on what date, and from which IP address**. If you uploaded your own receipt, yours is used.
2. **Customer communication:** a PDF with the text you pasted in that field.

## Save a draft and submit

<Steps>
1. Click **Save draft** as many times as you like. The draft stores only text in Cord: it doesn't upload files or send anything to the processor.
2. When ready, click **Review and submit evidence**. You'll see a **preview** with the exact list of what will be transferred.
3. Click **Confirm and transfer** and confirm your identity when Cord asks.
4. You'll see **Evidence submitted** and return to Payments.
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Submitting is irreversible">
Once submitted, the evidence can't be edited or completed. Review every field before confirming.
</Callout>

Saving, preparing the PDFs, and submitting are recorded in your organization's audit log.

## What happens next

The processor delivers the evidence and **the customer's bank decides**. It can take weeks and the outcome isn't guaranteed: Cord helps you build the best possible case, but doesn't decide. The status updates on its own on the dispute page.

| If… | Then |
|---|---|
| You win | The held amount returns to your balance. |
| You lose | The amount leaves your balance, along with a processor dispute fee. |
| Either way | Cord's fee on the original payment isn't returned. See [Fees](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones). |

## How to prevent disputes

- Require an **electronic signature** on approval: the signature's name, date, and IP are your best evidence. See [Electronic signature](/en/docs/interaccion/firma).
- Use clear item descriptions and a recognizable business name.
- Answer your customers' questions in the quote chat: that history is evidence too.

## Limits

- Card payments processed by Cord Payments only.
- A single evidence submission per dispute.
- No Mercado Pago disputes in Cord.

## Common problems

**I can't find the dispute.** Check the dashboard's date range and that you have the **Collections** or **Payment settings** permission.

**The approval IP is empty.** The quote had no electronic signature. Explain in the additional context how your customer confirmed the purchase.

**Submitting asks for my password again.** Identity confirmation lasts a few minutes; if more time passed, Cord asks again.
