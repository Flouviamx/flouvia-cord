## The split payment model

In service-based projects (agencies, consultancies, software development), it is very common not to charge 100% upfront, but rather to require an initial deposit (e.g., 50%) to kick off the work and settle the balance upon delivery.

In traditional invoicing platforms, this forces you to generate **two different payment links** or duplicate the invoice. In Cord, the quote is **smart** and handles split payments without you having to do double the work.

### How to configure an upfront payment

1. Open the quote editor.
2. In **Who are you quoting?**, choose the payment term. With **Due on receipt**, the balance can be paid as soon as the deposit is covered; with a credit term (**Net 7** to **Net 90**), the balance is due that many days after approval and is enabled for payment on that date.
3. In **Review and send**, enter the percentage in **Deposit (%)**, between 1 and 99 (e.g., `50`).
4. The summary updates instantly and shows how much your client will pay on approval and how much balance remains.

<Callout type="info">
**Productivity tip:** In *Settings > Quotes > Numbering & tax* you can set **Default deposit (%)**. If you set it to 50, all your new quotes will be pre-configured with that model.
</Callout>

## What does the client see?

When the client opens the public link of the quote, below the total amount they will see a clear breakdown:

- **Project total:** $10,000.00 MXN
- **Pay upfront today (50%):** $5,000.00 MXN
- **Balance:** $5,000.00 MXN (due according to the payment term)

### Automatic payment button mutation

Each part of the quote — the upfront payment and the balance — is an independent payment within the same link:

1. **First charge:** The client clicks "Approve and Pay". The button charges them **only the $5,000.00 MXN** of the upfront payment. Once processed, the quote switches to "Approved" and is ready for you to start working.
2. **Second charge:** Once the balance is enabled, the client can **re-enter the exact same link** from the original quote. The system will detect that the upfront payment has already been made, and the payment button will have automatically mutated to charge the **remaining Balance**.

You didn't have to send a second email, nor generate a "payment link for the balance". Everything lives in the same ecosystem.

<Callout type="info">
Each payment (the upfront payment and the balance) is an independent transaction for fee purposes: on payments in Mexican pesos (MXN), Cord Payments charges its fee on each one separately, using the same fee schedule as a normal payment. In other currencies, Cord applies no platform fee today. See [Fees and advantages](/en/docs/pagos/condiciones).
</Callout>
