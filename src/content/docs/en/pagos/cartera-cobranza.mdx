## What it is

**Collections** (sidebar › **Revenue**) is an operating list **as of today**. It has no date selector because it answers a single question: what needs action now? Historical analysis (full aging, days to collect, payment behavior) lives in **Reports › Collections and receivables**. See [Reports and performance](/en/docs/gestion/informes).

## What goes into the list

The Collections list comes from the [unified accounts receivable view](#the-unified-accounts-receivable-view), so it brings both rails together:

- **approved or invoiced quotes that aren't paid yet**, due on the approval date plus the payment term's days;
- **open invoices** with a balance, with their own due date, including those issued without a quote;
- except [recurring retainers](/en/docs/pagos/igualas), which charge themselves every month and are never treated as overdue receivables.

Each account shows its **balance**: for a quote, the total minus payments already received (deposits, installments); for an invoice, the total minus partial payments and credit notes. Amounts are expressed in your business currency; if you sell in several currencies, the note below the page says how they were converted and which one was left out for lack of an available exchange rate.

<Callout type="info" title="Invoices in the list">
An invoice is collected from its own detail, because that's where the payment is recorded in its history. That's why invoice rows show **View invoice** instead of **Paid**, and payment promises are recorded only on quote rows. Remind via WhatsApp works on both.
</Callout>

### The unified accounts receivable view

Cord keeps a single view that joins quotes and invoices with a common shape: origin, number, currency, total, paid, **balance**, and days overdue. Rules of that view:

- A quote with an **open invoice** appears only as the invoice, because the invoice carries the real balance; counting it twice would double your receivables.
- An invoice's balance already deducts partial payments and credit notes.
- Recurring retainers don't enter.

It's used by the [AI collections agent](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia), each customer's **account statement**, and [Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/consultas) receivables queries.

## The table and its actions

The table filters by **All**, **Overdue**, **With promise**, or **Untouched**. Each row shows customer, number, terms, due date, status, and balance, with these actions:

### Record a payment promise

<Steps>
1. In the row, open the payment promise.
2. Enter the **date** the customer committed to pay, the **amount** (optional), and a **note** of up to 400 characters.
3. Save. The promise appears in the widgets and the account no longer counts as "untouched".
4. When the date arrives, mark it **fulfilled** or let it lapse as broken. If you entered it by mistake, delete it.
</Steps>

A promise helps organize follow-up and measure compliance. It does **not** lower the balance, extend the due date, run a charge, or remove the account from overdue. If you edit an active promise, the new one replaces the previous one.

### Remind via WhatsApp

**Remind via WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message: customer, number, total, due date, and document link. If the customer record has a phone number, it opens the conversation with that number; otherwise you choose the contact. Review the text before sending it: delivery happens in WhatsApp, not in Cord.

### Record a manual payment

The **Paid** button marks the quote as paid, after a confirmation ("Record the payment of [number] for [amount]?"). On invoices, **View invoice** opens its detail so you record the payment there.

<Callout type="warning">
Recording a payment is a manual record: it doesn't create a charge or confirm a deposit. Use it only after verifying the transfer in your bank, and never to simulate revenue.
</Callout>

## Automatic reminders

Besides what you do from this page, Cord sends [automatic payment reminders](/en/docs/pagos/recordatorios) on its own: a ladder before and after each invoice's due date, and a notice in the days before each quote's due date.

## Late interest

Automatic late interest is **suspended** while the legal review by country concludes: new rates can't be configured today and the monthly process generates no charges. Interest recorded earlier still appears separately to keep the balance traceable.

## Limits

- A promise doesn't change the balance, and it's only recorded on quotes.
- **Paid** marks the whole quote as paid: record partial payments on the document. Invoices are collected from their detail.
- A sale in a currency without an available exchange rate isn't added to the totals until one exists.

## Common problems

**Collections doesn't appear.** Check that your plan is Pro or higher and that you have the **Collections** permission.

**A customer paid the deposit and the account is still on the list.** That's expected: the row shows the remaining balance, already without the deposit, until everything is paid.

**I don't see an overdue invoice.** The list includes open invoices with a due date and a balance. A draft, voided, already paid, or undated invoice doesn't appear; look for it in **Invoices**.

**An amount doesn't match the document.** The list expresses everything in your business currency. Check the note below the page: a currency without an available exchange rate isn't added.

**A promise didn't lower the balance.** That's intentional: only a recorded payment lowers it.
