## What it is

**Collections** (sidebar › **Revenue › Collections**; on desktop, **G** then **B**) is an operating list **as of today**. It has no date selector because it answers a single question: what needs action now? Historical analysis (full aging, days to collect, payment behavior) lives in **Reports › Collections and receivables**. See [Reports and performance](/en/docs/gestion/informes).

The orange-tinted number next to **Collections** in the sidebar counts overdue receivables from quotes and invoices, so you can see from any screen that something needs collecting. With the sidebar collapsed it becomes a dot. Below Collections, while you're in this section, the [AI agent](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia) unfolds.

## What goes into the list

The Collections list brings together, in a single table, everything you're owed today, from quotes and from invoices:

| Origin | It goes in when | Due |
|---|---|---|
| **Invoice** | It's issued and open (not a draft, and not paid, void, or marked uncollectible), has a due date, and still has a balance. This includes invoices issued without a quote. | On the invoice's due date. |
| **Quote** | It's approved or invoiced, not yet paid, and has no open invoice. | On the approval date plus the days of its payment term (with **Due on receipt**, the same day it was approved). |

Rules worth knowing:

- **Each account shows its balance due, not its total.** On a quote, the balance deducts what was already collected (for example, the deposit or paid installments); on an invoice, it deducts partial payments and credit notes. The table column is called **Balance**.
- **A sale counts only once.** When a quote already has an open invoice, it appears only as that invoice, because the invoice carries the real balance. So once you issue a quote's invoice, the account shows with the invoice's number and due date.
- **[Recurring retainers](/en/docs/pagos/igualas) don't go in:** they charge themselves every month and are never treated as overdue receivables.
- **Multiple currencies:** if you sell in more than one, amounts are restated in your business currency with the exchange rate set on each document (or, when there is none, today's published rate), and a note at the top of the page says so. If a currency has no exchange rate available, those accounts aren't added up for now and the note says that too.

These same receivables are what the [AI collections agent](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia), each customer's **account statement**, [Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/consultas) receivables queries, and the sidebar count use.

## The table and its actions

The table filters by **All**, **Overdue**, **With promise**, or **Untouched**. Each row shows customer, number (which opens the quote or the invoice), terms (quotes only), due date, status, and **Balance**, with these actions:

| Action | Quotes | Invoices |
|---|---|---|
| Payment promise (calendar icon) | Yes | Not yet |
| Remind via WhatsApp | Yes | Yes |
| Record the payment | **Paid**, right from the row | **View invoice**: the payment or partial payment is recorded on the invoice's detail |

### Record a payment promise

Today promises are recorded on quotes; invoice rows don't show the button.

<Steps>
1. In the row, click **Payment promise** (the calendar icon).
2. Enter the **date** the customer committed to pay, the **amount** (optional), and a **note** of up to 400 characters.
3. Save. The promise appears in the widgets and the account no longer counts as "untouched".
4. When the date arrives, mark it **fulfilled** or let it lapse as broken. If you entered it by mistake, delete it.
</Steps>

A promise helps organize follow-up and measure compliance. It does **not** lower the balance, extend the due date, run a charge, or remove the account from overdue. If you edit an active promise, the new one replaces the previous one.

### Remind via WhatsApp

**Remind via WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message: customer, number, balance due, due date, and document link (the quote's or the invoice's). If the customer record has a phone number, it opens the conversation with that number; otherwise you choose the contact. Review the text before sending it: delivery happens in WhatsApp, not in Cord.

### Record a manual payment

- **On a quote,** **Paid** asks for a confirmation ("Record the payment of [number] for [balance]?") and marks the quote as fully paid: it leaves the list with its whole balance.
- **On an invoice,** **View invoice** opens its detail, where you record the full payment or a partial payment. The invoice's balance goes down by what you record, and the invoice leaves the list once no balance remains.

<Callout type="warning">
Recording a payment is a manual record: it doesn't create a charge or confirm a deposit. Use it only after verifying the transfer in your bank, and never to simulate revenue.
</Callout>

## Automatic reminders

Besides what you do from this page, Cord sends [automatic payment reminders](/en/docs/pagos/recordatorios) on its own: a ladder before and after each invoice's due date, and a notice in the days before each quote's due date.

## Late interest

Automatic late interest is **suspended** while the legal review by country concludes: new rates can't be configured today and the monthly process generates no charges. Interest recorded earlier still appears separately to keep the balance traceable.

## Limits

- Only issued invoices with a due date and a balance due go in: drafts and paid, void, or uncollectible invoices don't appear.
- Payment promises are for quotes only.
- A promise doesn't change the balance.
- **Paid** marks the quote as paid for its whole balance: record a partial payment on the document.
- With multiple currencies, an account in a currency with no exchange rate available isn't added up until one is.

## Common problems

**Collections doesn't appear.** Check that your plan is Pro or higher and that you have the **Collections** permission.

**An account's amount doesn't match the document's total.** That's expected: the list shows the **balance due**, already without the deposit, installments, or partial payments your customer made, or an invoice's credit notes.

**A quote disappeared from the list.** If it was paid, nothing is owed anymore. If you issued its invoice, it now shows as that invoice, with the invoice's number and due date.

**I don't see an invoice.** Check that it's issued (not a draft), has a due date, and still has a balance. Paid, void, or uncollectible invoices don't go in.

**I can't record a promise on an invoice.** Today promises are recorded on quotes only. To pause automatic follow-up on an invoice while you wait for an agreed payment, add it to **Never email** in the [collections agent](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia).

**An account in another currency isn't added up.** There's no exchange rate available for that currency for now; the note at the top of the page says so.

**A promise didn't lower the balance.** That's intentional: only a recorded payment lowers it.
