## Your new collections manager

Cord includes an Artificial Intelligence Agent (`AI AR`) designed specifically to manage accounts receivable. This agent acts autonomously (if you enable it) to recover revenue from quotes and invoices that have exceeded their credit terms.

### How does it work?

1. **24/7 monitoring:** the agent scans your database daily looking for payments that have passed their due date (`Net 30`, `Net 60`, etc.).
2. **Smart contact:** when it detects a delay, it sends the delinquent client an email with a professional, friendly-but-firm tone, requesting that the account be regularized.
3. **Active negotiation:** if the client responds citing cash-flow difficulties, the agent is capable of negotiating. It can structure and offer **payment plans of up to 3 monthly installments**.
4. **Dynamic links:** once an agreement is reached or the client agrees to pay, the agent sends dynamic payment links (SPEI or Card) that update the balance in real time as they're processed.

### 2-Turn Flow

To ensure the AI doesn't send endless emails or drift off-goal, the agent is constrained to a strict **2-turn** architecture:

- **Turn 1:** detecting the overdue balance and first collection notice (or a payment-plan proposal if it detects a prior delay pattern).
- **Turn 2:** responding to the client's negotiation. If no agreement is reached on this turn, the agent "escalates" the ticket to a human (you or your finance team) and stops the automated interaction.

### Benefits

- **Zero confrontation:** removes the emotional friction between your sales team and delinquent clients.
- **Steady cash flow:** debtors respond better to immediate installment options than to plain reminders.
- **Transparent audit trail:** every email sent and every negotiated interaction by the Agent is logged in the quote's "Activity" tab, giving you full control and context.
