## What it is

The **collections agent** (sidebar › **My money › AI Collections**) reviews your overdue receivables every day, drafts an email for each account with the real balance, days overdue, and payment link, and, if the customer needs it, negotiates an [installment payment plan](/en/docs/pagos/cuotas). It starts **off**: it doesn't email anyone until you turn it on.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Plan | Scale or Developer. On Pro, collections are manual from [receivables](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza); Free and Starter don't include collections. |
| Permission | **Collections** to see the page and approve, edit, or discard drafts. **Settings** to turn on and configure the agent. |
| AI quota | Each email drafted or regenerated uses one AI unit from your plan: 500 included per month on Scale (overage is billed as usage) and unlimited on Developer. |
| Customers | With an email on file. Without one, the agent can't send. |
| Account | Doesn't run on test accounts or the demo account. |

## Turn it on

<Steps>
1. Open **AI Collections**. The first time you'll see what the agent does, a sample email, and how many accounts it would email today.
2. Click **Turn on with approval**. The agent starts in **Needs approval** mode: nothing goes out without your sign-off.
3. Click **Configure** to adjust grace, cadence, installments, tone, language, and signature.
4. Check **Up next** to see whom it will email on the next run and whom it won't.
</Steps>

## Settings

| Option | What it controls | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Operating mode** | With approval, the agent drafts and waits. On automatic, it sends on its own. | Needs approval | — |
| **Grace days** | How long it waits after the due date before writing. | 3 days | 0 to 90 |
| **Days between emails** | Minimum before emailing the same account again. | 7 days | 1 to 90 |
| **Offer a plan after** | Days overdue from which it may negotiate installments. | 15 days | 1 to 365 |
| **Maximum instalments** | Cap on a plan's monthly installments. | 3 | 2 to 6 |
| **Minimum amount** | It won't write for smaller balances. | No minimum | — |
| **Maximum per run** | Cap on emails per run. | 25 | 1 to 200 |
| **Tone** | Warm, Professional, or Firm. | Professional | — |
| **Email language** | Spanish or English. | Spanish | — |
| **Signature** | How the email signs off (up to 400 characters). | No signature | — |

## How it decides whom to email

Every day, the agent takes receivables from the [unified accounts receivable view](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza#the-unified-accounts-receivable-view), quotes and invoices, that are **more days overdue than your grace days**, from most to least overdue. From that list it **skips**:

| Reason in "Up next" | Why |
|---|---|
| **Already settled** | The balance is already zero. |
| **Excluded** | The customer, quote, or invoice is on **Never email**. |
| **Below minimum amount** | The balance is below your minimum. |
| **Emailed recently** | There's an unresolved draft, or the last email sent was fewer days ago than your cadence. |
| **Plan on track** | The account has an installment plan and no overdue unpaid installment. |
| **Over the run cap** | The per-run maximum was reached; it goes into the next run. |

Recurring retainers never enter: they charge themselves every month.

## The email

- The agent writes it with the **real balance**, days overdue, and chosen tone, in the configured language.
- It always includes the **payment link**: the invoice's or the quote's. If you collect online, the button says "Pay [amount] online" with the amount due today (the overdue installment, if there's a plan); otherwise "View quote and payment options".
- It goes out under your business's legal name "vía Cord" and replies go to your contact email.
- The footer includes your legal name, your tax ID, a notice that it's an automated collections message sent by Cord on your behalf, and how to ask for the messages to stop.
- **It never offers discounts on the principal.**

## Approval mode and automatic mode

| | Needs approval | Automatic |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Stays as a draft in the **Approval inbox**. | Sent during the run. |
| Installment plan | Stays **proposed**: it doesn't touch the customer's charges until you approve. | Activates when recorded. |
| Notice for you | A single email per run: "N collection emails awaiting approval". | Failed ones show in the inbox. |

In the inbox you can:

- **Approve and send.** If the draft proposes installments, Cord warns you that the plan will replace that quote's pending charges.
- **Edit** the text and **Save changes**.
- **Regenerate**, optionally with an instruction ("shorter and less formal").
- **Discard**: the email doesn't go out and its proposed plan is canceled. The agent may write again on the next run.
- **Don't email them**: adds the account to **Never email**.

If you approve several emails without changing a word, Cord suggests switching to automatic. Your call.

## Installment payment plans

When a quote reaches the **Offer a plan after** days and the customer agrees to pay in parts within the conversation, the agent can propose **2 up to your maximum monthly installments** (up to 6) that add up to exactly the balance. Cord validates the numbers and calculates the amounts; the agent can't invent them. Full details in [Installment payment plans](/en/docs/pagos/cuotas).

## Conversations, exclusions, and promises

- **Conversations:** one thread per account, with each message labeled **Agent**, **Customer**, or **You**. You can take over and write yourself from the thread.
- **Never email:** customers, quotes, or invoices the agent ignores entirely. Click **Remove from list** to bring them back.
- **Payment promises:** recorded from [receivables](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza#record-a-payment-promise). A promise doesn't stop the agent; to pause an account while you wait for an agreed payment, add it to **Never email**.

## Run now

**Run now** runs the agent at that moment with the same logic as the daily run. Use it to test your settings; it still respects cadence and exclusions.

## Indicators

| Indicator | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Recovered by the agent** | What was collected in the last 30 days on accounts the agent emailed. |
| **Awaiting your approval** | Pending drafts (on automatic, it says emails go out on their own). |
| **Active payment plans** | Amount committed in installments. |
| **Emails sent** | In the last 30 days, with the share that replied and those that couldn't be delivered. |

## Limits

- Scale and Developer only.
- One daily run plus the ones you trigger with **Run now**.
- Plans on quotes only, 2 to 6 monthly installments.
- Each email uses AI quota from the plan.
- The agent doesn't send via WhatsApp or SMS.

## Common problems

**The agent doesn't email an overdue account.** Check its reason in **Up next**: grace, cadence, exclusion, minimum amount, plan on track, or run cap.

**I don't see Configure.** You need the **Settings** permission.

**An email shows as "not sent".** The customer has no email or the send failed. Fix the record and try again from the inbox.

**Emails fail because of AI quota.** The period's AI usage cap was reached and the agent couldn't draft. Check your usage in [Subscription](/en/docs/cuenta/suscripcion).
