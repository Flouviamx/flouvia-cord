A Cord client is more than a contact. Its record stores reusable commercial and tax
identity plus terms that control suggested discounts, due dates, and credit exposure.

## Create a client

Open **Clients > New client**. Company name is the only field required to save, but
complete the remaining data before sending a quote that must be collected or invoiced.

| Group | Fields | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Contact | person, email, and phone | Recipient for quotes, invoices, and follow-up. |
| Tax identity | RFC or local tax ID, regime, CFDI use, and postal code | Receiver data used for the fiscal document. |
| Address | country, address lines, city, and region | Receiver address and country context. |
| Terms | due now, Net 30, or Net 60 | Default due date for new client operations. |
| Credit | limit at or above zero | Reference for exposure and over-limit alerts. |
| Price level | Standard, Silver, Gold, or Distributor | Commercial classification and suggested discount. |

Levels initially suggest 0%, 5%, 10%, and 15%. The percentage can be edited from 0%
to 100%; the level is a label while the stored percentage is the effective default.

<Callout type="warning">
The credit limit is an operating signal, not credit issued by Cord or a payment
guarantee. The seller decides whether to keep selling above that exposure.
</Callout>

## Client country

Country may remain empty and inherit the issuer's country. Select it explicitly for a
foreign receiver. Cord accepts receiver countries beyond the 12 markets where the
platform is sold.

Changing country adjusts the expected tax-ID label, but doesn't turn a commercial
invoice into an automatic filing with the receiver's authority.

## Import clients from CSV

The **Clients > Import CSV** flow lets you upload a `.csv`, map columns, preview rows,
and choose whether to update existing matches.

One import accepts up to **2,000 rows**. Rows without a company are skipped. Valid
term codes are `contado`, `net30`, and `net60`; anything else becomes due now.

With **Update existing** enabled, Cord matches by exact tax ID first and case-insensitive
company name second. Matches update contact, email, phone, ID, terms, and limit; other
rows create clients. With it disabled, rows are inserted without merging duplicates.

## Search, filter, and sort

Filter clients with or without quotes, with a non-standard level, with a credit limit,
or with incomplete tax data. Sort by company, closed amount, quote count, recent
activity, or credit limit. These controls only change the view.

## What a client record shows

Cord calculates quote count, pipeline, closed and collected amounts, average ticket,
win rate, average days to close, discounts conceded, 12-month activity, products sold,
origin, creation date, and inactive days.

When receivables exist, it also shows one statement across quotes and invoices: open
balance, overdue amount, oldest item, and current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ day
aging bands. An invoiced operation appears as an invoice only, avoiding a duplicate
balance.

## Client source

The profile shows under **Source** how the client arrived:

- **Added manually:** created from Clients, the quote editor, the CSV import, or the clients API.
- **Self-registered:** created automatically when a quote came in from your [embedded quote builder](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/elements/resumen) (or the quotes API) with a new client whose company or email didn't exist in your directory. The profile marks it with a label.

A self-registered client is never updated automatically: if the same company quotes
again from your site, Cord reuses the existing profile without changing its data.
Review these clients and complete their information before invoicing them.

## Export clients to CSV

<Steps>
1. Go to **Settings › Advanced › Data & privacy**.
2. Under **Catalog and clients (CSV)**, click **Clients**.
3. A CSV downloads with the columns empresa, contacto, email, telefono, rfc, terminos, and limite: the same format as the import, to open in Excel or migrate.
</Steps>

To open that screen you need the Settings permission, and to download the file, the
Clients permission. On the same screen, **Download everything (JSON)** exports products,
clients, quotes with their lines and events, tasks, and the audit log.

## Start a quote from the client

Select **New quote** in the client record (requires the Clients permission). The editor opens with that client selected
and uses its terms as defaults. Always review recipient, currency, tax, discounts, and
due date for the current document.

Later client edits don't silently rewrite issued fiscal documents; those keep the
issuer, recipient, line, and amount data they were issued with.

## Permissions, limits, and deletion

- Creating, editing, importing, and deleting requires the **Clients** permission.
- Free supports up to 50 clients and Starter up to 500. Pro, Scale, and Developer
  have no hard client limit under the current contract.
- Deletion is permanent and recorded in the audit trail. Review associated quotes and
  statement items first.

## Common problems

**New client or Import CSV is missing.** The member lacks Clients permission.

**Import updated a row that should have been separate.** Upsert matched its tax ID or
company name.

**Tax data is incomplete.** Review country, ID, address, and document-specific fields.

**Balance exceeds the limit.** This is an alert; decide whether to change terms,
request an upfront payment, or stop new sales.

**I can't find how to export.** Export lives in **Settings › Advanced › Data & privacy**, not in the directory.
