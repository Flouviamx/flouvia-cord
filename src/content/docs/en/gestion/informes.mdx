**Reports** is Cord's analytics surface: "One place to understand performance and decide
what to move." It brings together **15 reports in 7 groups** behind one picker. Seven are
**widget reports** (customizable boards), seven are **table reports** (compared KPIs, a
chart, a table with totals, and CSV export), and one is the **Custom report**, where you
choose how to group and what to measure. **Performance**, in the same menu group,
compares the people on the team.

## Where it is and how to pick a report

**Reports** is in the side menu, under the **Insights** group (shortcut <kbd>G</kbd> then
<kbd>A</kbd>). At the top right is the **report picker**: click the current report's name
to open it.

<DmReports lang="en" />

- The panel groups reports by category: **Overview**, **Sales**, **Clients**,
  **Products**, **Finance**, **Team**, and **Custom**. Only categories with a report your
  permission allows appear.
- Under each name it says **Date range** or **As of today**.
- **Search reports** filters by name in Spanish and English.
- With a keyboard: the search box gets focus when the panel opens, <kbd>↑</kbd> and
  <kbd>↓</kbd> move through the list, <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the report, and
  <kbd>Escape</kbd> closes the panel.
- Your organization's [saved reports](/en/docs/gestion/informes-personalizados) appear at
  the end, under **Custom**, labeled **Saved report**.

Each report has its own address (`/app/informes?r=...`), so you can bookmark it or share
it with a teammate who has the permission.

### If you're missing the permission or the plan

Each reason asks for a different action, and the screen tells them apart:

- **Without the Reports permission** you see no report: "No access. Your current role
  doesn't have permission to view analytics. Contact your organization's admin."
- **Without the Collections permission**, Payments received and Collections and
  receivables don't appear in the picker; if you open their link, you see "Restricted
  report" and a prompt to ask for the permission.
- **Without the plan**, Finance, Cash flow, and Collections do appear in the picker, but
  when you open them you see what they offer and the button to go to **Settings › Plans
  & subscription**. For example, Cash flow says "How much cash is coming in over the
  next 90 days".

The permission is called **Reports** in **Settings › Team & permissions**. See
[Team and security](/en/docs/cuenta/equipo-seguridad).

## Dates, time zone, and currency

### The date picker

**Date range** reports show the picker next to the report picker: **Today**,
**Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This month**,
**Last month**, **This quarter**, **Year to date**, **Last 12 months**, and **Custom**.
You can look back up to one year.

- When you open a report, the range comes, in this order: from the link's address; if it
  has none, from the last range you used **in that report** in this browser (each report
  remembers its own); otherwise, **Last 30 days**.
- The range is written into the page address, so a copied link opens the same period for
  whoever receives it.
- In widget reports, changing the range repaints the numbers without reloading. In table
  reports, the page reloads because the table, totals, and CSV are built in full on the
  server.

**As of today** reports have no picker (there would be nothing to ignore): they show the
label "As of today · \{date}". Inside a range report, widgets tagged **Today** also ignore
the range; hover over the tag to see the explanation.

### Time zone

Days are cut in your **business time zone**: "today", the start and end of each day, and
weeks and months are yours. A payment on the 31st at 11 p.m. in your city counts in that
month even if it's already the 1st in another zone.

### Currency: everything in your business currency

If you sell in several currencies, Cord doesn't add up amounts in different currencies
as if they were one: it **restates everything in your business currency**, the one set in
Settings. For each amount it uses the best real rate available:

1. if the sale is already in your business currency, as is;
2. if the quote's or invoice's accounting currency is your business currency, the
   **exchange rate set on that document** (the same one the invoice declares);
3. otherwise, **today's published** exchange rate.

If no exchange rate is available for a currency, its amounts **are left out of the sum**;
Cord never makes up a rate. Below the report you see the note:

> Amounts in \{currency}. Sales in \{currencies} are converted with the exchange rate set on each quote or invoice, or with today's published rate when there is none.

and, if a currency was left out: "No exchange rate is available for \{currencies}, so
those sales are not included for now." If you sell in a single currency, the note
doesn't appear.

### Which date each measure uses

| Measure | What it counts | Date |
|---|---|---|
| **Quotes** / **Quoted** | Quotes that left draft (including ones later rejected or expired). | Creation |
| **Sales** / **Sold** | Won quotes: Approved, Paid, or Invoiced. Sold is their total, including taxes and minus withholdings. | Approval |
| **Close rate** | Of what went out in the range, what percentage was won (cohort). | Creation |
| **Collected** | Money that came in. | Payment |
| **Balance today**, receivables, pipeline | Snapshot of the moment. | Today |

**"Collected" means the same thing across Cord** (Home, Reports, and Payments): quote
payments (deposits, installments, retainers) plus payments applied to invoices, plus
quotes marked paid by hand without any recorded payment, **minus refunds**. An invoice
payment that already came from a quote charge isn't counted twice.

<Callout type="info" title="A cohort is not the same as a close">
A close rate is calculated on the **cohort**: the quotes created in the range. A recent
cohort still has open quotes, so its rate can look low without the team selling worse.
Compare against a cohort of the same age or check **Cohort maturity** in Overview.
</Callout>

## How to read table reports

The seven table reports (Sales over time, Sales by client, Sales by product, Payments
received, Invoiced taxes, Repeat purchase by cohort, and Sales by team member) and the
Custom report share the same structure, top to bottom:

1. A one-line **description** of what the report answers.
2. **KPIs** compared against the previous period.
3. **Chart.**
4. The full **table**, sortable and with a **Total** row.
5. Notes specific to the report (margin, cohorts) and the currency note.

### KPIs and the previous-period comparison

Each KPI shows its value, an arrow with the change and, below, "vs. \{value} the previous
period".

- **The previous period** has the same number of days as your range and ends the day
  before your range starts. September 1–30 is compared against the 30 days before
  (August 2–31), not against "August".
- **Amounts and counts** change in **percent**. **Rates and percentages** (close rate,
  margin %, returning clients) change in **points**: from 38% to 43% is "5 pts", not
  "13%".
- **No base, no arrow.** If the previous period is zero, the arrow doesn't appear. If
  there's no previous value, the line says "No previous period to compare".
- With **Last 12 months** there's no comparison: the previous period would fall outside
  the queryable year. Repeat purchase by cohort doesn't compare either: it's a snapshot of
  today.

### The table

- **Sort:** click a column header. The first click sorts from highest to lowest; the
  second, lowest to highest. Empty cells always go last.
- **Total row:** at the bottom, pinned. It adds up what can be added; rates and averages
  in the total are those of the full period, not the sum of the rows. A column that isn't
  added stays blank.
- **Links:** when the first column is a client, a catalog product, a quote, or an
  invoice, it links to its record or detail.
- **Reading:** the header and first column stay pinned as you scroll. A dash (—) means
  "not applicable or no data", not zero.
- **No data:** "No data in this range. Adjust the date range or come back once there is
  activity in this period."
- **Mobile:** KPIs go in two columns, the table scrolls sideways with the first column
  pinned, and buttons are 44 px.

### The chart and drill-down

- In **client** and **product** charts, each bar opens that client's or product's record.
- In **Sales over time** and in the Custom report grouped by **Month** or **Week**, each
  bar opens that month or week. The period is trimmed to your range: if the range starts
  on the 15th, the month's bar opens the 15th through month end.
- On desktop, a click (or <kbd>Enter</kbd> with focus on the bar) navigates directly. On
  mobile, the first tap shows the value and the tooltip includes **View detail →**.

### Export CSV

**Export CSV**, at the top right, downloads exactly what you see: the same report, the
same range and, in Sales over time, the same grouping. The button appears when the table
has rows.

| Aspect | Format |
|---|---|
| File name | `cord-{report}-{from}_{to}.csv`, for example `cord-ventas-cliente-2026-09-01_2026-09-30.csv`. Repeat purchase by cohort uses today's date: `cord-recompra-2026-10-08.csv`. |
| Encoding | UTF-8 with BOM, so Excel opens accented characters correctly. Comma separator and Windows line breaks. |
| Headers | The same as the table, in your account language. |
| Amounts | As a **number**, with no symbol or thousands separator and a period as decimal point (`196469.20`). Whole numbers carry no decimals. |
| Currency | If the report has any money column, a **Currency** column is added at the end with your business currency on every row. |
| Percentages | As a whole number: `42` means 42%. |
| Dates | `YYYY-MM-DD`. In period reports, the date is the first day of the period. |
| Empty values | Empty cell (on screen they show as —). |
| Total row | Not exported: the file carries only the rows, so you can sum and filter your own way. |
| Protection | Text that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab, or carriage return is exported with a leading apostrophe, so a spreadsheet doesn't run it as a formula. Amounts are left alone. |

## Table reports, one by one

### Sales over time

"How much you quote, win and collect, period by period. Sales by close date; collected by
payment date."

<DmReportTable report="ventas" lang="en" />

- **KPIs:** Sold, Sales, Average ticket, and Collected.
- **Chart:** Sold per period, as bars.
- **Columns:** Period, Quotes, Quoted, Sales, Sold, Average ticket, Collected. **Every**
  period in the range appears, even at zero.
- **Day / Week / Month grouping:** at the top right. If you don't choose, Cord decides by
  the range's length: **day** up to 31 days, **week** up to 124 days, and **month** after
  that. Weeks start on Monday. The first and last periods can be partial if your range
  doesn't start on a Monday or on the 1st.
- Each column uses its own date: Quotes and Quoted by creation, Sales and Sold by
  approval, Collected by payment date. That's why a row can have sales without quotes:
  something quoted earlier was won in that period.

### Sales by client

"Each client with what they bought in the range, their ticket, last purchase and what
they owe you today."

<DmReportTable report="ventas-cliente" lang="en" />

- **KPIs:** Clients who bought, Sold, Average ticket, and **Returning clients**: the
  percentage of clients who bought in the range that had bought from you before (changes
  in points).
- **Chart:** the 8 clients with the most sold, as horizontal bars; each bar opens their
  record.
- **Columns:** Client, Quotes (sent in the range), Sales, Sold, Average ticket, **Last
  sale** and **Client since** (from your whole history, not the range), and **Balance
  today**: what they owe you right now across quotes and invoices, with partial payments
  deducted.
- Every client that received a quote or bought from you in the range appears, sorted by
  sold, up to 500 rows. Quotes without an assigned client aren't included.

### Sales by product

"Which products sell, how many units, at what margin and how often they win when quoted."

- **KPIs:** Units, Sold, Gross margin, and Margin %.
- **Chart:** the 8 products with the most sold; each bar opens the product's record.
- **Columns:** Product, SKU, Units, Sales (won quotes that include it), Sold, Gross
  margin, Margin %, Close rate (of the quotes created in the range that included it, how
  many were won). Totals for Units, Sold, Gross margin, and Margin %.
- **Sold here is per line:** negotiated (or list) price times quantity, **before taxes**
  and the document-level discount. That's why it doesn't match Sold in Sales over time,
  which uses the quote total.
- A free-text line, without a catalog product, appears with its description and no link.
  Lines the client didn't approve in a partial approval don't count.
- **Margin:** "Margin only counts lines with a cost captured when quoting; without a cost,
  the line counts toward sales but not margin." The cost is what the product had when it
  was quoted; a zero cost is treated as "I don't know", not "it was free". With no line
  carrying a cost, margin shows as —.

### Payments received

"Every payment that came in, from quotes and invoices, with its method and refunds."
It also requires the **Collections** permission.

- **KPIs:** Net collected, Gross collected, Refunded, and Payments.
- **Chart:** net collected by method.
- **Columns:** Date, Number, Client, Method, Amount, Refunded, Net. One row per payment,
  newest first, up to 1,000 rows. The number opens the quote or invoice.
- **Methods:** Card, SPEI, Bank transfer, Cash, Mercado Pago, Online payment, and
  **Recorded manually** (a quote marked paid without any recorded payment). A method Cord
  doesn't name yet appears as is.
- A refund is subtracted from the payment it belongs to, on that payment's date. This is
  the "Collected" definition used by Home and Sales over time.

### Invoiced taxes

"Taxes and withholdings from issued invoices, month by month: what you report."

- **KPIs:** Taxes, Withholdings, Subtotal, and Invoices.
- **Chart:** taxes by month.
- **Columns:** Month, Invoices, Subtotal, Taxes, Withholdings, Total. Always by month.
- It comes from **issued invoices** by issue date, not from quotes. It doesn't count
  drafts, voided invoices, or credit notes (those aren't subtracted here).
- It's a supporting read for your bookkeeping, not a filing: confirm it with your
  adviser.

### Repeat purchase by cohort

"Of the clients who first bought each month, how many came back to buy again." It's an
**As of today** snapshot: no picker and no comparison.

- **KPIs:** New clients (12 months) and Repeat purchase next month (of the cohorts that
  have already reached one month).
- **Heat map:** one row per month of first purchase over the last 12 months, with Cohort,
  Clients, and **Month 1** through **Month 6**. Each cell is the percentage of that cohort
  that bought again that many months later; darker means more repeat purchases.
- "A dash is a month that cohort has not reached yet": the August cohort has no Month 3
  in October. It isn't 0%.

### Sales by team member

"What each team member quoted and closed in the range, with their close rate and time to
close."

- **KPIs:** Sold, Sales, Average ticket, and Members with sales.
- **Chart:** the 8 members with the most sold.
- **Columns:** Team member, Sent (created in the range and out of draft), Sales, Sold,
  Close rate (of their quotes sent in the range, how many they won), Average ticket, and
  Days to close (average days from creation to approval, with one decimal).
- Each quote is attributed to the person who **created or duplicated** it. The table lists
  **active members**, even without sales. What people who have left the team created is
  grouped in a **Former team members** row, so the Total row matches the KPIs. Quotes
  without a creator (before attribution or created via the API) appear in neither the
  table nor the KPIs.

## Widget reports, one by one

These are boards you can [customize](#customizing-a-reports-widgets). Widgets marked
**optional** start hidden and are added from the library.

### Overview

Cohort of quotes created in the range.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Close rate** | Won over sent: "\{g} / \{n} sent quotes". |
| **Closed value** | Total won from the cohort created in the range. |
| **Pipeline velocity** | Closed value per day of the range. |
| **Lost value** | Rejected plus expired, with their count. |
| **Commercial movement** | Closed value by quote creation date. |
| **Cohort maturity** | What happened to quotes created in the range: Won, Lost, and Still open. With no sends in the range, it stays empty. |
| **Decided quotes** (optional) | Won, rejected, or expired in the cohort. |

### Commercial diagnosis

| Widget | What it shows | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Where conversion breaks** | Funnel sent → viewed → approved → paid. The base includes every quote that went out, even if rejected or expired; "viewed" is the client's first real open. | Range |
| **Pipeline** | Amount and count by open or won status. | Today |
| **Stalled** | "Quotes that need a next move": up to 5 sent or viewed quotes with no activity in 7 days or more, by amount. | Today |
| **Negotiation cost** | Products with the most discount given up against list price. Requires Starter or higher. | Range |
| **Median time by stage** | Created → sent, Sent → viewed, Viewed → approved, Approved → paid, in days. The median keeps a few outliers from distorting the cycle. | Range |
| **Stalled follow-ups** (optional) | How many quotes have been inactive for seven days or more; it counts all of them, even though the Stalled list shows only the 5 with the highest amount. | Today |

### Finance

Snapshot of today. Pro plan or higher.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **MRR** | Contracted MRR: monthly amount of active retainers. |
| **ARR** | Annualized MRR; not money already received. |
| **DSO** | Estimated time to collection, based on your history. |
| **MRR at risk** | Retainers with a late payment or set to cancel at period end. |
| **Weighted pipeline** | Pipeline by client weighted by probability and timing, based on your history. Not guaranteed cash. |
| **Concentration** | How much of the pipeline depends on the largest client. |
| **Profitability by tier** | By client tier: average discount, close rate, number of sends, and closed value. |
| **Active retainers** (optional) | Contracts contributing recurring revenue, with MRR and ARR. |

### Cash flow · 90 days

Snapshot and forecast as of today. Pro plan or higher. The forecast separates three
sources: **certain receivables** (approved or invoiced balances, with an estimated
collection date), **pipeline** (open opportunities weighted by probability), and
**retainers** (recurring installments within the horizon). The total is an expected
scenario, not a bank balance.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Expected inflow** | Next 90 days: receivables + pipeline + retainers. |
| **Beyond horizon** | What's expected from day 91, so it doesn't disappear from view. |
| **MRR** | Active retainers and their count. |
| **Certain receivables** | Outstanding balances not weighted by close rate. |
| **30-day concentration** | Which clients concentrate what you expect to collect in 30 days. |
| **Weekly flow** | Weekly flow · 90 days. |
| **Cumulative cash** | Cumulative cash curve. |
| **Expected daily inflow** (optional) | Daily forecast without accumulation. |

### Collections and receivables

Snapshot of today. Pro plan or higher and the Collections permission. It uses the same
receivables as the [Collections](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza) module: approved unpaid
quotes and open invoices, with partial payments deducted.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Receivables** | Outstanding amount and number of documents. |
| **Overdue** | Total overdue and number of accounts. |
| **Expected collections** | What you expect to collect in 30 days: certain receivables, estimated timing. |
| **Average delay** | Average days late against agreed terms. |
| **Aging** | Not yet due, 1–30 days, 31–60 days, and 60+ days. |
| **Client exposure** | Open balance against the configured credit limit. |
| **Promise compliance** | Kept, broken, and overdue pending promises. A promise records the commitment; it doesn't move money. |
| **Actual vs. agreed DSO** | How many payments arrive On time, 1–7 days late, 8–30 days late, and 30+ days late. |
| **Outstanding documents** (optional) | Documents awaiting collection today and their amount. |

### Clients

| Widget | What it shows | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Clients by closed value** | Who converts value, not just quote volume. | Range |
| **Client conversion** | Approved over quotes and rate, with closed value; each row opens the record. | Range |
| **New vs. returning** | New (their first quote falls in the range) against returning. | Range |
| **Clients at risk** | Clients with history and no quotes in 90 days, by closed value. | Today |
| **Tier and behavior** | Tier × payment behavior matrix (on time, slightly late, late). | Today |
| **Relationships to reactivate** (optional) | How many clients with history have been inactive for 90 days; it counts all of them, even though the Clients at risk list shows only the 10 with the most closed value. | Today |

### Products

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Products by quoted amount** | Demand inside the selected range. |
| **Product volume** | Quoted units and proposal presence. |
| **Discount elasticity** | Close rate by discount band, with the sample size. It uses your whole history and requires Starter or higher. |
| **Close rate by product** | Which products win, not only which are quoted. |
| **Units by product** (optional) | Quoted units in the selected range. |

## Customizing a report's widgets

The seven widget reports have the **Customize** button, with the same controls as Home:
**Library**, **Reset**, and **Done** as icon buttons; drag, **Move up**, **Move down**,
**Compact**/**Expanded**, and hide; full keyboard support and, on mobile, 44 px buttons.
**Reset** asks "Reset this report?". The full explanation is in
[Home and views](/en/docs/gestion/dashboard#customize-home).

- **Each report saves its own layout**, and the layout is yours: it doesn't change what
  your teammates see.
- Hiding a widget doesn't delete data or change calculations.
- Table reports and the Custom report aren't customized with widgets: their configuration
  is the table itself.

## Custom report and saved reports

When no report answers your question, the **Custom report** lets you choose how to group
(client, product, team member, status, month, week, day, country, client tier, or
currency) and up to six metrics, save it with a name for the whole team, and receive it
every Monday or every 1st by email with the CSV attached. It's documented in
[Custom reports and email delivery](/en/docs/gestion/informes-personalizados).

## Performance by team member

There are two places to look at the team, for different questions:

| | **Sales by team member** (report) | **Performance** (page) |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Reports › Sales by team member (Team group) | Side menu, **Insights** group (shortcut <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>E</kbd>) |
| Period | The range you choose, with comparison | Whole history |
| Columns | Sent, Sales, Sold, Close rate, Average ticket, Days to close | Quotes, Close, Closed, Collected, Ticket, Days |
| Exports CSV | Yes | No |

**Performance** requires the Reports permission and is a customizable widget board:
**Closed by the team** (approved, paid, and invoiced, with collected and unassigned in the
detail), **Average close rate** ("of what each rep sent"), **Leading seller**, **Active
reps**, **Contribution to closed sales**, **Sales activity**, **Ranking by rep** (by
amount closed, with your row marked **You**), and the optional **Team average deal**.
Amounts are in your business currency.

Both attribute each quote to the person who created or duplicated it. Documents without
a creator appear in Performance as **Unassigned** ("from before this report or via
API"). Cord doesn't redistribute that history or credit a sale to the last editor. The
team rate in Performance is the average of each rep's rate; it isn't a commission or a
payroll calculation.

## Common problems

**A report doesn't appear in the picker.** Check the Reports permission; Payments
received and Collections and receivables also require Collections. Finance, Cash flow,
and Collections do appear without the plan, but ask for Pro or higher when opened.

**Changing dates doesn't modify a widget.** It's a snapshot of today: it carries the
**Today** tag.

**A recent cohort's closed value looks low.** Part of it is still open; check maturity
and compare again once your normal sales cycle has passed.

**A total doesn't match between two reports.** Check which date each measure uses: Sold
goes by approval date, Quoted by creation, and Collected by payment date. In Sales by
product, Sold is per line and before taxes.

**Sales in another currency are missing.** Read the currency note: if a currency has no
exchange rate available, its sales are left out of the sum for now.

**I don't see the change arrow.** The previous period is zero, you picked Last 12 months,
or the report is a snapshot of today.

**I don't see the Export CSV button.** The table has no rows in that range, or you're in
a widget report: those don't export.

**The CSV opens with odd characters.** Open it with Excel or Google Sheets directly; the
file carries the UTF-8 mark they need. If you import it by hand, choose UTF-8 and comma
as the separator.

**A close doesn't appear in Sales by team member.** The quote doesn't keep the person who
created it (it predates attribution or was created via the API). If whoever created it is
no longer active on the team, the sale appears in the **Former team members** row.
