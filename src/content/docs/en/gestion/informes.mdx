**Reports** is Cord's single analytics surface. It contains seven views, while
**Team** compares results by the person who created or duplicated each quote.

## The seven reports

**Reports** is in the side menu, under the **Intelligence** group. Each report has its
own URL and its own widget layout.

| Report | Main question | Time scope | Permission | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overview | Close rate, closed and lost value, velocity, and maturity. | Selected range | Reports | All |
| Commercial diagnosis | Funnel, pipeline, stalled quotes, discount cost, and stage time. | Cohort plus current snapshot | Reports | All |
| Finance | MRR, ARR, DSO, MRR at risk, weighted pipeline, and concentration. | Current snapshot | Reports | Pro or higher |
| Cash flow · 90 days | Receivables, probable pipeline, retainers, and near-term concentration. | Current projection | Reports | Pro or higher |
| Collections and receivables | Open, overdue, aging, promises, and payment behavior. | Current snapshot | Reports and Collections | Pro or higher |
| Clients | Value and conversion by client, cohorts, and inactive clients. | Range plus current snapshot | Reports | All |
| Products | Quoted amount, volume, discount elasticity, and close rate. | Selected range | Reports | All |

If you're missing the permission, the report asks you to request it from whoever
manages the team. If you're missing the plan, it offers to take you to **Settings ›
Plans & subscription**.

## Range-based reports

Overview, Commercial diagnosis, Clients, and Products provide a date picker that
starts at the last 30 days and covers up to the last year. Cohort metrics assign a quote to the range by its creation date.
A quote created yesterday may still be open and shouldn't be compared directly with a
six-month-old cohort that had time to close.

<Callout type="info">
A low rate in a recent range may mean an **immature cohort**, not worse performance.
Check the “still open” share first.
</Callout>

## Current-snapshot reports

Finance, Cash flow, and Collections display **As of today** instead of an ineffective
date selector. Mixed reports also mark current widgets as **today**; for example,
Pipeline and stalled quotes describe the current state while the funnel uses the range.

## Funnel calculation

The commercial funnel is `sent → viewed → approved → paid`. Its base includes every
quote that left draft, even if later rejected or expired. Viewed records the client's
first real opening; internal activity and reloads don't create new conversions.

## Finance metrics

- **Contracted MRR:** monthly value of active retainers.
- **ARR:** annualized MRR, not cash already received.
- **MRR at risk:** retainers with a pending charge or scheduled for cancellation.
- **DSO:** estimated time to collection from available history.
- **Weighted pipeline:** expected value from historical probability and timing; not
  guaranteed cash.
- **Concentration:** the largest client's share of pipeline.

## Cash flow separates three sources

The 90-day projection keeps certain receivables, probability-weighted pipeline, and
contracted recurring revenue separate. The total is an expected scenario, not a bank
balance. Movements from day 91 remain visible outside the horizon.

## Collections and payment behavior

The report uses the same unified receivables as the operating module: open and overdue
totals, expected 30-day collections, average delay, aging, client-limit exposure,
promise compliance, and actual-versus-agreed payment timing. A payment promise records
a commitment; it doesn't move money or change the balance by itself.

## Customize widgets

Each report keeps its own member-level layout. Edit mode can reorder, resize, or hide
widgets; Reset restores that report's default. Hiding a widget doesn't remove data or
change calculations.

## Seller performance

**Team**, under the **Intelligence** group in the side menu, attributes each quote to
the person who created or duplicated it. It requires the Reports permission. It shows quotes created, close rate, closed value, collected value,
average ticket, and days to close.

Older documents without a creator appear as **Unassigned**. Cord doesn't redistribute
them or credit the latest editor automatically. The team rate is the average of the
displayed seller rates, not payroll or commission accounting.

## Common problems

**Report is missing.** Check the Reports permission; Collections has its own permission,
and Finance, Cash flow, and Collections require Pro or above.

**Changing dates doesn't change a widget.** It is a current snapshot marked “today.”

**A cohort's closed value looks low.** Some quotes may remain open; review maturity.

**A closing is Unassigned.** The quote has no stored creator: it predates the current
attribution model or was created through the API.
