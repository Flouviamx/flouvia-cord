**Reports** is Cord's single analytics surface. It brings together **15 reports** in
seven groups, a **custom report** you can save and get by email, and a shared picker.
**Performance** compares results by the person who created or duplicated each quote.

## The reports

**Reports** is in the side menu, under the **Insights** group. The picker in the top bar
groups them into **Overview**, **Sales**, **Clients**, **Products**, **Finance**,
**Team**, and **Custom**, and has a search box. Each report has its own URL.

The picker only shows the reports your permission allows. If you open one without the
permission, the report asks you to request it from whoever manages the team. If you're
missing the plan, it offers to take you to **Settings › Plans & subscription**.

Amounts are shown in your account's currency. A sale in another currency is converted with
the exchange rate set on its quote or invoice and, if it has none, with the rate published
that day; the note at the foot of each report says which currencies were converted.

## Ranged reports

Ranged reports show a date picker that starts at the last 30 days. The maximum range you
can query is the last year. When you change dates, Cord reloads the report without
reloading the whole page.

In cohort metrics, a quote belongs to the range by its origin date. A quote created
yesterday may still be open: don't compare it directly with a cohort from six months ago
that has had time to close.

<Callout type="info">
A low rate in a recent range may mean an **immature cohort**, not a worse team. Check the
"still open" share before drawing conclusions.
</Callout>

## Snapshot reports

Finance, Cash flow, Collections and receivables, and Repeat purchase by cohort show
**As of today** instead of a picker that would have no effect. They use the balances,
subscriptions, due dates, and pipeline that exist now.

Inside mixed reports, widgets marked **today** also ignore the range. For example,
Pipeline and stalled quotes describe the current state even though the funnel in the
same report uses a date cohort.

## Table reports

Sales over time, Sales by client, Sales by product, Repeat purchase by cohort, Payments
received, Invoiced taxes, Sales by team member, and the custom report share the same
shape:

- **Indicators at the top**, each compared with the **previous period** of the same length
  ("vs. [value] the previous period"), except in Repeat purchase by cohort.
- **A chart** and **the full table**, sortable by any column and, where it applies, with a
  totals row.
- **Export CSV**, with the same range and the same columns you see.

Some details per report:

| Report | Columns and specifics |
|---|---|
| **Sales over time** | Period, Quotes, Quoted, Sales, Sold, Average ticket, and Collected. Sales count by close date and collected amounts by payment date. It groups by day up to a month, by week up to about four months, and by month after that; you can change it. A month or week bar opens that period. |
| **Sales by client** | Client, Quotes, Sales, Sold, Average ticket, Last sale, Client since, and Balance today. At the top, clients who bought and returning clients. |
| **Sales by product** | Product, SKU, Units, Sales, Sold, Gross margin, Margin %, and Close rate. |
| **Repeat purchase by cohort** | One row per month of first purchase in the last 12 months, with the percentage that bought again in the following six months. A month that cohort hasn't reached yet shows a dash, not 0%. It reads as a heat map: it has no chart and no previous-period comparison. |
| **Payments received** | Date, Number, Client, Method, Amount, Refunded, and Net. At the top, net collected, gross collected, refunded, and number of payments. |
| **Invoiced taxes** | Month, Invoices, Subtotal, Taxes, Withholdings, and Total of the invoices issued. It helps you prepare your tax return; it doesn't replace it. |
| **Sales by team member** | Team member, Sent, Sales, Sold, Close rate, Average ticket, and Days to close. |

## Custom report

The **Custom report** counts the quotes created in the range and what happened to them,
grouped however you choose:

- **Group by:** Client, Product, Team member, Status, Month, Week, Day, Country, Client
  tier, or Currency.
- **Metrics**, up to six at a time: Quotes, Quoted, Sales, Sold, Close rate, Average
  ticket, Lost, Days to close, Units, and Gross margin. Units and Gross margin only apply
  when grouping by product; Days to close doesn't apply by product.

Click **Apply** to see the result. When grouping by month or week, a bar opens that period
one level finer.

### Save a report and get it by email

<Steps>
1. Build the custom report and click **Save**.
2. Give it a **Name** (1 to 80 characters), for example "Sales by country this quarter".
3. Under **Email delivery**, choose **No delivery**, **Every Monday**, or **On the 1st of every month**.
4. Save. The report appears in the picker's **Custom** group for your whole team.
</Steps>

- **Who sees it:** anyone with the Reports permission. **Save changes** or **Delete**:
  whoever saved it or an admin. **Save as new** creates your own copy.
- **Delivery** goes only to you, in your business's time zone, with the complete period
  that just ended (the previous week or month), its indicators, and the CSV attached. Only
  whoever saved the report can schedule it, and it stops arriving if that person leaves
  the team or loses the Reports permission.
- **Cap:** 50 saved reports per organization.
- Deleting a saved report doesn't delete any data.

## Funnel calculation

The commercial funnel is `sent → viewed → approved → paid`. Its base includes every
quote that left draft, even if later rejected or expired. Viewed records the client's
first real opening; internal activity and reloads don't create new conversions.

## Finance metrics

- **Contracted MRR:** monthly value of active retainers.
- **ARR:** annualized MRR, not cash already received.
- **MRR at risk:** retainers with a pending charge or scheduled for cancellation.
- **DSO:** estimated time to collection from available history.
- **Weighted pipeline:** expected value from historical probability and timing; not
  guaranteed cash.
- **Concentration:** the largest client's share of pipeline.

## Cash flow separates three sources

The 90-day projection keeps certain receivables, probability-weighted pipeline, and
contracted recurring revenue separate. The total is an expected scenario, not a bank
balance. Movements from day 91 remain visible outside the horizon.

## Collections and payment behavior

The report uses the same unified receivables as the operating module: open and overdue
totals, expected 30-day collections, average delay, aging, client-limit exposure,
promise compliance, and actual-versus-agreed payment timing. A payment promise records
a commitment; it doesn't move money or change the balance by itself.

## Customize widgets

Widget reports (Overview, Commercial diagnosis, Clients, Products, Finance, Cash flow, and
Collections and receivables) each keep their own member-level layout. Edit mode can
reorder, resize, or hide widgets; Reset restores that report's default. Hiding a widget
doesn't remove data or change calculations.

## Seller performance

**Performance**, under the **Insights** group in the side menu, attributes each quote to
the person who created or duplicated it. It requires the Reports permission. It shows
quotes created, close rate, closed value, collected value, average ticket, and days to
close. For an exportable table with the same focus, use the **Sales by team member** report.

Older documents without a creator appear as **Unassigned**. Cord doesn't redistribute
them or credit the latest editor automatically. The team rate is the average of the
displayed seller rates, not payroll or commission accounting.

## Common problems

**A report is missing from the picker.** Check the Reports permission; Payments received
and Collections and receivables also require the Collections permission. Finance, Cash
flow, and Collections and receivables require Pro or above.

**Changing dates doesn't change a widget.** It is a current snapshot marked "today."

**A cohort's closed value looks low.** Some quotes may remain open; review maturity.

**A closing is Unassigned.** The quote has no stored creator: it predates the current
attribution model or was created through the API.

**I can't schedule delivery of a saved report.** Only whoever saved it can schedule it;
use **Save as new** to get your own copy.

**Some sales aren't added up.** If no exchange rate is available for a currency, the
footnote says so and those sales aren't added until one is.
