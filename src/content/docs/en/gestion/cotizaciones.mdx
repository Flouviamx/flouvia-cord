## The Draft Lifecycle

Not all quotes are finished in 5 minutes. Sometimes you need to check prices with suppliers or wait for your manager's approval before sending it.

### 1. Save as Draft
- While creating a quote, you can click **Save draft**, in the **Review and send** panel on the right.
- This saves your progress (client, terms, line items, and negotiated prices).
- The quote appears in **Quotes** (*Open* filter) and, in the board view, in the **Draft** column.

### 2. Continue Editing
To resume a draft:
1. Go to **Quotes** in the main menu.
2. Click the row (or the board card) of the draft you want to continue.
3. The details view will open. Click **Continue editing**.
4. You'll be taken to the quoter with what you saved. When finished, use **Save and send** and its status will change to *Sent*. If you no longer need it, **Delete draft** removes it.

## Duplicating Quotes (Time-saver)

If you have a standardized service or want to sell the same thing to another client, you don't have to start from scratch.

**How to duplicate in the app:**
1. Go to the details view of any quote, regardless of its status — paid, rejected, or a draft.
2. In the actions panel, click **Duplicate quote**.
3. Cord creates a new draft, with its own number and a fresh 30-day validity period, that copies the client, payment terms, notes, and line items (description, quantity, list price, and negotiated price).

<Callout type="warning">
**What the copy doesn't carry over:** the sales currency (the new draft is set to MXN), the exchange rate, the deposit, the monthly retainer, the prices-include-tax option, and the tax rate chosen on each line (it goes back to your catalog's default). Open the copy with **Continue editing** and review those fields before sending it.
</Callout>

## Version Control

If you've already sent a quote and the client asks you to adjust line items or prices, you don't need to create another one: while it's *Sent*, *Viewed*, or *Expired*, the details show the **Modify and resend (V2)** button (the number is the version you're about to create).

- It opens an editor with the current line items: you can change products, quantities, negotiated prices, and taxes. The client and the payment terms can't be changed here.
- When you resend, Cord saves the new version, keeps the **same link**, emails the client again (if they have an email on file), and the quote goes back to *Sent*.
- If the client had the link open, they see the changes live and must review them before they can approve (see [Chat and negotiation](/en/docs/interaccion/chat)).
- In the details, the **Version history** section lists each version with its date and total.
- On an *Expired* quote, resending doesn't extend its validity date. To propose it again with a new validity period, duplicate it.

If you need to change something **Modify and resend** doesn't allow (the client, the currency, or the terms), duplicate the quote, adjust it, and send the new link. Then, on the original (while it's still *Sent* or *Viewed*), use **Mark rejected** to take it out of your active pipeline. Cord doesn't have a separate "Canceled" status: rejected is the status that marks a link as no longer active.
