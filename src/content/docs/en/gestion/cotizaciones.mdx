## What it is

**Quotes**, in the main menu, gathers all your organization's quotes, newest first.
From there you open each one's detail, where its actions, activity, conversation, and
versions live.

<DmQuotesIndex lang="en" view="list" />

## Requirements

- **View:** any member of the organization.
- **Quotes permission:** create, continue editing, send, modify and resend, delete drafts, and reply in the conversation.
- **Approvals permission:** mark approved or rejected, decide internal approvals, and record payment and issue the document from the detail.
- **Plan:** all. The number of active quotes is capped on Free (5) and Starter (50).

## Find a quote

**Filters** (pills above the list, each with its count):

| Filter | Includes |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every quote. |
| **Open** | Draft, Sent, and Viewed. |
| **Needs approval** | Those waiting for an [internal approval](/en/docs/cotizacion/aprobaciones-internas). |
| **Approved** | Approved. |
| **Paid** | Paid and Invoiced. |
| **Closed** | Rejected and Expired. |

**Search:** the list has no search box of its own. On desktop, open the command palette
(⌘K on Mac, Ctrl+K on Windows) and type at least two characters of the quote number or
the client's name; it shows up to 6 quotes, plus matching clients and products.

**From other screens:** the funnel and pipeline bars on Home open this list with the
matching filter already applied.

## Actions by status

| Status | Actions on the detail | Permission |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | **Send to client**, **Continue editing**, **Preview**, **Duplicate quote**, **Delete draft** | Quotes |
| **Draft pending approval** | **Approve and send**, **Reject** (in addition to the above) | Approvals |
| **Sent / Viewed** | **Mark approved**, **Mark rejected** | Approvals |
| | **Modify and resend (V2)**, **Open link**, **Copy link**, **PDF**, **WhatsApp**, **Duplicate quote** | Quotes |
| **Expired** | **Modify and resend**, sharing, **Duplicate quote** | Quotes |
| **Approved** | **Record payment**, **Stamp CFDI 4.0** / **Issue invoice** / **Issue pro forma** | Approvals |
| | Sharing, **Duplicate quote** | — |
| **Paid** | **Stamp CFDI 4.0** / **Issue invoice** / **Issue pro forma** | Approvals |
| **Invoiced** | Sharing, **Duplicate quote**; documents appear under **Tax documents** | — |
| **Rejected** | Sharing, **Duplicate quote** | — |

A quote with a monthly retainer doesn't show **Record payment**: its charge is the
subscription, which you can cancel with **Cancel retainer**.

## Drafts

- **Save draft** in the editor saves the client, terms, lines, prices, and conditions. A draft can be saved without a client; to send it you need one.
- To pick it up again, open it and click **Continue editing**: the same editor opens (**Edit draft**). From there, every change saves itself a few seconds later and **Save and send** sends it. See [Preview and sending](/en/docs/cotizacion/envio).
- **Delete draft** asks for confirmation ("This action cannot be undone. Delete this draft?") and removes it completely. Only drafts can be deleted: a quote that already went out isn't deleted, it's rejected or left to expire.

## Duplicate a quote

<Steps>
1. Open any quote, in any status.
2. Click **Duplicate quote**.
3. Cord creates a new draft with its own number and a 30-day validity, and takes you to it. The duplicate's activity notes which quote it was duplicated from.
</Steps>

**What it copies:** client, payment terms, notes, and lines (description, quantity,
list price, and price).

<Callout type="warning">
**What the duplicate doesn't copy:** the sales currency (the duplicate is in MXN), the
exchange rate, the deposit, the monthly retainer, **Prices include VAT**, each
line's tax rate (it goes back to your catalog's default), each line's cost, the
conversation, the signature, and the versions. Open the duplicate with **Continue
editing** and check those fields before sending it.
</Callout>

The duplicate counts as an active quote: if you've hit your plan's cap, it isn't created.

## Versions

While a quote is *Sent*, *Viewed*, or *Expired*, **Modify and resend (V2)** opens the
same quote editor as **New version of COT-0149** and, when you click **Send new
version**, creates a new version on the same link (the old
`/app/cotizaciones/[id]/editar` route only redirects to that editor):

- you can change products, quantities, prices, each line's tax, and **Prices include VAT**; the client, currency, and terms (payment terms, validity length, deposit, retainer, and note) stay as they were sent;
- if your organization uses [internal approvals](/en/docs/cotizacion/aprobaciones-internas), the version is checked against the caps before it's sent;
- the quote goes back to *Sent*, its validity restarts from that day with the same length it was sent with (if it was 30 days, it expires in 30 days), and the client gets the email again if they have an email on file. That's how an *Expired* quote comes back;
- the badge next to the client's name shows the current version (V1, V2, V3…);
- **Version history** on the detail lists each version with its date and total.

If the client has the link open, they see the changes live and must review them before
approving (see [Negotiation and counteroffers](/en/docs/interaccion/negociacion)).

### When a version isn't enough

To change something **Modify and resend** doesn't allow (client, currency, terms, or a different validity length),
duplicate the quote, adjust it, and send the new link. Then, on the original (while
it's still *Sent* or *Viewed*), use **Mark rejected** to take it out of the pipeline.
Cord has no "Canceled" status: *Rejected* signals that link is no longer active.

## Common problems

**I don't see Mark approved or Record payment.** Your role doesn't have the Approvals permission.

**I can't delete a quote.** Only drafts can be deleted.

**Cord says the quote can't move with this action.** The quote already changed status (for example, the client approved it while you had it open). Reload the detail.

**The duplicate came out in MXN.** The duplicate doesn't copy the currency; change it with **Continue editing**.
