  <p class="page-subtitle">Find out the moment your client makes a decision, by email, Slack or Teams, without having to keep checking the app.</p>
</header>

## How it works

Under **Settings › Notifications** there's a matrix of 7 events by 3 channels (email, Slack and Microsoft Teams). Check the boxes you want — every combination saves immediately and applies from that point on.

| Event | When it fires |
|---|---|
| Quote viewed | The client opened the public link |
| Quote approved | The client accepted the proposal |
| Quote rejected | The client rejected the quote from the link |
| Payment received | An online payment was recorded |
| Quote about to expire | 3 days before the validity date |
| Overdue payment | A receivable passed its due date |
| Someone joined the team | An invitee accepted your invitation |

<Callout type="info">
**Before you save your matrix for the first time**, Cord already emails you when a quote is approved, rejected, or paid — the three highest-value events, on by default. As soon as you save the screen once, your selection takes over: you can turn any of the three off or turn the rest on.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
**Messages and counteroffers don't trigger alerts.** When the client writes in the chat, comments on a line item, or sends a counteroffer, Cord records it in the quote's conversation, but doesn't alert you through these channels. If you want to know, create a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows) with the **A message is posted on a quote** trigger (see [Chat and negotiation](/en/docs/interaccion/chat)).
</Callout>

## Email

Email always goes to the business owner's account (`Settings › Your account`). It's never sent to the whole team — if several people need to know, use Slack or Teams.

## Slack

Paste the URL of a **Slack incoming webhook** in the **Slack channel** section of the same screen (`Settings › Notifications`) and check the Slack column for the events you want posted to your channel. The message includes the folio, the client, the total, and a direct link to the quote.

<Callout type="warning">
**"Someone joined the team" has no Slack or Teams version.** That alert isn't tied to any quote (no folio, no amount to show in the channel), so it can only be received by email.
</Callout>

## Microsoft Teams

Connect Teams with **Connect with Microsoft** in `Settings › Integrations` (you pick the channel, nothing to copy), or paste the URL of a Teams Power Automate flow in `Settings › Notifications`, and check the Teams column for the events you want. It's a separate channel from Slack: you can have both connected and choose where each alert goes. The card carries the same folio, client, total and link.
