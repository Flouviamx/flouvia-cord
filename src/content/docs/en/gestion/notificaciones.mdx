## How it works

Under **Settings › Notifications** there's a matrix of 7 events by 2 channels (email and Slack). Check the boxes you want — every combination saves immediately and applies from that point on.

| Event | When it fires |
|---|---|
| Quote viewed | The client opened the public link |
| Quote approved | The client accepted the proposal |
| Quote rejected | The client rejected or countered |
| Payment received | An online payment was recorded |
| Quote about to expire | 3 days before the validity date |
| Overdue payment | A receivable passed its due date |
| Someone joined the team | An invitee accepted your invitation |

<Callout type="info">
**Before you save your matrix for the first time**, Cord already emails you when a quote is approved, rejected, or paid — the three highest-value events, on by default. As soon as you save the screen once, your selection takes over: you can turn any of the three off or turn the rest on.
</Callout>

## Email

Email always goes to the business owner's account (`Settings › Your account`). It's never sent to the whole team — if several people need to know, use Slack.

## Slack

Connect an **Incoming Webhook** from Slack on the same screen (`Settings › Integrations`) and check the Slack column for the events you want posted to your channel. The message includes the folio, the client, the total, and a direct link to the quote.

<Callout type="warning">
**"Someone joined the team" has no Slack version.** That alert isn't tied to any quote (no folio, no amount to show in the channel), so it can only be received by email.
</Callout>
