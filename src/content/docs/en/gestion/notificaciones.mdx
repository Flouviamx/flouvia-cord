## What it is

In **Settings › Notifications › Notifications** there's a matrix of 8 events by 3
channels (Email, Slack, and Teams). You check the boxes you want and they apply from
then on to the whole organization.

<DmNotifMatrix lang="en" />

## Requirements

- **Permission:** Settings, to change the matrix.
- **Plan:** all.
- **Channels:** email goes to the account owner, except the task reminder, which goes to each assignee. Slack and Teams must be connected (see below).

## The events

| Event | When it fires | Triggered by |
|---|---|---|
| **Your client viewed the quote** | The first time the client opens the link (see below what counts as a view). | The client |
| **Quote approved** | The client signs the proposal, in full or in part, from the link. | The client |
| **Quote rejected** | The client confirms the rejection from the link. | The client |
| **Payment received** | Cord confirms an online payment that settles the quote. | Online payment |
| **Quote about to expire** | Exactly 3 days before the validity date, if it's still sent or viewed. | The daily process |
| **Overdue payment** | The day after the payment of an approved or invoiced quote comes due without being paid. | The daily process |
| **Someone joined the team** | An invitee accepts your invitation. | Your team |
| **Task reminder** | Every morning, from 8:00 in your business's time zone, if someone has tasks due today or overdue. Email only, sent to each assignee, not to the owner. | The daily process |

### What counts as a "view"

The view alert is there so you know **your client** opened the proposal, so Cord
discards anything that isn't the client:

- **Your team doesn't count.** If you open the link signed in to the organization, you see the preview notice and nothing fires.
- **Link preview generators don't count.** Pasting the link into WhatsApp, Slack, or an email doesn't mark it as viewed.
- **It's measured with the page open and visible.** The view is recorded from the link itself when the tab is visible, not when the link loads in the background.
- **Only once.** The alert goes out when the quote moves from *Sent* to *Viewed*. Reloading or reopening doesn't repeat it.

More in [What your client sees](/en/docs/interaccion/vista-del-cliente).

### What doesn't trigger an alert

- **Messages, per-line comments, and counteroffers.** They stay in the conversation and in the bell on the top bar, but don't trigger email, Slack, or Teams. To receive them, create a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/primer-workflow) with **A message is posted on a quote**.
- **What your team does:** **Mark approved**, **Mark rejected**, and **Record payment** change the status but don't send these alerts.
- **Internal approval requests:** they reach Slack with buttons to decide and Teams with a link to Cord, separately from this matrix (see [Internal approvals](/en/docs/cotizacion/aprobaciones-internas)).

## Before you save for the first time

Until you save the matrix, Cord alerts you **by email** about three events: quote
approved, rejected, and payment received, and it sends the **Task reminder**. Once you save
the screen, your selection rules: you can turn off any of those four or turn on the others.

## Channels

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Email">

Goes to the business owner's account. It isn't sent to the whole team: if several
people need to know, use Slack or Teams. The email carries the quote number, client,
total with its currency, and a button to open the quote.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Slack">

In the **Slack channel** section of the same screen, paste the URL of a **Slack
incoming webhook** and check the Slack column for the events you want. The message
includes the quote number, client, total, and a link to the quote. See
[Slack](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/slack).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Microsoft Teams">

Connect Teams with **Connect with Microsoft** in **Settings › Integrations** (you pick
the channel, nothing to copy), or paste the URL of a Teams Power Automate flow in
**Settings › Notifications**. Check the Teams column for the events you want. It's a
separate channel from Slack: you can have both and choose where each alert goes. See
[Microsoft Teams](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/teams).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
**The task reminder doesn't go to the owner; it goes to each assignee.** Each person gets
only their own tasks (see [Tasks](/en/docs/gestion/tareas#email-reminder)). Turning it off
here turns it off for the whole organization. Email only.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**"Someone joined the team" only arrives by email.** It isn't tied to a quote (no
number or amount to show in the channel), so Slack and Teams show "Not available for
this event".
</Callout>

## What Cord sends your client

Separately from this matrix, and with no setup, Cord emails the client a payment
reminder when an approved quote comes due within the next 3 days, as long as the client
has an email on file. Test organizations don't send reminders.

## Limits by plan

Alerts are on every plan. Customizing the sender and signature of emails to the client
is available from Starter (**Settings › Notifications › Email**).

## Common problems

**I didn't get the view alert.** You opened the link yourself while signed in, the quote was already *Viewed*, or the event isn't checked in your matrix (it's off by default).

**My partner doesn't get the emails.** Email only goes to the account owner; use Slack or Teams.

**Nothing arrives in Slack.** Check that the webhook URL is saved and the Slack column is checked for that event.
