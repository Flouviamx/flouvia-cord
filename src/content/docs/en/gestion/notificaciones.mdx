## What it is

Cord notifies you in two ways, independent of each other:

| | The bell | Email, Slack, and Teams alerts |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | In the app's top bar. | Outside the app: your email, a Slack channel, or a Teams channel. |
| **What it includes** | What your client did and the money that came in, on quotes and invoices. | The 8 events in the **Settings › Notifications** matrix. |
| **For whom** | Each member has their own "read" state. | Email goes to the account owner (the task reminder, to each assignee); Slack and Teams, to the connected channel. |
| **Configured** | It isn't configured: it's always on. | With the matrix, per event and per channel. |

Turning an event off in the matrix doesn't remove it from the bell, and reading the bell
doesn't change what is sent by email, Slack, or Teams.

## The bell in the top bar

The bell sits in the top bar on every screen of the app, on desktop and on a phone. It
gathers in one place what happened on your client's side so you don't have to open each
quote or invoice.

### What lights it up

Only two kinds of things: **what your client did** and **the money that came in**. It
covers quotes and invoices.

| Source | Notification | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Quote | **Your client viewed the quote** | Your client opens the link (with the same rules as [what counts as a view](#what-counts-as-a-view)). |
| Quote | **Quote approved** | Your client approves from the link. |
| Quote | **Quote rejected** | Your client rejects from the link. |
| Quote | **Counteroffer from your client** | Your client proposes another amount from the link. |
| Quote | The message text | Your client writes in the quote's general chat. The notification shows their message (longer ones are trimmed). Comments on a line item don't reach the bell: you see them in the quote's conversation. |
| Quote | **Payment received** | A payment for the quote comes in. |
| Invoice | **Your client viewed the invoice** | Your client opens the invoice. |
| Invoice | **Partial payment received** | A partial payment for the invoice comes in. |
| Invoice | **Invoice paid** | The invoice is paid in full. |

### What doesn't light it up

**Nothing you or your team do**: sending a quote, editing a draft, creating a new
version, marking it approved or rejected by hand, recording a payment by hand, issuing an
invoice, or the reminders Cord sends automatically. Those actions stay in each
document's activity, but they aren't news you need to act on.

Opening your own link while signed in doesn't light it up either: Cord doesn't count it
as a client view.

### The counter

A number appears on the bell with the notifications that arrived since the last time
you opened it: from **1** to **9**, and **9+** from ten on. With nothing new, there's no
number. The counter is calculated every time you open a page in the app.

In the [Test environment](/en/docs/operacion/mapa-de-la-app#test-environment) the bell
doesn't show a counter.

### The panel

Selecting the bell opens the **Notifications** panel with the **15 most recent** ones,
newest first. Each one shows:

<DmNotifPanel lang="en" />

- what happened (for example, **Quote approved**), with an icon in green for approvals
  and payments, amber for views and counteroffers, and red for rejections;
- the document number and the client (for example, "COT-0148 · Distribuidora El Zarco");
- when it happened: "this minute", "5 min. ago", "3 hr. ago", "yesterday"; after a week,
  the date in your business's time zone.

The ones that arrived since you last opened the panel are **highlighted**. Select any of
them to open its quote or invoice. If there is nothing, the panel says
"No recent activity".

### Reading and seeing everything

- **Opening the bell marks it as seen.** The counter goes to zero as soon as you open the
  panel; new ones stay highlighted while you have it open.
- **Mark as read** removes the highlight from all of them without closing the panel.
- **View all activity** takes you to the **Activity** widget on [Home](/en/docs/gestion/dashboard),
  which shows the 7 most recent movements on your quotes, **including** your team's (sends,
  for example). If you hid that widget, bring it back from the **Library** when you
  customize Home.

### Read per user, on all your devices

What you've already seen is saved **to your user**, not to the browser: if you open the
bell on your phone, those notifications no longer count as new on your computer. Each
team member has their own read state: you opening the bell doesn't clear it for your
teammates. If you work in several organizations, each one keeps its own read state.

## Email, Slack, and Teams alerts

In **Settings › Notifications › Notifications** there's a matrix of 8 events by 3
channels (Email, Slack, and Teams). You check the boxes you want and they apply from
then on to the whole organization.

<DmNotifMatrix lang="en" />

## Requirements

- **Permission:** Settings, to change the matrix.
- **Plan:** all.
- **Channels:** email goes to the account owner, except the task reminder, which goes to each assignee. Slack and Teams must be connected (see below).

## The events

| Event | When it fires | Triggered by |
|---|---|---|
| **Your client viewed the quote** | The first time the client opens the link (see below what counts as a view). | The client |
| **Quote approved** | The client signs the proposal, in full or in part, from the link. | The client |
| **Quote rejected** | The client confirms the rejection from the link. | The client |
| **Payment received** | Cord confirms an online payment that settles the quote. | Online payment |
| **Quote about to expire** | Exactly 3 days before the validity date, if it's still sent or viewed. | The daily process |
| **Overdue payment** | The day after the payment of an approved or invoiced quote comes due without being paid. | The daily process |
| **Someone joined the team** | An invitee accepts your invitation. | Your team |
| **Task reminder** | Every morning, from 8:00 in your business's time zone, if someone has tasks due today or overdue. Email only, sent to each assignee (not to the owner), at most once a day per person. | The daily process |

### What counts as a "view"

The view alert is there so you know **your client** opened the proposal, so Cord
discards anything that isn't the client:

- **Your team doesn't count.** If you open the link signed in to the organization, you see the preview notice and nothing fires.
- **Link preview generators don't count.** Pasting the link into WhatsApp, Slack, or an email doesn't mark it as viewed.
- **It's measured with the page open and visible.** The view is recorded from the link itself when the tab is visible, not when the link loads in the background.
- **Only once.** The alert goes out when the quote moves from *Sent* to *Viewed*. Reloading or reopening doesn't repeat it.

More in [What your client sees](/en/docs/interaccion/vista-del-cliente).

### What doesn't trigger an alert

- **Messages, per-line comments, and counteroffers.** They stay in the conversation; general chat messages and counteroffers also appear in the [bell](#the-bell-in-the-top-bar) (per-line comments don't). None of them trigger email, Slack, or Teams. To receive them, create a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/primer-workflow) with **A message is posted on a quote**.
- **What your team does:** **Mark approved**, **Mark rejected**, and **Record payment** change the status but don't send these alerts.
- **Internal approval requests:** they reach Slack with buttons to decide and Teams with a link to Cord, separately from this matrix (see [Internal approvals](/en/docs/cotizacion/aprobaciones-internas)).

## Before you save for the first time

Until you save the matrix, Cord alerts you **by email** about three events: quote
approved, rejected, and payment received, and it sends the **Task reminder**. Once you save
the screen, your selection rules: you can turn off any of those four or turn on the others.

## Channels

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Email">

Goes to the business owner's account. It isn't sent to the whole team: if several
people need to know, use Slack or Teams. The email carries the quote number, client,
total with its currency, and a button to open the quote.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Slack">

In the **Slack channel** section of the same screen, paste the URL of a **Slack
incoming webhook** and check the Slack column for the events you want. The message
includes the quote number, client, total, and a link to the quote. See
[Slack](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/slack).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Microsoft Teams">

Connect Teams with **Connect with Microsoft** in **Settings › Integrations** (you pick
the channel, nothing to copy), or paste the URL of a Teams Power Automate flow in
**Settings › Notifications**. Check the Teams column for the events you want. It's a
separate channel from Slack: you can have both and choose where each alert goes. See
[Microsoft Teams](/en/docs/automatizacion/integraciones/teams).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info">
**The task reminder doesn't go to the owner; it goes to each assignee.** Each person gets
only their own tasks, in a single email a day (see [Tasks](/en/docs/gestion/tareas#email-reminder)). Turning it off
here turns it off for the whole organization, which is why the email footer only invites
people with the Settings permission to turn it off. Email only.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
**"Someone joined the team" only arrives by email.** It isn't tied to a quote (no
number or amount to show in the channel), so Slack and Teams show "Not available for
this event".
</Callout>

## What Cord sends your client

Separately from this matrix, and with no setup, Cord emails the client a payment
reminder when an approved quote comes due within the next 3 days, as long as the client
has an email on file. Test organizations don't send reminders.

## Limits by plan

Alerts are on every plan. Customizing the sender and signature of emails to the client
is available from Starter (**Settings › Notifications › Email**).

## Common problems

**I didn't get the view alert.** You opened the link yourself while signed in, the quote was already *Viewed*, or the event isn't checked in your matrix (it's off by default).

**My partner doesn't get the emails.** Email only goes to the account owner; use Slack or Teams.

**Nothing arrives in Slack.** Check that the webhook URL is saved and the Slack column is checked for that event.

**I sent a quote and the bell doesn't show it.** That's by design: the bell only shows
what your client did or the money that came in. Your sends and changes are in the
quote's activity and in the **Activity** widget on Home.

**I already read my notifications and another tab still shows the number.** The counter
is calculated when each page loads: in the other tab it updates as soon as you change
pages or reload.

**My teammate still sees the counter.** Read state is per user: opening the bell only
marks it as seen for you.
