## What it is

**Home** is the first screen when you sign in to Cord: a money indicator at the top
and, below it, a board of widgets you can reorder, resize, and hide. Your full pipeline
lives in **Quotes**, with a **List** view and a **Board** view.

## Requirements

- **Permission:** every member sees Home. The analytics widgets require **Reports** and receivables aging requires **Collections**; without them, those widgets don't appear.
- **Plan:** the indicator, KPIs, lists, and tasks are on every plan. The data for **Expected cash flow**, **Pipeline health**, **Needs follow-up**, and **Expected pipeline** comes from the finance panel (Pro or higher), and **Receivables aging** from the collections module (Pro or higher); on Free and Starter those widgets appear empty.

## The money indicator

Fixed at the top is the period's amount with three metrics to choose from:

- **Closed:** what was approved in the period.
- **Collected:** what was paid in the period.
- **Quoted:** what was quoted in the period.

Next to the amount you see the change **vs. previous period** and, below, the period's chart.

## Date picker

The picker starts at **Last 30 days** and remembers your last choice in that browser.
Options: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**,
**This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **Year to date**, **All**, and
**Custom** (with **From**, **To**, and **Apply**).

As the note under the indicator says: "The range applies to revenue, trend, funnel and
rankings. Receivables, pipeline and health always show today."

## The widgets

| Widget | What it shows | Follows the range | Requires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In progress** | Total of sent and viewed quotes, how many there are, and a sparkline of what was quoted in the last 14 days. | No (today) | — |
| **Close rate** | Approved, paid, or invoiced quotes out of all quotes that left draft. | No | Reports |
| **Average ticket** | Average amount per closed quote, across your whole history. | No | Reports |
| **Needs follow-up** | *Viewed* quotes the client hasn't decided on yet. Links to Quotes. | No (today) | — |
| **Conversion funnel** | Sent → Viewed → Approved → Paid. Each bar opens Quotes with the matching filter. | Yes | Reports |
| **Pipeline** | Amount and number of quotes per status (Draft, Sent, Viewed, Approved, Paid). | No (today) | Reports |
| **Monthly trend** | Quoted versus closed over time. | Yes | Reports |
| **Receivables aging** | Receivable balances by age. | No (today) | Collections · Pro or higher |
| **Expected cash flow · 5 weeks** | Expected collections per week, with a link to **Treasury**. | No (today) | Reports · Pro or higher |
| **Pipeline health** | Average days to collect (DSO), risk concentration, margin given up, and weighted expected revenue. | No (today) | Reports · Pro or higher |
| **Clients by approval rate** | The 5 clients with the best approval rate. | Yes | Reports |
| **Products by revenue** | The 5 products with the highest amount in quotes that left draft. | Yes | Reports |
| **Needs follow-up** (list) | Open quotes with more than 7 days without activity, showing days without a reply and amount. | No (today) | Pro or higher |
| **Recent quotes** | The 5 latest quotes with their status. | No | — |
| **Activity** | The 7 most recent events (sends, views, approvals, payments). | No | — |
| **Tasks and reminders** | The most urgent tasks, grouped by due date, with a composer, a Mine/All filter, and a link to the full page (see [Tasks](/en/docs/gestion/tareas)). | No | — |

Two optional widgets start hidden and are added from the library: **Sent to paid**
(percentage of sent quotes that ended up paid; requires Reports) and **Expected
pipeline** (open pipeline weighted by probability; requires Reports and Pro or higher).

## Customize Home

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Desktop">

1. Click **Customize** in the top bar.
2. **Move** a widget by dragging it.
3. **Resize** it with **Compact** or **Expanded**.
4. **Hide** it with the hide button; it moves to the **Library**, where you can search for it ("Search widgets…") and show it again with **Add**.
5. Click **Done** to leave edit mode.

**Reset** asks "Reset the dashboard's widget order and hidden widgets?" and returns to
the original layout.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Mobile">

Below 880 px the widgets stack in a single column and can't be dragged. In
**Customize** mode, each widget shows **Move up**, **Move down**, and **Hide** buttons.
Hidden widgets come back from the **Library** just like on desktop.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Your layout is personal: it's saved to your user in the organization and follows you
across devices. It doesn't change what your teammates see. Hiding a widget doesn't
delete data.

## Quotes: List and Board

In **Quotes**, above the list you see the value of your open quotes, the value already
approved and, if there are internal approval requests, how many are **awaiting your
approval**.

### List view

The default view is a table with **Quote**, **Client**, **Terms**, **Valid until**,
**Total**, and **Status**. The filter pills carry their counts; each filter is described
in [Quote management](/en/docs/gestion/cotizaciones). Click a row to open the detail.

### Board view

Click **Board**, next to **List**. Each column shows its number of quotes and the sum of
their totals:

| Column | Statuses it groups |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Draft |
| **Sent** | Sent |
| **Viewed** | Viewed |
| **Approved** | Approved |
| **Paid** | Paid and Invoiced |

Rejected and expired quotes don't appear on the board; you find them in the List with
the **Closed** filter.

{/* MOCKUP: Board view with the five columns, each with its count and total ("Sent · 4 · MXN 186,400"), a card being dragged from Viewed to Approved with the target column highlighted in green, and the hint "↔ Drag cards to move each quote through its pipeline." */}

### Valid moves on the board

Dragging a card **runs the real action**, it doesn't just change a label. There are
only three valid destinations; the column turns green when the move is allowed:

| You drag to | From | What it does | Permission |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sent** | Draft | Sends the quote exactly like **Send to client**: activates the link, emails the client if they have an email, and on Free uses one of the month's sends. | Quotes |
| **Approved** | Sent or Viewed | Marks it as approved on the client's behalf, like **Mark approved**: enables payment and, if you asked for a deposit, creates the payment plan. No client signature is recorded. | Approvals |
| **Paid** | Approved or Invoiced | Records the payment manually, like **Record payment**, and cancels pending online charges. | Quotes |

Any other move, including moving a card back, is rejected with "That move isn't valid
in the pipeline." If the server rejects the action (for example, over permissions or the
send cap), the card returns to its column and you see the reason.

<Callout type="info">
**There's no board on mobile.** Below 880 px, Quotes shows only the list, with each
quote on two lines. To move a quote forward, open it and use the detail's buttons.
</Callout>

## Common problems

**I don't see the funnel or the trend.** Your role doesn't have the Reports permission.

**Pipeline health shows zeros.** Your plan doesn't include the finance panel (Pro or higher), or you have no open quotes.

**I changed the range and a widget didn't change.** That widget is a snapshot of today; see the table above.

**I can't drag a card.** You're on a phone, or the destination isn't valid for the current status.
