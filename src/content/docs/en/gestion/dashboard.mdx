## Home

When you log into your account you land on **Home**: a widget dashboard with a snapshot of your sales operation. At the top is **Closed this month**, with its comparison against the previous period, and below it the widgets: indicators (to close, close rate, needing follow-up), conversion funnel, pipeline, monthly trend, receivables aging, expected cash flow, pipeline health, client and product rankings, quotes that need follow-up, recent quotes, activity, and your **Tasks**.

With **Customize** you can reorder and hide widgets. Some depend on your role's permissions: for example, the analytics widgets only appear if your role has access to analytics.

## Your Sales Pipeline

The full list of your quotes lives in **Quotes**, in the main menu. At the top you'll see the total value of your open quotes, the value already approved, and, if applicable, how many are waiting on your internal approval — a quick snapshot of your pipeline without adding anything up by hand.

### 1. List View
By default, you'll see the **List** view: a dense, spreadsheet-style table, ideal if you handle a high volume and need to scan folio, client, terms, validity, total, and status at a glance.
- Use the filter pills above the table to narrow by pipeline stage: All, Open, Needs approval, Approved, Paid, or Closed (rejected/expired).
- Click any row to open that quote's full details.

### 2. Board View
Click **Board**, next to **List**, to switch views. This view organizes your quotes into columns based on their status, allowing you to quickly understand the health of your sales pipeline.
- **Draft:** Half-finished quotes.
- **Sent:** Waiting for the client to open them.
- **Viewed:** The client has opened the link (perfect time to call!).
- **Approved:** The client accepted the terms.
- **Paid:** Collected deals; invoiced quotes share this column.

**How to use it:** drag a card into the next column to move its status forward. The board only accepts three moves — from Draft to Sent, from Sent or Viewed to Approved, and from Approved to Paid —; any other move (including dragging a card back) is rejected. Cord automates most of these changes when the client interacts with the link, so dragging is for the cases where you need to do it yourself.

<Callout type="warning">
**Dragging runs the real action, it doesn't just change the label:**
- **To Sent:** sends the quote just like **Send to client** — it generates the link, emails the client if they have an email on file and, on the Free plan, uses one of your monthly sends.
- **To Approved:** marks it approved on the client's behalf, just like **Mark approved**: it enables payment and, if you requested a deposit, creates the payment plan. No client signature is recorded.
- **To Paid:** records the payment manually, just like **Record payment**, and cancels any online charges still pending.
</Callout>
