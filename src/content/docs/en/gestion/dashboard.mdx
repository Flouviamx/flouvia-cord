## What it is

**Home** is the first screen when you sign in to Cord. At the top, pinned, is the **money
indicator** with the date picker; below it, a board of widgets you can reorder, resize,
hide, and extend from the **Widget library**. Your full pipeline lives in **Quotes**,
with a **List** view and a **Board** view.

<DmHome lang="en" />

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Permission | Every member sees Home. The analytics widgets require **Reports**; **Receivables aging** and **Overdue invoices** require **Collections**. A widget your permission doesn't allow isn't drawn and doesn't appear in the library. |
| Plan | The indicator, KPIs, funnel, trend, rankings, lists, and tasks are on every plan. **Expected cash flow**, **Pipeline health**, and **Needs follow-up** take their data from the finance panel, as do **Receivables aging** and **Expected pipeline**: all five exist from Pro; on Free and Starter they don't appear and the library doesn't offer them. |

## Everything in your business currency and time zone

Every amount on Home is expressed in your **business currency** (the one in
**Settings**), even if you sell in several. Each sale in another currency is converted
with the exchange rate set on that quote or invoice and, when it has none, with today's
published rate. If no exchange rate is available for a currency, those sales **are left
out of the sum** instead of being added with a made-up rate. When you sell in more than
one currency, this note appears under the indicator:

> Amounts in {currency}. Sales in {currencies} are converted with the exchange rate set on each quote or invoice, or with today's published rate when there is none.

and, if one was left out: "No exchange rate is available for {currencies}, so those
sales are not included for now."

Days are counted in your **business time zone**: "today", "yesterday", and the cut-off of
each day are yours, not the server's.

## The money indicator

At the very top, pinned (it can't be moved or hidden), is the period amount with three
metrics to choose from:

| Metric | What it adds up | Date it uses |
|---|---|---|
| **Closed** | The total of won quotes (Approved, Paid, or Invoiced). | The approval date. |
| **Collected** | Money that came in: quote and invoice payments, minus refunds. Same definition as the **Payments received** report. | The payment date. |
| **Quoted** | The total of quotes that left draft. | The creation date. |

Next to the amount you see the change **vs. previous period** and, below, the period's
chart with the previous period as a reference. If the previous period adds up to zero, no
change is shown: a "+100%" against nothing says nothing.

## Date picker

The picker starts at **Last 30 days**. Options: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7
days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This month**, **Last month**, **This
quarter**, **Year to date**, **Last 12 months**, and **Custom** (with **From**, **To**,
**Cancel**, and **Apply**). Home keeps 12 months of history, so you can't pick an
earlier date.

- **The range is remembered in your browser**, not in the page address. When you come
  back to Home in the same browser you see the last range you picked; in another browser
  or device it starts at Last 30 days. An old link with `?rango=` is honored once and the
  address is cleaned up.
- Changing the range updates the indicator, KPIs, funnel, trend, and rankings without
  reloading the page. Cards waiting for new data are marked busy instead of showing the
  previous range's numbers.

As the note under the indicator says: "The range applies to revenue, trend, funnel and
rankings. Receivables, pipeline and health always show today."

### Scope tag: Today, Range, or All time

Every widget with data carries a tag that says which time window it measures. Hover over
it to see the explanation:

| Tag | Meaning (tooltip text) |
|---|---|
| **Today** | "A snapshot of right now; it does not follow the date picker." |
| **Range** | "Follows the date range selected above." |
| **All time** | "Your whole history, regardless of the range." |

That way you don't compare a range number with a snapshot of today without noticing.

## KPIs with change

**Close rate**, **Average ticket**, and the optional **Collected** follow the range, are
compared against the previous period, and carry a mini chart.

| KPI | How it is calculated | Subtitle | Change | Mini chart |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Close rate** | Of the quotes that **went out** in the range (by creation date), what percentage has been won. It is a cohort: it doesn't mix what was created in one period with what closed in another. | "{g} of {n} sent in the range" or "No quotes sent in this range" | In **points** (38% → 43% = 5 pts), not percent. | Progress bar with the rate. |
| **Average ticket** | What closed in the range divided by the number of sales that make it up (by approval date). | "across {n} closed quotes" or "No closed sales in this range" | In %. | Closed amount per day. |
| **Collected** (optional) | Money that came in during the range, net of refunds. | "vs. {amount} the previous period" or "No previous period to compare" | In %. | Collected amount per day. |

Comparison rules:

- **The previous period** has the same number of days and ends the day before your range
  starts. For "Last 30 days" it's the previous 30 days; for "This month" mid-month, the
  same number of days right before.
- **No base, no change.** If the previous period is zero, or falls before the 12 months
  of history (for example with **Last 12 months**), the arrow isn't drawn and the
  subtitle says "No previous period to compare".
- **The mini chart** goes by day up to about 45 days; for longer ranges it groups by week.
- A KPI without data shows a dash (—), not a zero.

### Optional widgets (start hidden)

These live in the library until you add them. A new widget never shows up by surprise on
a board you already customized.

| Widget | What it shows | Scope | Requires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sent to paid** | Percentage of sent quotes that ended up paid, with the Sent / Paid breakdown. "Conversion across your whole history". | All time | Reports |
| **Expected pipeline** | Probability-weighted open pipeline today, with the unweighted open pipeline. | Today | Reports · Pro or higher |
| **Collected** | KPI with change and mini chart: "Money that came in during the range and how it compares to the previous period". | Range | Reports |
| **Days to close** | "Average days from creating a quote to winning it". | All time | Reports |
| **Overdue invoices** | "{n} overdue · {amount}" and the overdue invoices that weigh the most, with "{n} d overdue" and their balance. Link **View all →** to Invoices. | Today | Collections |
| **Sales leaderboard** | "Who on the team closes the most value": the 5 members with the highest closed amount. Link **View all →** to Performance. | All time | Reports |

<Callout type="info" title="Plan and permission are not the same">
Without the **permission**, the widget disappears: it isn't drawn, its data doesn't
reach your browser, and the library doesn't offer it. Without the **plan**, the same happens
with Expected cash flow, Pipeline health, Needs follow-up, Receivables aging, and
Expected pipeline: they don't appear, instead of showing empty or zero as if your
receivables were empty.
</Callout>

## Customize Home

### Entering and leaving edit mode

1. Click **Customize** (sliders icon) in the top bar.
2. Three round icon buttons replace it, each with its name in a tooltip on hover:
   - **Library** (grid with a +): opens the [Widget library](#the-widget-library). It
     shows a counter with how many widgets you have to add.
   - **Reset** (undo arrow): asks "Reset the dashboard's widget order and hidden
     widgets?" and, if you confirm, goes back to the default order, sizes, and widgets.
     Optional widgets go back to the library.
   - **Done** (check mark, navy fill): leaves edit mode.
3. While you edit, widget content doesn't respond to clicks (so you don't open a quote by
   accident while dragging) and each widget shows its control bar.

### Desktop

- **Move:** drag the widget from anywhere on it; the others rearrange as you move it and
  the page scrolls by itself near the edge. **Move up** and **Move down** do the same with
  one click.
- **Size:** **Compact** or **Expanded**. It only appears on widgets that have two sizes; an
  indicator without a chart or detail has a single honest size. In Expanded, lists use two
  columns and KPIs put the mini chart beside the number.
- **Hide:** the X button. The widget moves to the library and the **Library** button
  pulses once and adds one to its counter. Hiding a widget doesn't delete data or change
  calculations.

### Keyboard

All of edit mode works with a keyboard:

- <kbd>Tab</kbd> goes through each widget's **Move up**, **Move down**, **Compact**,
  **Expanded**, and **Hide**; focus stays on the widget you moved.
- <kbd>Escape</kbd> closes the library if it's open and returns focus to its button;
  otherwise it leaves edit mode and returns focus to **Customize**. During a drag, it
  cancels it and the widget goes back to its place.
- Screen readers announce "{widget} moved", "{widget} added to the board", and
  "{widget} hidden; find it in the library".

### Mobile

Below 880 px widgets stack in one column and can't be dragged. In edit mode, each widget
shows **Move up**, **Move down**, and **Hide** as 44 px buttons; the size selector isn't
shown. The three top-bar buttons are also 44 px and have no tooltip.

## The widget library

<DmWidgetLibrary lang="en" />

Click **Library** in edit mode. On desktop it opens as a **floating card** anchored to the
button; on mobile, as a **bottom sheet** over a dimmed background.

- **Header:** "Widget library" and the summary "{n} to add · {m} on screen". The X
  ("Close library") closes it.
- **Search:** "Search widgets…" searches the name, the description, and the category.
  With no matches it says "No results".
- **Categories**, each with its count: **All widgets**, **To add**, and one per group:
  **KPIs**, **Pipeline**, **Finance**, **Rankings**, and **Activity** (a widget without a
  group falls under **Other**). Group categories only appear when the board has more than
  one group.
- **Cards:** each widget comes with a **thumbnail** drawn with the same data it has on
  your board today (your range, your numbers), its name, and its description. The ones
  you can add come first.
- **Add:** the **+** button ("Add {widget}") puts it on the board, scrolls it into view,
  and highlights it for a moment. The ones already visible show a check mark and **On
  screen**, disabled.
- **It isn't modal:** with the library open on desktop you can keep rearranging the board
  and add several widgets in a row. <kbd>Escape</kbd> or a click outside closes it. On
  mobile, the sheet closes by itself after you add so you see where the widget landed,
  and the search box isn't focused automatically (the keyboard would cover the sheet).
- **What you can't use doesn't appear.** A widget your permission or plan doesn't allow
  isn't in the library, and a layout saved before you lost access doesn't bring it back.

## Where your layout is saved

Your layout (order, sizes, and hidden widgets) is **personal**: it's saved in your
browser and in your user for that organization, so it follows you to other devices. If
you change the layout on two devices, the most recent complete change wins, without
mixing the two. It doesn't change what your teammates see, and each organization you
belong to has its own layout.

Every screen with widgets saves its own separately: Home, each report in
[Reports](/en/docs/gestion/informes), [Payments](/en/docs/pagos/dashboard),
[Collections](/en/docs/pagos/cartera-cobranza), [AI collections](/en/docs/pagos/cobranza-ia),
and Performance use the same buttons and the same library.

## Quotes: List and Board

In **Quotes**, above the list you see the value of your open quotes, the value already
approved and, if there are internal approval requests, how many are **awaiting your
approval**.

### List view

The default view is a table with **Quote**, **Client**, **Terms**, **Valid until**,
**Total**, and **Status**. The filter pills carry their counts; each filter is described
in [Quote management](/en/docs/gestion/cotizaciones). Click a row to open the detail.

### Board view

Click **Board**, next to **List**. Each column shows its number of quotes and the sum of
their totals:

| Column | Statuses it groups |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Draft |
| **Sent** | Sent |
| **Viewed** | Viewed |
| **Approved** | Approved |
| **Paid** | Paid and Invoiced |

Rejected and expired quotes don't appear on the board; you find them in the List with
the **Closed** filter.

<DmQuotesIndex lang="en" view="board" />

### Valid moves on the board

Dragging a card **runs the real action**, it doesn't just change a label. There are
only three valid destinations; the column turns green when the move is allowed:

| You drag to | From | What it does | Permission |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sent** | Draft | Sends the quote exactly like **Send to client**: activates the link, emails the client if they have an email, and on Free uses one of the month's sends. | Quotes |
| **Approved** | Sent or Viewed | Marks it as approved on the client's behalf, like **Mark approved**: enables payment and, if you asked for a deposit, creates the payment plan. No client signature is recorded. | Approvals |
| **Paid** | Approved or Invoiced | Records the payment manually, like **Record payment**, and cancels pending online charges. | Quotes |

Any other move, including moving a card back, is rejected with "That move isn't valid
in the pipeline." If the server rejects the action (for example, over permissions or the
send cap), the card returns to its column and you see the reason.

<Callout type="info">
**There's no board on mobile.** Below 880 px, Quotes shows only the list, with each
quote on two lines. To move a quote forward, open it and use the detail's buttons.
</Callout>

## Common problems

**I don't see the funnel or the trend, and they aren't in the library either.** Your role
doesn't have the Reports permission. Ask whoever manages the team.

**I don't see Expected cash flow, Pipeline health, or Needs follow-up.** Your plan
doesn't include the finance panel (Pro or higher) or your role lacks the Reports permission.

**Pipeline health or Expected cash flow shows zeros.** You have no open quotes and no
collections expected in the coming weeks.

**I changed the range and a widget didn't change.** Check its tag: **Today** and **All
time** don't follow the picker.

**The change doesn't appear.** The previous period is zero or falls outside the 12 months
of history.

**An amount looks low and I sell in several currencies.** Read the note under the
indicator: a currency without an available exchange rate is left out of the sum until
one exists.

**I see a different range on another device.** The range is remembered per browser; the
board layout does follow you.

**I can't drag a widget.** You're on a phone (use **Move up** and **Move down**) or you
haven't clicked **Customize**.

**I can't drag a card on the Quotes board.** You're on a phone, or the destination isn't
valid for the current status.
