## What it is

The **Custom report** is the explorer in [Reports](/en/docs/gestion/informes): "Choose
how to group and what to measure. Counts the quotes created in the range and what
happened to them." You decide the row (**Group by**) and the columns (**Metrics**), and
Cord builds the same kind of table report as the others: KPIs compared against the
previous period, a chart, a sortable table with totals, and **Export CSV**.

When a configuration works for you, you **save** it with a name: it stays in the report
picker for the whole team and, if you want, it reaches you **by email** every Monday or
every 1st with the CSV attached.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Plan | Every plan, including Free. |
| Permission | **Reports**, to view, build, save, and receive custom reports. |
| Edit or delete a saved report | Whoever saved it, or a **Super admin** or **Admin** of the organization. |
| Schedule email delivery | Only whoever saved the report. A Super admin or Admin can turn it off, not on. |

## Opening it

1. Go to **Reports** (side menu › **Insights**).
2. Open the report picker and choose **Custom report**, in the **Custom** group.
3. The first time, it starts grouped by **Client** with **Quotes**, **Sales**, **Sold**,
   and **Close rate**, over **Last 30 days**.

Above the table is the explorer bar: **Group by**, the **Metrics** chips and, on the
right, **Save**.

## The cohort: which quotes are included

The explorer measures **a single cohort**: the quotes **created in the range** that left
draft (Sent, Viewed, Approved, Paid, Invoiced, Rejected, or Expired). Each row answers:
"of what was quoted in this period, what happened?".

- **Drafts don't count.**
- **A sale counts in the cohort where the quote was created**, even if it was won after
  the range ended. That's why the explorer's Sold may not match **Sales over time**, which
  counts sales by approval date.
- **Dates in your time zone** and **all amounts in your business currency**, with the
  same conversion as the rest of Reports: the rate set on the quote first, today's
  published rate after that; a currency without a rate is left out of the sum and the
  currency note says so. See
  [Dates, time zone, and currency](/en/docs/gestion/informes#dates-time-zone-and-currency).

## Group by

Each option decides what a row is. There are ten:

| Group by | A row is | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Client** | One client. | Quotes without a client are gathered under **No client**. The row and the bar open the client's record. |
| **Product** | A catalog product or, for free-text lines, its description. | Measures **per line**, not per quote (see below). A catalog product opens its record; a free-text line has no link. Lines the client didn't approve in a partial approval don't count. |
| **Team member** | The person who created or duplicated the quote. | Without a creator (before attribution or created via the API): **No team member**. |
| **Status** | The quote's **current** status: Sent, Viewed, Approved, Paid, Invoiced, Rejected, or Expired. | Useful to see where the cohort ended up. |
| **Month** | A creation month. | In chronological order. Each bar opens that month grouped by **Week**. |
| **Week** | A creation week, Monday to Sunday. | Each bar opens that week grouped by **Day**. |
| **Day** | A creation day. | In chronological order. |
| **Country** | The client's country, named in your account language. | Clients without a country: **No data**. |
| **Client tier** | The tier assigned to the client. | Clients without a tier: **No data**. |
| **Currency** | The quote's sale currency (MXN, USD, EUR…). | Each row's amounts are still expressed in your business currency: the row tells you how much you sold **in** that currency, converted. |

By **Client**, **Product**, **Team member**, **Status**, **Country**, **Client tier**,
and **Currency**, rows are sorted by the first metric, highest to lowest, with empty ones
last. By **Month**, **Week**, and **Day**, in chronological order; only periods with at
least one quote appear. The table shows up to 500 rows.

## Metrics

Check the chips for the metrics you want to see. The exact definitions:

| Metric | Grouping by quote | Grouping by Product |
|---|---|---|
| **Quotes** | Number of quotes in the cohort. | Number of quotes that include the product. |
| **Quoted** | Sum of those quotes' **total** (including taxes and minus withholdings). | Sum of the product's **lines**: negotiated (or list) price times quantity, before taxes. |
| **Sales** | How many were won: Approved, Paid, or Invoiced. | How many won quotes include the product. |
| **Sold** | Total of the won quotes. | Sum of the product's lines in won quotes. |
| **Close rate** | Sales over Quotes, as a whole percentage. | Same, counting quotes that include the product. |
| **Average ticket** | Sold over Sales. | The product's Sold over the won quotes that include it. |
| **Lost** | How many ended up Rejected or Expired. | How many quotes with the product ended up Rejected or Expired. |
| **Days to close** | Average days from creation to approval for won quotes, with one decimal. | Not available. |
| **Units** | Not available. | Units of the product in won quotes. |
| **Gross margin** | Not available. | Sold minus cost, only on lines with a cost captured when quoting. |

### Which metrics don't apply to which grouping, and why

- **Units** and **Gross margin** exist **per line**: a quote with five products has no
  single "units" or cost. They can only be checked when grouping by **Product**; in any
  other grouping they appear disabled with the hint "Only when grouping by product".
- **Days to close** exists **per quote**: the time between creating and winning it isn't
  split across its lines. When grouping by **Product** it appears disabled with the hint
  "Not available by product".
- If you change the grouping and a checked metric stops applying, it's removed
  automatically. If none is left, the starting set (Quotes, Sales, Sold, and Close rate)
  comes back without the ones that don't apply.
- **Gross margin** only adds lines with a cost: a zero cost is treated as "I don't know",
  not "it was free". If no line in the row has a cost, the cell shows —.

### Up to six metrics

You can check **up to six metrics at a time**. Once you reach six, the other chips are
disabled and "Up to six metrics at a time." appears; uncheck one to pick another. At
least one always stays checked: unchecking the last one does nothing.

### How your chosen metrics are used

- **KPIs:** the first **four** checked metrics, in chip order, with their comparison
  against the previous period (in percent; close rate in points).
- **Chart:** the first money metric you checked (if there's none, the first metric). By
  Month, Week, or Day, vertical bars with every period; in other groupings, the first 10
  rows as horizontal bars.
- **Total row:** the values for the **whole range**, not the sum of the rows: a rate or a
  ticket can't be added. That's why the Quotes total when grouping by Product can be lower
  than the sum of the rows (a quote with two products counts once).

## Applying changes and sharing with a link

Every change in **Group by** or in the metrics applies on its own and reloads the table.
(If your browser doesn't run JavaScript, the **Apply** button appears.)

**The configuration lives in the page address**: the report, the range, the grouping,
and the metrics. Copy the link and whoever opens it, with the Reports permission, sees
exactly the same report under their own permissions. **Export CSV** downloads exactly
what you see, named `cord-explorar-{from}_{to}.csv`; the file format is described in
[Export CSV](/en/docs/gestion/informes#export-csv).

## Drill-down: from month to week to day

Grouped by **Month**, each bar in the chart opens that month grouped by **Week**; grouped
by **Week**, each bar opens that week grouped by **Day**. You keep the same metrics. The
period is trimmed to your range. Grouped by **Client** or **Product**, the bar opens the
record.

On desktop, a click (or <kbd>Enter</kbd>) navigates directly. On mobile, the first tap
shows the value and the tooltip includes **View detail →**.

## Saving a report

<DmSaveReport lang="en" />

1. Set up the grouping and metrics.
2. Click **Save**. **Save report** opens.
3. Type a **Name** of 1 to 80 characters (for example, "Sales by country this quarter").
   Repeated spaces are collapsed into one.
4. Choose **Email delivery**: **No delivery**, **Every Monday**, or **On the 1st of every
   month**. Below it says: "It goes to you, covering the period that just ended, with the
   CSV attached."
5. Click **Save**. The page reloads with the saved report open.

What is saved and what isn't:

- **Saved:** the name, the grouping, the metrics, and the delivery frequency.
- **The date range isn't saved.** When you open a saved report, the range is the link's,
  or the last one you used in the Custom report in that browser, or Last 30 days. That way
  the same report works for this month and the next.

### Where it appears

Saved reports appear in the report picker, under **Custom**, in alphabetical order,
labeled **Saved report**. When you open one, its name becomes the page and picker title.

They're **shared with the whole organization**: anyone with the Reports permission sees
and opens them. If someone else saved it, "Saved by another team member" appears under
the bar.

### Cap

Each organization can have up to **50 saved reports**. At the cap, Cord says "You reached
the maximum of 50 saved reports. Delete one to save another." It isn't a plan limit: it's
the same on every plan.

## Editing, duplicating, and deleting

<DmReportExplorer saved lang="en" />

With a saved report open, what you can do depends on who you are:

| Action | Whoever saved it | Super admin or Admin | Any other member with Reports |
|---|---|---|---|
| View it, change the range, export CSV | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Save changes** (name, grouping, metrics) | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Save as new** (a copy with another name) | Yes | Yes | — |
| **Save** (creates a new report of your own) | — | — | Yes |
| **Delete** | Yes | Yes | No |
| Schedule or change **Email delivery** | Yes | Only turn it off | No (yes on their own copy) |

- **Save changes:** change the grouping or metrics, click **Save changes**, and confirm in
  the dialog. The dialog includes **Save as new** to create a copy without touching the
  original. The copy is yours and takes the **Email delivery** chosen in the dialog; if it
  has delivery, it goes to you.
- **Delete:** the trash icon button, next to **Save changes**. It asks "Delete this saved
  report? This does not delete any data." Deleting it also turns off its email delivery.
- **If you can't edit it**, you see the **Save** button (without the trash icon). When you
  click it, **Save report** opens with an empty name and creates a **new report of your
  own** with the grouping and metrics on screen; the original isn't touched. That copy is
  yours: you can edit it, delete it, and schedule its email delivery.

## Receiving it by email

### Scheduling it

When you save (or with **Save changes** on a report you saved), choose under **Email
delivery**:

| Option | When it arrives | Which period it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Every Monday** | On Monday, in your business time zone. | The full previous week, Monday to Sunday. |
| **On the 1st of every month** | On the 1st, in your business time zone. | The full previous month. |
| **No delivery** | It doesn't arrive. | — |

- **It only goes to you**, at the email address of your Cord user. You can't add
  recipients: if someone else on the team wants it, they save their own copy and schedule
  it.
- **The first email arrives in the next period.** If you schedule weekly delivery on a
  Wednesday, the first one arrives the following Monday with the week that just ended; a
  monthly one scheduled on October 8 arrives on November 1 with all of October. Switching
  from weekly to monthly (or back) restarts that clock.
- **Nobody schedules it for you.** An email that would reach you is only turned on by
  you. What someone else sees when they open your saved report:
  - A **Super admin** or **Admin**, if the report **has** delivery: under **Email
    delivery**, only **No delivery** and the current frequency, with the note "As an admin
    you can turn this delivery off; only the person who saved the report can schedule it."
  - An admin, if the report has **no** delivery: "Email delivery is scheduled by whoever
    saved the report."
  - Any other member: the **Save** button creates their own copy, and that one they can
    schedule.

### What the email contains

<DmScheduledReportEmail lang="en" />

- **Subject:** "{name} · {period}", for example "Sales by country · Sep 1, 2026 – Sep 30,
  2026".
- **Header:** your business name, the report name, and the period dates.
- **KPIs:** the report's first four metrics with their value for the period and, below,
  "vs. {value} the previous period". In the email, the previous period is the **full
  previous calendar period**: the previous Monday-to-Sunday week, or the whole previous
  month (September against all of August, even though August has 31 days). On the Reports
  screen, by contrast, the comparison is still against the same number of days right
  before.
- "The full detail is in the attached CSV, ready to open in Excel or Google Sheets."
- **Open in Cord:** opens that saved report with exactly that period. It asks you to sign
  in and requires the Reports permission.
- **Attachment:** `cord-informe-{from}_{to}.csv`, in the same format as **Export CSV**.
- **Footer:** "You receive this email because you scheduled this report. Change or turn it
  off from Reports in Cord."

The email uses your account language and amounts in your business currency, without
decimals.

### Changing or turning it off

Open the saved report, click **Save changes**, and choose another frequency or **No
delivery**. It also stops arriving if the report is deleted. A Super admin or Admin can
turn off delivery on someone else's report (choosing **No delivery** and **Save
changes**) or delete it.

### When it stops arriving without you turning it off

- **You leave the team** or your user is deactivated in that organization.
- **You lose the Reports permission.**

In both cases the report isn't sent and its clock doesn't advance: if you get access back,
the next delivery covers the latest closed period. In the meantime nobody else receives
your numbers.

### If delivery fails

If the email couldn't be sent, the period **isn't marked as sent** and is retried on the
next run, the following day. A run that doesn't happen doesn't skip the week either: the
next one sends it. Two emails for the same period never arrive.

## Common problems

**A metric is disabled.** It doesn't apply to that grouping: Units and Gross margin only
by Product; Days to close not by Product. Or you already checked six.

**Sold doesn't match Sales over time.** The explorer counts the cohort of quotes
**created** in the range; Sales over time counts sales by **approval** date.

**The Quotes total is lower than the sum of the rows.** Grouped by Product, a quote with
several products appears in several rows but counts once in the total.

**I opened a saved report and the range isn't the one it had.** Saved reports don't save
the range: they use the link's or the last one you used in that browser.

**Monday's email didn't arrive.** Check that you saved the report, that its delivery says
**Every Monday**, that you're still active with the Reports permission, and that you
didn't schedule it this same week (the first one arrives in the next period). Also check
your spam folder.

**I can't schedule delivery.** Only whoever saved the report can schedule it. Open the
report, click **Save** to create your own copy, and schedule that one.

**"You reached the maximum of 50 saved reports."** Delete one that's no longer used; the
organization shares the cap.
