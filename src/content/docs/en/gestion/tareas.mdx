## What it is

Tasks are your team's to-dos: the next call, the pending delivery, the follow-up on a
quote. Each one has an assignee, a due date, and a priority, and Cord emails you when it's
due.

They show in two places:

- The **Tasks and reminders** widget on [Home](/en/docs/gestion/dashboard), with the most urgent ones.
- The **Tasks** page in the sidebar (shortcut <kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>), with every task and the completed ones.

## Requirements

- **Permission:** Quotes, Collections, or Clients (any of the three) to create, edit, complete, or delete. Without any of them you see tasks but can't change them.
- **Plan:** all.
- **Who sees them:** every member of the organization.

## Create a task

<Steps>
1. **From any screen:** click **Create** in the top bar and choose **Task** (or press <kbd>T</kbd>). Or use the composer on Home or on the **Tasks** page.
2. Type the task, for example "Call Luis to ask whether he reviewed the quote". Up to 200 characters.
3. Optional: pick the date with the **Today**, **Tomorrow**, or **Monday** shortcuts, or with the calendar.
4. Optional: turn on **High priority** and choose the **assignee**.
5. Click **Add** (or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>). The task shows up in the list without reloading the page.
</Steps>

If you don't choose an assignee, the task belongs to whoever typed it. In the composer you
can pick **Unassigned** on purpose.

## Organize your day

- **Groups by urgency:** Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later, and No date. "Today" is the day in your business's time zone, not the server's (change it in Settings › General).
- **Filters:** **Mine**, **All**, and **Unassigned**; on the page, also **Open** and **Completed**. With a single person on the account, the assignee filter doesn't show.
- **Complete:** click the circle. For a few seconds you can click **Undo**. Under **Completed** you see who completed it and when, and you can **Reopen** it.
- **Task menu (···):** **Edit** (title, notes, date, priority, and assignee), **Snooze until** tomorrow, Monday, or in a week, **Assign to me**, and **Delete**. Delete asks for confirmation because it can't be undone.
- **Linked to a document:** if the task comes from a quote or invoice, its number and the client show as a link to the document.
- In the sidebar, **Tasks** shows how many of yours or unassigned ones are due today or overdue; the number is red when some are overdue.

## Email reminder

Every morning, from 8:00 in your business's time zone, each assignee gets an email with
their tasks due today and overdue. An unassigned task goes to whoever created it. If no one on the team
created it (a workflow, the API, or a chargeback added it) or that person is no longer on
the team, it goes to the account owner.

- One email a day per person, at most.
- If you snooze a task, it reminds again on its new date.
- An overdue task that isn't completed shows up in each day's email until you complete or snooze it.
- To turn it off for the whole organization, uncheck **Task reminder** in [Settings › Notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones).

## Tasks Cord creates for you

When a payment operation needs your attention, Cord adds the task with **high priority**:

- **Chargeback response** (titled "Responder contracargo" with the amount): when a client disputes a card charge, with the deadline to submit evidence as the due date (see [Disputes](/en/docs/pagos/disputas)).
- **SPEI refund transfer** (titled "Transferir reembolso SPEI" with the amount): when you request a refund of a SPEI payment, which is transferred outside Cord, linked to the quote and assigned to whoever requested it (see [Refunds](/en/docs/pagos/reembolsos)).

## Other ways to create tasks

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Workflows">

The **Create a task** action in [Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/acciones)
adds a task with a title (it can use event data, such as the quote number or client)
and, optionally, **Due in (days)**, up to 365. If the event is about a quote, the task
is linked to it. Example: "when the client rejects a quote, create a task to call them
tomorrow".

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="API">

`POST /api/v1/tareas` creates a task with `titulo`, an optional `due_date`
(YYYY-MM-DD), and an optional `cotizacion_id`. It requires a key with write permission.
The API creates unassigned tasks; assigning, completing, and deleting them is done in the app. See [Tasks
API](/en/docs/desarrolladores/funciones/tareas).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="MCP">

The **crear_tarea** tool in the [MCP server](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/mcp)
lets an AI assistant create a follow-up task, optionally linked to a quote and with a date.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Task events

Each task emits events you can use in [workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/disparadores)
and [webhooks](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/webhooks):

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| **A task is created** | When it's created from the app, a workflow, the API, or MCP. |
| **A task is completed** | When it's marked complete. |

Editing, snoozing, reopening, or deleting a task emits no event.

## Limits by plan

Tasks have no cap by plan.

## Common problems

**I don't see the tasks widget.** You hid it when customizing Home: bring it back from the **Library**. The **Tasks** page in the sidebar is always available.

**I can't add a task.** Your role doesn't have the Quotes, Collections, or Clients permission.

**A task is missing from the list.** Check the filter: **Mine** only shows yours. The Home widget shows the 8 most urgent; the rest are on the **Tasks** page.

**I didn't get the reminder.** It only arrives if you have tasks due today or overdue, from 8:00 in your business's time zone. Check that **Task reminder** is still on in Settings › Notifications, and your spam folder.
