## Don't let your leads go cold

Timely follow-up is the difference between a closed sale and a lost one. Cord includes a lightweight **Tasks and reminders** system: an account-wide to-do list, always visible in the widget of the same name on your Dashboard.

Although it's a small feature, it's useful to ensure no deal slips through the cracks due to a lack of follow-up.

### How to create a task

There are two paths, and both land in the same place:

1. **From any screen in the app:** click the **"Create"** button in the top bar and select **"Task"**. A quick form opens over whatever you were viewing.
2. **From the Dashboard:** in the **"Tasks and reminders"** widget, use the text field and date picker at the top of the list.

Either way, fill in:
- **Description:** for example, *"Call Luis to ask if he has reviewed the quote"*.
- **Due date** (optional): pick the day on the calendar.

Click **Add** (or **Create**, depending on where you opened it from), and the task appears immediately in your Dashboard's task widget, alongside the rest of your pending items.

<Callout type="info">
**A task belongs to your account, not to a specific quote.** There's currently no way to attach it to a particular quote from the interface — it's a general follow-up list for your operation, meant to keep a pending call or email from slipping through, regardless of which client it's tied to.
</Callout>

### Visual tracking

- Pending tasks are listed sorted by due date, soonest first.
- Once the due date has passed, the task gets a red **"Overdue"** tag so it stands out.
- Check the box next to a task to mark it done — it disappears from the pending list. The **×** button deletes it entirely.

<Callout type="info">
**Pro tip:** create a *"Follow-up call"* task right after sending the Magic Link, with a due date 48 hours after sending.
</Callout>
