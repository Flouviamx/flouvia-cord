## What it is

Tasks are your team's to-dos: the next call, the pending delivery, the follow-up on a
quote. Each one has an assignee, a due date, and a priority, and Cord emails you when it's
due.

They show in two places:

- The **Tasks and reminders** widget on [Home](/en/docs/gestion/dashboard), with the most urgent ones.
- The **Tasks** page in the sidebar (shortcut <kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>), with every task and the completed ones. Its counter tells you how many of yours are due today or overdue (see [The sidebar counter](#the-sidebar-counter)).

## Requirements

- **Permission:** Quotes, Collections, or Clients (any of the three) to create, edit, complete, or delete. Without any of them you see tasks but can't change them.
- **Plan:** all.
- **Who sees them:** every member of the organization.

## Create a task

<Steps>
1. Go to the composer in the **Tasks and reminders** widget on Home or on the **Tasks** page.
2. Type the task, for example "Call Luis to ask whether he reviewed the quote". Up to 200 characters.
3. Optional: pick the date with the **Today**, **Tomorrow**, or **Monday** (next Monday) shortcuts, or with the calendar.
4. Optional: turn on **High priority** and, if there's more than one person on the account, choose the **assignee**.
5. Click **Add** (or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>). The task shows up in the list without reloading the page.
</Steps>

**From any screen:** click **Create** in the top bar and choose **Task**, press the
<kbd>T</kbd> key (on desktop, while not typing in a field), or choose **New task** in the
[command menu](/en/docs/operacion/mapa-de-la-app#command-menu) (<kbd>⌘K</kbd>). The
**Task** option and the key show if you have the Quotes, Collections, or Clients
permission, even if you can't create anything else. **New task** opens, with the title
and the due date, without leaving the page you're on; click **Add task**. If you have
Home or the **Tasks** page open, the list updates on its own. The
task is assigned to you with normal priority; priority, notes, and assignee are changed
later with **Edit**.

**Assignee:** with a single person on the account, every task you add is yours. With a
team, the selector starts on you ("Your name (me)"); you can pick another active member or
**Unassigned**. Only an active team member can be the assignee.

**Notes** aren't entered when you create a task: you add them with **Edit** (up to 2,000
characters).

## Organize your day

- **Groups by urgency:** Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later, and No date. "Today" is the day in your business's time zone, not the server's (change it in Settings › General).
- **Filters:** on the page, **Open** and **Completed**, and by assignee **Mine**, **All**, and **Unassigned**; the Home widget only has **Mine** and **All**. With a team, both views open on **Mine**. With a single person on the account, the assignee filter doesn't show.
- **Complete:** click the circle. For a few seconds you can click **Undo**. Under **Completed** you see who completed it and when, and you can **Reopen** it.
- **Task menu (···):** **Edit** (title, notes, date, priority, and assignee), **Snooze until** tomorrow, Monday, or in a week, **Assign to me**, and **Delete**. Delete asks for confirmation because it can't be undone.
- **Linked to a quote or invoice:** if the task is linked to a quote (for example, a workflow, the API, a chargeback, or a SPEI refund created it), the quote number and the client show as a link to the quote. If it's linked to an invoice (a workflow on an invoice event, the API, or MCP created it), the link goes to the invoice.
- **Long lists:** the page shows 200 tasks per view and says below how many there are in total ("Showing 200 of 260"); the **Show more** button opens the next ones, 200 at a time (in the example, **Show 60 more**), up to 1,000. Beyond that, filter by assignee or complete the ones you already did.
- **Notes:** on the page they show below the title; in the widget, an icon tells you the task has notes.

### The sidebar counter

In the sidebar, **Tasks** shows a number when you have something due today:

- It counts **open** tasks that are **yours or unassigned** and are due **today** or
  already **overdue**. Other members' tasks, completed ones, ones due later, and ones
  without a date don't count.
- "Today" is the day in your business's time zone, the same one the urgency groups use.
- The number is gray; it turns **orange** if at least one is overdue. On hover it says,
  for example, "4 tasks due today or overdue".
- With the sidebar collapsed, the number becomes a dot: gray, or red if some are overdue.
- It's recalculated every time you open a page; with nothing due today, it doesn't show.

## Email reminder

Every morning, from 8:00 in your business's time zone, each assignee gets an email with
their tasks due today and overdue, with dates in your account's language and your country's
format. An unassigned task goes to whoever created it. If no one on the team
created it (a workflow, the API, MCP, or a chargeback added it) or that person is no longer on
the team, it goes to the account owner.

- Each person gets at most one email a day per organization. If you add a task for today, or move or assign one to today, after that person got their morning email, no second email goes out: it shows up in tomorrow's, as overdue. If that person hadn't gotten an email that day yet (nothing was due in the morning), it arrives at the next check.
- If you snooze a task, it reminds again on its new date.
- An overdue task that isn't completed shows up in each day's email until you complete or snooze it.
- Tasks without a due date don't trigger a reminder.
- The **Open my tasks** button in the email opens the **Tasks** page with the **Mine** filter.
- **Test mode** doesn't send reminders.
- It's turned off for the whole organization, not per person: someone with the Settings permission unchecks **Task reminder** in [Settings › Notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones). The email footer says so to whoever can turn it off; everyone else is asked to talk to whoever manages the account.

## Tasks Cord creates for you

When a payment operation needs your attention, Cord adds the task with **high priority**:

- **Respond to the [amount] chargeback:** when a client disputes a card charge, with the day (in your business's time zone) the evidence deadline falls on as the due date. It's linked to the payment's quote and created unassigned (see [Disputes](/en/docs/pagos/disputas)).
- **Send the [amount] SPEI refund:** when you request a refund of a SPEI payment, which is transferred outside Cord, linked to the quote and assigned to whoever requested it (see [Refunds](/en/docs/pagos/reembolsos)). Completing the task doesn't record the refund as paid: it only closes your to-do.

The title is written in your account's language, and these tasks emit **A task is created**
like any other.

## Other ways to create tasks

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Workflows">

The **Create a task** action in [Workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/acciones)
adds a task with a title (it can use event data, such as the quote number or client)
and, optionally, **Due in (days)**, up to 365. If the event is about a quote, the task
is linked to it; if it's about an invoice (for example **An invoice is paid** or **An
invoice becomes overdue**), it's linked to that invoice and, if the invoice came from a
quote, to the quote too. Example: "when the client rejects a quote, create a task to call them
tomorrow".

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="API">

`POST /api/v1/tareas` creates a task with `titulo`, an optional `due_date`
(YYYY-MM-DD), an optional `cotizacion_id`, and an optional `factura_id`. It requires a key with write permission.
The API creates unassigned tasks; assigning, completing, and deleting them is done in the app. See [Tasks
API](/en/docs/desarrolladores/funciones/tareas).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="MCP">

The **crear_tarea** tool in the [MCP server](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/mcp)
lets an AI assistant create a follow-up task, optionally linked to a quote or an invoice and with a date.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Task events

Each task emits events you can use in [workflows](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/disparadores)
and [webhooks](/en/docs/desarrolladores/herramientas/webhooks):

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| **A task is created** | When it's created from the app, a workflow, the API, or MCP, and when Cord creates it for you (chargeback or SPEI refund). |
| **A task is completed** | When it's marked complete. |

Editing, snoozing, reopening, or deleting a task emits no event.

## Limits by plan

Tasks have no cap by plan.

## Common problems

**I don't see the tasks widget.** You hid it when customizing Home: bring it back from the **Library**. The **Tasks** page in the sidebar is always available.

**I can't add a task.** Your role doesn't have the Quotes, Collections, or Clients permission. Without them you don't see **Task** in the **Create** menu either.

**A task is missing from the list.** Check the filter: **Mine** only shows yours. The Home widget shows the 8 most urgent; the rest are on the **Tasks** page, which shows 200 per view and opens more with **Show more**.

**I didn't get the reminder.** It only arrives if you have tasks with a due date of today or overdue, from 8:00 in your business's time zone, and once a day: what becomes due today after your email goes out shows up in tomorrow's. Someone else's task goes to them, not to you. Check that **Task reminder** is still on in Settings › Notifications, and your spam folder.
