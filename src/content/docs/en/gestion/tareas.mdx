## Don't let your leads go cold

Timely follow-up is the difference between a closed sale and a lost one. Cord includes a lightweight **Tasks and reminders** system: an organization-wide to-do list, visible in the **Tasks** widget on your Home.

Although it's a small feature, it's useful to ensure no deal slips through the cracks due to a lack of follow-up.

### How to create a task

There are two paths, and both land in the same place:

1. **From any screen in the app:** click the **"Create"** button in the top bar and select **"Task"**. A quick form opens over whatever you were viewing.
2. **From Home:** in the **"Tasks and reminders"** widget, use the text field and date picker at the top of the list.

Either way, fill in:
- **Description:** for example, *"Call Luis to ask if he has reviewed the quote"*.
- **Due date** (optional): pick the day on the calendar.

Click **Add** (or **Create**, depending on where you opened it from), and the task appears immediately in the task widget on your Home, alongside the rest of your pending items.

<Callout type="info">
**A task belongs to your whole organization.** It isn't assigned to a person: every member with access to Home sees the same list. From the interface it is created as general follow-up for your operation; tasks created by a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows) or the API can be linked to the quote that originated them.
</Callout>

### Visual tracking

- Pending tasks are listed sorted by due date, soonest first.
- Once the due date has passed, the task gets a red **"Overdue"** tag so it stands out.
- Check the box next to a task to mark it done — it disappears from the pending list. The **×** button deletes it entirely.

<Callout type="warning">
**Cord doesn't send task reminders.** When the due date arrives you don't get an email or alert: the task is only marked as overdue in the widget. To keep it in sight, check your Home often.
</Callout>

### Tasks Cord creates for you

Besides your own, Cord adds tasks automatically when a payment operation needs your attention:

- **Chargebacks:** when a client disputes a card charge, a task to respond to the chargeback appears with the amount and, as its due date, the deadline to submit evidence (see [Disputes](/en/docs/pagos/disputas)).
- **SPEI refunds:** when you request a refund of a SPEI payment, which is transferred outside Cord, a task to transfer the SPEI refund appears with the amount, linked to the quote (see [Refunds](/en/docs/pagos/reembolsos)).

<Callout type="info">
**Pro tip:** create a *"Follow-up call"* task right after sending the link, with a due date 48 hours after sending.
</Callout>
