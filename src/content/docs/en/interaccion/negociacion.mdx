  <p class="page-subtitle">The client proposes, accepts only part, or rejects from the same link. You reply with a new version without changing the link.</p>

## What it is

In Cord, negotiation happens on the document, not in an email thread. While the quote
is *Sent* or *Viewed*, the client has four ways to respond: write a message, send a
**counteroffer**, **approve only some lines**, or **reject** with a reason. Your answer
is another version of the same quote, which arrives at the same link.

## Requirements

- **Status:** *Sent* or *Viewed*. An approved, rejected, or expired quote no longer accepts counteroffers or a signature.
- **Portal option:** counteroffers and messages require **Comments and counteroffers** to be on in **Settings › Brand appearance › Client portal**. Partial approval and rejection are always available.
- **Permission:** Quotes to reply to messages and to **Modify and resend**; Approvals to **Mark approved** or **Mark rejected**.
- **Plan:** all.

## Counteroffer

<Steps>
1. In the link's **CONVERSATION** section, the client enters an amount in the proposal field (in the quote's currency) and, optionally, a message.
2. They click **Counteroffer**.
3. The proposal appears in the conversation with a distinct style, showing the proposed amount followed by the message.
</Steps>

<DmQuoteThread lang="en" view="counter" />

A counteroffer **doesn't change the quote's status** or amounts: it's a message with an
amount. To accept it, adjust the prices and resend (see below). If you don't accept it,
reply in the conversation.

## Partial approval

Before signing, the client can uncheck the lines they don't want with **Include this
line in your approval**. The link shows how many lines they'll approve and the new
total, calculated with the same engine Cord uses when saving.

When a partial approval is signed:

- The quote moves to *Approved* with the **total recalculated** from the included lines only: subtotal, per-line taxes, and withholding.
- The other lines are marked **Not included** on the link and on the detail, which shows "Partial approval — the client only signed the included lines".
- The activity logs who signed, how many lines they approved out of the total, and the approved amount against the original.
- If you asked for a deposit, it's calculated on the approved total.
- The invoice includes only the approved lines.

At least one line must stay included: with every line unchecked, the approve button is
disabled.

## Rejection with a reason

<Steps>
1. From review, the client clicks **Reject**.
2. They write a **Reason (optional — helps the seller improve)**.
3. They click **Confirm rejection**.
</Steps>

The quote moves to *Rejected*. The activity logs the client's reason (or that they
rejected it from the link without a reason), and the client sees "We notified [your
business]". A rejection can't be undone: to propose again, duplicate the quote.

## How you reply: Modify and resend

While the quote is *Sent*, *Viewed*, or *Expired*, the detail shows **Modify and resend
(V2)**; the number is the version you're about to create.

<Steps>
1. Click **Modify and resend (V2)**. The quote editor opens as **New version of COT-0149**, with the current lines and the **Version 2** tag in the summary.
2. Change products, quantities, prices, each line's tax, or **Prices include VAT**. The client, currency, and terms can't be changed here: "A new version changes lines and prices. Client, currency and terms stay as they were sent."
3. Click **Send new version** (or **⌘ Enter**). If your organization uses [internal approvals](/en/docs/cotizacion/aprobaciones-internas), the version is checked against the caps before it goes out.
</Steps>

When you resend, Cord:

- saves the new version and adds it to the detail's **Version history**, with its date and total;
- keeps the **same link**;
- emails the client again if they have an email on file;
- moves the quote back to *Sent* and restarts its validity from today;
- emits the quote-modified event with the previous total, so your automations can tell an unchanged resend from one with a discount.

Every new version restarts the validity from the day you send it, with the same length
the quote was sent with (if it was 30 days, it expires in 30 days). That's how an
*Expired* quote comes back: it returns to *Sent* with a new date. For a different
length, client, currency, or terms, use **Duplicate quote**.

## If you change the proposal while the client reads it

The link is connected in real time. If the client has it open when you resend:

1. Changed lines update without reloading, with a highlight and the previous amount for a few seconds ("was …").
2. **The proposal was updated** appears with the **See changes** button.
3. The approve button is **locked** until the client clicks **See changes**, which takes them to the lines.

On top of that, every signature carries the document revision the client had on
screen. If it doesn't match the current one, Cord **rejects the signature**, takes the
client back to review with the notice on, and never saves an approval on a version that
no longer exists.

<Callout type="info">
Cord detects changes through the document revision, which advances with any write to
the quote (lines, charges, messages, and events). That's why the notice can also appear
after someone writes in the conversation. Clicking **See changes** is enough to approve
again.
</Callout>

## What Cord notifies you about and what it doesn't

| Client action | Changes the status | Email, Slack, or Teams alert | Webhook and workflow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Message in the conversation | No | No | Yes: "A message is posted on a quote" |
| Comment on a line | No | No | Yes: "A message is posted on a quote" |
| Counteroffer | No | No | Yes: "A message is posted on a quote", flagged as a counteroffer |
| Full or partial approval | Yes, to *Approved* | Yes: **Quote approved** (by email, on by default) | Yes: "A quote is approved" |
| Rejection | Yes, to *Rejected* | Yes: **Quote rejected** (by email, on by default) | Yes: "A quote is rejected" |

Messages and counteroffers do appear in the bell on the top bar, which lists the most
recent activity. To get an alert outside Cord, create a
[workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows/primer-workflow) with the **A message is
posted on a quote** trigger, for example to notify your Slack channel. Email, Slack,
and Teams alerts are configured in [Notifications](/en/docs/gestion/notificaciones).

**Mark approved** and **Mark rejected** from the app change the status and fire the
webhook, but don't trigger the Notifications alerts, which are for the client's decisions.

## Limits by plan

Negotiation is on every plan. On Pro or higher, the quote detail receives the client's
messages live; on Free and Starter, reload the detail to see them.

## Common problems

**The client doesn't see the counteroffer field.** **Comments and counteroffers** is off, or the quote is no longer *Sent* or *Viewed*.

**Cord rejects the counteroffer.** The quote was already approved, rejected, or expired.

**The client can't approve after I resent.** They need to click **See changes** first; if the button is still disabled, check that at least one line is included.

**I don't see Modify and resend.** The quote is a draft (use **Continue editing**), it's already approved or paid, or your role doesn't have the Quotes permission.
