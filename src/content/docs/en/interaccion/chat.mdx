## The quote as a workspace

In the traditional business-to-business sales model, if a client wants to change a quantity or ask for a different discount, they reply by email, the sales rep edits the Excel file, generates a new PDF (Quote_v2_final.pdf), and sends it back.

In Cord, **the quote's link is interactive.**

### General Chat

Below the quote, in the web view, there's the **Conversation** section.
- The client can write general questions like: *"Can we adjust the delivery time to 15 days?"*
- You see it in the **Conversation** panel of the quote's details and reply from there; your reply shows up on the client's link (instantly, if they have it open). The reply isn't emailed to them.
- Each message shows who wrote it: **You** for your own and the other party's name for theirs.
- The entire conversation is logged in the history of that quote, giving sales managers visibility without having to dig through personal emails.

<Callout type="info">
**How you find out about a message:** Cord doesn't email or alert you for each client message. On the Pro, Scale, and Developer plans, the quote's details update live while you have them open; on other plans, reload the details to see new messages. If you want an alert, create a [workflow](/en/docs/automatizacion/workflows) with the **A message is posted on a quote** trigger.
</Callout>

### Counteroffer

Next to the message box, the client can enter an amount in the proposal field and send it with **Counteroffer**. The counteroffer shows up in the conversation with a distinct style, with the proposed amount and the message, and doesn't change the quote's status: if you accept it, adjust the prices with **Modify and resend** from the details. Like messages, a counteroffer doesn't trigger an email alert.

### Line-Item Comments (Product)

If the quote is very extensive (e.g., 40 different items) and the client has doubts about a specific product, they don't need to write *"Regarding line item 14, the X model server..."*.

Cord allows **line-item level comments**.
1. Next to each product's name there's a comment button, always visible (on mobile too), with a counter of that line's messages.
2. The client clicks the button on the line they care about.
3. Leaves their question: *"Can you swap this model for the 16GB RAM one?"*
4. You see the comment on that same line in the quote's details (**Comments on this line**) and reply there.

Chat and line-item comments close once the quote is approved or rejected. If you don't want to offer them, turn them off in **Settings › Brand appearance › Client portal**, with the **Comments and counteroffers** option.

### Live Update

When you adjust a quantity, change a product, or lower a price after the negotiation, use **Modify and resend** from the details: a new version is created and **the client doesn't need a new link**.

If they have the link open, they see the changes applied without reloading, plus a **The proposal was updated** notice with a **See changes** button. The approve button is locked until they review the changes, so they never sign a version that's no longer current.
