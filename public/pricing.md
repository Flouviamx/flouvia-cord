# Pricing — Cord

> Last reviewed: 2026-09-06.
>
> New subscriptions in Mexico use MXN; Spain, Germany and France use EUR.
> Other supported markets use USD. Existing subscriptions keep their currency.
> Annual billing charges the equivalent of 10 monthly
> payments for 12 months of service. Current checkout terms and an existing
> customer's locked billing currency take precedence over this reference.

## Free

- Monthly price: MXN 0 | USD 0 | EUR 0
- Active quotes: 5
- Sent quotes: 5 per calendar month; renews on the first day of each month (UTC)
- Products: 50
- Customers: 50
- Users: 1
- AI quote drafts: 3 per month
- Invoices: 3 per month
- Public API calls: 100 per month
- Branding: “Powered by Cord” remains visible
- Overage: not available; included limits are hard caps

Free has no expiry date and requires no credit card. After 5 sends, new sends
pause until the next monthly allowance or an upgrade. Active quotes are a
separate limit: closing a quote frees an active slot but does not restore sends.
There is currently no monthly collected-revenue threshold requiring an upgrade.
Online payment processing fees still apply where payments are available.

## Usage and billing notes

- “Invoice” is the shared usage dimension. In Mexico it can represent a CFDI 4.0
  issuance; in other supported markets it represents a commercial invoice.
- Overage unit prices depend on plan and billing currency and are disclosed in
  the subscription flow and account billing surface. This file does not publish
  a single universal unit price.
- Overage-enabled dimensions have safety limits designed to stop runaway usage.
- Free has no paid overage. Starter's user limit is a hard cap. From Profesional
  onward, users can generate metered overage.
- Plans can be changed or canceled according to the terms shown in the billing
  surface. Developer is not a self-service checkout plan.
- Your logo and colors are included in Free. Removing “Powered by Cord” is
  available from Starter onward. Scale adds email from your domain via SMTP.
  Custom domains for hosted quote links are not currently available.

## Availability

Cord offers signup in MX, US, CA, BR, ES, GB, DE, FR, CO, AR, CL, and PE.
Cord Payments is available to eligible businesses in MX, US, CA, BR, ES, GB,
DE, and FR. Payment availability is separate from the subscription plan.
