# Pricing — CordHQ

> Last updated: July 2026. Prices in MXN (Mexican pesos), IVA included.
> Annual billing: pay 10 months, get 2 months free.
> No contracts. Cancel anytime. Free plan forever, no credit card required.

## Free
- Price: $0 MXN/month (forever)
- Limits: 5 active quotes, 50 products, 50 clients, 3 AI drafts/month
- Features: Quote editor, public link + PDF, real-time tracking, "Powered by Cord" branding
- Best for: Testing the platform

## Starter
- Price: $240 MXN/month (billed monthly) | $200 MXN/month (billed annually)
- Limits: 50 active quotes, 500 products, 500 clients, 20 AI drafts/month, 3 CFDI/month
- Features: Everything in Free + your brand (no "Powered by"), CSV import, CFDI 4.0 invoicing
- Best for: Solo operators and freelancers

## Profesional (Most Popular)
- Price: $590 MXN/month (billed monthly) | $492 MXN/month (billed annually)
- Limits: Unlimited quotes, up to 5 users, 50 AI drafts/month, 20 CFDI/month
- Features: Everything in Starter + live tracking analytics, immutable audit log, team access
- Best for: Sales teams that need control and visibility

## Scale
- Price: $1,390 MXN/month (billed monthly) | $1,158 MXN/month (billed annually)
- Limits: Unlimited quotes, up to 15 users, 500 AI drafts/month, 100 CFDI/month
- Features: Everything in Profesional + approval workflows, autonomous AI collections agent, custom SMTP domain
- Best for: Operations requiring governance and credit control

## Developer
- Price: $2,990 MXN/month (billed monthly) | $2,492 MXN/month (billed annually)
- Limits: Unlimited quotes, unlimited users, unlimited AI, 1,000 CFDI/month, 50,000 API calls/month
- Features: Everything in Scale + full REST API access, webhooks, MCP server, Cord Elements Web Component, lowest overage rates
- Best for: Integrating Cord into your own stack or product

## Overage Pricing
- Additional CFDI: $15 MXN each
- Additional API calls: $0.10 MXN each
- Additional AI drafts: $5 MXN each
- Additional users: varies by plan
